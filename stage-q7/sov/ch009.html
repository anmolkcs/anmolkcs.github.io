When Wang Yigao turned around and saw Lu Chen standing at the courtyard gate, his eyebrows lifted in brief surprise before curling into a sneer.

Ten days ago, when his retainers had cornered Lu Chen in the alley, the boy had been pale, thin, and gasping for breath after taking a single glancing kick to the ribs. Today, although Lu Chen wore a simple black cloth robe and carried an old iron sword wrapped at his waist, his posture was straight as a javelin, and his footsteps on the wet flagstones didn’t make a sound.

Still, in Wang Yigao’s eyes, a Grade 2 Upper-Tier waste who worked ten hours a day chopping meat at the Crimson Scale Pavilion could never bridge the chasm between them. Two months ago, after consuming three Viscera-Nourishing Pills bought by his father for four hundred taels of silver, Wang Yigao had stepped into the Early Third Stage of Body Transformation—Viscera Training, boasting 2,100 jin of strength and practicing the Wang Clan’s prized Grade 1 Human-Step Martial Skill: Gale-Splitting Sword Art!

“Chen’er, get back!” Lu Shan hurried forward despite the bleeding cut on his forehead, grabbing Lu Chen’s sleeve. “Don’t provoke him—I’ll go to the Clan Elder’s hall right now—”

“It’s alright, Father,” Lu Chen said gently. With his left hand, he supported his father’s elbow, and at the same time slipped a small porcelain vial containing Rank 1 Blood-Staunching Powder into his mother’s palm. Looking at the dark bruise and split skin on his father’s temple, a cold glint flickered in the depths of Lu Chen’s eyes.

He turned back to Wang Yigao.

“You want my Lu family’s Seven Star Martial House Recommendation Token,” Lu Chen said, his voice ringing clearly across the entire lane so every merchant and martial artist watching could hear. “According to the laws of the Azure Sky Kingdom, an ancestral Recommendation Token cannot be seized by force—it can only be transferred through a notarized contract or a Public Martial Wager on the stone stage at Cloudstride Plaza.”

Wang Yigao laughed aloud, snapping his folding fan shut. “You actually dare mention the stone stage at Cloudstride Plaza? Good! I was worried you’d hide behind your mother’s apron like a rat. Tell me—what wager do you want?”

“One hour from now, on the Cloudstride Plaza martial stage, we fight a single match,” Lu Chen stated evenly. “If I lose, the Seven Star Martial House Recommendation Token belongs to you unconditionally. Wait—what if you lose, Wang Yigao?”

“Me? Lose to a First Stage butcher’s boy?” Wang Yigao sneered, looking around at his laughing retainers. “If you can stay standing on the stage against me for ten breaths—let alone defeat me—what do you want?”

“Your thirty taels of silver aren’t even enough to pay for my father’s medical bill and the two tables your dogs kicked over,” Lu Chen said coldly. “If I win, you put up two things on the notary table before the fight begins: first, the 420-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Heavy Spear displayed in your Wang Hardware Pavilion’s main hall; second, the sealed jade jar of Scarlet Serpent Blood Paste your father bought last month for your Third Stage consolidation—plus one hundred taels of silver in compensation for my parents’ tavern!”

A collective gasp rippled through the crowd in Willow-Lane Alley.

The 420-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Spear was a peak Grade 1 Human-Step Heavy Weapon worth one hundred and eighty taels of silver, forged by a master smith who had tempered black-sand iron a hundred times; because no young martial artist in Cloudstride City had the monstrous arm and waist strength to wield a 420-jin spear efficiently, it had sat on display as the centerpiece of the Wang Hardware Pavilion for three years. And the Scarlet Serpent Blood Paste, refined from the marrow and blood of a Second-Order Scarlet Serpent, was worth another two hundred and fifty taels!

Together with the hundred taels of silver, Lu Chen was demanding over five hundred taels of goods in a single breath!

“Greedy fool—you’re daydreaming about treasures you won’t have unbroken hands to hold!” Wang Yigao’s face flushed dark red at being spoken down to in front of half the street. “Fine! I’ll have the steward bring the Black Iron Heavy Spear, the Scarlet Serpent Blood Paste, and one hundred taels of silver directly to the notary elder at Cloudstride Plaza! If you try to flee through the city gates instead of showing up, my men will smash this tavern to rubble!”

Half an hour later, Cloudstride Martial Plaza at the center of the city was packed shoulder-to-shoulder with over five hundred spectators.

News that the third branch’s Lu Chen had staked his ancestral Seven Star Martial House Recommendation Token against Wang Yigao’s five hundred taels of treasures had spread like wildfire through the martial halls and taverns—even Master Liu from the Crimson Scale Pavilion and Appraiser Qin from the Sky Treasure Pavilion stood near the front of the stone platform, watching with contrasting expressions.

On the notary table beside the ten-foot-high bluestone stage rested Lu Chen’s ancient bronze-jade Recommendation Token on one side, and on the other side lay an eight-foot-long, pitch-black iron spear whose shaft was as thick as a baby’s wrist, a crimson jade jar sealed with gold foil, and a stack of gleaming silver ingots.

“Both parties have signed the blood-print wager before the City Guard Notary!” the presiding deacon announced loudly. “On the stage, blades have no eyes—anything short of deliberate murder after surrender is permitted! Begin!”

“Lu Chen, kneel and hand over the token!”

The moment the gong sounded, Wang Yigao didn’t hold back a fraction of his strength. Stepping forward with a booming stomp that shook the dust off the bluestone tiles, his Early Third Stage (Viscera Training) blood-qi erupted from his organs with a muffled roar, and the Grade 1 refined steel longsword in his hand whipped out three overlapping crescents of cold silver light—

《Gale-Splitting Sword Art — Triple Wind Slash!》

Three whistling sword-arcs sealed off Lu Chen’s throat, chest, and right shoulder simultaneously! Among the spectators below, several apprentices cried out, certain that Lu Chen would be forced to draw his rusty iron sword in panic and have it knocked flying by Wang Yigao’s 2,100-jin strength.

On the stage, however, Lu Chen didn’t even touch the hilt of the inscribed iron sword at his waist.

In his eyes—calm under Prism Stillness—Wang Yigao’s flashy Triple Wind Slash was riddled with flaws: because Wang Yigao had relied on expensive pills to force his way into the Third Stage, his footwork floated at the heels, his shoulder stiffened before the second slash, and his three sword-shadows were really two feints masking a single diagonal chop toward the right collarbone!

Compared to the thirty-six interlocking ring-bones of a Second-Order Python, this sword work is as clumsy as chopped firewood.

Just as the gleaming steel blade came within three inches of his shoulder, Lu Chen shifted his weight half a step to the left—an economical, two-inch pivot honed from fifteen thousand cuts in the deboning yard.

Whoosh!

Wang Yigao’s full-strength slash grazed past the black cloth of Lu Chen’s lapel without cutting a single thread!

Before Wang Yigao’s wrist could pull back from the overextended swing, Lu Chen stepped straight inside the guard of his longsword.

Inhale—Silk-Unwinding Star Breath!

Beneath Lu Chen’s black robe, thirty-six micro-meridian nodes snapped taut in unison, and 2,300 jin of coiled spiral strength surged from his heels, twisted through his waist, and erupted out of his bronze-lustered right palm!

“You didn’t even draw your sword?! Die—!” Wang Yigao snarled, hastily raising his left forearm to block while twisting his sword pommel back toward Lu Chen’s ribs.

BANG—CRACK!

Lu Chen’s open palm didn’t strike Wang Yigao’s sword—it slapped squarely against the joint where Wang Yigao’s left forearm met his chest guard, releasing a hidden second wave of Silk-Unwinding Spiral Force!

A crisp pop of a dislocated shoulder joint echoed across the entire plaza!

Lu Chen had no desire to be a bloodthirsty crippler in a hometown wager, yet he showed zero mercy to Wang Yigao’s arrogance—his measured 2,300-jin spiral palm cleanly dislocated Wang Yigao’s sword-arm at the shoulder and sent his Grade 1 steel sword spinning twenty feet into the air! With his chest guard dented inward and his breath knocked completely out of his lungs, Wang Yigao was blasted backward across the bluestone stage, crashing through the wooden railing at the edge of the platform and landing in a groaning heap right in front of his stunned retainers!

One move.

Without even drawing his weapon, Lu Chen had cleanly disarmed and defeated an Early Third Stage opponent in a single palm strike!
