Silence—followed by a tsunami of cheers that shook the clouds above Seven Star Mountain—erupted from three thousand outer and inner disciples!

In less than two months since walking through the mountain gate with borderline Grade 3 talent and an 820-jin iron spear on his back, fifteen-year-old Lu Chen had swept aside Lin Xuan, Zhu Hong, Lin Sen, and now #2 Core Disciple Ouyang Ming to stand in the Top 3 of the Seven Star Martial House!

On the high golden dais, Grand Marshal Su stroked his white beard and laughed heartily, casting a sidelong glance at the ashen-faced Third Prince Zhao Heng whose two proudest lieutenants (Ouyang Ming and Lin Sen) had both been carried off the stage in a single day.

Even more unexpectedly, at the #1 seat of the Core Disciple terrace, a tall, quiet eighteen-year-old youth dressed in plain snow-white robes with a silver longsword across his knees slowly opened his eyes and stood up.

It was #1 Core Disciple Murong Zi—the undisputed number-one genius of the Seven Star Martial House for the past two years, possessing Grade 5 Upper-Tier Talent, a cultivation at the Absolute Peak of the Sixth Stage (Twelve Meridians 100% Condensed — Half-Step Houtian!), and the comprehension of a Half-Step Sword Intent!

Murong Zi did not belong to the Crown Prince’s Faction or any political clique; he lived only for the sword.

Stepping lightly onto the center of the Grand Star Ranking Arena, Murong Zi bowed respectfully with his sword in hand toward Lu Chen.

“Junior Brother Lu,” Murong Zi said, his voice clear and resonant as a jade chime. “In two years at the Seven Star Martial House, no one has forced me to draw my Grade 3 Human-Step Star-River Sword at full strength. Today, both you and I have already secured our spots in the Profound Gravity Cave, so there is no grudge of life and death between us—only a battle for the peak of the Martial House. Dare to exchange three strikes with me for Rank #1?”

Looking at Murong Zi—whose aura was sharp and pure as an unsheathed sword without a trace of Lin Sen’s or Ouyang Ming’s petty malice—a genuine fire of martial fighting spirit ignited in Lu Chen’s chest.

“Please enlighten me, Senior Brother Murong!”

SHING—!

Murong Zi drew his Grade 3 Human-Step Star-River Sword. The moment the silver blade left its scabbard, an invisible, razor-sharp Half-Step Clear-Wind Sword Intent swept across the entire fifty-yard arena, slicing floating autumn leaves in midair into four neat pieces without touching them!

  • First Strike: Murong Zi unleashed 《Grade 2 Peak Star-River Nine Swords — Seventh Sword: Falling Star Stream》, with 17,500 jin of Peak Sixth Stage True Essence! Lu Chen met it head-on with 《Wildfire Sweeps the Plains — Eighth Wave》, the purple-thunder spear and silver star-sword colliding in a blinding explosion of shockwaves that forced both youths back three steps simultaneously!
  • Second Strike: Murong Zi’s Half-Step Sword Intent condensed into a hair-thin silver line—《Eighth Sword: Severing the Milky Way》 (19,000 jin)! Lu Chen’s Golden Roc Nine-Flash Step flashed six times in a single breath, his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear unleashing a double thrust that shattered the silver sword-line at the cost of a shallow cut on his left sleeve!

“Two strikes tied!” the elders on the dais breathed in shock. “Now comes the third and final strike!”
