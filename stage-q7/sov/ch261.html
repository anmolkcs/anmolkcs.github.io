Two thousand miles east of Central Emperor City, the fertile plains of the Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain dipped into a vast, mist-shrouded inland depression surrounded by ring-shaped cliffs of silver-white moonstone:

The Sunken Star-Sea Basin!

Ten millennia ago, during the final battle of the Pre-Epoch Bronze Dynasty, a fragment of an ancient Upper-Realm Time-Domain Relic had fallen into the center of this basin, creating a three-hundred-mile canyon where time flowed in chaotic, stationary eddies—the Abyssal Chrono-Rift.

Inside the Abyssal Chrono-Rift, one step to the left might accelerate time by tenfold, aging an ordinary Revolving Core cultivator's cells by a decade in a single breath, while one step to the right might freeze a flying bird motionless in midair for a hundred years! Even Divine Sea Realm ancestors dared not venture past the outer ten miles of the rift without a time-law treasure.

At dawn on the second day after slaying the Blood-Sovereign Shadow-Envoy, eighteen-year-old Lu Chen—accompanied by Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang (who had flown out from Star-Fall Peak after Lu Chen sent word that his parents were safely consolidating their Peak Revolving Cores at Warm-Spring Immortal Manor)—descended onto the silver moonstone cliff overlooking the Abyssal Chrono-Rift.

Below the cliff, rivers of mercury-colored Time-Mist coiled lazily through forests of petrified silver trees whose leaves remained suspended in mid-fall between autumn and winter.

"What a strange canyon," Chu Kuang murmured in awe, touching the hilt of his Grade 5 Golden-Scale Broadsaber as he looked at a waterfall half a mile down the cliff whose upper half was roaring white water while its lower half hung frozen in midair like sculpted glass—not from cold, but because the time-flow at the bottom of the waterfall was ten thousand times slower than at the top! "If an ordinary Life-and-Death Wheel warrior charged in there recklessly, his left foot would be in tomorrow while his right foot was still in yesterday—the temporal shear alone would rip his Dharma Body apart!"

"That temporal shear is why even Divine Sea cultivators stay away," Lu Chen smiled calmly, his dark eyes reflecting the nine-faceted silver-gold radiance of the Chrono-Soul Prism inside his spiritual sea. "However, for the Chrono-Soul Prism—and for Senior Su Qingxue, whose ancestral bloodline carries the Primordial Chrono-Yin Seal—these chaotic time-eddies are the purest cradle in the lower world."
