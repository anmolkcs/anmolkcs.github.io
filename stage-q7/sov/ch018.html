For five consecutive days inside Courtyard #72, Lu Chen didn’t step outside his gate once.

From the fifth watch before dawn until midnight, the courtyard echoed with the deep, thunderous whir—BOOM! whir—BOOM! of the 820-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Spear cleaving the air.

One thousand Level Thrusts. One thousand Mountain-Splitting Smashes. One thousand Coiling Dragon Sweeps.

Every day, Lu Chen thrust and swept the 820-jin spear five thousand times until the skin between his thumb and index finger tore open, soaked the black iron shaft with blood, and healed overnight into calluses as hard as ironhorn.

By the fifth evening, under the relentless calibration of Prism Stillness and the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath, when Lu Chen thrust the 820-jin spear forward at an ironwood dummy in the corner of his courtyard, the eight-foot black shaft blurred into a single straight black line—

SHHHK—BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Three distinct, overlapping sonic booms erupted half a second apart inside the six-inch ironwood core of the dummy, blowing the back of the post into sawdust while leaving only a single coin-sized hole on the front!

“The Third Layer of the Nine-Layered Wave Force—increasing my burst strike power by a full 30% on top of the spear’s 38% Flame-Edge & Heavy-Mountain Inscription!” Lu Chen exhaled a long white plume of breath, his dark eyes gleaming.

On the morning of the sixth day, carrying his 820-jin spear and one of his Free Entry Tokens, Lu Chen walked toward the booming drumbeats at the northern peak of the Martial House:

The Ten Thousand Killing Array!

Constructed over the core spirit vein of Seven Star Mountain, the Ten Thousand Killing Array was the crowning glory of the Seven Star Martial House. Once a disciple stepped onto one of the hundred stone array platforms and inserted a True Essence Stone (or an Entry Token), their spiritual consciousness and full physical data—down to the exact weight of their weapon and the strength of their muscles—were projected into an ancient, ultra-realistic Illusionary Slaughtering World.

Inside the Ten Thousand Killing Array, pain was 100% real, blood sprayed like rain, and an endless tide of phantom Battlefield Veterans, Fierce Spirit Beasts, and Ancient Martial Shadows besieged the challenger from all sides! Only when a challenger was “killed” inside the array were they ejected back to their body, exhausted in soul force but physically unharmed.

And standing beside the entrance of the Killing Array Square was the towering, hundred-foot-tall Killing Array Ranking Stele, which recorded the top two hundred disciples of the entire Seven Star Martial House based on their kill-score inside the array. Every month, anyone ranked on the stele received generous True Essence Stone rewards and the right to enter the second floor of the Library Pavilion!

When Lu Chen arrived at the Killing Array Square, over two hundred outer and inner disciples were gathered around the base of the Ranking Stele, pointing upward as a golden line of text flashed at Rank #142:

Rank #142: Lin Xuan (New Disciple — Early Fourth Stage: Altering Muscle & Tendons) — First Challenge Score: 480 Killing Points!

“Lin Xuan broke through to the Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) last night and charged straight onto the Killing Array Ranking Stele at #142 on his very first attempt!” a second-year disciple gasped. “In the entire history of the Martial House, fewer than five freshmen have ever broken into the top 150 during their first week!”

Just then, Lin Xuan stepped off Platform #1, his golden-threaded robe fluttering as he accepted the congratulations of the crowd—and then his gaze landed coldly on Lu Chen walking into the square.

“Lu Chen,” Lin Xuan said loudly, a proud smirk curving his lips as all two hundred disciples turned to watch. “Five days ago at the entrance trial, you relied on a lucky gravity endurance trick to take first place. Today I stepped into the Fourth Stage and took Rank #142 on the Killing Array Stele—where neither illusion tricks nor brute-force weight carrying can save you from hundreds of Third and Fourth Stage martial phantoms. Dare to step onto Platform #2 right now and see if you can even pass 200 Killing Points?”

Without saying a word, Lu Chen walked straight onto Platform #2, slotted his Entry Token into the altar groove, and gripped his 820-jin Black Iron Spear as a column of blood-red array light swallowed his figure.
