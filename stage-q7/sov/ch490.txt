<p>The underground detonation of thirty barrels of <strong>Black-Oil Fire-Powder</strong> inside a confined three-hundred-fathom rock tunnel was apocalyptic!</p>
<p>First came a blinding, supersonic shockwave of compressed air and roaring red flame that tore down the half-mile length of Drift Four like the breath of an angry dragon, blowing out every sulfur-moss lamp on the walls!</p>
<p>Second—and far more terrifying—with the four main timber fault-pillars at the mouth of the drift vaporized by the blast, the unstable <strong>Eighteenfold-Gravity Earth-Marrow Fault</strong> directly overhead lost its equilibrium!</p>
<p><em>CRACK—RUMMMMMMMMMMBLE—CRASH!!</em></p>
<p>Like a row of giant dominoes, tens of thousands of tons of black granite ceiling began collapsing progressively from the mouth of Drift Four straight toward the dead-end cul-de-sac at the rear, sending a choking hurricane of rock-dust and flying boulder-shrapnel racing down the tunnel at a hundred feet per second!</p>
<p>At the rear of the drift, right below the cleared opening of the four-foot <strong>Emergency Ventilation Chimney</strong>, <strong>Tie Niu</strong> and two strong miners were already halfway up the rock ladder hauling bags of rations and ropes, while twenty-six miners had scrambled up behind them!</p>
<p>Still on the tunnel floor below, however, were the last twelve miners—including sixty-year-old, hunchbacked <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong>, whose foot had just caught between two shifting floor-slabs as the first shockwave knocked him flat!</p>
<p>"Leave me, Young Hero Lu—climb!" Old Mole Qian screamed over the deafening roar of the collapsing tunnel as the progressive roof-cave-in reached twenty yards from the cul-de-sac! "The main ten-ton granite lintel over the chimney mouth is cracking—it's coming down in three breaths!"</p>
<p>Directly above the entrance to the vertical ventilation chimney, a colossal <strong>Twelve-Ton Keystone Lintel of Black Basalt</strong>—subjected to eighteenfold gravity and the violent shear of the collapsing tunnel—split loose from the upper rock wall with a sound like a thunderbolt!</p>
<p>If that twelve-ton keystone lintel dropped four feet, it would not only crush Old Mole Qian and the last twelve miners into meat-paste—it would shear off the mouth of the Emergency Ventilation Chimney and seal all forty-one men alive in a tomb of solid rock forever!</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not climb a single rung of the ladder.</p>
<p>Springing back down into the choking dust cloud beneath the mouth of the ventilation chimney, he wrenched the trapped stone off Old Mole Qian's ankle with one hand, shoved the old miner toward the ladder, and looked up as the <strong>twelve-ton basalt keystone lintel</strong> dropped straight down on his head!</p>
<p>"NOBODY DIES IN THIS PIT!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen planted his bare feet shoulder-width apart on the bedrock floor in the supreme <strong>Anvil-Root Horse Stance</strong>, jammed his <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> vertically into the left corner of the rock frame as an iron prop-post, and threw both <strong>Late Tier Four</strong> iron-boned forearms, shoulders, and his arched spinal column directly beneath the center of the falling twelve-ton lintel!</p>
<p><em>BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!</em></p>
<p>Under the subterranean fault's gravity, <strong>over one hundred and eighty thousand jin of dead geological pressure</strong> slammed down onto the 160-jin Cold-Marrow Spear and onto Lu Chen's skeleton!</p>
<p>The solid bedrock beneath Lu Chen's feet exploded into a two-foot-deep crater of pulverized stone!</p>
<p>Every single blood vessel across Lu Chen's back, shoulders, and forearms burst into a fine red mist of blood-sweat; bright crimson poured from both nostrils and the corners of his jaw; and all <strong>206 Late Tier Four iron bones</strong> in his body let out a continuous, ear-splitting metallic groan—<em>CREEEEEEEEAK—DONG!</em>—as the titanic weight compressed the last uncalcined ten percent of his inner marrow-walls!</p>
<p>Yet the twelve-ton basalt lintel <strong>stopped</strong>—held three feet above the floor on the unyielding iron arch of Lu Chen's back and shoulders!</p>
<p>"CLIMB!!" Lu Chen roared, a sound like two iron mountains grinding together as blood sprayed from his lips!</p>
<p>Weeping aloud in awe and terror, the last twelve miners hauled Old Mole Qian up over Lu Chen's blood-soaked shoulders into the vertical ventilation chimney!</p>
<p>Ten breaths! Thirty breaths! Sixty agonizing breaths!</p>
<p>Only when the boot of the fortieth miner cleared the upper lip of the chimney did Lu Chen reach out with his left hand, yank his 160-jin Cold-Marrow Spear free, and launch himself upward into the vertical chimney like a released ballista bolt half a heartbeat before the twelve-ton lintel crashed down behind his heels—sealing Drift Four behind them in a cloud of dust!</p>
