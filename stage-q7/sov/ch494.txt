<p>Pin-drop silence fell over the snow-swept basalt bridge for three full heartbeats.</p>
<p>Behind Lu Chen, the forty freed debt-miners, <strong>Tie Niu</strong>, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>, and <strong>Audit Clerk Fang</strong> stared at the corpse of <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong>—an <strong>Early Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining)</strong> official who had lorded over the northern ridge villages like a god of life and death for ten years—impaled like a dead crow against the bridge parapet after just <strong>three exchanges</strong>!</p>
<p>Look closely at the dead Sub-Bailiff's shattered ninety-jin iron whip-rod lying in two pieces on the flagstones, sixty-year-old Old Tie Shan touched his own healed right collarbone and let out a long, fierce breath through his teeth.</p>
<p>Thirty paces across the bridge, the eighteen mounted pit-wardens yanked hard on their Crag-Mules' reins, their horses rearing in panic as they looked from the dead Zhao Kuan to the blood-stained young spearman standing in the middle of the bridge.</p>
<p>Only one man among the enemy did not retreat.</p>
<p>Slowly, <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> swung his thick, iron-armored leg over the saddle of his Crag-Mule and dropped onto the basalt flagstones of the bridge.</p>
<p><em>THOOOOOOOM—CRACK!</em></p>
<p>When Luo Shan's iron-shod boots hit the bridge, two radial cracks half an inch wide shot through the solid basalt paving stones beneath his heels!</p>
<p>Unclasping his heavy wolf-skin cloak and tossing it into the snow, the forty-five-year-old tyrant of Pit #7 stood in a suit of dark, double-layered <strong>Black-Slag Riveted Plate Armor</strong>. He gripped the two-inch-thick iron handle of his <strong>Two-Hundred-and-Twenty-Jin Eight-Flanged Iron Bone-Crushing Mace</strong> with both bare, dark-gray hands.</p>
<p>Unlike Zhao Kuan—who had barely entered Early Tier Four a year ago using diluted apothecary salts—<strong>Scarface Luo Shan</strong> had spent twenty brutal years in the frontier military and the bottom of Black-Slag Canyon calcining every single one of his 206 bones to the absolute limit of <strong>Peak Tier Four of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Slag-Bone Perfection</strong>!</p>
<p>When Luo Shan rolled his bull neck and inhaled a deep breath of the freezing night air, his entire skeleton emitted a low, continuous, metallic <em>HUMMMMM</em> that resonated with the heavy iron-armor plates on his chest—radiating <strong>seven thousand eight hundred jin</strong> of crushing, mountain-like physical dominance!</p>
<p>"So that's where the missing <strong>Heart-of-Marrow Crystals</strong> at the bottom of my sulfur pool went," Scarface Luo Shan said, his voice deadly quiet as his pulled-down left eye fixed on the frost-blue 160-jin spear in Lu Chen's hand and the unmistakable <strong>Late Tier Four</strong> iron luster beneath Lu Chen's bruised skin. "In fourteen days down in Drift Four, you swallowed my two thousand taels of marrow-crystals, forged a hundred-and-sixty-jin cold-marrow spear in the magma vent, and pushed your bones from Tier Three all the way to <strong>Late Tier Four</strong>!"</p>
<p>Luo Shan raised his 220-jin Eight-Flanged Iron Mace until the eight jagged flanges pointed straight between Lu Chen's eyebrows.</p>
<p>"A pity for you, boy—between <strong>Late Tier Four</strong> and my <strong>twenty-year Peak Tier Four Slag-Bone Perfection</strong>, the inner marrow-walls of your bones are still ten percent uncalcined! Every time my two-hundred-and-twenty-jin mace hits your spear tonight, that ten-percent gap will crack your bones from the inside out! Wardens—stay back and shoot anyone who steps onto the bridge! Watch me hammer this little thief's skeleton back into marrow-paste!"</p>
