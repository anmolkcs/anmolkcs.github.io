<p>Once the first wave of overwhelming grief and relief passed, Lu Chen guided Tie Niu to sit on a dry basalt boulder at the corner of the cul-de-sac, uncorked a bamboo canteen of clean snow-water, and watched the starving young man wolf down half a pound of dense <strong>Peak Tier Two King-Boar Meat</strong> and two millet cakes.</p>
<p>As the rich, high-vitality beast meat hit Tie Niu's stomach, color returned visibly to his grey lips. Before his imprisonment three years ago, Tie Niu had already completed <strong>Tier One (Ash-Skin Scouring)</strong>, and three years of swinging a thirty-pound pickaxe under fifteenfold gravity had actually hammered his back and arm muscles halfway into <strong>Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong>—only starvation and his crushed left ankle had been draining his life-lamp toward extinction.</p>
<p>"Hold onto the rock, Brother Tie Niu," Lu Chen said quietly, kneeling in the warm sulfur-brine before the young man's swollen, thirty-degree-twisted left ankle. "Whoever left this talus bone dislocated three weeks ago wanted you crippled for life. The false callus around the joint hasn't fully calcified yet—I'm going to break the bad union, slide the ankle-mortise back into true alignment, and splint it with <strong>Earth-Marrow Calcining Salt</strong> and iron strips."</p>
<p>"Don't worry about me yelling, Brother Lu," Tie Niu gritted his teeth, locking his calloused right hand around a granite projection on the wall. "After listening to my old man's hammer since I was three, I know when a bone needs straightening. Do it!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen sank his awareness into the three-pace microscopic clarity of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>.</p>
<p>Through the swollen purple skin of Tie Niu's ankle, Lu Chen's fingers mapped the exact millimeter where the fractured fibula and dislocated talus bone had jammed sideways and grown a brittle bridge of fibrous scar-bone.</p>
<p>With one lightning-fast, precisely angled <strong>Thumb-Shear &amp; Heel-Traction Snap</strong> powered by his <strong>Tier Three Bowstring Sinews</strong>—</p>
<p><em>KRAK—CLACK!</em></p>
<p>The brittle false union snapped cleanly without nicking a single blood vessel or tendon; the talus bone slid smoothly back into the center of the ankle mortise with a solid click; and before the pain could even register in Tie Niu's brain, Lu Chen packed the ankle in a thick poultice of <strong>Red-Lead Bone-Washing Powder</strong>, bound it tightly between four custom-shaped iron splints from his pack, and used his bare fingers to bend and snap the heavy thirty-pound iron leg-shackle right off Tie Niu's ankle!</p>
<p>As Tie Niu gasped in amazement at the sudden relief of pressure inside his straightened ankle joint, an old, hunchbacked miner with white hair and only four teeth left—<strong>Old Mole Qian</strong>, who had been trapped in Pit #7 for nine years and worked the adjacent stall—crept forward to keep watch down the tunnel and spoke in an urgent, rasping whisper:</p>
<p>"Young Hero Lu... breaking that shackle won't matter if we're still down here on the nineteenth night! Listen to this old mole: <strong>Scarface Luo Shan</strong> has zero intention of letting your brother or any of the forty men in Drift Four ride that winch-cage up on the twentieth—not even if you chip out a thousand carts of ore!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen's eyes sharpened as he tied off the last linen knot around Tie Niu's splint. "Tell me what you found behind that boulder wall, Old Qian."</p>
<p>Old Mole Qian pointed a trembling, gnarled finger toward a four-foot cleft at the very back of the cul-de-sac, currently hidden behind a false wall of stacked waste-shale.</p>
<p>"Twelve days ago, when Tie Niu and I broke through that shale fault," Old Mole Qian whispered, "we didn't hit poison sulfur-gas! We broke into a sealed, pre-epoch cavern containing a natural <strong>Boiling Earth-Marrow Sulfur Pool</strong>—and lining the bottom of that pool were over three hundred jin of <strong>Natural Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong>! Three hundred jin of those crystals are worth <strong>two thousand taels of silver</strong> to <strong>True-Rune Foundation</strong> weapon-forgers in Grey-Wall Prefectural City!"</p>
<p>"The night we found it," Tie Niu hissed through clenched teeth, "Scarface Luo Shan came down the shaft alone with his two-hundred-and-twenty-jin mace, murdered the two miners who reported it, and put Drift Four under fake 'Sulfur Quarantine'! For the last ten days, he's been forcing the forty of us to dive into the outer edge of that scalding pool to pry out the marrow-crystals for his private chest—and two days ago, Old Qian saw Luo Shan's wardens stacking thirty barrels of <strong>Black-Oil Fire-Powder</strong> around the main timber roof-supports at the mouth of Drift Four! Once we pry out the last marrow-crystal on the eighteenth night, Luo Shan is going to ignite those fire-barrels and drop ten thousand tons of granite down on all forty-one of us!"</p>
