<p>Inside the splintered doorway of the Witness-Shack, sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> stood frozen with his thirty-pound hammer still raised in his single scarred hand, staring wide-eyed at the tall, bare-chested young man standing in the snow before him.</p>
<p>Lu Chen's matte-bronze torso was crusted with dried crimson blood-sweat and black basalt dust from holding up the twelve-ton lintel; in his right hand rested a monstrous, seven-foot-six-inch blue-black heavy spear that radiated a piercing, bone-chilling aura; and behind him, scrambling up the timber ramp from the lower staging-ledge into the lantern-light, came forty gaunt, fierce-eyed men armed with short trench-spears!</p>
<p>And running at the very front of those forty men—not limping, not chained, his broad shoulders corded with <strong>Late Tier Two</strong> muscle and his bearded face wet with tears—was a twenty-two-year-old youth wearing the complete, re-joined <strong>Iron Tally #7</strong> around his neck!</p>
<p>"F-Father...!"</p>
<p><strong>Tie Niu</strong> dropped his eighty-pound mine-sledge into the snow at the top of the ramp, stumbled three steps forward, and fell straight to his knees before the one-armed old blacksmith!</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan's thirty-pound hammer slipped from his fingers and hit the frozen planks with a heavy thud.</p>
<p>For three years—one thousand and ninety-five freezing nights in the smithy shed of Ash-Brook Village—the old man had woken up staring at his empty left sleeve and the cold corner by the coal heap, terrified that he would die before ever seeing his boy's face again.</p>
<p>Now, Old Tie Shan reached out with his single trembling, coal-scarred right hand, grabbed a fistful of his son's matted black hair and beard to make sure he wasn't a ghost, and pulled the burly twenty-two-year-old youth fiercely against his chest!</p>
<p>"Niu'er... my Niu'er...!" the sixty-year-old blacksmith wept aloud, his forehead pressed against his son's shoulder while Tie Niu wrapped both strong arms around his father's waist and sobbed like a child. "Your leg... Zhao Kuan said your ankle was crushed—!"</p>
<p>"Brother Lu set my ankle bone and broke my chains on the first night down there, Father!" Tie Niu choked out, pointing back at the forty armed miners and at Lu Chen, who was busy cutting the ropes off the shaken <strong>County Audit Clerk Fang</strong>. "Luo Shan and Zhao Kuan blew up the mouth of Drift Four tonight to murder all forty of us and steal two thousand taels of <strong>Natural Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong>! Brother Lu held up a twelve-ton falling rock lintel on his bare back so every single one of us could climb out through the old air-shaft!"</p>
<p>On the floor of the shack, <strong>County Audit Clerk Fang</strong> tore the gag from his mouth, his face livid with shock and fury as he looked at the dead bailiff guards who had been about to murder him alongside Old Tie Shan to fake a report for the Prefectural Censor.</p>
<p>"Embezzling a Grade-One Marrow-Crystal vein, blowing up a quarantined shaft to murder forty tax-cleared men, and attempting to assassinate a County Treasury Audit Clerk!" Clerk Fang screamed, clutching his official bronze witness-seal to his chest. "The <strong>Prefectural Censor from Grey-Wall City</strong> is already resting at the Five-Mile Post-Station on the north road tonight! If I reach the Five-Mile Post-Station alive with your forty testimonies—Luo Shan and Zhao Kuan will both be beheaded at the market gate!"</p>
<p>Suddenly, before the cheering miners could move toward the canyon road, the frozen ground beneath their boots began to thrum with the thunder of iron-scaled hooves!</p>
<p><em>THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD!</em></p>
<p>Up from the main winch-house plaza two hundred yards down the rim galloped <strong>twenty armored Scale-Hoofed Crag-Mules</strong> carrying torches and crossbows—led by <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong> and <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong>, who had heard the shouting from the upper ledge after climbing up from the main shaft!</p>
