Three months after the eradication of the Blood-Eclipse Sect, golden autumn winds swept across the seven towering peaks of the Seven Star Valley Sect.

Over those three months, with the resources of the entire Southern Sky-Spill Region flowing into the sect and Lu Chen's Rank 5 Seven-Star Heaven-Locking Grand Array drawing pure origin energy from three hundred miles around: - Murong Zi and Chu Kuang had both successfully crossed their Seven-Wave Heavenly Core Tribulations—condensing a Flawless Sword-Star Core and a Gold-Scale Core respectively to step officially into the Early Revolving Core Realm! - Su Wan'er had reached 100% Peak Xiantian, standing half a step from her own Revolving Core tribulation while her Inscription mastery reached the Peak of Rank 4!

On the morning of the tenth autumn month, a colossal, three-hundred-yard Grade 5 Spirit-Metal Sky-Barge bearing the banner of a silver spear splitting the heavens descended through the clouds above Dubhe Peak, radiating an aura that made even the Revolving Core Grand Elders bow in respect!

Standing on the deck of the Sky-Barge was Elder Cang Feng (Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng)—an emissary from the Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain (the true heartland of the continent, separated from the barren Southern Region by the ten-thousand-mile Astral Storm-Trench), whose cultivation stood at the terrifying Second Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel Realm!

However, standing on a second, black-iron airship half a mile behind Elder Cang Feng was a cold-eyed old man in dark-crimson robes—Elder Yin-Sha (First Life-Renewal Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel Realm), an outer emissary of the Nether-Blood Cult of the Central Domain (the parent overlord sect of the destroyed Blood-Eclipse Sect)!

"Once every ten years, the spatial storms inside the ten-thousand-mile Astral Storm-Trench weaken enough for our Central Imperial Domain to open the Astral Ferry-Bridge and recruit the top young Revolving Core prodigies under age twenty-five from the outer regions," Elder Cang Feng declared, his keen gaze locking onto seventeen-year-old Lu Chen standing at the front of the Seven Star Valley Sect's elders with undisguised admiration. "However, three days ago, a subterranean tremor at the bottom of the Astral Storm-Trench shifted the ancient Sunken Bronze Dynasty Sky-Anchor, blocking the ferry-bridge's spatial lock! Because Life-and-Death Wheel cultivators have too heavy a Law aura and will trigger the trench's Spatial-Lightning Collapse if they descend below five thousand feet, only Revolving Core experts can descend to the bottom of the trench to re-align the Sky-Anchor!"

Elder Cang Feng held up a radiant Golden Dragon-Gate Medallion:

"Whoever descends to the bottom of the Astral Storm-Trench, clears the corrupted Sixth-Order guardian beast, and restores the Sunken Bronze Sky-Anchor within ten days will receive three Golden Dragon-Gate Medallions—granting direct entry into the Continental Young Sovereign Tournament in the Central Imperial Domain!"
