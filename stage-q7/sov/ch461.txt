<p>In the howling whiteout at the mouth of the S-bend, Lu Chen slung his eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Boar-Spear diagonally across his back, drew two short, triangular <strong>Cold-Slag Masonry Chisels</strong> from his belt, and stepped up to the base of the fifty-foot vertical slate buttress.</p>
<p>Above him, the black slate cliff leaned outward by three degrees, coated in a two-inch glaze of grey <strong>Black-Frost Ice</strong> slick as greased glass. Sixty-mile-an-hour gusts of freezing wind screamed around the corner of the gorge, threatening to peel a man off the rock face like a dry leaf and dash him onto the boulders below under tenfold gravity.</p>
<p>Down at the base of the cliff, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> and <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> held their breath as they watched Lu Chen's silhouette fade into the swirling snow five feet up the wall.</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not hammer the chisels noisy into the rock.</p>
<p>Within the three-pace microscopic sphere of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, every hidden vertical cooling-joint and frost-crack beneath the two-inch glaze of black ice stood out clearly in his mind. Moving with the silent, rhythmic fluidness of a giant gecko, he pressed the tip of his left-hand chisel through a soft seam in the ice into a quarter-inch slate crevice—not striking it with a hammer, but using the <strong>three thousand four hundred jin</strong> of elastic force in his <strong>Thirty-Three Drawn Bowstring Sinews</strong> to push the hardened steel point two inches deep into solid stone with silent, hydraulic pressure!</p>
<p><em>Left chisel in. Right toe wedged on a fingernail-wide slate lip. Left Achilles and spinal bowstring sinews coiling and releasing.</em></p>
<p>Ten feet. Twenty feet. Thirty-five feet.</p>
<p>Every time a ferocious gust of gorge wind slammed against his back, trying to rip him off the vertical face, Lu Chen flattened his chest against the freezing black ice, letting the wind press him <em>into</em> the cliff rather than pry him off.</p>
<p>Within two and a half minutes, his left hand hooked silently over the crumbling crenellated parapet at the top of the fifty-foot slate buttress—directly behind the upper watch-platform of the ruined stone fort!</p>
<p>Huddled behind a three-foot wind-wall on the platform, less than four paces in front of Lu Chen, squatted two <strong>Peak Tier One</strong> outlaw lookouts wrapped in thick dog-skin cloaks. Between their boots hung the bronze alarm-gong and a padded mallet; both men were busy warming their hands over a small clay brazier of charcoal while squinting down at the empty southern road on the wrong side of the ice-barricade.</p>
<p>"Cold enough to freeze a bull's balls off," one lookout muttered through chattering teeth, blowing into his mittens. "Why does Boss Han think those Ash-Brook rats would haul iron sledges in a solstice whiteout? Even a Crag-Mule couldn't pull a cart through these drifts—"</p>
<p>He never finished the sentence.</p>
<p>Rising silently over the snow-covered parapet behind them like a phantom forged of dark bronze, Lu Chen did not draw a blade that might clink against their armor.</p>
<p>With thirty-three bowstring sinews snapping simultaneously from his heels through his hips and shoulders, both of his matte-bronze hands shot out through the driving snow and clamped around the backs of the two lookouts' necks!</p>
<p><em>KRAK! KRAK!</em></p>
<p>Three thousand four hundred jin of silent, vise-like finger and forearm torque crushed the cervical vertebrae of both Peak Tier One bandits in a tenth of a breath—before a single gasp could leave their throats or a single boot could kick the bronze alarm-gong!</p>
<p>Lowering the two limp bodies noiselessly into the snowdrift behind the parapet, Lu Chen stepped to the inner edge of the fifty-foot wall and looked down into the roofless stone courtyard of the ruined watch-fort thirty feet below.</p>
<p>Through the swirling snow-curtain, twelve Broken-Tooth outlaws were crowded around two roaring log fires inside the sheltered walls, roasting quarters of stolen Mud-Well mutton and gambling with bone dice. Guarding the rear of the six-foot ice-and-boulder barricade across the pass road stood their last surviving <strong>Middle Tier Two</strong> lieutenant—a burly spearman named <strong>Iron-Back Gou</strong>—while inside the intact stone blockhouse at the rear of the courtyard burned a separate charcoal stove where <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> was resting with his two personal bodyguards.</p>
<p>Lu Chen unslung the seven-foot-two-inch, <strong>eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Boar-Spear</strong> from his back, lifted the sixty-pound granite corner-stone of the parapet with his free hand, and hurled it straight down onto the burning log fire in the center of the courtyard!</p>
