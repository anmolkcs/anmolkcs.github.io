<p>Because an official <strong>Quarantine Descent Permit</strong> required the counter-signature of the County Mine-Audit Clerk inside the inner hall of the Tally-House—and also required the presence of a neutral <strong>County Audit Witness</strong> at the canyon rim winch-house so a pit-overseer couldn't blatantly murder a tax-cleared guarantor in daylight—Senior Clerk Master Sun led <strong>Lu Chen</strong>, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>, and <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> into the grand inner hall to register Lu Chen's name and pay the ten-tael witness bond.</p>
<p>While Old Tie Shan and Uncle Shi counted out ten taels of the silver taken from Broken-Tooth Camp at the audit counter, Lu Chen stood in the center of the cavernous basalt hall and looked up at the northern wall.</p>
<p>Bolted directly into the black stone wall with twelve-inch iron spikes was a colossal, thirty-foot-wide <strong>Cast-Bronze Topographical &amp; Administrative Relief Map</strong> forged by the <strong>Heaven-Shackling Bailiff Court</strong> three centuries ago.</p>
<p>Even though the bronze surface was tarnished dark green by coal-soot and sulfur fumes, Lu Chen stepped within three paces of the lower border and sank his awareness into <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>.</p>
<p>With a museum antiquities restorer's trained eye for ancient cartographic inscriptions and seal-script hierarchy, Lu Chen read the entire thirty-foot bronze relief from bottom to top in two minutes—committing every mountain pass, garrison road, river-vein, and star-fortress coordinate to permanent memory:</p>

<p>Lu Chen stood motionless before the tarnished bronze wall, his breathing so still that not a single hair on his sheepskin collar moved.</p>
<p>The <strong>Heaven-Shackling Mother-Tally</strong> that controlled the Twelve-Pillar Blood-Drawing Stakes outside the <strong>Nine-Epoch Inverse Chrono-Cocoon</strong> of Warm-Spring Valley was not even in some unattainable central heaven millions of light-years away—its primary anchor-tally was locked inside the <strong>Prefectural Bailiff Vault of Grey-Wall Prefectural City</strong>, three hundred miles north of where he was standing!</p>
<p>Yet Lu Chen did not let a single ripple of impatience cloud his eyes.</p>
<p>Grey-Wall Prefectural City was guarded by <strong>Desolate Pulse (Second Great Realm)</strong> captains, <strong>True-Rune Foundation (Third Great Realm)</strong> generals, and an <strong>Origin Furnace (Fourth Great Realm)</strong> Prefectural Commander. Right now, Lu Chen had just stepped into <strong>Tier Three of the First Great Realm (Mortal-Shedding Stage)</strong>! If he marched three hundred miles north to Grey-Wall City today, he wouldn't even survive the outer gate-guards.</p>
<p><em>First, reach Peak Tier Four of the Mortal-Shedding Stage down in the fifteenfold gravity and boiling sulfur-brine of Deep-Shaft Pit #7,</em> Lu Chen charted calmly in his mind. <em>Rescue Tie Niu, secure Ash-Brook Village's survival, and forge a foundation of iron bones so solid that when I cross the nine tiers of Mortal-Shedding and open my Twelve Heavy Origin Meridians, nothing in Grey-Wall Prefecture will ever crack my spine.</em></p>
