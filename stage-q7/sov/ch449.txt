<p>"YOU MURDERING RAT—DIE!!"</p>
<p>At the rear of the ambushed column, <strong>Blood-Axe Scar-Six</strong> had been saved from the worst of the boiling tar-flood only because two of his own subordinates in front of him had taken the full blast of the kiln backdraft!</p>
<p>With his red wolf-skin bandana burned off his bald skull and his eyebrows singed to black stubs, the six-foot-four <strong>Peak Tier Two</strong> lieutenant kicked one of his burning subordinates out of his path, vaulted twelve feet across the pool of flaming black pitch with a roar like a wounded bear, and charged straight up the ravine slope toward Uncle Shi and Big-Tall Zhao!</p>
<p>Every muscle in Scar-Six's massive frame was compacted to the absolute limit of <strong>Peak Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Perfection)</strong>—resembling dark, oiled walnut wood wrapped around heavy bones, carrying <strong>two thousand four hundred and fifty jin</strong> of ferocious brute power! As he charged, he whirled his twin fifty-pound <strong>Butcher's Battle-Axes</strong> in a overlapping figure-eight storm that sheared a six-inch iron-pine sapling cleanly in half along his path!</p>
<p>Neither Uncle Shi (at eighteen hundred jin) nor Big-Tall Zhao could survive three exchanges against that twin-axe hurricane!</p>
<p>"Back to Kiln #2!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen's calm voice rang out as he stepped squarely into the center of the fire-lit slope between Scar-Six and the village hunters, his seven-foot-two-inch, <strong>eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Boar-Spear</strong> held low at his hip!</p>
<p>"Just the brat I'm looking for!" Scar-Six grinned savagely, his split three-piece upper lip peeling back from yellow teeth as he recognized the heavy black spear that Blackie had failed to report from the eastern basin. "Let's see if your spear is harder than my twin axes!"</p>
<p><em>WHIRRRRR—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Leaping down from a boulder with all two thousand four hundred and fifty jin of his Peak Tier Two strength behind him, Scar-Six brought both fifty-pound battle-axes down in a scissor-like <strong>Double-Guillotine Chop</strong> aimed at shearing Lu Chen's spear shaft in half and taking his head off at the shoulders!</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not retreat half a step down the icy slope.</p>
<p>Compared to ten days ago in the Boiling-Mud Wallow—when he had been only at Complete Tier One (1,200 jin) facing Ma Liu—Lu Chen now stood at <strong>Middle Tier Two (2,300 jin)</strong>, only a hundred and fifty jin below Scar-Six in raw strength, while his weapon was eighty jin of nine-folded <strong>Black-Marrow Core Steel</strong> and his mind rested in the absolute zero of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>!</p>
<p>Instead of blocking the scissor-chop horizontally with the middle of his shaft, Lu Chen drove his back heel into the frozen basalt and executed a ferocious upward <strong>Coiling-Dragon Pillar Thrust</strong>—driving the four-edged diamond spearhead straight into the exact one-inch intersection point where the cheeks of the two fifty-pound axe-heads crossed!</p>
<p><em>BOOOOOOOM—KRAK!</em></p>
<p>A deafening metallic thunderclap echoed off the walls of Bitter-Pine Ravine, sending a ring of orange sparks ten feet into the snow!</p>
<p>The eighty-jin solid iron spear did not bend a fraction of an inch. Caught right at the crossing fulcrum of their swing before their edges could close, both fifty-pound battle-axes were blasted violently outward, numbing Scar-Six's thick thumbs and stopping his downward leap dead in midair!</p>
<p>Simultaneously, the two-thousand-four-hundred-jin downward force slammed straight down the vertical spear shaft into Lu Chen's hands, shoulders, spine, and braced legs!</p>
<p>"Hmph—a Middle Tier Two brat dares match strength with me?!"</p>
<p>Landing heavily in the snow three paces in front of Lu Chen, Scar-Six didn't give Lu Chen a single breath to unload the force! Pivoting on his broad hips like a spinning iron top, the bandit lieutenant unleashed his signature slaughter art—<strong>Eighteen Continuous Whirlwind Butcher Chops</strong>!</p>
<p><em>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Left axe high to the temple, right axe low to the kneecap; left axe hooking the wrist, right axe hacking the ribs!</p>
<p>In the blazing orange light of the breached charcoal kiln, the twin fifty-pound axes became two overlapping wheels of howling cold iron! Each chop carried over two thousand four hundred jin of compacted flesh-power, turning the twelve-foot snow clearing between Kilns #4 and #5 into a storm of flying ice, shattered basalt gravel, and blinding sparks!</p>
