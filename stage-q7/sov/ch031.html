Two hundred miles west of the Azure Sky Royal Capital, the earth turned a deep, rust-colored red from centuries of oxidized iron ore, and jagged granite peaks rose like rows of broken spearheads into perpetual thunderclouds.

This was the Crimson Iron Mountain Range—a sprawling wilderness stretching over eight hundred miles that teemed with First-Order and Second-Order spirit beasts in its outer valleys, and terrifying Third-Order Spirit Beasts (equivalent to human Houtian Realm masters) deep inside its core magnetic canyons.

Dismounting at the fortified garrison town of Iron-Pass Outpost at the mountain’s edge, Lu Chen stabled his post-horse and stepped into the dense, mist-wrapped red-pine forest on foot.

Even with the 820-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Spear strapped across his back, Lu Chen moved through the tangled undergrowth without snapping a single dry twig. With his cultivation at the Peak of the Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) and his mastery of the Third Flash of the 《Golden Roc Nine-Flash Step》, each tap of his toes propelled him thirty feet from boulder to tree root like a silent black hawk.

Along the outer twenty miles of the mountain trail, groups of rough-looking mercenary hunters and outer sect disciples traveled in five-man squads, keeping their blades drawn against packs of First-Order Blood-Fanged Wolves and Iron-Quill Boars.

Along the mountain trail on the first afternoon, when an aggressive Early Second-Order Red-Backed Bear ambushed a three-man father-and-son herb-gathering team from Iron-Pass Outpost, Lu Chen didn’t hesitate for a breath—stepping between the terrified herb-gatherers and the beast, he knocked the bear unconscious with a single spiral palm strike and left the grateful family a packet of inscribed healing powder before continuing deeper into the range. Whenever a stray fierce beast lunged out of the brush along his path, he didn’t even need to draw the 820-jin spear from his back.

A single step forward, a spiral twist of his waist, and a bare-handed palm or finger strike infused with 6,800 jin of Dragon-Sinew strength and his fifteen thousand cuts of deboning anatomy comprehension would pierce straight through the beast’s weakest throat or cranial joint, killing it in a single heartbeat!

By the evening of the second day, as Lu Chen crossed the thirty-five-mile boundary ridge into the Inner Magnetic Canyon—where ordinary Fourth and Fifth Stage mercenaries rarely dared tread alone—the air grew noticeably charged with static electricity. Fine iron sand on the canyon floor stood up in tiny needles, and the smell of ozone and scorched ironwood lingered on the wind.

Crouching beside a split ironstone boulder, Lu Chen sank his perception into Prism Stillness and inspected the four-inch-deep hoofprints pressed into the red bedrock ahead.

“Each hoofprint is nine inches wide, ringed with crystallized black lightning slag—and the back-left print drags slightly with drops of dried purple-red blood,” Lu Chen analyzed calmly, touching a warm flake of coagulated blood on a fern leaf. “This is the Early Third-Order Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros reported by the garrison scouts. Based on the blood oxidation, the subterranean lightning strike cracked its rear carapace and damaged its lung meridian, cutting its combat power from Early Houtian down to roughly the Peak of the Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation).”

Even weakened to the Peak of the Sixth Stage, a Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros possessed a hide impervious to ordinary Grade 2 weapons and over 9,500 jin of brute trampling force!

Yet instead of retreating, Lu Chen’s eyes gleamed with quiet anticipation as he followed the blood trail two miles deeper into a horseshoe-shaped canyon: Thunder-Scar Gorge.
