At dawn the next morning, guided by the rainbow beam of the restored Star-Refracting Compass Lens, Lu Chen and his three companions rode the Cloud Roc into the howling, three-hundred-mile-wide Spatial-Glass Razor Storm surrounding the Eye of the Mirage.

Inside the storm, hurricane-force winds whipped millions of razor-sharp shards of ancient star-glass at speeds exceeding ten times the speed of sound—each shard carrying a trace of chaotic spatial-cutting force capable of shredding ordinary Grade 3 Xiantian Armor to ribbons!

Even the Blood-Eclipse Sect's vanguard three days ago had lost two flying bone-ships and thirty Xiantian disciples forcing their way through the storm!

Whenever a stray vortex of spatial-glass razors bypassed the safe corridor of the compass lens and shrieked toward their mount, Lu Chen stood calmly at the prow with his nine-foot, 7,200-jin Primordial Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear planted upright beside him.

DONG—!

Without even swinging the spear, the Living Dragon-Bell Spear Spirit inside the 7,200-jin shaft chimed automatically in harmony with Lu Chen's Complete Concept of Acoustic-Space Resonance, projecting a thirty-yard golden acoustic-standing-wave dome around the Cloud Roc! Every razor shard of spatial glass that touched the standing-wave dome was vibrated into harmless fine powder before it could come within twenty feet of Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, or Chu Kuang!

Four hours later, they broke through the inner wall of the storm into a dead-silent, fifty-mile-wide circular depression of smooth, mirror-flat black obsidian:

The Eye of the Mirage.
