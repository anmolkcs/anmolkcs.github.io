Before the Seven Star Valley Sect’s flying chariots departed north for the sect mountains three thousand miles away, Elder Ling granted Lu Chen, Su Wan’er, and Murong Zi a three-day stopover in the Azure Sky Royal Capital to settle their family affairs.

When the Azure-Winged Cloud Eagle Chariot descended directly outside the gates of Clear-Brook Manor, the entire Royal Capital shook with the news that sixteen-year-old Lu Chen had broken into the Houtian Realm, swept First Place across Five Kingdoms, and been named a Core Inheritor Disciple of the Seven Star Valley Sect!

Even the Azure Sky Emperor personally sent the Royal Steward to Clear-Brook Manor with a golden imperial plaque naming Lu Shan an Honorary Marquis of Cloudstride and Meng Wan a First-Rank Royal Lady, assigning an elite squad of royal guards to patrol the outer street respectfully—while Third Prince Zhao Heng was ordered into six months of closed-door reflection by the Emperor for offending a future pillar of the Seven Star Valley Sect!

Inside the sunlit courtyard of Clear-Brook Manor, however, Lu Chen couldn’t care less about imperial titles.

As soon as the manor gates closed behind the visitors, he rolled up his sleeves, helped his father Lu Shan carry out the cedar dining table under the osmanthus tree, and sat in the kitchen watching his mother Meng Wan fry golden scallion pancakes and simmer spirit-chicken soup.

“Mother, let me control the stove fire—with Houtian True Essence, I can keep the heat even down to a single degree,” Lu Chen grinned, channeling a gentle thread of golden-jade Houtian fire-essence beneath the clay stove.

“Using Houtian True Essence that can split boulders just to fry scallion pancakes for your mother—if those Xiantian Elders on the flying chariot saw you right now, their beards would fall off!” Meng Wan laughed until her eyes crinkled, tapping his forehead affectionately with her wooden spatula before popping a crisp, piping-hot piece of pancake into his mouth.

That night, in the manor’s meditation chamber, Lu Chen took out one third of the Thousand-Year Star-Marrow Liquid he had won as First Place in the Five-Kingdom Trial, diluted it into two bowls of warm Jade-Marrow Spirit Soup engraved with a Rank 3 Essence-Locking Rune, and personally guided his parents’ meridians for two full nights.

With the miraculous, gentle nourishment of the Thousand-Year Star-Marrow Liquid and the fourfold spirit concentration of the manor’s Rank 3 Spirit-Gathering Array: - His father Lu Shan broke cleanly through the Peak of the Fourth Stage and stepped directly into the Middle Fifth Stage (Bone Forging) with 6,500 jin of golden-iron bone strength! - His mother Meng Wan advanced smoothly through the Fourth Stage to reach the Early Fifth Stage (Bone Forging), her vitality so abundant that she looked like a young woman in her twenties!

Leaving his parents one hundred Low-Grade True Essence Stones, twenty Rune-Inscribed Fifth & Sixth Stage Pills, and a Winged-Falcon Communication Jade so they could exchange letters every ten days, Lu Chen embraced his father and mother warmly at dawn and boarded the Seven Star Valley Sect’s flying chariot.
