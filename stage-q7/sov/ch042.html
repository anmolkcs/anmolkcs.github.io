Fifteen years old, Middle Fifth Stage (Bone Forging), yet possessing over 14,000 jin of explosive spear power capable of crushing a Half-Step Sixth Stage veteran in a single breath!

In the eighty-year history of the Seven Star Martial House, not even the current #1 Core Disciple Murong Zi had displayed such terrifying physical dominance at the Fifth Stage!

Standing at the top of the jade stairs was a twenty-year-old youth dressed in an azure robe embroidered with four-clawed silver pythons, holding a jade-hilted longsword at his waist.
