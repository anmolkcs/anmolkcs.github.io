<p>That night, after the first two baskets of <strong>Black-Gold Pine Charcoal</strong> were fed into the smithy hearth—sending up a smokeless, roaring column of dazzling blue-white flame hot enough to soften Black-Marrow Ore in half the usual time—Lu Chen and Old Tie Shan hammered out four ten-jin bars of Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron before midnight.</p>
<p>As they paused to quench the sixty-sixth bar in the brine trough, Old Tie Shan watched how Lu Chen set down the sixty-pound iron sledgehammer and rolled his right shoulder, where a faint, dry <em>creak</em> came from the tendon attachment at the acromion process.</p>
<p>"Your muscles are completely wrung dry of mortal water now, Lu Chen," the old blacksmith observed sharply, gesturing with his tongs toward the dense, cable-like ridges of Lu Chen's forearms and shoulders. "Two thousand five hundred jin of <strong>Complete Tier Two Iron-Flesh</strong>. In all of Black-Rock County outside the garrison captains and the big clan heirs, you can count on two hands the number of men who've compacted their meat that tight."</p>
<p>"Yet when I swung the eighty-jin spear at full power to break Scar-Six's axes last night," Lu Chen said quietly, flexing his fingers as he looked down at his wrist, "my muscles had plenty of strength left, but the tendons connecting the muscle bellies to the elbow and shoulder bones felt stiff—like trying to shoot a heavy iron arrow with a hemp rope instead of a horn-bow string. If I pushed past twenty-five hundred jin of snap-force, the tendon would tear off the bone before the muscle tired."</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan nodded gravely.</p>
<p>"That's the great wall between <strong>Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong> and <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Drawing)</strong>," the old smith explained, sitting down on the log stool by the blue-white fire. "Nine out of ten frontier hunters who reach Tier Two stay trapped there until they die of old age. Why? Because muscle is soft red meat full of blood vessels—you feed it beast meat, hammer it hard, and it compacts in a month. But the <strong>Thirty-Six Major Sinew Lines</strong>—the white tendons and deep fascia sheets that tie your heels to your knees, your hips to your spine, and your shoulders to your fingers—have almost no blood flow in them! Under our world's tenfold gravity, a mortal's natural tendons are short, dry, and calcified by the <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong>."</p>
<p>"To step into <strong>Tier Three: Bowstring-Sinew Drawing</strong>," Old Tie Shan continued, tapping the iron anvil with his tongs, "a warrior has to soften those thirty-six white tendons with boiling <strong>Beast-Tendon &amp; Pine-Resin Paste</strong>, and then stretch them under continuous, bone-tearing counterweights while vibrating every sinew line from heel to fingertip until all thirty-six tendons lengthen by half an inch and turn translucent gold-white like cured <strong>Dragon-Horn Bowstrings</strong>! Once your thirty-six sinews are drawn into bowstrings, you don't just push your spear with muscle—every step and every thrust snaps with <strong>elastic tendon-recoil</strong>, jumping your strength from twenty-five hundred jin straight past <strong>three thousand six hundred to four thousand eight hundred jin</strong>!"</p>
<p>"And <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> in Dead-Crow Gorge," Lu Chen murmured, "is at <strong>Peak Tier Three</strong>."</p>
<p>"All thirty-six of Han Wu's sinew lines have been drawn since his days as a border-garrison corporal ten years ago," Old Tie Shan said grimly. "When a Peak Tier Three warrior swings a hundred-jin saber, the sound of his tendons snapping inside his sleeves sounds like ten crossbows firing at once. Now that you've killed both Ma Liu and his sworn brother Scar-Six, the moment the snow clears enough for Han Wu to realize Bitter-Pine Ravine wiped out his raiders, he'll come with every cutthroat in Dead-Crow Gorge. You can't fight a Peak Tier Three bowstring-warrior with Tier-Two tendons, Lu Chen—his saber speed will tear your shoulder capsules apart after fifty exchanges."</p>
<p>Lu Chen looked toward the pile of two-foot-thick <strong>Bitter-Iron Pine</strong> logs stacked outside the shed and the four-inch-thick tendons stripped from the legs of the <strong>Scarred King-Boar</strong> and the <strong>Silver-Backed Alpha Wolf</strong> hanging from the rafters.</p>
<p>"Then before Han Wu moves," Lu Chen said calmly, "I draw the bow."</p>
