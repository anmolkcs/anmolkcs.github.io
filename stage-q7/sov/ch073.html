“He chose Broken Spear Peak?!”

The inner disciples watching around Star-Gathering Square gasped in surprise, while a tall, bare-armed old man with muscles like cast bronze and a wild mane of iron-gray hair standing at the corner of the dais let out a booming laugh that shook the tiles of the hall!

That old man was Elder Tie Cang (Iron-Spear Elder)—the Peak Master of Broken Spear Peak, a Peak Xiantian Realm powerhouse who wielded a 6,000-jin Earth-Step heavy spear!

“Good! Good! For ten years, every #1 trial genius has run to Alkaid Sword Peak to wave three-foot toothpicks—finally, a true spear seed with Ten Thousand Jin Golden-Jade Bones and Mountain-Thunder Spear Intent has come to my Broken Spear Peak!”

Elder Tie clapped Lu Chen’s shoulder with genuine paternal warmth and carried him alongside Chu Kuang (who also chose Broken Spear Peak out of admiration for Lu Chen’s heavy weapon path!) straight up to the sixth peak.

Although Broken Spear Peak had only twenty-six disciples, there were zero scheming cliques or aristocratic factions here.

Lu Chen’s Senior Brothers—led by the honest, barrel-chested Eldest Senior Brother Fang Shi (Peak Houtian Realm) and Second Senior Sister Lin Ying (Late Houtian Realm)—welcomed Lu Chen and Chu Kuang like younger brothers, personally helping Lu Chen clean out the best independent stone residence beside the Ancient Star-Spear Cliff and cooking a massive cauldron of Third-Order Star-Horned Ox Stew to celebrate their arrival!

“Junior Brother Lu,” Elder Tie said warmly after dinner, handing Lu Chen a heavy black-iron token. “Because our Broken Spear Peak has few people, all our peak’s cultivation resources are shared without bureaucratic red tape. With this Core Peak Token, you may meditate beneath the Ancient Star-Spear Cliff at any time, and receive ten Middle-Grade True Essence Stones (equivalent to one thousand Low-Grade True Essence Stones!) every month!”
