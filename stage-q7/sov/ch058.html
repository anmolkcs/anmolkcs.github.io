Three days later, a deafening eagle cry echoed across the clouds above the Azure Sky Royal Capital.

Descending from the northern horizon was a giant Peak Third-Order Spirit Beast—an Azure-Winged Cloud Eagle with a wingspan of over sixty feet, pulling a bronze-and-jade flying chariot engraved with the emblem of seven silver stars!

Standing at the prow of the flying chariot was a middle-aged elder in starry silver robes whose aura radiated the vast, unmistakable pressure of the Middle Xiantian Realm—hovering thirty feet in midair without stepping on any support!

He was Elder Ling, the supervising envoy sent by the Seven Star Valley Sect (a true Fourth-Grade Xiantian Sect governing five mortal kingdoms across a hundred-thousand-mile territory of the southern Sky Spill Continent) to oversee the triennial Five-Kingdom Core Disciple Trial!

On the main square of the Seven Star Martial House, Palace Master Qin led all the elders and the three representatives—#1 Lu Chen, #2 Murong Zi, and #3 Su Wan’er—to bow in welcome.

“Three days from now, the Five-Kingdom Core Disciple Trial will open at Sky-Fall Canyon, eight hundred miles north at the border of the five kingdoms,” Elder Ling announced from midair, his keen Xiantian gaze sweeping across Lu Chen, Murong Zi, and Su Wan’er and pausing with a flicker of surprise on the sixteen-year-old Lu Chen. “Fifty elite disciples—ten from each of the five kingdoms (Azure Sky, Black-Wind, Great Yue, Crimson Sand, and Iron-Peak)—will enter the Ancient Sky-Fall Battlefield for seven days.”

Elder Ling raised three fingers:

“The rules of the Five-Kingdom Trial are simple: 1. Inside the Ancient Sky-Fall Battlefield, you will hunt Second-Order Peak and Third-Order Spirit Beasts and collect Ancient Star-Fallen Tokens to accumulate trial points. 2. Any disciple who ranks in the Top 15 enters the Seven Star Valley Sect as an Inner Sect Disciple; 3. The Top 3 directly become Core Inheritor Disciples of our Seven Star Valley’s seven peaks—and First Place Overall will be rewarded with one drop of Thousand-Year Star-Marrow Liquid and a Grade 3 Upper-Tier Xiantian Treasure!”

“However,” Elder Ling added with a faint smile, “this year, both the Great Yue Kingdom’s #1 prodigy Chu Kuang and the Black-Wind Kingdom’s #1 prodigy Yan Luo broke through to the Houtian Realm before age eighteen. If your Azure Sky Kingdom doesn’t want to finish dead last again like three years ago, you’d better sharpen your blades.”
