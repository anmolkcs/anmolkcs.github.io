<p>In the lower worlds, a cultivator at the Houtian or Xiantian Realm could wield a thousand-jin or ten-thousand-jin weapon because they circulated True Essence and comprehended gravity laws to lighten the shaft in their own hands.</p>
<p>Here in the <strong>Great Desolate Origin Universe</strong>, at <strong>Tier One of the Mortal-Shedding Stage</strong>, there was no True Essence—only raw bone, tendon, muscle, and tenfold gravity!</p>
<p>Under tenfold Desolate Gravity, an <strong>eighty-jin solid iron spear</strong> carried a downward gravitational weight equivalent to <strong>eight hundred jin</strong> in the lower realms, and when thrust or swept at the end of a seven-foot shaft, the rotational torque on the wielder's front wrist exceeded two thousand jin! Even a <strong>Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong> frontier guard usually wielded a forty- or fifty-jin ash-shafted spear because a solid iron shaft was too slow to recover if a thrust missed.</p>
<p>Yet to Lu Chen—who had practiced ten thousand unadorned thrusts every morning from Cloudstride City to the summit of the Star-Soul Universe—an eighty-jin solid iron shaft was the exact weight needed to turn every single thrust into a live <strong>Iron-Flesh Compacting</strong> exercise!</p>
<p>For the next five nights, after spending twelve hours each day hammering out the village's tribute bars of Cold-Slag Iron alongside Shi Tou and the boys, Lu Chen stayed alone at the smithy hearth from midnight until the third cock-crow.</p>
<p>He did not cast the spear in a mold—cast iron in this world was brittle and dead. Instead, working entirely by hand with the foot-treadle vise and his forty-pound sledge, he forge-welded the hundred and ten jin of dark-blue <strong>Black-Marrow Core nuggets</strong> into three long rods: a central octagonal core of resilient, medium-carbon tough iron to absorb lateral shock without bending, wrapped in two outer twisted cables of high-carbon hard-slag steel.</p>
<p>Night after night, the solitary <em>CLANG—CLANG—CLANG</em> of his hammer echoed softly through the snow-muffled village.</p>
<p>With <strong>Prism Stillness</strong> enveloping the anvil within three paces, not a single air bubble, flux pocket, or cold-shut flaw survived beneath his hammer. Over five nights and three thousand strikes, the hundred and ten jin of core nuggets were drawn out, twisted in tight spiral ridges like a dragon's spine to maximize structural stiffness, and compacted down into a seven-foot-two-inch, <strong>eighty-jin</strong> unadorned heavy spear!</p>
<p>On the fifth night—the twenty-seventh of the tenth month—Lu Chen shaped the fourteen-inch spearhead: a thick, four-edged <strong>Armor-Splitting Diamond Point</strong> with two short horizontal boar-wings at the base of the socket so an impaled beast could not slide up the shaft to maul the wielder.</p>
<p>Finally, before quenching the spearhead, Lu Chen picked up the <strong>three-inch rusted iron collar</strong> of his shattered Primordial Sovereign Spear.</p>
<p>Even heated in the white-hot Bitter-Pine charcoal, the ancient collar did not melt—for though its divine laws were dead, its base material had once been tempered in the River of Time. Lu Chen did not try to melt it; instead, he hot-shrunk the glowing socket of the eighty-jin boar-spear directly around the rusted three-inch collar, locking it permanently right at the balance point where his lead hand gripped the throat of the spear!</p>
<p><em>HSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS—!</em></p>
<p>When he plunged the glowing seven-foot iron spear vertically into a deep trough of melted mountain snow mixed with <strong>Iron-Jaw Hyena Blood</strong> and <strong>Char-Willow Ash</strong>, a thick column of crimson-white steam rose to the rafters of the shed.</p>
<p>Lu Chen lifted the cooled spear out of the trough with his right hand.</p>
<p>It had no spirit-treasure glow, no dragon-roar phenomenon, and no carved ornamentation—just seventy-two inches of oil-black, spiral-grained iron shaft topped by a fourteen-inch matte-gray four-edged point, with a rough, rusted three-inch ring sitting squarely under his left thumb.</p>
<p>Yet when Lu Chen planted his feet in the snow outside the shed and thrust the eighty-jin iron spear forward in a single <strong>Level Thrust</strong>, the displaced winter air cracked with a low, dull <em>WHOOM</em> that shook the icicles off the smithy eaves!</p>
