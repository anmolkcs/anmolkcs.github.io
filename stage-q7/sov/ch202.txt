<p>After flying three thousand miles northward across the southern prefecture of the Central Imperial Domain, the Grade 5 Sky-Barge arrived before a colossal mountain range where <strong>nine fifty-thousand-foot stone peaks</strong> rose straight into the stratosphere like nine unsheathed divine spears piercing the vault of heaven!</p>
<p>This was the headquarters of one of the seven great <strong>Second-Grade Continental Overlord Sects</strong> of the Central Imperial Domain:</p>
<p><strong>The Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect!</strong></p>
<p>Whereas the Seven Star Valley Sect had been a Fourth-Grade sect with four Revolving Core elders, the Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect boasted over <strong>one hundred thousand Revolving Core and Xiantian disciples</strong>, <strong>three hundred Life-and-Death Wheel Realm Elders</strong> (divided across the <strong>Six Revolutions: Three Life-Renewals &amp; Three Death-Tribulations</strong>), and three supreme <strong>Divine Sea &amp; Void-Bridge Realm Ancestors</strong>!</p>
<p>As the Sky-Barge descended onto the vast <strong>Outer Sovereign Reception Plaza</strong> at the base of the <strong>Third Spear Peak</strong>, over a thousand outer and inner Revolving Core disciples of the Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect were gathered around a towering bronze monument—the <strong>Sect Young Overlord Roll</strong>—discussing the upcoming <strong>Continental Young Sovereign Summit</strong>.</p>
<p>Standing at the front of the plaza in a gleaming silver-scale armor robe, holding a <strong>12,000-jin Grade 5 Lower-Tier Silver-Frost Spear</strong>, was a twenty-four-year-old youth whose cultivation stood at the <strong>Peak of the First Life-Renewal Revolution (Half-Step First Death-Tribulation!)</strong>:</p>
<p><strong>Zhao Wuji</strong>—the nephew of the Sect's Law Enforcement Grand Elder (<strong>Elder Zhao</strong>, a <strong>Fourth-Revolution / Second Life-Renewal</strong> powerhouse) and currently ranked <strong>#6</strong> among the young generation of the Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect!</p>
<p>When <strong>Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng</strong> led Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang off the Sky-Barge onto the reception plaza, Zhao Wuji and a dozen aristocratic inner disciples glanced over at the southern borderland banner on the barge and let out a low, dismissive chuckle.</p>
<p>"Martial Uncle Cang Feng," Zhao Wuji bowed perfunctorily toward Cang Feng before sweeping his cold, arrogant gaze over Lu Chen's plain black cloth robe and the three Early Revolving Core companions behind him. "You traveled ten thousand miles to the barren Southern Region across the Astral Storm-Trench, and instead of bringing back the <strong>Sixth-Order Astral-Thunder Leviathan's Core</strong> for our Law Enforcement Peak, you brought back four eighteen-to-twenty-year-old country bumpkins from a Fourth-Grade border sect? Wait—don't tell me you wasted our sect's precious <strong>Golden Dragon-Gate Medallions</strong> on three <strong>Early Revolving Core</strong> kids?!"</p>
<p>Because Lu Chen had used the <strong>Chaotic Star Immortal Dharma Art</strong> and <strong>Prism Stillness</strong> to retract 100% of his <strong>First Life-Renewal Wheel</strong> aura inward so that he appeared no different from an ordinary Early Revolving Core youth to anyone below the Third Revolution, Zhao Wuji assumed Lu Chen was also just an Early Revolving Core borderlander!</p>
<p>"Watch your tongue, Zhao Wuji!" <strong>Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng's</strong> brows furrowed sharply. "Little friend Lu Chen is the one who descended thirty thousand feet to the bottom of the Astral Storm-Trench, slew the corrupted Sixth-Order Astral-Thunder Leviathan, re-anchored the Sky-Bridge, and defeated the Nether-Blood Cult's First Life-Wheel envoy <strong>Elder Yin-Sha</strong> in a single spear thrust! I have invited him to join our sect as an <strong>Honorary Peak Guest Elder &amp; Core Seed</strong>!"</p>
<p>"An eighteen-year-old kid from the Southern Region slaying a Sixth-Order Leviathan and defeating a Life-and-Death Wheel envoy?"</p>
<p>Zhao Wuji and the dozen Law Enforcement Peak disciples burst into open, mocking laughter!</p>
<p>"Martial Uncle Cang Feng, everyone knows your <strong>Seventh Spear Peak (Star-Fall Peak)</strong> has been declining for fifty years since your peak master was injured, dropping to the bottom of our nine peaks—but making up fairy tales about an eighteen-year-old borderland boy defeating a Life-and-Death Wheel master just to claim a <strong>First-Class Guest Elder Manor</strong> and <strong>three hundred High-Grade True Essence Stones</strong> from the sect treasury is going too far!"</p>
<p>Zhao Wuji stepped forward, planting his <strong>12,000-jin Grade 5 Silver-Frost Spear</strong> onto the plaza tiles with a booming <em>CLANG</em> that sent a wave of <strong>4,200,000-jin Peak First-Revolution</strong> frost-spear pressure rolling directly toward Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang to force them to their knees:</p>
<p>"According to the ironclad rule set by our Founding Ancestor, anyone from outside the Central Domain who wishes to claim a <strong>Guest Elder Manor</strong> and a <strong>Golden Dragon-Gate Medallion</strong> without starting from the outer barracks must either withstand three strikes from a Top 10 Young Overlord on the plaza—or step up to the <strong>Pre-Epoch Bronze Sundial</strong> at the center of the square and light up <strong>three epoch-marks</strong>! If this southern kid can't even stand in front of my spear aura, let alone light the Bronze Sundial, he and his three friends can go sweep the outer stables!"</p>
