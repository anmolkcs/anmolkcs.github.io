Just as the three-hundred-foot blood halberd descended within fifty yards of the array dome—

DONG————————!!

From the western horizon thirty miles away, a single, heaven-shaking chime of an ancient Golden-Jade Dragon Bell rolled across the sky at five times the speed of sound—shattering the ten-mile Blood-Eclipse War Cloud in half like a stone thrown through a spiderweb!

Before the echo of the bell even finished ringing in Xue Cangqiong's ears, space above Broken Spear Peak tore open in a blaze of purple-gold lightning and starlight!

Seventeen-year-old Lu Chen stepped out of the void directly above the golden array dome, his black Grand Elder robe fluttering in the astral wind and his nine-foot, 7,200-jin Primordial Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear raised in one hand:
