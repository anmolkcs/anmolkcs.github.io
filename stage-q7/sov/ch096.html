Even with his father now an Early Houtian master, Lu Chen knew that as he prepares to challenge the Seven-Sect Martial Conference and eventually step into the wider Sky Spill Continent, keeping his parents in a mortal kingdom three thousand miles away was far less safe than having them right inside the Xiantian Grand Mountain Array of the Seven Star Valley Sect!

When Lu Chen mentioned this to Elder Tie, the Peak Master of Broken Spear Peak patted his chest immediately:

“Why even ask, Little Chen? Our Broken Spear Peak has thirty empty spirit-spring courtyards on the sunny southern slope! Bring Uncle Lu and Aunt Meng straight back with us on the flying chariot today—not only will they live under our sect’s Rank 5 Xiantian Guardian Formation, but your Aunt Meng’s cooking is ten times better than our peak’s kitchen hall!”

And because Su Wan’er had also risen to become Pill-Star Peak’s prized Rank 2 Peak Inscription genius, Grand Marshal Su retired from his post in the capital to accompany them to the sect’s outer valley Villa District as well!

Three days later, inside Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 on the southern slope of Broken Spear Peak—just three hundred paces below Lu Chen’s own cultivation residence—Lu Shan and Meng Wan settled joyfully into their new mountain home surrounded by spirit orchards, crane ponds, and the warm company of Elder Tie, Fang Shi, Chu Kuang, and Su Wan’er.

With his parents living safely beside him in a fourfold spirit-vein valley and advancing steadily every day, Lu Chen’s martial heart became completely free of worry.
