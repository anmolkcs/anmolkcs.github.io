Before entering the thirty-day seclusion inside the Profound Gravity & Earth-Fire Cave, Lu Chen spent a full day at Clear-Brook Manor at the foot of Seven Star Mountain with his parents.

Early in the morning, as golden sunlight filtered through the osmanthus trees and white mist rose off the spirit-water spring in the central courtyard, two figures stood side by side on the bluestone flagstones, moving through a slow, rhythmic breathing stance.

They were Lu Chen’s father, Lu Shan, and his mother, Meng Wan.

Compared to two months ago when they had been exhausted, prematurely graying tavern keepers in Cloudstride City, the transformation in the couple was remarkable. Dressed in comfortable indigo-silk martial robes, forty-year-old Lu Shan looked like a vigorous man in his late twenties, his broad shoulders brimming with Early Third Stage (Viscera Training) blood-qi; beside him, Meng Wan’s skin had regained a rosy, youthful glow as she stabilized her cultivation at the Peak of the Second Stage (Flesh Training).

“Father, sink your left heel half an inch deeper when exhaling on the seventh beat; Mother, let your shoulders relax as the breath rises through the lung meridian,” Lu Chen said with a warm smile, walking across the courtyard with a wooden tray holding two porcelain bowls of steaming Golden-Marrow Spirit Porridge.

Seeing their son, Lu Shan and Meng Wan immediately finished their stance and hurried over to take the heavy wooden tray from his hands.

“You’re the #1 Core Disciple of the Seven Star Martial House now, Chen’er, and you’re entering the sacred cave at noon—how can you be serving porridge to your father and mother?” Meng Wan scolded him fondly, though her eyes curved into crescents of pure happiness as she smelled the rich aroma of the porridge, which Lu Chen had simmered with slivers of Five-Hundred-Year Golden Marrow Ginseng and sealed with a Rank 2 Essence-Locking Rune.

“No matter how high I climb in the Martial House, in this courtyard I’m just your son,” Lu Chen laughed, sitting down with them on the stone bench beneath the osmanthus tree. “Drink it while it’s warm. I added a drop of purified Jade-Scale Flood-Dragon Blood to each bowl. While I’m inside the Profound Gravity Cave for the next thirty days, this porridge and the six Rune-Inscribed Tendon-Nourishing Pills in the study will help Father break through into the Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) and Mother step smoothly into the Third Stage.”

As Lu Shan drank the warm golden porridge, a gentle, surging current of dragon-marrow vitality spread through his limbs, making the tendons along his arms hum with a crisp resonance.

“With my son feeding me treasures that even Royal Capital clan patriarchs fight over, if I can’t even reach the Fourth Stage in a month, I won’t have the face to drink tea with Old Liu when he visits next spring!” Lu Shan laughed heartily, clapping his chest.

Meng Wan reached out and gently smoothed the collar of Lu Chen’s Grade 3 Coiling-Dragon Inner Armor beneath his black outer robe. “Inside that Earth-Fire Cave, don’t push yourself recklessly just to advance faster, Chen’er. Your father and I don’t care whether you’re number one in five kingdoms or ten—we just want you coming home whole to eat your mother’s cooking.”

“I promise, Mother. Thirty days from now, I’ll be back in time for dinner.”
