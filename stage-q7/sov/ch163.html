Welcoming Lu Chen and his companions into the central glass-forge hall of the oasis with steaming bowls of sweet desert-melon tea and crystal dates, Clan Chief Liu-Li Yan explained what had happened three days earlier:

"Benefactor Lu, three days ago, five flying bone-ships carrying Blood-Eclipse Sect elders—led by their Late Revolving Core vice-sect master, Blood-Bone Old Demon, and a proud young man named Xue Wuhen—flew over our oasis toward the Eye of the Mirage eight hundred miles west," Clan Chief Yan sighed, pointing toward a shattered, six-foot-wide disk of ancient nine-layered star-glass resting on an iron cradle in the hall. "They demanded our clan's ancestral Star-Refracting Compass Lens, which points the safe path through the Spatial-Glass Razor Storm surrounding the Eye of the Mirage. Rather than let demonic cultists use our ancestor's relic, I shattered the lens into forty-eight pieces—and they released those parasite-scorpions to slaughter us while they went ahead to force the storm with blood sacrifices!"

"Forty-eight pieces of nine-layered lead-quartz star-glass, bonded originally with ancient bronze-mercury solder," Lu Chen murmured, walking over to the iron cradle as his antiquities-restorer's eyes lit up with quiet focus. "Clan Chief Yan, the internal rune pathways inside the forty-eight shards haven't dissipated yet. Give me two hours and a quiet flame—I can restore your ancestral lens better than new."

Under the astonished gazes of Clan Chief Yan, little Liu-Li Yao, and Su Wan'er, Lu Chen entered Prism Stillness.

Releasing forty-eight gossamer threads of his Rank 4 Inscription Grandmaster soul force, he suspended all forty-eight jagged shards of ancient star-glass in midair, aligning their microscopic crystal-lattice fracture planes down to a ten-thousandth of an inch—a feat that combined his eight years of museum glass-and-bronze restoration on Earth with the Cold-Flame Micro-Forging Art of the Chrono-Soul Prism!

With his right hand, Lu Chen drew a Rank 4 Star-Light Bridging Inscription along the hairline seams using molten silver-amber ink.

HUM—FLASH!

One hour later, the forty-eight shards fused into a single, seamless, six-foot disk of rainbow star-glass without a single visible scar!

Grateful beyond words, Clan Chief Yan not only lent them the restored Star-Refracting Compass Lens to cross the storm, but also gifted Lu Chen a pouch of rare Ten-Thousand-Year Vitrified Lightning-Sand and three boxes of sweet Jade-Crystal Desert Dates (which Lu Chen happily stored in a separate compartment of his spatial ring for his mother Meng Wan!).
