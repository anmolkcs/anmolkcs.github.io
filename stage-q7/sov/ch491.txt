<p>Inside the dark, four-foot-wide diagonal shaft of the <strong>Emergency Ventilation Chimney</strong>, forty freed debt-miners climbed upward through the mountain rock like men reborn from hell.</p>
<p>At the rear of the column climbed Lu Chen, his <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> slung across his blood-stained back.</p>
<p>Every breath he drew inside the narrow rock chimney tasted of iron and fire. Holding up that twelve-ton basalt keystone lintel for sixty full breaths at the bottom of the pit had pushed all <strong>206 Late Tier Four bones</strong> in his skeleton to the very precipice of final transformation. Across the innermost marrow-walls of his femurs, spine, ribs, and forearm bones, a deep, rhythmic pulsing echoed in time with his heartbeat—like white-hot steel waiting on the anvil for one final tempering battle to lock into <strong>100% Peak Tier Four Perfection</strong>!</p>
<p>After forty minutes of relentless climbing up the ancient stone-chiseled rungs of the diagonal ventilation shaft, the crushing fifteenfold and eighteenfold gravity of the deep Earth-Marrow fault dropped away behind them, returning to the lighter tenfold gravity of the upper canyon.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a draft of freezing, clean winter air smelling of pine snow blew down the chimney!</p>
<p>At the top of the shaft, <strong>Tie Niu</strong> and <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong> put their shoulders together against a rusted iron ventilation grate set into the cliff wall of the <strong>Upper Winch-Landing</strong>, ten fathoms below the canyon rim, and heaved!</p>
<p><em>KRAK—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>The rusted bolts sheared off, and the forty-one men poured out of the mountainside onto the snow-covered timber staging-platform under the cold stars of the seventeenth night!</p>
<p>Thirty feet above them on the main rim of <strong>Black-Slag Canyon</strong>, lanterns blazed around the stone guardhouse of <strong>Pit #7</strong> and the adjacent wooden <strong>Witness-Shack</strong>.</p>
<p>Outside the Witness-Shack, four of <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan's</strong> mounted bailiff guards had just drawn their notched ring-pommel sabers and kicked open the wooden door—where sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> stood cornered beside a terrified, bound <strong>County Audit Clerk Fang</strong>, wielding only his thirty-pound smith's hammer in his single right hand!</p>
<p>"You heard that rumble three hundred fathoms down, old cripple!" the lead bailiff guard laughed cruelly, raising his saber toward Old Tie Shan's neck while two other guards held Clerk Fang down on the floor with a gag in his mouth. "A tragic 'sulfur-gas explosion' just buried Drift Four—along with your bastard son Tie Niu and that arrogant Tier-Three brat! Sub-Bailiff Zhao says you lost your mind with grief and attacked the witness-shack, so we had to put you down!"</p>
<p>"Even if my boy died in the dark," sixty-year-old Old Tie Shan roared, tears freezing in his gray beard as he swung his thirty-pound hammer with his single arm, "I'll take two of you dogs down the cliff with me—!"</p>
<p><em>WHOOOOOOOOOOSH—SHHHHK!!</em></p>
<p>Before the lead bailiff guard's saber could fall an inch, a seven-foot-six-inch bolt of blue-black lightning screamed up from the lower staging-ledge forty feet away!</p>
<p>Hurled by Lu Chen's <strong>seven-thousand-two-hundred-jin Late Tier Four</strong> arm, a four-foot <strong>Cold-Slag Trench-Spear</strong> punched straight through the back of the lead guard's iron brigandine, lifted him three feet off the snow, and pinned him dead against the timber frame of the Witness-Shack doorway!</p>
<p>In the next breath, Lu Chen vaulted up the forty-foot vertical basalt cliff-face in three bounds of his iron legs, landing squarely in the doorway of the Witness-Shack with his <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> in hand!</p>
<p><em>CLANG! WHACK! CRUNCH!</em></p>
<p>Three flicks of the 160-jin octagonal spear shaft—moving too fast for the eye to follow—shattered the collarbones and skulls of the remaining three bailiff guards before they could even turn around!</p>
