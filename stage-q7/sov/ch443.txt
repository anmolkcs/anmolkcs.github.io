<p>Before dawn on the fifth day of the eleventh month, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> walked into the smithy shed to check on Old Tie Shan—only to find Lu Chen waiting by the hearth with the covered clay jar of boiled <strong>Early Tier Two Alpha Wolf Spine-Marrow</strong> and the second jar of <strong>Ma Liu's Tier-Two Sinew-Warming Ointment</strong>.</p>
<p>"I took the King-Boar's heart at the wallow because my muscles were already half-torn from the charge," Lu Chen said before the grizzled hunter could refuse, pushing the steaming clay jar into Uncle Shi's hands. "You've been stuck at <strong>Peak Tier One (Half-Step Tier Two)</strong> for twelve winters because every time you hunted a Tier-Two beast, you traded the marrow-bones at the town apothecary to buy grain for the widows and orphans in Ash-Brook. Drink this down while it's boiling, Uncle Shi, and let me use theforty-pound sledge-butt to tap your back meridians while the marrow is hot."</p>
<p>Uncle Shi stared down at the steaming clay jar of crimson-gold wolf marrow.</p>
<p>"Lu Chen," the fifty-year-old hunter rasped, "if you drink this second half of the Alpha's marrow yourself, you can push straight from Early Tier Two toward <strong>Middle Tier Two</strong> before the week is out—"</p>
<p>"And if <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> brings twenty bandits down on Ash-Brook while I'm holding the front gate, who holds the western wall if you're still at Tier One?" Lu Chen asked quietly. "A village doesn't stand on one spear, Uncle Shi. Drink."</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan barked from the lean-to doorway: "Stop yapping like an old woman and drink it, Shi Lao-Er! Are you going to let the boy talk sense into you twice?"</p>
<p>Swallowing the lump in his throat, Uncle Shi tilted the clay jar back, drank the scorching <strong>Tier-Two Alpha Wolf Marrow</strong> down to the dregs, and stripped off his boar-skin coat.</p>
<p>For the next hour in front of the roaring hearth, while Uncle Shi held a deep horse stance with two hundred-jin pig-iron bars across his thighs, Lu Chen smeared the <strong>Sinew-Warming Ointment</strong> across the old hunter's scar-chewed back and shoulders. Using the three-pace microscopic clarity of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen spotted the seven places around Uncle Shi's shoulder blades and lower lumbar where twenty years of cold <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong> had formed calcified knots blocking the muscle bundles from compacting.</p>
<p>Gripping the rounded iron pommel of a ten-pound hand-hammer, Lu Chen delivered a series of rapid, precisely calibrated <strong>Vibrating Percussive Taps</strong>—each blow carrying exactly four hundred jin of penetrating shock that shattered the stagnant ash-knots without bruising the old hunter's underlying ribs!</p>
<p><em>THUMP—THUMP—THUMP—KRAK!</em></p>
<p>On the forty-ninth tap, Uncle Shi let out a hoarse, lung-clearing roar as two mouthfuls of cold, greyish phlegm and stagnant blood burst from his throat onto the cinders! Across his lean, scarred back and single-handed left arm, the loose, aging muscle fibers suddenly pulled tight and condensed into dark, iron-hard cords!</p>
<p><strong>Early Tier Two of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Iron-Flesh Compacting!</strong></p>
<p>At fifty-two years old—an age when most frontier hunters were already stiffening toward the grave—Uncle Shi clenched his right fist and felt <strong>one thousand eight hundred jin</strong> of rejuvenated, explosive strength surge through his frame!</p>
<p>"Eighteen hundred jin..." Uncle Shi laughed aloud, his eyes bright as a young man's as he swung a hundred-jin pig-iron bar overhead with one arm. "Let Han Wu's bastards come try the west wall now!"</p>
<p>That afternoon, in the trampled snow of the village square, Lu Chen and the newly advanced Tier-Two Uncle Shi organized the twenty strongest men and youths of Ash-Brook—including <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, <strong>Cross-Eyed Liu</strong>, healed <strong>Old Ma</strong>, and fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong>—into a disciplined <strong>Three-Rank Frontier Spear &amp; Crossbow Militia</strong>: - A front rank of six shield-and-spear men holding heavy oak-plank pavises reinforced with scrap iron; - A second rank of eight long-spearmen trained to thrust simultaneously over the shoulders of the front rank on the drum-beat; - And a rear rank of four marksmen armed with the four captured <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong>, drilling until they could span, load, and loose a volley every twelve breaths.</p>
