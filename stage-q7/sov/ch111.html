Twelve hundred miles northwest of the Seven Star Valley Sect, the southern mountain ranges ended abruptly at a sheer, vertical precipice that plunged twenty thousand feet into a bottomless sea of churning silver-white clouds:

The Thousand-Fathom Wind-Chasm!

Stretching eight thousand miles across between the Southern Region and the border of the Central Plains, the Thousand-Fathom Wind-Chasm was a natural forbidden zone for ordinary flying beasts and even Early-to-Middle Xiantian cultivators. Why? Because beneath the churning cloud layer raged permanent Subterranean Inversion Gales and chaotic magnetic vortices that tore flying mounts out of the sky and drained a Xiantian cultivator's Innate Origin Qi within two hundred miles!

Yet five thousand years ago, the Artisan & Meridian-Weaver Guild of Loom-Star City had accomplished an engineering miracle that still left every first-time traveler breathless:

Spanning across the eight-thousand-mile chasm from cliff to cliff—anchored every fifty miles atop towering stone Sky-Piers rising out of the abyss—stretched three parallel, yard-thick cables braided from Ten-Thousand-Year Meteoric Star-Iron and Fourth-Order Wind-Dragon Sinew!

This was the Cloud-Sea Sky-Rail!

When Lu Chen (Early Xiantian), Su Wan'er (Half-Step Xiantian), Murong Zi (Peak Houtian), and Chu Kuang (Peak Houtian)—accompanied by Grandmaster Gu—arrived at Wind-Cliff Port, a three-hundred-foot-long Runic Magnetic Sky-Galleon constructed of polished ironwood and bronze-gold array plates was floating three feet above the three giant star-iron cables, held aloft by a humming Rank 4 Repulsion-Levitation Formation along its keel!

"Even after seeing it three times, the Cloud-Sea Sky-Rail is still a marvel of the continent's inscription craft," Grandmaster Gu sighed in admiration as he led Lu Chen and the others up the boarding gangplank onto the upper deck of the Star-Weaver VII. "By locking its keel array onto the magnetic field of the three star-iron cables, this Sky-Galleon can carry five hundred passengers and ten thousand tons of cargo across the eight-thousand-mile Wind-Chasm at four hundred miles an hour, completely immune to the Inversion Gales below!"

Standing at the prow railing of the Sky-Galleon, Lu Chen rested his palm lightly on the bronze gunwale and sank his perception into Prism Stillness.

Through the soles of his boots, he could feel the intricate 3,600-Node Rank 4 Magnetic-Levitation & Wind-Splitting Array pulsing inside the ship's keel—and he noticed immediately that the central Gyroscopic Star-Compass in the ship's wheelhouse shared the exact same pre-epoch runic architecture as the broken Antiquarian Star-Compass he had spent the past five nights restoring!
