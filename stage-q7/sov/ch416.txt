<p>"Get him to the smithy hearth—fast! Before the hyena corpse-rot gets into his elbow vein!"</p>
<p>Uncle Shi dropped his horn-bow and moved to support Lu Chen's right shoulder, while Old Tie Shan cursed fiercely under his breath and ran ahead to blow the forge coals back to white heat. Behind them, two young hunters dragged the three dead Ridge Hyenas toward the butchering rack, while another ran to the western wall to tend to poor old Watchman Ge, who had lost three fingers and half his ear before the alarm sounded, though Lu Chen's shout had scared the pack off before they could finish tearing out the old man's throat.</p>
<p>Inside the smithy shed, Lu Chen sat down on the stone anvil-block without needing Uncle Shi's support.</p>
<p>Now that he had broken through the doorway of <strong>Early Tier One (Ash-Skin Scouring)</strong> during that life-or-death clash in the doorway, the difference in his mortal body was immediately palpable. Even though the four fang-punctures on his left forearm were half an inch deep and oozing blackish-red blood tainted with the hyena's septic saliva, the toughened dermal layer around the wounds had contracted tightly like cinched leather, preventing the flesh from ripping laterally and slowing the bleeding to a sluggish trickle.</p>
<p>"Granny Liu is stitching Watchman Ge's neck at the west gate," Old Tie Shan said grimly, pulling a flat iron cauterizing spatula from the white-hot heart of the charcoal fire with his single hand. The old blacksmith's face was pale under his coal-soot. "Hyena spit carries <strong>Grey-Mire Rot</strong>. If we wait half an hour for her herb-pot, the poison will climb to your shoulder and I'll have to take your left arm off just like mine. Lu Chen—hold out your arm while Uncle Shi holds your shoulders down."</p>
<p>"No need to hold me, Uncle Shi," Lu Chen said quietly.</p>
<p>He laid his bleeding left forearm flat across the cold iron face of the anvil, palm down, fingers spread evenly against the metal.</p>
<p>First, using his right thumb and the three-pace clarity of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen pressed sequentially along three acupoint nodes two inches below his left elbow—squeezing two mouthfuls of dark, foaming, purple-tinted blood out of the four fang-holes until the blood emerging turned bright, clean crimson.</p>
<p>Then he nodded once to Old Tie Shan.</p>
<p>"Do it."</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan's jaw clenched. Bringing the glowing orange iron spatula down from the forge, he pressed it squarely across the four fang-punctures on Lu Chen's forearm!</p>
<p><em>HSSSSSSSSSS—!</em></p>
<p>A thick plume of white smoke and the smell of seared flesh filled the shed.</p>
<p>Uncle Shi, who had braced both hands just above Lu Chen's shoulders expecting the young man to buck off the anvil block, stared in disbelief. Lu Chen's forearm did not twitch by a tenth of an inch against the cold anvil. Only a low, controlled hum vibrated in the back of his throat as his eyes reflected the orange glow of the hot iron, his mind submerged completely in <em>Prism Stillness</em>.</p>
<p>Three seconds later, Old Tie Shan lifted the iron spatula away, revealing a clean, sealed scar across the forearm, and immediately packed the burn with cooling <strong>Willow-Ash Tallow</strong> and clean linen.</p>
<p>"You're not human, boy," Uncle Shi breathed, slowly lowering his hands and shaking his head. "Watchman Ge owes you his life tonight, and Widow Qin and little Xiao Ya owe you two more. By village law, two of those three hyena hides and the alpha's <strong>Gallbladder and Neck-Sinew</strong> belong to you."</p>
<p>"Give the second hide and half the meat to Watchman Ge's family so he has broth while his neck heals," Lu Chen said, flexing his freshly bandaged left fingers to confirm that the tendons beneath the burn were completely undamaged. "Keep the alpha's gallbladder for Granny Liu's medicine pot, and leave the alpha's hide and neck-sinews here at the smithy. Tomorrow is the fourteenth—we still have to haft all twenty-eight tribute mattocks with seasoned ash-wood before Sub-Bailiff <strong>Zhao Kuan</strong> rides in on the fifteenth."</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan looked down at the seared bandage on Lu Chen's left arm, then at the stack of twenty-eight iron mattock-heads by the wall, and had to turn back toward the hearth quickly so the two men wouldn't see the moisture in his single good eye.</p>
