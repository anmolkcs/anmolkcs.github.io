<p>Paying a two-copper gate-toll per sledge to the cold-eyed garrison sentries—who barely spared a glance for a group of sheepskin-clad village hunters hauling tax-iron—the Ash-Brook column entered the noisy, soot-grimed streets of <strong>Black-Rock Frontier Town</strong>.</p>
<p>Under the tenfold gravity of the plain, the flagstones of the main thoroughfare were rutted three inches deep by iron-wheeled ore-wagons pulled by teams of six Scale-Hoofed Crag-Mules. Gaunt mine-laborers wrapped in sacking hurried past fur-cloaked clan stewards and scarred mercenaries from the <strong>Iron-Mark Hunting Guild</strong>, while from the high taverns drifted the smell of spiced mule-meat and harsh frontier liquor.</p>
<p>After stabling their Crag-Mule and locking the two heavy iron-laden sledges inside the high-walled courtyard of <strong>Old Carter Meng's</strong> caravanserai in the southern shanties—leaving <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, <strong>Cross-Eyed Liu</strong>, and five men on armed rotation directly atop the iron bars—<strong>Lu Chen</strong>, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>, and fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> walked up the lantern-lit Stone-Mortar Street toward the <strong>Hundred-Herb Apothecary</strong>.</p>
<p>Before confronting the Bailiff Tally-House tomorrow morning, Lu Chen had two urgent errands at the apothecary: buying the medicine for little blind <strong>Xiao Ya's</strong> eyes, and procuring the mineral catalyst needed to cross from <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Drawing)</strong> into <strong>Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining)</strong>.</p>
<p>Inside the high-ceilinged apothecary hall, which smelled strongly of sulfur-root, dried centipede shells, and scorched tortoise plastron, Uncle Shi laid the <strong>Peak Tier Two Scarred King-Boar Gallbladder</strong>, the <strong>Early Tier Two Silver-Backed Alpha Wolf Gallbladder</strong>, and twelve prime wolf and boar pelts onto the counter.</p>
<p>The shopkeeper—a thin, sharp-nosed man wearing brass spectacles and possessing the tough hands of a <strong>Middle Tier Two</strong> herbalist—weighed the two intact, frost-preserved galls on his ivory balance, and his eyebrows shot up behind his lenses.</p>
<p>"A full-grown Peak Tier Two Iron-Bristle King-Boar gall, bled clean without a drop of bile-leak, and an Early Tier Two Alpha Wolf gall!" the shopkeeper exclaimed in surprise. "Together with these twelve prime winter pelts, that comes to <strong>twenty-six taels of standard Prefectural Silver</strong>!"</p>
<p>"We don't want all of it in silver, Shopkeeper," Lu Chen said, stepping up to the counter with his eighty-jin black iron spear wrapped in coarse canvas over his shoulder. "First, give us three stalks of three-year-old <strong>Three-Leaf Clear-Eye Frost-Grass</strong> and two ounces of <strong>Snow-Python Bile</strong> to dissolve <strong>Ash-Fever Cataracts</strong> in a seven-year-old child."</p>
<p>"Second," Lu Chen continued quietly, "give me twenty jin of <strong>Subterranean Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts</strong> and five catties of <strong>Red-Lead Bone-Washing Powder</strong>."</p>
<p>The shopkeeper paused with his hand on the abacus, looking up over his brass spectacles at Lu Chen's calm, youthful face—and then down at the way the solid oak floorboards beneath Lu Chen's rawhide boots creaked under the unmistakable, bow-taut physical density of a <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Drawing)</strong> warrior!</p>
<p>Immediately, the shopkeeper's haughty merchant demeanor turned respectful.</p>
<p>"Three stalks of Clear-Eye Frost-Grass and two ounces of Snow-Python Bile come to fourteen taels of silver—boiled with morning dew for seven days, it will peel Ash-Fever cataracts right off a child's eyes without leaving a scar," the shopkeeper said promptly, wrapping a jade-lined wooden box in oiled cloth and handing it to Uncle Shi. "And twenty jin of Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts plus five catties of Red-Lead Powder come to twelve taels—an exact trade for your twenty-six taels of galls and pelts! Though if this old herbalist may speak boldly, Young Hero: Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts are so fierce and cold-heavy that unless they are dissolved in a natural <strong>Boiling Subterranean Sulfur-Iron Spring</strong> under twelve- to fifteenfold gravity, soaking in them in an ordinary wooden tub only calcines the outer skin of the bones without reaching the marrow-wall!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen's dark eyes flickered almost imperceptibly as he tucked the heavy forty-pound sack of <strong>Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts</strong> under his arm.</p>
<p>A natural <strong>Boiling Subterranean Sulfur-Iron Spring</strong> under fifteenfold gravity—like the exact geothermal veins running through the bottom of <strong>Deep-Shaft Pit #7 in Black-Slag Canyon</strong>!</p>
