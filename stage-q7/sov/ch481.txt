<p>Inside the steaming, eighteenfold-gravity geode cavern at the back of Drift Four, Lu Chen stood silently before the dark bronze-iron skeleton chained to the basalt wall.</p>
<p>He did not know what crime or betrayal had sent discarded soldier <strong>Shi Potian</strong> from the Grey-Wall Prefectural Bailiff Command down into this three-hundred-fathom grave a century ago. Yet as a spearman who had also been cast down into the mud without a spiritual root to rely upon, Lu Chen felt a profound, wordless kinship with the unyielding man who had spent twelve years in the dark chiseling a path for future mortals with his bare finger-bones.</p>
<p>Setting his eighty-jin spear aside, Lu Chen placed both hands together and bowed three times to the upright iron skeleton.</p>
<p>"Senior Shi," Lu Chen said quietly, his voice echoing softly over the bubble of the sulfur-iron pool. "Your twenty-four postures will not stay buried in this pit. When I walk into <strong>Grey-Wall Prefectural City</strong> and tear down the Bailiff Command's vault, your name will see the sun again."</p>
<p>Stepping forward, Lu Chen gripped the two wrist-thick <strong>Origin-Iron Chains</strong> driven through the skeleton's collarbones. Even rusted for a hundred years, the chains had resisted every pickaxe of the debt-miners; yet as Lu Chen coiled all <strong>Thirty-Six Tier-Three Bowstring Sinews</strong> and pulled with four thousand four hundred jin of force, the rusted anchor-pins groaned and slid out of the basalt wall!</p>
<p>With the gentle care of a museum restorer handling an emperor's relics, Lu Chen gathered all <strong>206 dark-iron bones</strong> of Shi Potian, wrapped them in a clean canvas sheet from his pack, and buried them inside a dry stone cairn atop the highest basalt shelf overlooking the pool.</p>
<p>Then he stepped back out through the four-foot rock cleft into the dead-end cul-de-sac of Drift Four.</p>
<p>Waiting there in the dim green glow of the moss-lamp were twenty-two-year-old <strong>Tie Niu</strong>—standing upright on his iron-splinted left ankle—and sixty-year-old <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong>, along with three trusted crew-leaders representing the thirty-eight other starving debt-miners working the lower half of Drift Four.</p>
<p>Lu Chen opened his canvas pack and distributed ten jin of salted, high-energy <strong>Scarred King-Boar Meat</strong> and thirty roasted <strong>Iron-Husk Millet Cakes</strong> among the men—enough for every single one of the forty starving miners in Drift Four to put three solid mouthfuls of meat and grain into their bellies that night.</p>
<p>"Listen to me carefully," Lu Chen said in a low voice that carried only ten paces through the heavy, humid air. "Today is the night of the third of the twelfth month. <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> plans to keep you diving into the sulfur pool for <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> until the eighteenth night, and then detonate the thirty barrels of <strong>Black-Oil Fire-Powder</strong> at the tunnel mouth to bury all forty-one of us alive."</p>
<p>The three miner crew-leaders clenched their scarred fists, their sunken eyes burning with desperate hatred. "Young Hero Lu—tell us what to do! Even if we die rushing the wardens' iron picks barehanded, it's better than being crushed like rats in the dark!"</p>
<p>"Nobody is rushing iron picks barehanded, and nobody is dying in the dark," Lu Chen said calmly. "We have fourteen days before Luo Shan lights those barrels. Starting tonight, we follow three rules: First, during the day shifts, thirty men chip just enough ordinary black-vein ore to fill Warden He Da's daily cart quota at the landing, while harvesting only the small, impure outer marrow-crystals from the rim of the pool to hand over to Luo Shan every third night so he suspects nothing. Second, <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong> and four experienced tunnelers work in secret two-hour shifts behind the false shale wall to clear the collapsed rubble plugging the ancient <strong>Emergency Ventilation Chimney</strong> that leads up to the two-hundred-fathom staging ledge. And third—in that geode cavern behind us is a natural magma-vent and a boiling pool of <strong>Earth-Marrow Sulfur Brine</strong>. Over the next fourteen nights, every man in Drift Four will drink diluted marrow-broth to rebuild his strength, <strong>Tie Niu</strong> and I will melt down every broken pickaxe head to forge <strong>forty short trench-spears</strong>, and I will use that boiling brine pool under eighteenfold gravity to forge my own bones into <strong>Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining)</strong> so we can smash our way out of Black-Slag Canyon!"</p>
