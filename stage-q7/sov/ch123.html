Sixty miles offshore, a thick, milky-white Magnetic Brine Fog rolled across the surface of the Salt-Sea, reducing visibility to less than fifty yards and scrambling ordinary compasses.

Standing at the prow of the Iron-Scale Osprey with his nine-foot, 3,600-jin Star-Shattering Heavy Spear planted beside his boot, Lu Chen closed his eyes and extended his Rank 4 Inscription Grandmaster Soul Perception joined with Prism Stillness three miles through the brine fog.

"Two miles ahead at ten o'clock and two o'clock," Lu Chen said calmly without opening his eyes. "Two eighty-foot Bone-Hulled Raider Ships hiding behind the submerged salt-reefs, waiting to catch us in a crossfire."

Almost the instant Lu Chen's voice fell—

BOOM—WHOOSH! BOOM—WHOOSH!

From both sides of the milky brine fog, two eerie, eighty-foot warships constructed entirely from the bleached white rib-cages and skulls of giant sea-beasts lunged out of the mist!

Standing on the bone-prows of the two warships were two Middle Xiantian pirate captains of the Nether-Bone Armada (Black-Fin Gui and White-Skull Sha), accompanied by forty Houtian bone-corsairs!

"A lone Brine-Iron merchant ship dares sneak toward the Seven-Spire Islands?!" Black-Fin Gui laughed savagely as the jawbones of both Bone-Shipswinched open, revealing four glowing green Rank 3 Peak Corrosive Bone-Fire Cannons! "Fire the Bone-Melting Salvo—sink the ship and hook their Xiantian cores from the brine!"

KABOOM—KABOOM—KABOOM—KABOOM!

Four basketball-sized spheres of screaming, emerald-green Corrosive Bone-Fire—each carrying 140,000 jin of explosive acid-fire force capable of melting three inches of Brine-Iron into slag—howled across the water and slammed squarely into the port and starboard hulls of the Iron-Scale Osprey!

Captain Cang Hai instinctively braced the helm—

DONG—ZZZZZT!

The moment the four green Bone-Fire spheres struck the Iron-Scale Osprey, the 3,600 Rank 4 Brine-Cleaving Dragon Scale Runes along the hull flashed with blinding purple-gold starlight!

Not only did the corrosive green fire fail to melt a single millimeter of the iron hull, but the Acoustic Counter-Shock Layer absorbed 80% of the kinetic impact and converted it into a roaring, ring-shaped Sonic Tsunami Wave that blasted outward in both directions!

SPLASH—CRACK!

The rebounding sonic tsunami slammed into the two eighty-foot Bone-Ships, snapping six of their rib-timbers and knocking half the Houtian corsairs flat on the decks!

"Now—board them!"

Before the stunned Black-Fin Gui and White-Skull Sha could recover their balance, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, and Su Wan'er soared off the starboard rail toward White-Skull Sha's ship—while on the port side, Lu Chen took a single step off the prow of the Iron-Scale Osprey!
