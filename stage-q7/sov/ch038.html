On the tenth morning since Lu Chen had departed for the Crimson Iron Mountains, the atmosphere around the Killing Array Square of the Seven Star Martial House was buzzing with rumors.

“Today is the tenth and final day of Lu Chen’s mountain hunting leave, and he still hasn’t checked in at the Deacon Hall!” Zhang Cang laughed loudly among a crowd of outer and inner disciples gathered below the Killing Array Ranking Stele. “I heard three hunters came back from Iron-Pass Outpost yesterday saying a pack of Second-Order and Third-Order beasts went berserk near Thunder-Scar Gorge six days ago. A Peak Fourth Stage freshman who goes into the inner Crimson Iron Mountains alone to hunt materials? He’s probably been turned into beast dung already!”

Up on the stone steps of the Inner Abode pavilion, Lin Sen (Ranked #11 on the Killing Array Stele, Peak Fifth Stage: Bone Forging) stood with his arms folded, a faint, cold smile playing at the corner of his lips as he glanced toward the mountain gate path.

Six days ago, his messenger hawk had delivered the order to Ghost-Eye Gui Yan and four Fifth-Stage Black-Skull mercenaries. Even though Gui Yan hadn’t sent a return hawk yet—likely laying low after splitting the spoils—Lin Sen didn’t believe for a second that a fifteen-year-old boy at the Fourth Stage could survive a Half-Step Sixth Stage Soul-Curse master and four Fifth-Stage veterans in the wilderness.

Only Su Wan’er, standing near the pavilion balustrade with a worried crease between her brows, kept looking toward the main stone stairway of the Martial House.

Just as the midday bronze bell began to toll its first strike—

CLANK… CLANK… CLANK…

Steady, heavy footsteps echoed up the white-jade stairway leading into the Killing Array Square.

The conversations among the three hundred disciples in the square died away instantly as a fifteen-year-old youth in a travel-worn black robe stepped onto the plaza, carrying an eight-foot-six-inch oilcloth bundle across his back that radiated a faint, suffocating pressure like a sleeping volcano.

“Lu Chen?! He’s alive!”

“Wait—look at his aura! Why do I feel like my chest is being pressed by an iron boulder just standing twenty paces away from him?!”

Up on the Inner Abode steps, Lin Sen’s smile froze solid on his face, his pupils constricting sharply as he stared at the unharmed Lu Chen walking across the square. Gui Yan and four Fifth-Stage mercenaries failed?! Impossible—unless he never entered the inner mountains!

However, Lu Chen didn’t walk toward the Deacon Hall first.

Instead, under the gaze of over three hundred disciples, he walked straight across the square and stopped twenty paces below the stone steps where Lin Sen was standing.

Reaching into his robe, Lu Chen pulled out a blood-stained slip of hawk-parchment and a tarnished bronze skull badge—the personal emblem of Ghost-Eye Gui Yan—and tossed both items onto the flagstones at the base of Lin Sen’s steps!

Clatter.

“Senior Brother Lin Sen,” Lu Chen’s calm, resonant voice carried clearly across the entire Killing Array Square. “Six days ago in Thunder-Scar Gorge, Ghost-Eye Gui Yan and four Black-Skull mercenaries dropped this letter signed with your seal before they died under my spear. Since you went to such trouble to send five Fifth-Stage testers to check my progress, it would be rude of me not to thank you in person.”

A dead, suffocating silence dropped over the square!

Every inner disciple who recognized the bronze skull badge of Ghost-Eye Gui Yan sucked in a sharp breath of cold air. Ghost-Eye Gui Yan was a notorious Half-Step Sixth Stage killer accompanied by four Fifth-Stage (Bone Forging) mercenaries—and Lu Chen had just tossed Gui Yan’s blood-stained badge onto the ground, meaning he had slaughtered all five of them alone in the Crimson Iron Mountains!

Lin Sen’s face turned as dark as the bottom of a cauldron. Before he could speak to deny the unsigned hawk-seal, Lu Chen raised his hand and pointed his index finger straight at Lin Sen’s chest:

“According to the rules of the Seven Star Martial House, once a disciple enters the Top 20 of the Killing Array Stele, they may issue an Unrestricted Life-and-Death Arena Challenge to any inner disciple within ten ranks. Wait right there for forty-five minutes, Lin Sen—after I step out of the Ten Thousand Killing Array, I’m taking your #11 spot and settling Thunder-Scar Gorge’s debt on the Central Martial Stage!”
