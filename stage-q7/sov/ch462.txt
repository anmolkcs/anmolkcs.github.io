<p><em>BOOOOOOOOOM—CRASH!</em></p>
<p>Multiplied by tenfold Desolate Gravity across a thirty-foot vertical drop, the sixty-pound granite parapet block struck the center of the outlaws' roaring log fire with the kinetic force of a catapult stone—exploding the burning logs, boiling mutton cauldrons, and red-hot embers in a twenty-foot fireball across the faces of the twelve gambling bandits!</p>
<p>"AAAHHH! THE ROOF CAVED IN—!" "ENEMY ATTACK!"</p>
<p>In the exact heartbeat that the exploding fire blinded the courtyard below, Lu Chen stepped off the thirty-foot wall directly above the six-foot ice-barricade where <strong>Middle Tier Two Lieutenant Iron-Back Gou</strong> was spinning around in shock!</p>
<p>Dropping thirty feet through the howling snow with his eighty-jin solid iron spear pointed straight down between both hands—every muscle compacted to <strong>Complete Tier Two</strong> and thirty-three bowstring sinews coiled taut—Lu Chen became a falling bolt of black lightning:</p>
<p><strong>Sky-Splitting Plunge Thrust!</strong></p>
<p>Down behind the barricade, Iron-Back Gou barely managed to raise his fifty-pound iron-reinforced oak shield overhead to block the falling shadow!</p>
<p><em>KRAK—SHHHHHHHK—BOOM!</em></p>
<p>The fourteen-inch four-edged diamond point of the eighty-jin spear punched straight through two inches of iron-shod oak shield, sheared through Iron-Back Gou's <strong>Middle Tier Two</strong> forearm bone and iron brigandine, and exited through the bandit lieutenant's spine—pinning him flat into the frozen mud road as the shockwave cracked the ice-crust for three yards around Lu Chen's boots!</p>
<p>At the sound of that thunderous impact, sixty yards up the northern curve of the S-bend, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> blew his bone whistle!</p>
<p>"Push the sledges! Forward—MARCH!"</p>
<p>Out of the blinding whiteout rounded the twelve men of Ash-Brook and Mud-Well!</p>
<p>Pushed across the glare-ice by <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, <strong>Cross-Eyed Liu</strong>, and the four strongest hunters, the <strong>Two Iron-Clad Tribute Sledges</strong>—weighted with eight hundred jin of Cold-Slag Iron bars—slid into the fifty-foot throat of the pass, swung thirty degrees sideways, and locked together with two heavy iron drop-pins!</p>
<p><em>CLACK—THUD!</em></p>
<p>Down dropped the chest-high, iron-bolted oak side-boards, transforming the two sledges in five breaths into a solid, twelve-foot-wide <strong>Armored Sledge-Bastion</strong> sealing the northern road!</p>
<p>Through the four square loopholes in the iron-clad oak boards poked the heavy iron prods of all four <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong> manned by <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Old Ma</strong>, <strong>Hunter Feng</strong>, and young <strong>Shi Tou</strong>!</p>
<p><em>TWANG! TWANG! TWANG! TWANG!</em></p>
<p>Four armor-piercing bodkin bolts screamed across the thirty-yard gap into the fire-lit courtyard of the watch-fort, punching straight through the chests of four panicked bandits who were still wiping burning mutton-grease from their eyes!</p>
<p>Meanwhile, right behind the enemy's own six-foot ice-barricade, Lu Chen wrenched his eighty-jin spear out of the dead Iron-Back Gou, planted his shoulder against the central keystone boulder of the bandits' barricade, and heaved with all <strong>three thousand four hundred jin</strong> of his muscle-and-sinew power!</p>
<p><em>RUMBLE—CRASH!</em></p>
<p>Half of the six-foot ice-and-boulder barricade collapsed inward into the courtyard, opening a clear line of fire for the second volley from Ash-Brook's sledge-fortress while Lu Chen waded into the remaining eight courtyard bandits like a tiger entering a flock of sheep!</p>
<p><em>CLANG! WHACK! SHHHHK!</em></p>
<p>One horizontal sweep of the eighty-jin solid iron shaft shattered two notched sabers and caved in a Tier-One bandit's ribs; a reverse snap of the butt-spike crushed the kneecap of a second; and a lightning-fast <strong>Level Thrust</strong> impaled a third through the throat before he could even reach the alarm horn!</p>
