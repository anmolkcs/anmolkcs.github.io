<p>For the next five days—from the sixth to the tenth of the eleventh month—the double hammers of Ash-Brook's smithy rang from dawn until midnight.</p>
<p>Because Lu Chen had broken into <strong>Early Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong> and possessed over two thousand one hundred jin of tireless strength, he no longer needed eighteen blows to draw out a billet of <strong>Black-Marrow Core Ore</strong>. Swinging a newly cast <strong>sixty-pound heavy iron sledge</strong> with both hands while his left boot worked the foot-treadle vise and Old Tie Shan called the heats from his stool, Lu Chen could fold and compact a thirty-pound bloom into a ten-jin bar of <strong>Grade-One Refined Cold-Slag Iron</strong> in just twelve thunderous blows!</p>
<p>By the evening of the tenth day of the eleventh month, <strong>sixty-two ten-jin bars</strong> (620 jin) were stacked neatly on the slate rack—leaving only <strong>eighteen bars (180 jin)</strong> to reach the eight-hundred-jin quota needed to save <strong>Tie Niu</strong> from Pit #7!</p>
<p>Yet just as the sixty-second bar was quenched in the brine trough, <strong>Shi Tou</strong> crawled out of the stone charcoal vault behind the smithy with his face black with soot and his hands empty.</p>
<p>"Uncle Tie, Big Brother Lu," Shi Tou reported anxiously, brushing coal-dust from his eyelashes. "I just scraped the bottom corners of the vault. There's only half a basket of <strong>Bitter-Pine Charcoal</strong> left—barely enough for two more heats! And none of the ordinary birch or willow wood around the village burns hot enough to melt Black-Marrow Core Ore!"</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan cursed softly, looking out at the snow-buried ridges. "Smelting sixty bars of nine-folded Cold-Slag Iron ate through our entire winter charcoal reserve twice as fast as normal. To smelt the last eighteen bars of tribute iron—plus enough extra iron to shoe the two heavy tribute sledges and forge thirty-six iron spearheads for our village militia—we need at least <strong>three thousand jin of high-heat Bitter-Pine Charcoal</strong>."</p>
<p>"Where is the nearest stand of mature Bitter-Pine?" Lu Chen asked, wiping the sweat from his chest with a dry rag.</p>
<p>"Three miles west along the foot of the ridge, in <strong>Bitter-Pine Ravine</strong>," Uncle Shi said, stepping into the shed with his sixty-pound horn-bow over his shoulder. "The village has seven permanent clay-and-stone charcoal kilns built into the sheltered bank of that ravine. Usually we burn our winter charcoal there in the ninth month before the snow falls. Going there now in the eleventh month means a six-man crew has to camp in the ravine for eight days and nights tending the kiln vents so the domes don't crack in the frost—and Bitter-Pine Ravine sits only five miles east of <strong>Dead-Crow Gorge</strong>, where <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> main outlaw camp is hidden."</p>
<p>"We have no choice," Old Tie Shan said grimly. "Without those three thousand jin of Bitter-Pine Charcoal, the forge dies tomorrow, and we show up at the Black-Slag Canyon Tally-House eighteen bars short."</p>
<p>Lu Chen picked up his eighty-jin solid black-iron boar-spear and tested its balance across his palm.</p>
<p>Since breaking into <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> at Split-Stone Defile six days ago, he had eaten five pounds of boiled <strong>King-Boar Loin</strong> every day and swung the sixty-pound sledge over four thousand times—pushing his muscle compaction steadily toward <strong>Middle Tier Two</strong>. What he needed now to drive the compaction into the deepest postural muscles along his spine and intercostals was sustained, heavy woodsman's labor and a real battle against a worthy opponent.</p>
<p>"We leave for Bitter-Pine Ravine at first light tomorrow," Lu Chen decided calmly. "Uncle Shi, Big-Tall Zhao, Cross-Eyed Liu, Shi Tou, and I will take the felling axes and two crossbows to fire the seven kilns. Old Ma and the militia stay here behind the barred palisade with the other two crossbows to guard Old Tie, the women, and the sixty-two iron bars. In eight days, we bring back three thousand jin of black-gold charcoal."</p>
