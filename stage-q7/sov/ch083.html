Inspecting one of the concealed flags under Prism Stillness, Lu Chen’s expression turned grave:

“This is a Rank 3 Peak Spirit-Vein Eroding Array planted by the Shadow-Moon Sect! They aren’t just here to hunt treasures—their #1 Houtian prodigy Mo Wuji is using the Secret Realm’s subterranean node to poison our Seven Star Valley Sect’s core spirit vein, while sending a branch of Shadow-Moon infiltrators south toward our Azure Sky Kingdom to create chaos and distract our Xiantian Elders!”
