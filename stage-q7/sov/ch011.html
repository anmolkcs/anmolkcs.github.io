Seven days north of Cloudstride City, the rugged hills flattened into the vast, fertile plains of the Central Prefecture.

Even from ten miles away, the walls of Azure Sky Royal Capital rose against the horizon like an unbroken mountain range of dark ironstone—one hundred andtwenty feet tall, wide enough along the battlements for four war chariots to gallop abreast, and patrolled day and night by armored soldiers mounted on First-Order Wind-Steed Beasts.

Lu Chen walked along the shaded willow road beside the outer moat, his rough oilcloth bundle and the eight-foot-long 820-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Spear strapped across his back.

Over the seven-day journey of walking forty miles each day while carrying 820 jin of dead weight on his spine, Lu Chen had never once paused his circulation of the 《Chaotic Star Body Art — Silk-Unwinding Star Breath》. Every step he took pressed a faint half-inch footprint into the compacted dirt of the imperial highway, and his Early Third Stage (Viscera Training) foundation had solidified until his internal organs hummed with tireless vitality.

Inside the city gates, the wealth and martial atmosphere made Cloudstride City look like a barren border village. Martial artists at the Third and Fourth Stages of Body Transformation walked openly on every street, and the towering pavilions lining Vermilion Bird Avenue gleamed with glazed tiles and spirit-array banners.

With two days remaining before the Seven Star Martial House Entrance Trial opened on the fifteenth of the month, Lu Chen decided to convert the five Rank 1 Flame-Edge and Heavy-Mountain Inscription Talismans he had drawn during his roadside nights into True Essence Stones at the Royal Capital’s headquarters of the Sky Treasure Pavilion.

The Royal Capital branch of the Sky Treasure Pavilion stood seven stories tall—three stories higher than the branch in Cloudstride City.

When Lu Chen presented the bronze guest token given to him by Appraiser Qin, a white-robed steward immediately led him to a quiet tea room on the third floor, where an elegant, silver-haired elder in violet brocade (Grand Appraiser Mu) inspected the five talismans.

“Thirty-eight percent power amplification on this Heavy-Mountain Earth Rune… and zero soul-force stagnation at the one-hundred-and-eighth sealing node!” Grand Appraiser Mu’s eyes shone with unconcealed shock as he set down his jade loupe. “Appraiser Qin sent a winged-falcon letter three days ago mentioning a mysterious master in Cloudstride City, yet seeing these five talismans in person still surpasses this old man’s expectations! Even our Pavilion’s resident Rank 2 Inscription Master rarely achieves such crisp energy containment on a Rank 1 carrier sheet.”

Just as Grand Appraiser Mu was about to quote a price, a soft, melodious voice floated through the carved lattice screen separating the third-floor VIP gallery from the adjoining Celestial Zither Courtyard:

“Uncle Mu, is that the Flame-Edge Talisman mentioned in Appraiser Qin’s letter? May I take a look?”

The lattice screen slid open, and a sixteen-year-old girl in a flowing pale-cyan dress stepped into the room, accompanied by an old maidservant whose aura hovered at the terrifying Peak of the Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation)!

The girl’s skin was as clear as羊脂 (mutton-fat) white jade, her brows curved like distant misty peaks, and around her slender waist hung the purple-gold core disciple badge of the Seven Star Martial House. Even at sixteen, she carried the poised, aristocratic grace of someone born above the clouds.

She was Su Wan’er—the grand-niece of the Azure Sky Kingdom’s Grand Marshal, a peerless prodigy born with Grade 6 Lower-Tier Talent, and the youngest person in the Royal Capital to become both a Core Disciple of the Seven Star Martial House and an official Rank 1 Inscription Apprentice!

When Su Wan’er’s clear, star-bright eyes fell upon Lu Chen—taking in his plain black cloth robe, his calloused hands, and the massive oilcloth-wrapped spear behind his shoulder—she paused slightly, expecting to see a grey-bearded scholar, yet she maintained impeccable courtesy and offered a slight bow.

“Su Wan’er greets Senior’s envoy.”

She picked up one of the Heavy-Mountain Inscription Talismans with two slender fingers, her eyebrows lifting in genuine admiration as she sensed the microscopic spiral structure of the runes.

“Whoever drew this rune did not rely on an expensive spirit brush or high-grade vermilion ink,” Su Wan’er murmured softly, her voice filled with reverence for the craft. “Look at the forty-ninth turning node, Uncle Mu—the master used a harmonic spiral flick of soul force to braid three unstable earth-element lines into a self-reinforcing triangle. Even my teacher at the Martial House says such brush control requires ten thousand hours of uninterrupted meditation.”

Hearing Su Wan’er accurately identify the exact spiral node he had deduced under Prism Stillness, Lu Chen gave her an inward nod of respect. Unlike pampered young masters who coasted on pills, this girl genuinely understood the fundamentals of Inscription.

“Miss Su has sharp eyes,” Lu Chen said calmly. “My teacher asked me to trade these five talismans for six Low-Grade True Essence Stones and three stalks of Three-Leaf Jade Marrow Grass.”

“Six True Essence Stones and three stalks of Jade Marrow Grass is actually below their true market value,” Su Wan’er said without hesitation, turning to Grand Appraiser Mu. “Uncle Mu, please charge two of these Heavy-Mountain Talismans to my personal account so I may study their stroke structure, and fulfill this young master’s request with seven Low-Grade True Essence Stones plus the three stalks of Three-Leaf Jade Marrow Grass.”

She then took a small white-jade token engraved with a crescent zither from her sash and handed it to Lu Chen.

“Young Master, I see the bronze corner of a Seven Star Martial House Recommendation Token in your pouch, so you must be here for the entrance trial in two days,” Su Wan’er said with a gentle smile. “If you or your teacher ever wish to exchange pointers on Inscription runes—or need rare beast-blood inks from the royal treasury—you may send word to the Listening Rain Pavilion inside the Martial House with this token.”
