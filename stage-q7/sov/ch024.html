News that the #1 Freshman Lu Chen had defeated #86 Ranked Inner Disciple Zhu Hong—a Peak Fourth Stage veteran—in a single spear exchange swept through the entire Seven Star Martial House before noon.

Previously, many second-year inner disciples had assumed Lu Chen’s #118 score in the Ten Thousand Killing Array relied on kite-and-thrust tactics against unthinking array phantoms. Now, after seeing Zhu Hong carried to the infirmary with two shattered forearms and a cracked Grade 2 broadsaber, no outer or lower-ranked inner disciple dared cast greedy eyes on Courtyard #72 again!

More importantly, with the twenty Low-Grade True Essence Stones won from Zhu Hong, Lu Chen decided it was time to convert his Pill Inscription skill into a steady, high-margin stream of cultivation resources—without exposing his identity to the public.

That afternoon, carrying a small wooden box containing two Rune-Inscribed Origin-Gathering Pills (each bearing the 92%-purity Essence-Locking Pill Rune) and wearing Su Wan’er’s crescent-zither jade token at his waist, Lu Chen walked up the bamboo-lined path of the Inner Abode toward the Listening Rain Pavilion.

Nestled beside a tranquil mountain lake on the central peak of the Martial House, the Listening Rain Pavilion was Su Wan’er’s private residence and inscription study.

When the Sixth-Stage old maidservant, Granny Liu, saw Lu Chen at the pavilion gate, she led him immediately into a sunlit courtyard fragrant with spirit orchids, where Su Wan’er was sitting before a white-jade table, practicing a Rank 1 Peak Wind-Blade Inscription on a sheet of talisman paper.

At the eighty-sixth stroke, Su Wan’er’s brush tip hesitated for a fraction of a second at the wind-convergence node, and with a soft poof, the talisman paper turned to grey ash.

“Still two percent too slow on the eighty-sixth transition,” Su Wan’er sighed softly, setting down her jade brush before looking up with a warm, surprised smile as Lu Chen stepped into the pavilion. “Junior Brother Lu! Ever since you took first place in the Entrance Trial and broke into #118 on the Killing Array Stele, the entire Martial House has been talking about you—and just an hour ago, I heard you sent Zhu Hong to the apothecary hall in a single spear stroke!”

“He broke my courtyard gate and offered twenty True Essence Stones to fix it; I couldn’t refuse his generosity, Senior Sister Su,” Lu Chen replied with a faint smile. Glancing at the pile of half-burnt Wind-Blade talisman sheets on her table, he added casually, “On the eighty-sixth stroke of the Wind-Blade Rune, if you shift the Silver-Leaf Amber ratio down by half a mace and use a three-beat counterclockwise wrist release before entering the convergence loop, the wind-elemental friction drops by forty percent.”

Su Wan’er froze.

For the past ten days, even her teacher—Master Yan, the lone official Rank 2 Inscription Master of the Azure Sky Royal Capital—had told her that the eighty-sixth stroke could only be forced through by raising her soul force another tier. Yet Lu Chen had taken a single glance at the ash pattern on her table and pointed out an exact material-and-brush adjustment!

Picking up a fresh sheet of talisman paper, Su Wan’er immediately adjusted her ink dish by half a mace and tried the three-beat counterclockwise release on the eighty-sixth stroke.

Hum—!

The azure wind-lines glided through the eighty-sixth node without a trace of friction, flowing all the way to the final one-hundred-and-twentieth stroke and condensing into a flawless Rank 1 Peak Wind-Blade Inscription!

Su Wan’er stared at the glowing azure rune on her desk, then slowly raised her head to look at the fifteen-year-old youth standing in front of her, her cherry lips parting in shock.

“There was never any ‘traveling senior master’ behind you at the Sky Treasure Pavilion…” Su Wan’er whispered, her star-bright eyes widening in disbelief. “Those 38%-amplification Heavy-Mountain Runes—and that harmonic spiral node—were all drawn by you?!”

Instead of denying it, Lu Chen placed the small wooden box on her white-jade table and slid the lid open, revealing the two emerald-green Origin-Gathering Pills wrapped in shimmering silver cloud-runes.

“A Pill Inscription?!”

This time, even Granny Liu standing behind Su Wan’er gasped out loud!

In the entire Azure Sky Kingdom, Master Yan could barely draw Rank 2 Weapon Inscriptions—not a single person in the kingdom knew the lost art of Pill Inscription, because pill runes required microscopic three-dimensional soul control that mortal inscription masters simply did not possess!

“Ninety-two percent medicinal retention, and over ninety percent of the pill toxins purged…” Su Wan’er’s fingers trembled slightly as she sensed the pure, sealed vitality inside the two Origin-Gathering Pills. “Junior Brother Lu, a single one of these inscribed Origin-Gathering Pills is worth at least twelve Low-Grade True Essence Stones to any core clan elder trying to break through a bottleneck without meridian damage!”

“Senior Sister Su, I need a reliable partner to supply raw pills and high-order spirit beast blood essence while keeping my name completely confidential,” Lu Chen said directly. “For every three raw Rank 1 or Rank 2 pills you provide, I will return two Rune-Inscribed Pills to you—or trade them at a discount for Fourth and Fifth Stage Body-Tempering Herbs and Peak Second-Order / Third-Order Spirit Beast Heart-Blood from your Grand Marshal’s treasury.”

Su Wan’er’s eyes lit up immediately. For a genius aiming to break into the Xiantian Realm before age twenty, having an exclusive supply of toxin-free, rune-inscribed pills was worth more than ten gold mines!

“Deal!” Su Wan’er said solemnly, raising three fingers. “I swear on my martial heart that not a single soul outside this courtyard will learn of your Pill Inscription craft. Wait right here—two days ago, my grandfather’s northern army hunted a Peak Second-Order Iron-Scale Earth Drake and sent a jar of its Bone-Tempering Marrow Blood and six Crimson-Gold Tendon Pills to my residence. For these two inscribed Origin-Gathering Pills, take two of the Crimson-Gold Tendon Pills and a third of the Earth Drake Marrow Blood right now!”
