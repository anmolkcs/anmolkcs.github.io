“Rank #9—2,040 Killing Points?!”

The three hundred disciples in the Killing Array Square—and even the elders standing on the pavilion roofs—stared up at the single-digit rank gleaming in purple-gold light at the top of the monument as if they had seen a ghost in broad daylight!

In the entire Seven Star Martial House, the Top 10 of the Killing Array Ranking Stele was the exclusive domain of Core Disciples who had reached the Peak of the Fifth Stage or the Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation)—such as #3 Su Wan’er, #2 Ouyang Ming, and #1 Murong Zi!

Not only had fifteen-year-old Lu Chen jumped straight from #56 into the Top 20—he had vaulted clean past Lin Sen (#11, 1,850 points) to seize Rank #9, becoming an official Top 10 Core Disciple on the spot!

On the red-light platform, the array beam faded, revealing Lu Chen standing tall and unhurried.

Despite fighting for fifty minutes inside the array and slaughtering a Half-Step Sixth Stage Phantom General, his breathing was deep and steady, not a single hair at his temples was out of place, and the eight-foot-six-inch 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear in his right hand still crackled with faint arcs of silver-violet lightning!

Why was his stamina still over ninety percent full? Because with Golden-Jade Bone Marrow manufacturing oxygen-rich blood continuously and the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath recycling eighty percent of his kinetic force on every swing, fifty minutes of combat was barely a warm-up!

Lu Chen stepped off Platform #1, the butt of his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear striking the white-jade flagstones with a deep THUD that made every disciple along the path instinctively scramble three steps back.

He raised his dark, ice-calm eyes toward the Inner Abode pavilion steps, where Lin Sen now stood at Rank #12 (pushed down one spot by Lu Chen’s rise to #9), his face pale beneath its dark flush.

“Rank #9 challenges Rank #12,” Lu Chen’s voice rang out like cold iron across the silent square. “Lin Sen, forty-five minutes are up. Step onto the Central Martial Stage—or kneel right here in front of the Killing Array Stele and confess to hiring the Black-Skull Mercenaries before the Law Enforcement Elders!”
