<p>On the first day of the eleventh month, the <strong>Black-Frost</strong> arrived on schedule.</p>
<p>Unlike ordinary white winter hoarfrost, the Black-Frost of the Great Desolate borderlands was formed when high-altitude currents of dense <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong> condensed with freezing moisture and settled over the mountains at night. By dawn, every slate roof, palisade log, and barren tree branch in Ash-Brook Village was coated in a quarter-inch crust of greasy, soot-gray ice that smelled faintly of sulfur and iron rust.</p>
<p>Any wild greens, late turnips, or unpicked berries left outside under the Black-Frost withered and turned black within an hour, becoming poisonous to mortal stomachs.</p>
<p>That afternoon, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> convened a meeting of the village heads of households inside the communal grain shed. Even with his right arm still bound in splints across his chest, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> attended alongside Lu Chen, <strong>Granny Liu</strong>, and <strong>Widow Qin</strong>.</p>
<p>In the center of the dirt floor stood the village's six large earthenware communal reserve jars. Uncle Shi lifted the wooden lids off all six jars one by one.</p>
<p>Four of the six jars were completely empty, their bottoms scraped clean down to the glazed clay. The fifth jar held less than a foot of coarse <strong>Iron-Husk Millet</strong>, and the sixth contained only dried <strong>Bitter-Oak Acorn Meal</strong> mixed with chaff.</p>
<p>A heavy, suffocating silence fell over the thirty-odd gaunt men and women gathered in the shed.</p>
<p>"After paying Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan's autumn tax and feeding the boys hauling ore from Frost-Tooth Gully for the past fifteen days," Uncle Shi said heavily, resting his three-fingered left hand on the rim of the fifth jar, "every household in Ash-Brook is down to less than eight days of millet. Even if we stretch the porridge with boiled elm-bark and acorn chaff, the children and the old folks like Watchman Ge won't make it past the twentieth of this month without meat and marrow-fat."</p>
<p>"What about the outer snare-lines along White-Salt Creek?" asked a hollow-cheeked villager named <strong>Old Ma</strong>, a forty-five-year-old veteran hunter whose left ear had been frostbitten off years ago.</p>
<p>"Frozen barren," Uncle Shi replied grimly. "Once the Black-Frost drops, even the ridge-hares and rock-badgers burrow six feet under the shale or retreat into the geothermal hollows of the <strong>Inner Black-Briar Woods</strong>. If Ash-Brook wants meat to survive the eleventh month—and if we want to take enough <strong>Tier-One and Tier-Two Beast Pelts and Gallbladders</strong> to the trading post to help Old Tie and Lu Chen clear Tie Niu's Pit #7 bond—we have to take a six-man hunting party five miles past the <strong>Dead-Pine Boundary</strong> into the Inner Woods tomorrow morning."</p>
<p>A murmur of fear rippled through the shed.</p>
<p>"Five miles past the Dead-Pine Boundary?" Old Ma swallowed hard. "Uncle Shi, that's the territory of the <strong>Gray-Scab Wolf Packs</strong> and the mature <strong>Iron-Bristle Boars</strong>! A full-grown boar in the inner woods has hide thick as a <strong>Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong> warrior! And last winter, two hunters from <strong>Mud-Well Village</strong> ran into <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> poacher gang near the inner sulfur-bogs and came back stripped naked with their throats slit!"</p>
<p>All eyes in the grain shed turned instinctively toward the quiet young man standing beside Old Tie Shan.</p>
<p>In his right hand, Lu Chen held the seven-foot-two-inch, eighty-jin black-iron boar-spear upright against the dirt floor. Under the smithy's round-the-clock schedule over the past sixteen days, thirty-eight of the eighty required bars of <strong>Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron</strong> were already stacked on the rack—nearly half the quota completed in just over two weeks.</p>
<p>"Old Tie and the boys can keep the charcoal kilns and the smelting furnace running for five days while I'm in the woods," Lu Chen said calmly, his voice cutting through the fear in the shed. "Tomorrow at dawn, my spear goes with Uncle Shi's party into the Inner Black-Briar Woods."</p>
