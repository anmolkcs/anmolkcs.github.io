According to the ironclad founding rules of the Seven Star Martial House, the top three finishers of the Entrance Trial—regardless of birth or meridian talent grade—were automatically awarded: 1. Direct entry into the Main Mountain Abode (skipping the menial outer-foot barracks where Zhang Cang and the lower sixty disciples were sent). 2. A one-time reward of ten Low-Grade True Essence Stones and one bottle of Origin-Gathering丹 (Pills). 3. Three Free Entry Tokens to the Martial House’s two supreme cultivation sacred grounds: the Martial Skill Library Pavilion and the Ten Thousand Killing Array!

As Presiding Elder Sun personally handed the jade identity plaque and the pouch of ten True Essence Stones to Lu Chen on the central dais, Lin Xuan stood three paces to the right, his handsome face as cold as frost as he accepted the second-place reward.

“Lu Chen,” Lin Xuan transmitted his voice quietly as they descended the jade stairs side by side, his eyes filled with aristocratic chill. “Enjoy holding that first-place plaque for a month. Inside the Seven Star Martial House, rankings aren’t decided once a year—they are decided every month inside the Ten Thousand Killing Array. With borderline Grade 3 meridians, the gap between us will become a chasm the moment I step into the Fourth Stage: Altering Muscle & Tendons next week. Wait until the Freshman Ranking Battle in thirty days.”

“I’ll be waiting,” Lu Chen replied without even turning his head.

Half an hour later, guided by a respectful steward deacon, Lu Chen arrived at his assigned residence on the eastern slope of Seven Star Mountain: Courtyard #72 of the Pine-Wind Cliff.

Unlike the crowded four-man dormitories of the ordinary freshmen, the top three finishers each received an independent stone courtyard complete with a quiet meditation chamber, an inscription/alchemy workbench room, and a private courtyard paved with ironstone slabs for weapon practice.

After bolting the courtyard gate, Lu Chen sat cross-legged in the meditation room and poured out his current wealth onto the stone table: - Seventeen Low-Grade True Essence Stones (seven from Su Wan’er’s trade at the Sky Treasure Pavilion, plus ten from winning First Place in the Entrance Trial). - One porcelain bottle containing three Rank 1 Origin-Gathering Pills. - His Three Free Entry Tokens to the Ten Thousand Killing Array and the Martial Skill Library Pavilion.

“Seventeen True Essence Stones sounds like a fortune to an ordinary Third Stage disciple,” Lu Chen mused, tapping his knee as he reviewed the requirements for the next two stages of the 《Chaotic Star Body Art》. “However, cultivating the third and fourth layers of the Chaotic Star Body Art—Viscera Perfection and Dragon-Sinew Tendon Forging—requires at least fifty True Essence Stones worth of Second-Order Spirit Beast Blood Essence and Crimson-Gold Tendon Grass over the next month alone!”

Even more importantly, inside his spiritual sea, the second facet of the Chrono-Soul Prism was already 30% unlocked after absorbing the residual blood essence from the Scarlet Serpent Blood Paste. If he could obtain one more drop of fresh, high-purity Second-Order Peak Spirit Beast Heart-Blood, he would be able to unlock the second outer soul fragment inside the Prism!

“Tomorrow morning, I’ll visit the Martial Skill Library Pavilion to find a heavy spear technique that matches my 820-jin Black Iron Spear, and then test the waters of the legendary Ten Thousand Killing Array,” Lu Chen decided.

For the rest of the night, he didn’t sleep. Instead, he pulled out his Iron-Whiskered Weasel brush and spent six hours in Prism Stillness, refining a brand-new diagram from the First Technique Bubble that was ten times harder than the Flame-Edge Rune:

The Rank 1 Peak Armor-Inscription: Tortoise-Shell Mountain Guard—and the foundational strokes of the priceless Rank 1 Pill Inscription: Essence-Locking Spirit Rune!
