Three thousand miles south of the Seven Star Valley Sect, dark storm clouds rolled low over the Azure Sky Royal Capital at midnight.

While the main royal garrison was tied down at the northern palace gates by a staged rebellion launched by Third Prince Zhao Heng’s diehard retainers, twelve black-robed figures wearing the silver crescent masks of the Shadow-Moon Sect glided silently across the rooftops of the Pine-Brook Villa District toward Clear-Brook Manor!

Leading the twelve raiders were two terrifying Peak Houtian / Half-Step Xiantian Deacons of the Shadow-Moon Sect—Deacon Hei and Deacon Yin (each possessing 85,000 jin of Half-Step Xiantian True Essence)—accompanied by ten Middle-to-Late Houtian elite bladesmen!

“Young Master Mo Wuji gave strict orders before leaving the Secret Realm,” Deacon Hei sneered coldly as they landed outside the courtyard wall of Clear-Brook Manor. “That sixteen-year-old brat Lu Chen ruined our sect’s Spirit-Vein Eroding Array at Star-Bridge Gorge and killed the Fourth-Order Flood Dragon! Capture his parents alive inside this manor tonight—when Lu Chen returns from the Secret Realm, we’ll use his parents to force him to cripple his own dantian and hand over the Flood Dragon’s treasures!”

“What are we waiting for? Two mortal parents in a country kingdom—break the gate and grab them!” Deacon Yin raised hisGrade 3 black iron claw and slashed a thirty-foot crescent of 85,000-jin Half-Step Xiantian blade qi straight at the courtyard gate of Clear-Brook Manor!
