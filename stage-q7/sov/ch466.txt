<p>Above the crossed weapons, <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> single yellow eye bulged in utter disbelief!</p>
<p>He had expected his five-thousand-jin overhead execution cleave to break the young man's unfinished finger-tendons and drive the eighty-jin spear shaft down into Lu Chen's own skull.</p>
<p>Instead, the moment the <strong>Thirty-Six Bowstring Sinews</strong> inside Lu Chen's body chimed in unison, the eighty-jin black iron spear shaft didn't sink—it bowed downward by bare half an inch like a giant steel spring, absorbed the entire kinetic energy of Han Wu's falling saber into the thirty-six gold-white sinew lines of Lu Chen's frame, and then <strong>snapped back</strong>!</p>
<p>"All thirty-six sinews linked mid-strike?!" Han Wu's voice cracked for the first time in ten years of border slaughter. "Impossible—!"</p>
<p>"My turn."</p>
<p>Lu Chen's ten fingers—now anchored by translucent gold-white flexor sinews tough as braided steel wire—closed around the rusted three-inch iron collar and the spiral shaft of his spear with a grip that made the cold-slag iron groan!</p>
<p>Without taking a step, Lu Chen released the stored elastic energy of all thirty-six drawn sinews from his heels up through his spine and wrists in a single, whip-cracking upward spiral:</p>
<p><strong>Nine-Wave Bowstring Recoil — Coiling Dragon Flips the Sea!</strong></p>
<p><em>TWANG—BOOOOOOOOM!</em></p>
<p>Four thousand four hundred jin of pure elastic bow-recoil—compounded by the rebounded momentum of Han Wu's own downward chop—exploded upward through the eighty-jin spear shaft!</p>
<p>The tremendous spiral shockwave ripped the 110-jin Ghost-Head Great-Saber straight out of Han Wu's numb left hand, wrenching his right shoulder tendon with a wet <em>pop</em> as the heavy saber was flung forty-five degrees skyward and Han Wu's six-foot-five frame was blasted four paces backward across the glare-ice with both boots skidding out of control!</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not give the stunned bandit chief half a breath to recover his balance!</p>
<p>Before Han Wu's skidding heels had even stopped on the ice, the twelve lower-body bowstring sinews in Lu Chen's feet and legs fired like twin siege catapults:</p>
<p><em>WHOOSH!</em></p>
<p>Ten paces of snow-covered road vanished beneath Lu Chen's boots in a tenth of a heartbeat!</p>
<p>Seeing the dark bronze youth appear directly in front of his chest like a teleporting specter, Han Wu howled in sheer terror and desperation, whipping his 110-jin Ghost-Head Great-Saber down one-handed to block his torso while grabbing a poisoned throwing-dagger from his belt with his left hand!</p>
<p>Lu Chen's left thumb pressed tightly against the <strong>rusted three-inch iron collar</strong> of his shattered Sovereign Spear.</p>
<p>Every muscle in his <strong>Complete Tier Two</strong> body and every one of his <strong>Thirty-Six Tier-Three Bowstring Sinews</strong> snapped forward into a single point the size of a grain of millet:</p>
<p><strong>Basic Spear — Triple-Snap Armor-Piercing Level Thrust!</strong></p>
<p><em>TWANG—TWANG—TWANG—KRAK!!</em></p>
<p>Three rapid, overlapping tendon-snaps traveled down Lu Chen's spine, shoulder, and forearm into the eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Spear!</p>
<p>The fourteen-inch four-edged diamond point struck the flat center of Han Wu's four-inch-wide <strong>110-Jin Ghost-Head Great-Saber</strong>—and did not deflect!</p>
<p>Under four thousand four hundred jin of triple-snapped bowstring force concentrated onto a cold-slag diamond tip, the military-steel blade of the Ghost-Head Saber cracked down the middle with an ear-splitting <em>BANG</em> and shattered into two spinning halves!</p>
<p>Unchecked, the blood-stained spearhead punched straight through Han Wu's double-linked garrison chainmail, shattered his sternum, and burst two feet out the back of his bear-skin cloak!</p>
<p><em>THUD!</em></p>
<p>Lifted clean off the ice on the horizontal boar-wings of the eighty-jin spear, <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> stared down with his single wide yellow eye at the black iron shaft impaling his heart, blood pouring from his mouth onto the snow as the poisoned dagger fell unfired from his limp fingers.</p>
<p>"Boss... Boss Han is dead!!"</p>
<p>At the flanks of the sledge-barricade, the last three surviving Broken-Tooth bandits took one look at their invincible <strong>Peak Tier Three</strong> chieftain hanging dead on Lu Chen's spear, screamed in terror, dropped their shields, and tried to flee southward down the pass—only to be cut down in ten paces by a final volley from <strong>Uncle Shi's</strong> and <strong>Hunter Feng's</strong> crossbows!</p>
