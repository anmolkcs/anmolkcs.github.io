<p>In his brief test under the tip of Luo Shan's mace just now, Lu Chen had confirmed a vital combat truth through <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>:</p>
<p>Scarface Luo Shan's <strong>220-Jin Eight-Flanged Iron Bone-Crushing Mace</strong> was forged from high-density <strong>Black-Slag Core Steel</strong>, and behind it stood twenty years of <strong>Peak Tier Four (Slag-Bone Perfection)</strong> cultivation carrying nearly <strong>eight thousand jin</strong> of blunt, armor-shattering force.</p>
<p>Lu Chen's current <strong>eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Boar-Spear</strong> had been more than enough to pierce a Peak Tier Three saber like Han Wu's—but against a two-hundred-and-twenty-jin eight-flanged mace swung with eight thousand jin of force, an eighty-jin shaft lacked the sheer mass-inertia to trade head-on blows without bending!</p>
<p>To shatter a 220-jin mace head-on, he needed a spear twice as heavy and twice as hard—forged directly in the eighteenfold-gravity heart of the Earth-Marrow vein!</p>
<p>Inside the hidden geode cavern, at the rear wall opposite the boiling brine pool, a narrow vertical fissure in the black basalt vented a continuous stream of <strong>Two-Thousand-Degree Subterranean Magma-Gas</strong>—hotter even than the Black-Gold Pine Charcoal of Ash-Brook Village!</p>
<p>And sitting right beside the magma fissure was a flat-topped, ten-ton boulder of ultra-dense <strong>Subterranean Meteoric Basalt</strong> that soldier Shi Potian had once used as a striking platform.</p>
<p>Over the next five nights—from the seventh to the eleventh of the twelfth month—while thirty miners stood watch in the outer drift and Old Mole Qian's crew dug upward through the ventilation chimney, the hidden geode cavern became a secret subterranean forge!</p>
<p>Having drunk diluted <strong>Earth-Marrow Brine</strong> and eaten King-Boar meat for eight days while swinging the sixty-pound sledge under eighteenfold gravity, twenty-two-year-old <strong>Tie Niu</strong> had completely healed his left ankle and broken cleanly through into <strong>Middle Tier Two of the Mortal-Shedding Stage (Iron-Flesh Compacting — 2,200 jin force)</strong>!</p>
<p>Stripped to the waist in the blistering red glow of the magma fissure, the son of Old Tie Shan stood on one side of the basalt boulder-anvil wielding a <strong>seventy-pound iron mine-sledge</strong>, while Lu Chen stood on the other side wielding an <strong>eighty-pound sledge</strong>!</p>
<p>Into the roaring two-thousand-degree magma fissure, Lu Chen placed three extraordinary materials: 1. His existing <strong>eighty-jin nine-folded Cold-Slag Boar-Spear</strong>; 2. The <strong>one hundred jin of military high-carbon spring steel</strong> from the two shattered halves of One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's Ghost-Head Great-Saber; 3. And <strong>twenty-four fist-sized, top-grade Natural Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> harvested from the deepest center of the boiling brine pool!</p>
<p>Under ordinary surface gravity and charcoal heat, <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> could only be melted by a <strong>True-Rune Foundation</strong> master's spirit-flame.</p>
<p>Yet here inside the eighteenfold-gravity magma fissure that had birthed those very crystals—and hammered by Lu Chen's <strong>Early Tier Four (5,400 jin)</strong> iron-boned strikes paired with Tie Niu's <strong>Middle Tier Two (2,200 jin)</strong> striker blows—the white-hot Marrow Crystals softened into a silvery-black liquid metal that fused seamlessly between the layers of military spring-steel and Black-Marrow Core Iron!</p>
<p><em>BOOM—CLANG! BOOM—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>To prevent the sound of their double sledges from carrying out into Drift Four, Old Mole Qian hung three layers of wet, mud-soaked hemp ore-sacks across the narrow rock cleft, muffling the thunderous anvil blows into a low subterranean rumble indistinguishable from the natural groaning of the canyon fault.</p>
<p>Over five nights and <strong>nine thousand six hundred alternating hammer strikes</strong>, the two hundred and ten jin of raw materials were folded <strong>eighteen times</strong> under eighteenfold gravity, squeezing out every last impurity until they condensed into a seven-foot-six-inch, wrist-thick octagonal shaft and an eighteen-inch four-edged armor-piercing spearhead weighing a staggering <strong>One Hundred and Sixty Jin (equivalent to 1,600 jin under surface gravity, and 2,400 jin at the bottom of Pit #7)</strong>!</p>
<p>Just as before, at the exact throat of the spearhead socket where his front thumb rested, Lu Chen hot-shrunk the indestructible <strong>rusted three-inch iron collar</strong> of his shattered Primordial Sovereign Spear!</p>
<p>On the eleventh night, when Lu Chen lifted the white-hot <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> and plunged it straight into the center of the <strong>Boiling Earth-Marrow Sulfur Pool</strong> to quench—</p>
<p><em>HSSSSSSSSSSSS—HUMMMMMMMMM!</em></p>
<p>The spear drank in a swirling vortex of silvery-black mineral essence from the brine, emerging with a frosty, blue-black metallic sheen along its spiral-grooved shaft and four razor-sharp silver-white marrow edges along its point that made the surrounding air drop ten degrees!</p>
