<p>Lu Chen stood up slowly, his eyes fixed on the stacked wall of loose waste-shale hiding the four-foot cleft at the back of the cul-de-sac.</p>
<p>Everything fit together with cold, ruthless logic.</p>
<p>No wonder <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> had smiled when allowing Lu Chen to descend into Pit #7 as a quarantine guarantor! To Luo Shan, letting one extra Tier-Three youth down into Drift Four simply meant one more strong back to pry out the <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> over the next fifteen days—and one more nameless skeleton buried beneath ten thousand tons of collapsed granite when the fire-barrels detonated on the eighteenth night!</p>
<p>"How often do Luo Shan and his wardens come to the end of Drift Four to collect the crystals?" Lu Chen asked quietly.</p>
<p>"Only once every three nights at the midnight bell," Old Mole Qian replied quickly. "Because the heat and the <strong>fifteenfold Earth-Marrow gravity</strong> inside that hidden cavern are so crushing that even the <strong>Tier-Two</strong> wardens get nosebleeds and joint-cramps if they stay inside for more than half an hour! Only Boss Luo Shan himself—with his <strong>Peak Tier Four Slag-Bone</strong> skeleton—can walk into the center of that cavern unharmed, and he's staying up at the rim guardhouse drinking until the sixth night!"</p>
<p>"Show me the cavern," Lu Chen said.</p>
<p>Pulling aside three slabs of stacked waste-shale, Old Mole Qian held up a dim sulfur-moss lamp as Lu Chen ducked through the four-foot cleft into the hidden subterranean chamber beyond.</p>
<p>The moment Lu Chen stepped through the rock cleft, a wave of blistering, mineral-heavy heat and an abrupt jump in gravitational density—from fifteenfold to a staggering <strong>Eighteenfold Gravity</strong> right over the exposed fault-core—pressed down on his shoulders like an invisible mountain!</p>
<p>The chamber was a dome-shaped natural geode thirty feet wide. At its center bubbled a twelve-foot pool of dense, silvery-red <strong>Boiling Earth-Marrow Sulfur Brine</strong>, fed directly by the primordial iron vein beneath the canyon. Around the bottom of the simmering pool glinted dozens of fist-sized, hexagonal <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> radiating a piercing, bone-chilling metallic aura amidst the boiling water.</p>
<p>Yet what caught Lu Chen's eyes first was not the treasure in the pool, but what sat chained against the smooth black basalt wall on the far side of the cavern.</p>
<p>There, sitting upright in a meditation posture with two rusted, wrist-thick <strong>Origin-Iron Chains</strong> driven through its collarbones into the cliff, was the intact skeleton of an ancient prisoner who had perished here at least a century ago.</p>
<p>All the flesh and tendons had long since dissolved in the sulfur steam—yet the <strong>206 bones</strong> of the skeleton were not white or yellow. Every single rib, vertebra, skull-plate, and finger-bone gleamed with a dark, metallic, bronze-iron luster that had resisted a hundred years of boiling sulfur corrosion without a single pit of decay!</p>
<p>And carved into the black basalt wall beside the skeleton's right hand—chiseled not with True Origin Qi, but with the sheer, unadorned percussive force of a single iron finger-bone—were <strong>twenty-four deep, geometric postural diagrams</strong> and two lines of stark frontier script:</p>
<p><em>(I am Shi Potian, discarded soldier of the Grey-Wall Bailiff Command—born without a spiritual root, entombed in this dead cavern for twelve years. Using eighteenfold Earth-Marrow gravity as my hammer and boiling sulfur-iron brine as my furnace, I forged all 206 mortal bones into <strong>Peak Tier Four: Hundred-Refined Cold-Iron Bone</strong>! Should a later mortal enter this pit, beg not from Heaven—trust only your own bones!)</em></p>
<p>Standing before the unyielding iron skeleton of that long-dead mortal soldier, holding the forty-pound sack of <strong>Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts</strong> in one hand and the <strong>two broken halves of the 110-jin military saber</strong> in the other, Lu Chen felt every drop of blood in his body ignite with a silent, towering flame.</p>
<p>Luo Shan had given him fifteen days before the fire-barrels detonated.</p>
<p>Fifteen days in this eighteenfold-gravity furnace was all he needed to forge <strong>Peak Tier Four: Hundred-Refined Cold-Iron Bones</strong>—and turn Deep-Shaft Pit #7 into Scarface Luo Shan's grave!</p>
