<p>"Brother Lu—your back!"</p>
<p>Tie Niu and Old Mole Qian scrambled across the wet basalt through the settling dust, their faces white with terror after watching a single man catch a falling three-ton ceiling slab on his bare shoulders under eighteenfold gravity!</p>
<p>Yet when Tie Niu held the sulfur-lamp close to Lu Chen's bare back and shoulders, both men stopped and stared in awe.</p>
<p>Where the jagged underside of the three-ton granite slab had slammed into Lu Chen's spine, shoulder blades, and collarbones, the granite itself had been crushed into white powder—while beneath Lu Chen's matte-bronze skin, the spinous processes of his twenty-four vertebrae and the curve of his ribs gleamed with a hard, indestructible dark-iron contour without a single fracture or inward dent!</p>
<p>When Lu Chen tapped his own breastbone twice with his knuckles, his chest cavity echoed with a deep, hollow <em>BONG—BONG</em> like a cast-iron temple bell!</p>
<p>"My spine and ribs have crossed into <strong>Middle Tier Four</strong>," Lu Chen said calmly, wiping the trace of grey slag-blood from his chin and picking up his <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> with one hand as effortlessly as if it were a bamboo cane. "That fault-tremor just did three nights' worth of bone-compacting in one breath. How far is the crew from breaking through the upper plug of the <strong>Emergency Ventilation Chimney</strong>?"</p>
<p>"Only six feet of loose shale left before we reach the two-hundred-fathom ledge, Young Hero Lu!" Old Mole Qian reported excitedly, pointing up toward a narrow, four-foot-wide diagonal shaft in the ceiling of the outer drift that had been bricked over twenty years ago when an upper vein ran dry. "And look what the boys brought into the cavern while you were in the pool!"</p>
<p>Stacked beside the magma-vent forge were forty-two worn-out, broken, or hidden <strong>thirty-pound iron pickaxe heads</strong> and six cracked ore-cart axles that the forty debt-miners of Drift Four had secretly dug out of the waste-heaps over the past week!</p>
<p>"A man with a chain on his leg and an empty belly is a slave," Lu Chen said, his eyes sweeping over the pile of scrap iron. "A man with meat in his belly, his shackles broken, and three feet of cold steel in his hands is a soldier. Tie Niu—light the magma-vent! Tonight and tomorrow night, we forge <strong>forty short Cold-Slag Trench-Spears</strong> and <strong>forty iron-boss bucklers</strong> out of those ore-cart plates!"</p>
<p>For the next three nights—the thirteenth, fourteenth, and fifteenth of the twelfth month—the secret forge in the eighteenfold-gravity cavern worked at fever pitch.</p>
<p>Now that Lu Chen possessed <strong>six thousand four hundred jin</strong> of <strong>Middle Tier Four</strong> strength and his leg-bones, hip-bones, spine, and ribs were forged of black iron, he didn't even need a tongs-vise: gripping red-hot thirty-pound pickaxe heads with a pair of heavy iron pliers in his left hand, he hammered them out on the basalt boulder with the eighty-pound sledge in his right hand—shaping each pickaxe head in twenty blows into a thick, two-foot <strong>Double-Edged Short Trench-Spear</strong> mounted on a four-foot handle of iron-hard mine timber!</p>
<p>Meanwhile, in the outer drift after midnight, Lu Chen walked down the line of all thirty-nine debt-miners one by one, snapping their thirty-pound iron ankle-shackles open with his bare hands—fitting them with removable деревянные (wooden) split-pegs so the shackles looked intact when the wardens glanced down the dim tunnel during the day, yet could be kicked off in half a breath when the signal sounded!</p>
