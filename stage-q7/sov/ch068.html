At noon on the seventh and final day of the Five-Kingdom Core Disciple Trial, the spatial rift above Sky-Fall Canyon hummed open, teleporting all surviving disciples out onto the black ironstone plaza!

Instantly, every envoy and Palace Master looked up at the hundred-foot Sky-Fall Trial Stele floating in midair as the final point tallies of all fifty disciples lit up from bottom to top:

  • 11th Place: Su Wan’er (Azure Sky Kingdom — Early Houtian) — 780 Points! (Qualifies as Inner Sect Disciple!)
  • 7th Place: Murong Zi (Azure Sky Kingdom — Early Houtian) — 960 Points! (Qualifies as Inner Sect Disciple!) …
  • 2nd Place: Chu Kuang (Great Yue Kingdom — Middle Houtian) — 1,820 Points!
  • 1st Place: Lu Chen (Azure Sky Kingdom — Early Houtian, 16 years old) — 2,450 Points!

“2,450 Points—six hundred points ahead of #2 Chu Kuang?! And all three of the Azure Sky Kingdom’s lead disciples broke into the Houtian Realm and finished in the Top 12?!”

On the viewing dais, Palace Master Qin laughed until tears came to his eyes, while the five Xiantian Envoys of the Seven Star Valley Sect—led by Elder Ling—stared at the sixteen-year-old Lu Chen with blazing eyes as if looking at an uncarved piece of divine jade!

Sixteen years old, Early Houtian Realm, and sweeping First Place across five kingdoms!
