Before the dust of the twenty-foot crater could rise, Lu Chen’s boots touched the flagstones outside Clear-Brook Manor without making a sound.

Golden Roc Nine-Flash Step — Seventh Flash!

With the Concept of Mountain-Gravity locking down the entire fifty-yard street like an invisible quagmire of tenfold gravity, the ten Middle-to-Late Houtian Shadow-Moon bladesmen couldn’t even lift their feet to flee!

《Primordial Star-Forge Spear — Second Form: Drawing the Star River!》

WHIR—KABOOM!

The nine-foot, 3,600-jin Star-Shattering Heavy Spear swept out in a dazzling silver-purple arc that looked like the Milky Way crashing down to earth!

All ten Houtian bladesmen had their weapons shattered and were blasted unconscious into the street walls in a single breath, while Deacon Hei—who had burned blood essence to flee three hundred yards down the avenue—was struck squarely in the back by a spear-beam of compressed purple thunder, collapsing with his dantian cyclone shattered just as Palace Master Qin, Grand Marshal Su, and the royal guards arrived to lock him in iron chains!

From the moment Lu Chen descended from the clouds to the moment all twelve Shadow-Moon raiders were neutralized, less than five breaths had passed!

Lu Chen immediately sheathed his aura, stored the 3,600-jin heavy spear into his spatial ring so the cold metal wouldn’t chill his parents, and opened the golden array gate of Clear-Brook Manor.

“Father! Mother!”

“Chen’er!”

Meng Wan and Lu Shan ran across the osmanthus courtyard and threw their arms around Lu Chen’s shoulders. Feeling his mother’s warm hands patting his cheeks and shoulders to make sure he didn’t have a single scratch, and seeing his father’s eyes shining with fierce pride, Lu Chen let out a long, deep breath of relief and smiled warmly:

“I’m back, Father, Mother. Not a single tile in our courtyard was broken—and tonight I’m not going anywhere until I eat three bowls of Mother’s hot noodles.”
