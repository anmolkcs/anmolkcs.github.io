<p>Fifty miles up in the purple-black stratosphere above <strong>Central Emperor City</strong>, the golden-silver dome of the <strong>Continental Star-Seal</strong>—which had protected the Sky Spill Continent for ten thousand years since the era of the Sunken Bronze Dynasty—rippled like disturbed water under a suffocating, crimson shadow.</p>
<p>Anchored in the void just outside the planetary barrier were <strong>three mile-long Grade 6 Quasi-Saint Void-Warships</strong>, their hulls cast from dark-red <strong>Astral Blood-Iron</strong> and lined with seventy-two <strong>Rank 6 Peak Void-Breaking Cannons</strong>!</p>
<p>Standing on the command prow of the central flagship (<em>The Blood-Sun Vanguard</em>) was a tall, cold-eyed man in scarlet star-armor whose dantian radiated the unmistakable, world-warping pressure of a true <strong>Upper-Realm Powerhouse</strong>:</p>
<p><strong>Commander Xue Ao</strong> of the <strong>Blood-Sovereign Clan's Outer Star-Patrol Fleet</strong>—an expert who had already opened a <strong>30-Mile Embryonic Inner World</strong> (<strong>Early Inner Embryo World Realm / Divine Transformation</strong>), flanked by <strong>six Peak Divine Sea (Void-Bridge Realm) Vice-Commanders</strong> and three hundred <strong>Life-and-Death Wheel / Early Divine Sea</strong> Upper-Realm blood-armored soldiers!</p>
<p>"Commander Xue Ao," one of the Peak Divine Sea Vice-Commanders reported, looking down at a cracked soul-compass in his hand. "Both Envoy Xue Ling's Shadow-Clone and our continental apostle Xue Wuji had their soul-lamps extinguished within the past ten days—and someone inside this lower continent repaired the <strong>Pre-Epoch Continental Star-Astrolabe</strong>, raising the planetary Star-Seal's defense back to 300%!"</p>
<p>"A mere lower-world cage whose strongest native cultivators are capped at the <strong>Divine Sea &amp; Void-Bridge Realm</strong>, yet someone inside dared destroy my Blood-Sovereign Clan's advance nodes?"</p>
<p>Commander Xue Ao sneered coldly, the <strong>30-Mile Embryonic Inner World</strong> inside his dantian pulsing with a dark-red <strong>World Force</strong> that made the surrounding void creak.</p>
<p>"Because the <strong>Continental Star-Seal</strong> is still intact at 300%, if I forcibly project my <strong>Inner Embryo World</strong> across the barrier right now, the planetary Star-Seal will rebound with <strong>Rank 7 Pre-Epoch Counter-Lightning</strong>. Connect all three Void-Warships into the <strong>Three-Sun Blood-Boring Array</strong>—in forty-eight hours of continuous bombardment, we will drill a ten-mile breach through the Continental Star-Seal above Central Emperor City, descend in full force, and harvest every Divine Sea core and the <strong>Chrono-Soul Prism</strong> on this continent!"</p>
<p><em>HUMMMMM—KABOOOOOM!</em></p>
<p>Three thirty-foot beams of dark-crimson <strong>Void-Boring Blood Light</strong> erupted from the prows of the three mile-long Void-Warships, slamming down continuously against the golden-silver dome of the <strong>Continental Star-Seal</strong> fifty miles above Central Emperor City!</p>
