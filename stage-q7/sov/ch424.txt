<p>Old Tie Shan stared into Lu Chen's calm, blazing dark eyes, gritted his teeth until his gray beard bristled, and threw his whole bruised weight back against the waist-strap of the ox-hide bellows!</p>
<p><em>WHOOOOOOOSH—ROOOOOOAR!</em></p>
<p>The charcoal dome erupted into a blinding column of white-gold fire.</p>
<p>Lu Chen pulled the thirty-pound bloom of <strong>Black-Marrow Ore</strong> from the heart of the furnace, locked it beneath the oak foot-treadle vise on the anvil, and raised the forty-pound iron sledgehammer high above his head with both hands.</p>
<p>He did not strike eighteen times per heat now.</p>
<p>Drawing his breath deep into his lower abdomen—locking ankle, knee, hip, spine, shoulder, and wrist into a single unbroken bow—Lu Chen drove the forty-pound sledge down in a relentless, thundering tempest!</p>
<p><em>BOOM—CLANG!</em> <em>BOOM—CLANG!</em> <em>BOOM—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>With every downward crash of the hammer, the internal heat trapped beneath his sealed seventh skin-husk spiked higher. By the tenth strike, steam was whistling out of the corners of his eyes and nose. By the twentieth strike, the veins across his chest, traps, and forearms stood out like pulsing iron roots against the tight, suffocating black shell of dried Blood-Briar paste and forged iron-scale.</p>
<p><em>Twenty-five!</em> <em>Twenty-eight!</em> <em>Thirty!</em></p>
<p>On the thirty-sixth continuous strike—as the thirty-pound billet compressed into a flawless, ten-jin rectangular bar of dark-blue <strong>Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron</strong>—the boiling blood-pressure inside Lu Chen's capillaries and the percussive shockwave rebounding up the sledgehammer shaft met at the center of his sternum!</p>
<p><em>KRAK—SHHHHHHHK!</em></p>
<p>A crisp, audible tearing sound—like a dry locust shell splitting down the spine, or cured bull-hide snapping taut on a stretching frame—rippled from the center of Lu Chen's spine all the way down to his heels and out to the tips of his ten fingers!</p>
<p>Across his entire body, the blackened, calcified seventh husk of dead mortal epidermis cracked into thousands of small, hexagonal scales and sloughed off in a single shower onto the cinder floor!</p>
<p>Simultaneously, the freezing midnight blizzard wind blowing into the shed struck his newly exposed skin.</p>
<p>It did not cut him, nor did it make him shiver.</p>
<p>In the orange glow of the forge, Lu Chen's new skin carried a faint, matte bronze-iron sheen—smooth, tight-grained, and tough as three-layered cured ox-hide boiled in mineral oil! When a stray scatter of red-hot charcoal embers blew out of the hearth and landed directly against his bare ribs and knuckles, they simply rolled off onto the floor without leaving a single red mark!</p>
<p>Even more importantly, as the seventh husk shed, all thirty-six thousand pores across his body opened and closed in unison like microscopic iron valves—effortlessly blocking the grey, corrosive <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong> of the frontier wind on the inhale, while venting every trace of stagnant mortal fatigue on the exhale!</p>
<p><strong>Tier One of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Complete Ash-Skin Scouring!</strong></p>
<p>His baseline physical strength under the Great Desolate Origin Universe's tenfold gravity surged cleanly past <strong>one thousand two hundred jin</strong>, and for the first time since falling through the Dimensional Slag-Trench twenty-one days ago, his entire outer vessel was sealed into an unbreachable mortal fortress.</p>
<p>Lu Chen slowly lowered the forty-pound sledgehammer to the cinder floor.</p>
<p>Across the hearth, Old Tie Shan slipped out of the bellows waist-strap, stumbled two steps forward, and touched the matte bronze skin of Lu Chen's shoulder with his trembling right hand.</p>
<p>"Twenty-one days..." the old blacksmith whispered in awe, looking down at the pile of shed black skin-flakes around Lu Chen's rawhide sandals and the twenty gleaming bars of Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron stacked on the rack. "From a half-dead boy coughing blood in the straw to <strong>Complete Tier One Ash-Skin</strong> in twenty-one days... Even the young masters of the big clans in Black-Rock Town, soaking in ten-tael spirit-herb tubs every night, take half a year to shed the seventh husk!"</p>
