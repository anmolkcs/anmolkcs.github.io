Three hundred miles south of the Abyssal Salt-Sea, the white salt-flats rose into a jagged ring of nine active volcanic calderas whose plumes of violet-gold sulfur smoke painted the sky day and night.

Built directly across the bridges linking those nine volcanic craters stood one of the most unique forging and alchemical metropolises of the Sky Spill Continent: Nine-Kiln Crucible City.

Unlike ordinary blacksmithing towns that merely hammered iron ore over coal fires, the grandmasters of Nine-Kiln Crucible City practiced an ancient, two-thousand-year craft that combined High-Temperature Glaze-Porcelain Kiln Firing with Meteoric Heavy-Metal Smelting—creating Celadon-Glazed Spirit Steel and Living Porcelain-Core Weapon Embryos!

Why? Because pure metal, no matter how hard, suffered from spirit-qi dissipation at the Grade 4 (Earth-Treasure) level unless its internal grain was sealed by a vitrified Star-Glaze Ceramic Lattice fired at ten thousand degrees inside a subterranean magma kiln!

Even more importantly for Lu Chen, his nine-foot Star-Shattering Heavy Spear currently weighed 3,600 jin and stood at the Peak of Grade 3 Upper-Tier. Now that he had broken through to the Middle Xiantian Realm with 185,000 jin of baseline strength and 385,000 jin of full-strength burst power, even a Peak Grade 3 weapon trembled at the edge of structural fatigue during his Fourth Chime strikes!

To upgrade the Star-Shattering Heavy Spear into a true Grade 4 Lower-Tier Earth-Treasure weighing 7,200 jin—and awaken an embryonic Living Weapon Spirit inside its shaft—he needed to fire the spear inside Kiln #1 (The Primordial Earth-Core Crucible) at the center of Nine-Kiln Crucible City!

When Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang walked through the towering celadon-iron gates of Nine-Kiln Crucible City, they immediately felt the dry, scorching waves of Rank 4 Earth-Fire Origin Qi radiating up from the magma channels beneath the streets.

Before heading to the central Crucible Guild, however, Lu Chen stopped at the Sky-Rail Express Pavilion near the city gate.

Opening his spatial ring, he packed two stalks of Millennium Halite-Marrow Lotus (one for Elder Tie to accompany the already-sent Rank 4 Meridian Silk so Elder Tie could begin his final arm-regeneration preparation, and one divided into twelve gentle Halite-Marrow Longevity Pills for his parents Lu Shan & Meng Wan), along with a warm, detailed letter recounting their victory over the Nether-Bone Armada, and dispatched them via an armored Fourth-Order Flame-Falcon Courier straight back to Broken Spear Peak!
