Eight hundred miles north of Loom-Star City, the temperate cloud-forests vanished behind a towering, two-thousand-foot wall of blue-black prehistoric ice that stretched across the northern horizon like the frozen spine of a dead titan.

This was the Star-Burial Glacier—an ancient polar rift where a shower of frost-meteors had struck during the era of the Sunken Bronze Dynasty eight thousand years ago, freezing an entire subterranean bronze citadel beneath three hundred fathoms of never-melting Profound Xuan-Ice.

Stepping off the Sky-Rail Northern Terminus into a howling blizzard whose wind-blades carried the cutting force of Grade 2 steel, seventeen-year-old Lu Chen (having just turned seventeen during the thirty-six-hour journey north!), Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang wrapped their Xiantian Origin Qi around their bodies and sped across the crevasse-scarred ice shelf toward Ice-Crevasse Gorge forty miles ahead.

Even in the middle of the frozen gale, the Rank 4 Meridian-Nourishing Inner Vests Lu Chen had woven for his three companions radiated a gentle, unceasing starlight warmth through their meridians, neutralizing 100% of the subterranean frost-poison.

"Look at the snow-clouds twenty miles ahead above Ice-Crevasse Gorge!" Su Wan'er pointed northward, her clear eyes narrowing as she sensed the violent collision of three distinct elemental auras staining the white blizzard crimson, black, and fiery orange. "That's the Blood-Moon Demonic Cult's Blood-Sea Miasma combined with the Shadow-Moon Sect and Crimson-Furnace Sect's siege formations—they've already trapped Elder Tie's expedition inside the outer rim of the glacier!"

"Eighth Flash—full speed!"

Lu Chen's dark eyes flashed with purple-gold lightning as his boots stamped the ancient blue ice, propelling him across the glacier at three hundred miles an hour with his nine-foot, 7,200-jin Grade 4 Primordial Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear humming like a waking dragon across his back!
