<p>"Out of my road, little mule!"</p>
<p>Seeing Lu Chen cut off his charge ten paces in front of the sledge-barricade, <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> didn't even slow down!</p>
<p>At <strong>Peak Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Perfection)</strong>, all thirty-six of Han Wu's major tendon lines from heel to fingertip had been soaked in military spirit-salves and drawn taut over ten years of border slaughter. As he planted his front boot on the glare-ice and whipped his <strong>110-jin Ghost-Head Great-Saber</strong> around in a horizontal <strong>Waist-Severing Wolf-Fang Slash</strong>, the thirty-six tendons across his spine, shoulders, elbows, and fingers snapped in a synchronized chain reaction:</p>
<p><em>TWANG-TWANG-TWANG—WHOOOOOOOOM!</em></p>
<p>Four thousand six hundred jin of razor-sharp, high-velocity cutting force—twelve hundred jin heavier than Lu Chen's current thirty-three-sinew strength—hurtled toward Lu Chen's ribs!</p>
<p>Lu Chen knew that with a twelve-hundred-jin gap in elastic tendon-snap, meeting the flat of that 110-jin great-saber in a stationary block would shatter the uncalcined bones of his wrists.</p>
<p>Within the three-pace glacial calm of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen planted the butt-spike of his eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Spear into the ice at a forty-five-degree slant, gripped the rusted three-inch collar with his left hand and the mid-shaft with his right, and met the howling Ghost-Head Great-Saber with <strong>Slanted Bridge-Arc Deflection</strong>!</p>
<p><em>BOOOOOOOOOOM—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>A blinding ring of blue-white sparks erupted between the two men in the driving snow!</p>
<p>The four-inch-wide blade of the 110-jin Ghost-Head Saber bit a tenth of an inch into the octagonal spiral ridge of Lu Chen's Cold-Slag spear shaft before sliding upward off the slanted iron rod and roaring three inches over Lu Chen's ducked head!</p>
<p>Even deflected at an angle, the remaining twelve hundred jin of excess <strong>Peak Tier Three</strong> tendon-snap crashed straight into Lu Chen's palms and fingers!</p>
<p><em>RIP!</em></p>
<p>Because the final three deep <strong>Digital Flexor Sinew Lines</strong> inside Lu Chen's palms and fingers had not yet finished drawing on the iron-pine bow-rack, the ferocious vibration of Han Wu's 110-jin saber tore open the skin between Lu Chen's thumbs and index fingers, sending warm crimson blood streaming down the black iron spear shaft and skidding his boots three feet backward across the glare-ice!</p>
<p>"Hahaha! Thirty-three drawn sinews!" Han Wu's single yellow eye gleamed with savage glee as he felt the slight give in Lu Chen's finger-grip through the blade collision. "Your legs, spine, and shoulders have entered Tier Three, boy—but the three palm-sinews in your hands are still stuck at Tier Two! Every time my Ghost-Head Saber hits your spear, the bow-snap goes straight into your unfinished fingers! Let's see how many chops it takes before your hands burst open and you drop that iron rod!"</p>
<p>Before his laugh had even died on the wind, Han Wu's boots stamped the ice—<em>TWANG!</em>—and he unleashed the military slaughter technique of the Grey-Wall Vanguard:</p>
<p><strong>Thirty-Six Continuous Wolf-Pack Fang Cleaves!</strong></p>
<p><em>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</em></p>
<p>In the fifty-foot stone corridor of Howling-Wind Pass, while <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>, <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, and <strong>Hunter Feng</strong> fought desperately at the flanks of the sledge-barricade to hold back the five remaining Broken-Tooth bandits, the center of the snow-swept road became a terrifying vortex of clashing heavy iron!</p>
<p>Ten exchanges! Twenty exchanges! Thirty-five exchanges!</p>
<p>Every single blow of Han Wu's 110-jin Ghost-Head Saber carried four thousand six hundred jin of explosive thirty-six-sinew recoil.</p>
<p>Every single parry, spiral deflection, and counter-thrust of Lu Chen's eighty-jin spear forced that monstrous shockwave directly through the <strong>three unfinished deep flexor tendons</strong> inside his bleeding palms!</p>
<p>By the fortieth exchange, both of Widow Qin's hyena-hide bracers on Lu Chen's wrists were soaked dark red with blood. His boots had been driven back step by step until his heels were less than two paces in front of the oak sledge-barricade—where fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> was frantically cranking a Triple-Prod Crossbow behind the loophole and sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> was swinging his thirty-pound smith's hammer over the side-board at a lunging bandit!</p>
