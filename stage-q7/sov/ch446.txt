<p>For the next five days and nights—from the twelfth to the sixteenth of the eleventh month—the five of them lived beside the warm clay walls of the seven smoking earthen kilns in Bitter-Pine Ravine.</p>
<p>Charcoal burning in the deep Black-Frost was an art that tolerated zero carelessness. If a sudden gust of ridge wind cracked the wet clay dome of a kiln and let too much oxygen rush in, three thousand jin of timber would flare up and burn to worthless white ash in an hour; if the vents were choked too tight, the fire inside would smother and leave half-charred "red-brand" wood that smoked in the smithy hearth.</p>
<p>With <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen became the infallible pulse-reader of all seven kilns.</p>
<p>Walking the line of clay domes every two hours through the dark, he didn't even need to open a spy-hole: by resting his calloused palm against the warm clay brick of each kiln, he could feel the exact convective rumble of the pyrolysis gases inside, smell the transition from sour white water-steam to acrid yellow <strong>Pine-Tar Smoke</strong>, and adjust the mud-plugs on the vents by half a finger's breadth so all seven kilns carbonized at a uniform, optimal pace.</p>
<p>On the fifth night—when the smoke from Kilns #1 and #2 was beginning to thin from yellow tar-smoke to a faint, transparent blue haze signifying near-complete carbonization—the snow outside stopped, revealing a vault of cold, unfamiliar stars between the jagged walls of the ravine.</p>
<p>Sitting on pine logs packed against the warm clay flank of Kiln #1, roasting strips of salted King-Boar meat on iron ramrods while fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> dozed with his head against his grandfather's sheepskin shoulder, Lu Chen looked up at those harsh, metallic-white stars and spoke quietly to <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>.</p>
<p>"Uncle Shi. In Black-Rock County, everyone fears the <strong>Heaven-Shackling Bailiff Court</strong>, yet Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan is only a town-level rider. How far up does the Bailiff Court actually reach?"</p>
<p>Uncle Shi turned his boar strip over the coals with his three-fingered hand, his grizzled face solemn in the red glow of the kiln vent.</p>
<p>"Higher than any bird in the <strong>Great Desolate Origin Universe</strong> can fly, Lu Chen," the old hunter murmured softly so as not to wake his grandson. "Two hundred years ago, my great-grandfather wasn't a villager here—he was a mason drafted from the inner prefectures to haul foundation stone for the <strong>Grey-Wall Prefectural Bailiff Tower</strong>, three hundred miles north of Black-Rock Town. Before he died, he told my father what was carved on the great bronze stele in the Prefectural Square."</p>
<p>Lu Chen leaned forward slightly, his eyes steady. "What was carved on the stele?"</p>
<p>"It said that this <strong>Great Desolate Origin Universe</strong> is divided into <strong>Thirty-Six Prefectural Heavens</strong>, each one wider than a thousand mortal kingdoms," Uncle Shi said quietly. "Our <strong>Black-Rock County</strong> is just a tiny speck of mud at the southernmost edge of <strong>Grey-Wall Prefecture</strong>, which itself is only a borderland in the <strong>Ninth Outer Frontier Sector</strong>. And ruling over the prison-mines, tribute-ledgers, and world-chains of all Thirty-Six Prefectural Heavens is the <strong>Heaven-Shackling Bailiff Court</strong>."</p>
<p>Uncle Shi pointed his iron ramrod upward toward a faint, cold cluster of reddish-bronze stars hanging low near the northern horizon—shaped like an inverted cage of nine chains.</p>
<p>"My great-grandfather said the high Bailiff Enforcers in the inner prefectures aren't even mortals or Desolate Pulse warriors—they are terrifying beings at the <strong>Origin Furnace</strong>, <strong>Domain Lord</strong>, and <strong>Immortal Bailiff Sovereign</strong> realms who ride bronze star-barges across the void outside our sky! They cast colossal chains into the outer chaos to hook entire 'Lower Embryo Worlds' like fishermen netting carp in a pond, dragging those sealed worlds into the <strong>Nether-Chain Star Sea</strong> at the center of the Ninth Sector to drain their world-marrow over centuries!"</p>
<p>Beneath his hemp shirt, Lu Chen's heart gave a single, thunderous beat against his ribs.</p>
<p><em>Sector Nine-Four-Seven of the Outer Slag-Belt... Grey-Wall Frontier... The Nether-Chain Star Sea!</em></p>
<p>Every word the bored voice aboard the Harvester Barge had spoken above the Primordial Wall of Warm-Spring Valley locked together with Uncle Shi's ancestral memory like two halves of a broken bronze tally!</p>
<p>His home universe—where his father Lu Shan, his mother Meng Wan, Su Qingxue, and Su Wan'er waited frozen inside the <strong>Nine-Epoch Inverse Chrono-Cocoon</strong>—was floating somewhere out in the outer slag-belt bordering the <strong>Nether-Chain Star Sea</strong> of this very Ninth Sector! And the Mother-Ledger recording Sector Nine-Four-Seven was held along the chain of command stretching from <strong>Black-Rock County</strong>, to <strong>Grey-Wall Prefecture</strong>, and up to the <strong>Central Bailiff Star-Fortress</strong> of the Ninth Sector!</p>
<p>For the first time since falling into the mud of Ash-Brook Village, the nameless, suffocating fog of the cosmos parted in Lu Chen's mind, revealing a concrete, step-by-step road upward.</p>
