<p>In the four-foot-wide granite throat of the Split-Stone Defile, sheer numbers meant nothing. Only two wolves could fit abreast between the vertical rock walls at a time—and standing squarely across that four-foot gap was Lu Chen.</p>
<p><em>ROOOOOOAR—SNAP!</em></p>
<p>Driven mad by the smell of fresh boar meat on the litters retreating up the trail behind him, the first two <strong>Peak Tier One Gray-Scab Wolves</strong> launched themselves through the sulfur mist simultaneously—one leaping high at Lu Chen's throat to knock him back, the other lunging low through the mud to hamstring his knees!</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not take a single step backward.</p>
<p>Within the three-pace sphere of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, his eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Boar-Spear moved with the terrifying, unadorned speed of a thunderbolt compressed into four feet of space: <strong>Two-Stage Linked Thrust — Swallow-Return &amp; Earth-Pin</strong>!</p>
<p><em>SHHHHK—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>His initial high thrust drove the fourteen-inch four-edged diamond point straight through the leaping wolf's breastbone in midair, stopping its thousand-jin pounce dead against the horizontal boar-wings of the spearhead; then, without even pulling the point out, Lu Chen whipped his back hip and slammed the impaled wolf violently downward like a giant meat-mallet directly onto the spine of the second wolf lunging at his knees!</p>
<p><em>CRUNCH!</em></p>
<p>Pinned together in the gravel, both wolves howled as Lu Chen wrenched the eighty-jin spear free with a twist of his wrists that sheared their ribs in half, kicking their twitching carcasses forward into the faces of the third and fourth wolves charging behind them!</p>
<p>Up on the ten-foot granite shelf to Lu Chen's left, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> and <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong> fired two of the captured <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong> down into the packed bottleneck!</p>
<p><em>TWANG! TWANG!</em></p>
<p>One iron bolt pinned the fifth wolf's shoulder to the mud, while the other grazed the sixth—yet the remaining three <strong>Peak Tier One</strong> wolves and the massive <strong>Early Tier Two Silver-Backed Alpha</strong> didn't even slow down! Using the bodies of their fallen packmates as a springboard, all three wolves and the Alpha surged into the four-foot rock cleft at once!</p>
<p>Every thrust and sweep of the eighty-jin spear under tenfold gravity was now straining the uncompacted muscles of Lu Chen's lower back, hips, and thighs to the breaking point. Lactic acid burned like molten iron in his quadriceps; the freezing sulfur-air scorched his lungs; yet every time his back heel drove into the granite gravel to meet a thousand-jin impact, he could feel the sluggish mortal water being squeezed out of his leg muscles inch by inch!</p>
<p><em>WHACK! SHHHHK!</em></p>
<p>A short, ferocious <strong>Horizontal Iron-Bar Sweep</strong> of the eighty-jin shaft crushed the jaw of the third wolf against the left rock wall; a reverse thrust of the butt-spike pierced the eye-socket of the fourth; and then—while the point of Lu Chen's spear was momentarily wedged two inches deep in the granite cleft—the nineteen-hundred-jin <strong>Early Tier Two Silver-Backed Alpha Wolf</strong> vaulted directly over the dying fourth wolf's back, its yellowing four-inch fangs aimed straight at the bridge of Lu Chen's nose!</p>
