<p>At noon on the third day of the twelfth month, outside the gate of Old Carter Meng's caravanserai, Lu Chen and Old Tie Shan divided the party.</p>
<p>Now that both Ash-Brook's and Mud-Well's winter taxes were officially stamped clear in the County Treasury—with the red cinnabar seal drying on the wooden tallies—and <strong>Senior Clerk Master Sun</strong> had assigned a stiff-backed <strong>County Audit Clerk named Fang</strong> (accompanied by two neutral courthouse guards) to ride out to the rim of <strong>Black-Slag Canyon</strong> that afternoon to witness the Pit #7 quarantine redemption, there was no need for the village hunters to linger in town.</p>
<p>In fact, keeping <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong>, <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, and <strong>Cross-Eyed Liu</strong> near Black-Slag Canyon would only give <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong> and <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> hostages to threaten at the canyon rim!</p>
<p>"Uncle Shi," Lu Chen said quietly, handing the jade-lined wooden box containing the three stalks of <strong>Three-Leaf Clear-Eye Frost-Grass</strong> and the Snow-Python Bile to the old hunter, along with twenty taels of remaining silver to buy forty sacks of winter reserve grain from the town granary. "Load the grain onto the two sledges and take Shi Tou and the men straight back through Howling-Wind Pass before dark. The sooner Granny Liu boils this Frost-Grass with morning dew, the sooner little <strong>Xiao Ya's</strong> eyes see the light."</p>
<p>Fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> grabbed the hem of Lu Chen's sheepskin coat with both hands, his eyes red-rimmed. "Big Brother Lu—let me come to the canyon rim with you and Uncle Tie! I can carry your spare arrows and watch the horses—"</p>
<p>"A spearman doesn't argue with his formation orders, Little Stone," Lu Chen said gently, tapping the red hemp cord that Xiao Ya had tied around Shi Tou's wrist. "Your post is guarding the grain sledges, watching over your grandfather on the pass road, and keeping the smithy hearth warm until Old Tie, Tie Niu, and I walk back through the north gate. Can I trust you with Ash-Brook while I'm underground?"</p>
<p>Shi Tou swallowed hard, wiped his nose fiercely on his sleeve, and stood straight as an iron-pine sapling. "Yes, Big Brother Lu! I'll practice three hundred rooted thrusts every morning in the snow until you and Big Brother Tie Niu come back!"</p>
<p>Uncle Shi gripped Lu Chen's forearm with his three-fingered hand—no words needed between the two men who had bled together in the Boiling-Mud Wallow, Bitter-Pine Ravine, and Howling-Wind Pass—before turning to lead the loaded grain sledges out the northern gate of Black-Rock Town.</p>
<p>Half an hour later, <strong>Lu Chen</strong> and <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>—accompanied by <strong>Audit Clerk Fang</strong> on his donkey—walked out the eastern iron gate of the town toward the smoking abyss of <strong>Black-Slag Canyon</strong>.</p>
<p>Over his right shoulder, Lu Chen carried his seven-foot-two-inch, <strong>eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Boar-Spear</strong>. Strapped across his back inside a canvas roll were the <strong>two broken halves of Han Wu's 110-jin military Ghost-Head Saber</strong> (weighing a hundred jin of high-carbon spring steel), his thirty-six-pound Cold-Slag hammer-head, and the forty-pound sack of <strong>Subterranean Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts</strong> he had bought at the apothecary—a total deadweight of nearly <strong>two hundred and sixty jin</strong> (2,600 jin under tenfold gravity) that Lu Chen carried up the rocky canyon road without a single change in his breathing rhythm!</p>
