<p>At the exact center of the <strong>Primordial Origin Kiln</strong>, floating above a ten-mile crater-lake of sealed <strong>Nine-Colored Origin-World Marrow</strong>, stood a breathtaking, ten-thousand-foot-tall monolith of primordial dark-gold silicate stone:</p>
<p><strong>The Pre-Epoch Creation Tablet!</strong></p>
<p>Even from a mile away, Lu Chen felt the <strong>Chrono-Soul Prism</strong> inside his spiritual sea—and its <strong>Twenty-Third and Twenty-Fourth Technique Bubbles</strong> (which were already at 95% illumination)—vibrate with a deafening, joyful chime!</p>
<p>Why? Because the <strong>Pre-Epoch Creation Tablet</strong> was cast from the exact same primordial grey-gold silicate mother-stone as the outer shell of the <strong>Chrono-Soul Prism</strong>!</p>
<p>Surrounding the base of the ten-thousand-foot Creation Tablet were twelve <strong>Early and Middle Great World King</strong> elders from neutral <strong>First-Grade Star-Palaces</strong> (including the upright white-robed <strong>Star-Artisan Sovereign Mo Jing</strong> of the <strong>Heavenly-Craft Star Palace</strong>, a distant upper-realm ancestor-branch of Master Mo Ban's guild!).</p>
<p>"Sigh... Over the past three thousand years, the <strong>360,000 Primordial Entropy-Fractures</strong> across the lower nine tiers of the Creation Tablet have widened by another inch," <strong>Star-Artisan Sovereign Mo Jing</strong> (<strong>Middle Great World King</strong>) shook his head in sorrow as he examined the ten-thousand-foot monolith. "Because those entropy-fractures disrupt the tablet's <strong>Rank 8 World-Creation Rune Matrix</strong>, the pool of <strong>Nine-Colored Origin-World Marrow</strong> beneath the tablet is 90% sealed off—and anyone who tries to force the seal with brute World Force triggers an <strong>Eighth-Order Entropy Backlash</strong> that can cripple a Great World King!"</p>
<p>Just then, <strong>Blood-King Xue Yan</strong>, <strong>Flame-King Yan Fen</strong>, and <strong>Bone-King Gu Ming</strong> flew onto the outer rim of the altar plaza, their faces twisting with venomous glee when they saw that Lu Chen and Su Qingxue were blocked by the sealed Creation Tablet:</p>
<p>"Hahaha! Sovereign Mo Jing is right—the Pre-Epoch Creation Tablet's Marrow Pool is sealed by 360,000 entropy-fractures! Even a <strong>Rank 8 World-King Inscription Grandmaster</strong> can't repair Primordial Entropy-Fractures! Lu Chen, Su Qingxue, you risked your lives crossing the Fire-Storm first, only to hit a dead end! Once our <strong>Middle Great World King</strong> reinforcement—<strong>Blood-Sovereign Elder Xue Cang</strong>—arrives in two hours, neither of you will leave this altar alive!"</p>
<p>Standing before the base of the ten-thousand-foot <strong>Pre-Epoch Creation Tablet</strong>, however, eighteen-year-old <strong>Lu Chen</strong> didn't even spare Blood-King Xue Yan a glance.</p>
<p>Walking up to the lower tier of the dark-gold silicate monolith under the curious eyes of <strong>Sovereign Mo Jing</strong> and the twelve neutral Great World Kings, Lu Chen pressed his bare left palm lightly against the cold, fractured stone and sank his consciousness into the deepest state of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>.</p>
