Twenty paces away on the bronze platform, seeing Lu Chen absorb the Fifth-Order Phoenix Blood and reach for the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade, Blood-Moon Envoy Xue Cang went completely mad with desperation!

"If I can't have the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade, I'll detonate the Fifth-Order Blood-Moon Demon Lord's Soul-Seed sealed inside my banner and annihilate your spiritual sea!"

Xue Cang bit off the tip of his tongue and drove his own heart-blood into the Grade 4 Blood-Soul Banner, sacrificing the entire Grade 4 treasure to release a terrifying, crimson-black Revolving Core Peak Soul-Curse Avatar that bypassed physical space and plunged straight into Lu Chen's Upper Dantian (Spiritual Sea)—while at the same instant, the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade in Lu Chen's left hand resonated with the seven lit facets of the Chrono-Soul Prism!

Inside Lu Chen's spiritual sea:

As the hideous Blood-Moon Demon Lord's Soul-Seed roared into the outer mist of the Prism, and the pure, nine-colored divine soul-light of the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade bathed the Chrono-Soul Prism's seven glowing facets—

DONG...

Time across the entire universe seemed to pause for a single, eternal beat.

At the center of the Chrono-Soul Prism's inner cosmos, the Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus—which had remained in deep, motionless hibernation ever since the day of its master's ancient self-destruction—slowly bloomed open its first three crystal petals!

Within the blooming ice lotus, the peerless young woman in snow-white robes—Su Qingxue—slowly opened her eyes.

Her eyes were like two quiet galaxies of eternal starlight and winter snow—carrying neither arrogance nor coldness toward Lu Chen, only a deep, gentle clarity that had watched over his silent, grueling ten-thousand-thrust practice sessions from the very beginning.

She didn't even raise her hand toward the intruding Fifth-Order Blood-Moon Soul-Seed.

She simply looked at it once.

Freeze.

Under a single glance of Su Qingxue's awakened Upper-Realm Soul-Origin, the terrifying Fifth-Order Blood-Moon Soul-Seed froze into harmless, pure soul-crystal dust—which drifted down into Lu Chen's Golden-Jade Spiritual Sea, expanding his spiritual sea from 120 yards straight to 200 yards wide—while outside in the real world, Blood-Moon Envoy Xue Cang let out a blood-curdling scream as the backlash shattered his spiritual sea and knocked him unconscious onto the ice floor!
