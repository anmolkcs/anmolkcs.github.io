<p>When the sun broke over the eastern rim of Bitter-Pine Ravine on the morning of the eighteenth day of the eleventh month, the seven earthen charcoal kilns had cooled enough to be opened.</p>
<p>While Big-Tall Zhao and Cross-Eyed Liu buried the twelve dead Broken-Tooth raiders in a deep gravel sinkhole half a mile down the gulch and stripped their weapons, armor, and pouches—recovering another <strong>nine taels of broken silver</strong>, twelve iron-boss bucklers, and Scar-Six's pair of fifty-pound broad-axes—Uncle Shi, Shi Tou, and Lu Chen broke open the clay brick doors of Kilns #1 through #7.</p>
<p>Even Kiln #6, whose front firing-door Lu Chen had smashed during the night ambush to trigger the tar-gas backdraft, had been quickly resealed with wet mud by Uncle Shi as soon as the battle ended.</p>
<p>Inside the seven dark chambers lay stacked rows of gleaming, jet-black <strong>Black-Gold Pine Charcoal</strong>.</p>
<p>Every four-foot billet of Bitter-Iron Pine had shrunk by a third in thickness while retaining its exact spiral wood-grain, now transformed into pure, crystalline carbon infused with trace mineral iron. When Lu Chen picked up two sticks of the finished charcoal and struck them together, they did not crumble or smudge his fingers with soft soot; they rang with a crisp, high <em>clink-clink</em> like two bars of cast bronze!</p>
<p>"Over three thousand six hundred jin of top-grade Black-Gold Charcoal!" Uncle Shi exclaimed, hefting a hundred-jin basket of the gleaming black fuel with his newly compacted <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> arm. "Not a single kiln burned to ash, and not a single piece of half-baked red-brand! With this fuel, Old Tie's furnace will run white-hot in half the time!"</p>
<p>Loading the three thousand six hundred jin of charcoal onto four heavy timber rock-sleds they had built during the week, the five of them harnessed themselves to the braided rawhide tow-ropes.</p>
<p>On the way out to Bitter-Pine Ravine seven days ago, hauling empty sleds through the knee-deep snow under tenfold gravity had made the hunters sweat.</p>
<p>On the return march, Lu Chen hitched himself alone to the heaviest double-sled loaded with <strong>one thousand six hundred jin</strong> of charcoal, ten captured bandit sabers, and twelve iron bucklers!</p>
<p>With his muscles now compacted to <strong>Complete Tier Two of the Mortal-Shedding Stage (2,500 jin baseline force)</strong>, Lu Chen leaned forward forty-five degrees into the rawhide chest-harness, his eighty-jin boar-spear strapped across his back, and drove his boots through the two-foot snowdrifts like an iron snowplow!</p>
<p>Every stride of his compacted thighs and calves crushed the frozen snow-crust into flat ice-tracks so that Uncle Shi, Big-Tall Zhao, Cross-Eyed Liu, and young Shi Tou pulling the lighter sleds behind him could walk on packed ground.</p>
<p>When the five-man column marched back through the northern gate of Ash-Brook Village that afternoon with thirty-six hundred jin of Black-Gold Charcoal, twelve bandit shields, and the news that <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> second-in-command <strong>Blood-Axe Scar-Six</strong> had been slain in a head-on duel by Lu Chen, the entire village erupted for the second time that month.</p>
<p>Standing by the smithy hearth—where his broken right collarbone had knitted solidly enough after twenty days in Granny Liu's bone-mud splints that he had just shed the outer sling that morning—<strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> looked at the nine taels of silver Uncle Shi placed on the anvil beside the twenty-five taels from the eastern hunt.</p>
<p>"Thirty-four taels of silver in the pouch, and sixty-two bars of Cold-Slag Iron already on the rack," Old Tie Shan said, his rough voice steady and fierce as he picked up his twelve-pound guide-hammer with his healed right hand. "Yet we all know how <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> and <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong> operate. If we walk into Black-Rock Town carrying thirty-four taels of loose silver, Zhao Kuan will invent a new tax on the spot and take both the silver and the iron. We finish forging all <strong>eighty bars (800 jin) of Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron</strong> to throw directly onto the Bailiff Tally-House scales in front of the public clerks—and we keep the thirty-four taels of silver hidden to buy <strong>Clear-Eye Frost-Grass</strong> for little Xiao Ya, winter grain for the village, and the release seal for my boy Tie Niu!"</p>
