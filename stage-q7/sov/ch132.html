Guided by the recommendation seal of Master Weaver Shen Qinghe of Loom-Star City, Lu Chen and his companions arrived at the Grand Nine-Kiln Pavilion perched atop the central caldera rim.

Inside the pavilion's main hall, a squat, bare-chested old man with skin baked as red-brown as unglazed terracotta and an aura at the Extreme Xiantian Realm—Chief Kiln-Lord Tao Zhen—was pacing anxiously in front of a massive, thirty-foot-tall bronze-and-celadon kiln door (Kiln #2), arguing with a cold-faced, purple-robed middle-aged man whose chest bore the emblem of the Raging-Flame Sect (one of the rival Fourth-Grade Xiantian Sects from the southern border, now backed by a Third-Grade Overlord Sect from the west: the Crimson-Furnace Sect!).

"Kiln-Lord Tao, the annual Nine-Kiln Sacred Fire Festival opens in three days!" the purple-robed envoy (Elder Huo of the Crimson-Furnace Sect, accompanied by his proud twenty-three-year-old Late Xiantian disciple Yan Chi) said arrogantly. "Because the subterranean Celadon-Jade Fire Conduit beneath Kiln #1 cracked last month during an earth-tremor, your Nine-Kiln Guild can't even open Kiln #1 without causing a magma blowout! Hand over the management rights of Kiln #1 and the Ten-Thousand-Year Earth-Core Fire Lotus inside it to my Crimson-Furnace Sect, and my disciple Yan Chi will use our sect's Brute-Flame Sealing Method to force the kiln open!"

"Your Brute-Flame Sealing Method uses explosive fire-runes to blast the crack wider—it'll let you snatch the Earth-Core Fire Lotus once, while permanently destroying the Sunken Bronze Dynasty Glaze-Conduit that has sustained our Nine-Kiln City for two thousand years!" Kiln-Lord Tao roared angrily, his beard bristling.

"Without a Rank 4 Inscription Grandmaster who also understands Ancient High-Temperature Glaze-Vitrification & Micro-Fracture Restoration, nobody in the Central-South Prefecture can repair a ten-thousand-degree Celadon-Jade Conduit from the inside!" Yan Chi (Late Xiantian, Rank 3 Peak Smith) sneered, folding his arms. "Do you see such a person in your city, Kiln-Lord Tao?"

"Who says there isn't?"

A calm, clear voice echoed from the entrance of the Grand Nine-Kiln Pavilion as sixteen-year-old Lu Chen stepped across the threshold alongside Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang!
