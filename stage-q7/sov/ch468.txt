<p>Before marching southward out of Howling-Wind Pass, Lu Chen and Uncle Shi made a decision that bound <strong>Mud-Well Village</strong> and <strong>Ash-Brook Village</strong> together as sworn life-and-death brothers for generations.</p>
<p>They hitched two of the three captured <strong>Scale-Hoofed Crag-Mules</strong> to Han Wu's heavy supply-sled, loaded all <strong>forty sacks of recovered winter millet</strong>, all <strong>three hundred jin of Mud-Well's stolen tax-iron</strong>, and <strong>ten taels of the captured silver</strong> onto the sled, and handed the reins to <strong>Hunter Feng</strong> and his two Mud-Well comrades, accompanied by Ash-Brook's <strong>Old Ma</strong> and two militia spearmen to escort them safely back up the pass.</p>
<p>"Take the grain back to the ridge caves today so your forty women and children don't starve in the blizzard," Uncle Shi told Hunter Feng, gripping the wounded hunter's good right forearm. "And leave your three hundred jin of tax-iron on our second sledge—we'll deliver Mud-Well's winter iron quota alongside Ash-Brook's at the Tally-House tomorrow morning so Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan has zero excuse to issue a forfeiture warrant against your village!"</p>
<p>Forty-year-old Hunter Feng dropped straight to both knees in the bloody snow of the pass, knocking his forehead three times against the ice before Lu Chen and Uncle Shi.</p>
<p>"From this winter until our bones turn to dust," Hunter Feng choked out, "if Ash-Brook Village or Big Brother Lu Chen ever gives the word, every man and boy in Mud-Well Village will carry a spear behind your saddle!"</p>
<p>Watching the relief sled head north with the grain while the remaining eight men of Ash-Brook—now pulling their two iron-clad sledges with the help of the third armored <strong>Scale-Hoofed Crag-Mule</strong>—marched southward out of the gorge, Lu Chen felt a deep, quiet certainty in his chest.</p>
<p>In the lower realms, a sovereign could rule a continent alone with a single flying sword. Here in the brutal, high-gravity reality of the <strong>Great Desolate Origin Universe</strong>, where the <strong>Heaven-Shackling Bailiff Court</strong> squeezed every drop of marrow from the bottom up, genuine strength was built like nine-folded iron—layer by layer, brother by brother, root by root.</p>
<p>By late afternoon on the second day of the twelfth month, as the Great Solstice Blizzard finally blew itself out into a cold, clear dusk, the eight-man caravan emerged from the southern jaws of Howling-Wind Pass onto the bleak <strong>Black-Rock Alluvial Plain</strong>.</p>
<p>Five miles across the frozen, wind-scoured tundra stood <strong>Black-Rock Frontier Town</strong>.</p>
<p>Even as the smallest county seat at the outermost edge of <strong>Grey-Wall Prefecture</strong>, the town radiated a harsh, oppressive industrial might completely different from the mortal cities of Lu Chen's old universe.</p>
<p>Ringed by thirty-foot-high walls constructed from vitrified black slag-blocks six feet thick—designed to withstand the charges of <strong>Tier-Four and Desolate Pulse Beast Tides</strong>—the town bristled with heavy iron ballista towers at every fifty paces. Above the eastern quarter rose a thick pall of yellow-black coal smoke from the Garrison Smelting Foundries, and three miles further east, where the plain dropped into a jagged, mile-wide chasm in the earth that glowed dull red against the twilight clouds, lay the infamous <strong>Black-Slag Canyon</strong>—and <strong>Deep-Shaft Pit #7</strong>.</p>
<p>"Look at the town gate," Uncle Shi murmured quietly as they halted the Crag-Mule fifty yards outside the southern queue to check their sledge lashings. "Ten garrison spearmen in full iron lamellar armor—every single one at <strong>Tier Two (Iron-Flesh)</strong>, led by a sergeant at <strong>Late Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew)</strong>! Keep your heads steady, boys. Tonight we rest at Old Carter Meng's stable-yard in the outer shanties, and tomorrow at the first drum, we roll our eight hundred jin of iron straight up to the <strong>Bailiff Tally-House</strong>!"</p>
