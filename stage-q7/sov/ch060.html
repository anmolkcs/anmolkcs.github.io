With a mighty beat of its sixty-foot azure wings, the Peak Third-Order Cloud Eagle lifted the bronze-and-jade chariot into the morning clouds, carrying Elder Ling, Palace Master Qin, and the Azure Sky Kingdom’s ten selected disciples—led by Lu Chen, Murong Zi, and Su Wan’er—northward at two hundred miles an hour!

Standing at the railing of the flying chariot as rivers, cities, and mountain ranges shrank into tiny threads thousands of feet below, sixteen-year-old Lu Chen felt the vast wind of the Sky Spill Continent whip against his black martial robe.

Beside him stood Su Wan’er in her cyan dress and Murong Zi with his silver sword—both of whom now regarded Lu Chen not just as their #1 ranked peer, but as a trusted brother-in-arms who had shielded them from the Earth-Fire Tide inside the Profound Gravity Cave.

Four hours later, as the afternoon sun slanted across a colossal, hundred-mile-wide rift in the earth where an ancient meteor shower had struck ten thousand years ago, the flying chariot descended toward the towering black stone plaza of Sky-Fall Canyon.

Already parked around the canyon plaza were four other giant flying beasts belonging to the Great Yue Kingdom, Black-Wind Kingdom, Crimson Sand Kingdom, and Iron-Peak Kingdom—and standing at the front of their respective formations were fifty of the most dazzling young geniuses under age十九 (nineteen) across five kingdoms!

As Lu Chen stepped off the Azure Sky chariot with the 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear strapped across his back, dozens of sharp, probing gazes—including the cold, mocking eyes of the Black-Wind Kingdom’s Early Houtian prodigy Yan Luo and the battle-hungry gaze of Great Yue’s Middle Houtian霸主 (overlord) Chu Kuang—converged onto his figure at once.

The Five-Kingdom Core Disciple Trial had arrived.
