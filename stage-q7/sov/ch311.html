Thirty thousand miles north of Myriad-Loom Star-City, the three golden-violet star-suns of the Outer Star-Continent dimmed behind a perpetual aurora of silver-white Frozen Time-Clouds.

Cruising through the upper stratosphere aboard the two-mile-long Grade 6 Saint-Vessel: The Primordial Star-Ark (which Lu Chen had upgraded in Myriad-Loom Star-City with Rank 7 Saint Stealth & Time-Current Runes), the entire family and core companions gathered on the heated jade-glass observation deck.

In the center of the deck, Meng Wan and Lu Shan—both radiating the vigorous, 10,000-year-lifespan aura of the Late Divine Sea Realm—had set out a steaming brass chafing dish of Seventh-Order Star-Sturgeon & Millennium Snow-Bamboo Hotpot, accompanied by warm cups of Astral World-Tree Leaf Tea brewed with water from Warm-Spring Lake inside Lu Chen's 600-mile Inner World.

"Qingxue, child, eat more of this star-sturgeon belly," Meng Wan smiled with maternal warmth as she placed a ladle of tender, glowing fish fillets into Su Qingxue's porcelain bowl, and another into Su Wan'er's bowl beside her. "Even though you're an upper-realm fairy who has lived across epochs in that ice lotus, your physical body was just reborn from the Three Saint Lotuses a month ago. In Aunt Meng's kitchen, nobody is allowed to go into battle on an empty stomach!"

Holding the warm porcelain bowl in both hands, Su Qingxue—who had spent three thousand years in lonely, freezing hibernation after watching her clan fall—felt a soft, genuine warmth mist her starry eyes.

"Thank you, Aunt Meng, Uncle Lu," Su Qingxue said gently, tasting the rich, savory broth that carried a trace of Lu Chen's inscribed essence-locking warmth. "For three thousand years inside the Prism, whenever I sensed the fragrance of your cooking in Cloudstride City and on Broken Spear Peak, I wished I could sit at this table with all of you. It tastes even warmer than I imagined."

"Hahaha! From now on, whether we're in the Outer Star-Continent or at the top of the Upper Realm, this table travels right inside Chen'er's Inner World!" Lu Shan laughed heartily alongside Elder Tie, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang.

After dinner, as The Primordial Star-Ark descended through the silver aurora clouds into a vast, ten-thousand-mile basin of suspended, motionless glaciers and floating silver-jade ruins, Su Qingxue stood beside Lu Chen at the prow railing.

Below them lay the Chasm of Eternal Yesterday—the ancestral mountain range of the Primordial Chrono-Yin Clan.
