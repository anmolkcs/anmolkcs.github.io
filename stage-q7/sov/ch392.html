Once Warm-Spring Valley #1 on the Chrono-Yin Mother-Star was complete, Lu Chen did not forget the lower realm where his journey had begun three years ago.

Accompanied by his parents Empyrean Lu Shan and Empyrean Meng Wan, Su Wan'er, Su Qingxue, Elder Tie, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang, Lu Chen descended to the Southern-Western Star-Gate and used his 1,080,000-jin Grade 7 Primordial Epoch-Sovereign Spear to carve a permanent, two-way Rank 9 Empyrean Realm-Bridge connecting the Upper Star-Soul Realm directly to Mount Kunwu and Broken Spear Peak in the Sky Spill Continent below!

Not only did Lu Chen infuse a river of Upper-Realm Origin Qi into the Sky Spill Continent's spirit veins—permanently lifting the ancient cultivation ceiling of his home continent so lower-realm cultivators could ascend peacefully without spatial storms—he and his parents flew all the way back to Broken Spear Peak, the Azure Sky Royal Capital, and Cloudstride City!

In Cloudstride City, when sixty-year-old Master Liu (who was now the prosperous, respected Master of the Cloudstride Butcher & Martial Guild at the Houtian Realm!) and Appraiser Qin looked up at the sky and saw Lu Chen, Lu Shan, and Meng Wan descend from a golden Empyrean cloud-chariot to invite them to the Upper Realm for Lu Chen's nineteenth birthday feast, Master Liu dropped his brass pipe in shock before bursting into hearty tears of laughter:

"Little Chen! Three years ago in the third kitchen yard of the Crimson Scale Pavilion, this old man told you not to nick the marrow-bone of a Black-Spined Boar—and today you've become the Supreme Sovereign of the Upper Heavens, yet you still came back to invite an old butcher to drink wine in the immortal realm!"

Bringing Master Liu, Appraiser Qin, Grandmaster Gu, Sect Master Tian Shu, Eldest Senior Brother Fang Shi, Senior Sister Lin Ying, Grand Marshal Su, Granny Liu, and Master Mo Ban aboard the Primordial Star-Ark, Lu Chen welcomed all his old friends and elders up through the Realm-Bridge to Warm-Spring Valley #1 in the Upper Star-Soul Realm!
