With his parents safely established as Early Xiantian masters with five-hundred-year lifespans on Broken Spear Peak, seventeen-year-old Lu Chen had zero lingering worries holding back his own advancement.

In the Sky Spill Continent, when an Extreme Xiantian powerhouse condensed their liquid Origin Qi into a Revolving Core (Ning Dan), the quality of the core was divided into four historic grades based on the number of core-revolutions and crystalline facets: 1. Ordinary White Core (1–3 facets): Condenses 1x baseline Revolving Core power. 2. Earth-Grade Crimson Core (4–6 facets, like Elder Tie's): Condenses 2x power. 3. Heaven-Grade Gold Core (7–9 facets, achieved only by the #1 prodigies of Third-Grade Overlord Sects!): Condenses 4x power. 4. And recorded only in the Seventh Technique Bubble's 《Chaotic Star Core Art》: the supreme, mythical Twelve-Faceted Flawless Star-Crystal Core—which required compressing a 200-yard Spiritual Sea, Flawless Golden-Jade Bones, and Three Complete Elementary Dao Concepts through Twelve Cosmic Revolutions, unleashing ten times the combat power of an ordinary Revolving Core master and laying the indispensable foundation for opening an Embryonic Inner World in the Upper Realms!

On the first day of the winter solstice, Lu Chen stepped onto the highest peak of the Seven Star Valley Sect—the Ten-Thousand-Foot Dubhe Star Altar—with the remaining half of the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade, the Fifth-Order Frost-Star Phoenix Core, and five hundred Middle-Grade True Essence Stones.
