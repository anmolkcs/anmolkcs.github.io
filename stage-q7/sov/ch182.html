While Elder Yin-Sha of the Nether-Blood Cult secretly dispatched his Peak Revolving Core disciple Xue Mingyuan and twelve elite Revolving Core cultists into the Astral Storm-Trench ahead of time—plotting to ambush Lu Chen at the bottom of the trench where Elder Cang Feng couldn't intervene—Lu Chen spent the evening before his departure in Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 on Broken Spear Peak with his parents.

Under the golden osmanthus tree in the courtyard, Meng Wan had set out a steaming hotpot of Fourth-Order Snow-Carp & Spirit-Mushroom Broth, while Lu Shan poured cups of aged Hundred-Flower Spirit Wine.

"Drink an extra bowl of broth before you head to that Astral Storm-Trench tomorrow, Chen'er," Meng Wan smiled fondly, piling tender white snow-carp fillets into Lu Chen's bowl and then into Su Wan'er's bowl beside him. "Your father and I heard from Elder Tie that the winds down in that trench are cold enough to freeze iron. Even if you're a Grand Elder with a Star-Crystal Core now, in your mother's eyes you still need a warm stomach before a long trip!"

"Mother is right—this snow-carp broth is warmer than any Rank 4 spirit pill," Lu Chen laughed warmly, clinking his teacup with his father's wine cup.

After dinner, inside the courtyard's quiet cultivation chamber, Lu Chen took out a jade box containing two glowing, golden-violet pills etched with 1,080 Rank 4 Nine-Star Pill Runes:

He had spent three days refining the second Six-Leaf Golden Core Lotus (harvested from the Buried Clockwork Dynasty) into two 350%-potency, zero-toxin Nine-Star Lotus Spirit-Sea Pills specifically tailored for his parents' meridians!

Over the next six hours, as Lu Chen gently guided the warm, boundless lotus energy through his parents' Upper Dantian Spiritual Seas:

HUM—BOOM!

Both Lu Shan and Meng Wan's Spiritual Seas expanded from thirty yards straight to sixty yards across, their liquid Xiantian Origin Qi condensing into shimmering golden-jade clouds as both of them broke through smoothly from the Middle Xiantian Realm into the Late Xiantian Realm!

"Late Xiantian already!" Lu Shan clenched his fist joyfully, feeling 120,000 jin of pure Golden-Jade Xiantian strength coursing through his limbs while Meng Wan's complexion glowed with the timeless youth of a twenty-year-old fairy. "At this rate, by the time you come back from the Astral Storm-Trench in ten days for your eighteenth birthday, your mother and I will be ready for the Peak of Xiantian and the Revolving Core Realm!"

"Ten days from now—on my eighteenth birthday—I'll be right back in this courtyard to help both Father and Mother condense your Golden Cores," Lu Chen promised with a warm smile, embracing his parents at dawn before setting out with Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang toward the Astral Storm-Trench!
