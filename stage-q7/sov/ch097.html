One month later, the triennial Southern Sky-Spill Seven-Sect Martial Conference opened at Sky-Spill Plaza on the neutral Cloud-Peak Plain—where the seven great Fourth-Grade Xiantian Sects of the southern continent gathered to rank the Southern Continent Heavenly Dragon Roll and allocate the ownership of three newly discovered Middle-Grade True Essence Stone Mines!

Sitting on the high Xiantian viewing platforms were the Sect Masters and Revolving Core Grand Elders of all seven sects, while down on the white-jade square stood the top Houtian prodigies of the southern region.

At the very front of the Shadow-Moon Sect’s formation stood a twenty-two-year-old youth in pitch-black robes holding a curved shadow-saber—Mo Wuji, ranked #1 on the Southern Continent Heavenly Dragon Roll, who had reached the Absolute Peak of the Houtian Realm (Half-Step Xiantian) with 50% of his True Essence already converted into Half-Step Xiantian Origin Qi, and who had comprehended the Elementary Concept of Shadow-Blade!

In the first two rounds of the conference, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, and Fang Shi won three consecutive matches for the Seven Star Valley Sect—before Mo Wuji stepped onto the central hundred-yard Sky-Spill Arena, defeated two Peak Houtian geniuses of the Raging-Flame Sect in a single slash each, and pointed his black shadow-saber straight at Lu Chen:

“Sixteen-year-old Lu Chen of Broken Spear Peak!” Mo Wuji’s icy voice echoed across the plain. “You ruined my sect’s array in the Fallen Star Secret Realm and defeated my two Half-Step Xiantian deacons in the Azure Sky Kingdom. Step onto the stage—today, in front of all seven sects, let’s settle the ownership of the three True Essence Stone Mines and the #1 spot on the Southern Continent Heavenly Dragon Roll in a single match!”
