That evening, with four days remaining before Wang Yigao’s ten-day ultimatum expired, Lu Chen headed straight from the Crimson Scale Pavilion to the Southgate Martial Bazaar—the busiest street in Cloudstride City where rogue martial artists, hunters, and apprentice alchemists traded materials.

Tucked inside his sleeve were the four packets of remaining Crimson Ironwood Bark (which he sold back to a street stall for one tael and two mace of silver) plus the eighty coppers he had earned today, giving him a total working capital of strictly two taels of silver.

In a formal store like the Sky Treasure Pavilion, a single pre-mixed jar of Rank 1 Inscription Spirit Ink cost fifteen taels of silver—seven times everything Lu Chen owned.

Fortunately, the Prism’s foundational Inscription compendium contained dozens of dirt-cheap substitute formulas using raw, unprocessed materials that ordinary Sky Spill Continent inscription masters didn’t know how to refine!

Weaving through the lantern-lit stalls at the back of the bazaar, Lu Chen stopped at three different scrap-material booths and spent his two taels down to the last copper coin: - One pound of raw, uncrushed Purple-Vein Cinnabar Ore (forty coppers—cheap because ordinary craftsmen couldn’t separate its toxic lead slag without ruining the spirit conductivity). - Three vials of fresh First-Order Flame-Crow Blood and a small lump of Silver-Leaf Amber Resin (ninety coppers). - One worn brush made from the tail-hairs of an Iron-Whiskered Weasel and thirty sheets of yellow Blank Talisman Paper used by beginner apprentices for dry-run practice (seventy coppers).

Returning to his small shed after midnight, Lu Chen placed an earthenware mortar between his knees and began preparing his own Rank 1 Flame-Edge Inscription Ink.

Using the spiral vibration of the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath in his fingers, he crushed the Purple-Vein Cinnabar Ore into powder finer than flour, then poured in hot water at exactly seventy degrees and tapped the rim of the mortar forty-nine times in a rhythmic cadence from the Inscription compendium.

Thrum, thrum, thrum.

Under the precise harmonic vibration, the heavy grey lead slag sank cleanly to the bottom of the bowl, while pure, shimmering vermilion cinnabar floated to the top! Skimming the pure cinnabar and blending it with three drops of Flame-Crow blood and melted Silver-Leaf Amber Resin over a tiny tallow flame, Lu Chen produced half a saucer of thick, glowing crimson liquid that smelled faintly of pine and ozone.

“Enough ink for roughly fifteen attempts on talisman paper, and one final inscription on steel if I succeed,” Lu Chen murmured, setting a sheet of yellow talisman paper flat on his wooden table.

He chose the most practical and sought-after combat symbol among Rank 1 diagrams: the Rank 1 Flame-Edge Inscription.

Once engraved onto a blade or spearhead, the Flame-Edge Inscription increased the weapon’s armor-piercing sharpness and fire-attribute True Essence amplification by 30% to 35%. Even a mortal warrior in the Body Transformation stages who hadn’t condensed True Essence could trigger a scorching crimson blade-glow simply by infusing fierce blood-qi into the hilt!

However, completing a single Rank 1 Flame-Edge Inscription required drawing one hundred and eight continuous micro-runes in a single breath without lifting the brush, while simultaneously injecting a steady, unwavering thread of Soul Force into every stroke to bind the volatile fire energy.

Lu Chen dipped the Iron-Whiskered Weasel brush into the crimson ink, entered Prism Stillness, and pressed the tip onto the first sheet of talisman paper.

Sss—

Stroke one. Stroke ten. Stroke twenty-four.

The crimson lines glowed faintly on the yellow paper as Lu Chen’s soul force guided the Flame-Crow blood.

At the thirty-seventh stroke, where the rune pattern required a sharp hair-pin reversal to lock in the fire node, Lu Chen’s soul force fluctuated by less than a twentieth of a breath.

Poof!

A dull red spark snapped across the paper, and the entire sheet curled into black ash in half a second, sending a sharp sting rebounding into Lu Chen’s eyebrows.

“Failed on stroke thirty-seven. My soul force output spiked by two percent when turning the brush corner, causing the Flame-Crow essence to clash with the amber resin.”

Under Prism Stillness, Lu Chen didn’t waste a single second feeling frustrated. Every failure was recorded with microscopic precision inside his mind.

He laid down the second sheet of talisman paper.

Poof! Failed at stroke fifty-two.

Third sheet: Poof! Failed at stroke sixty-eight.

Seventh sheet: Failed at stroke eighty-one.

Drawing runes with soul force was ten times more mentally taxing than swinging a thirty-two-jin boning knife for ten hours. By the tenth sheet of talisman paper, Lu Chen’s face was paper-white, cold sweat dripped from his chin onto his collar, and his eyes were webbed with red blood vessels as a leaden exhaustion weighed on his temples.

Normally, an apprentice Inscription Master in the Sky Spill Continent could only attempt three or four inscriptions a night before their spiritual sea grew too turbulent to hold a brush.

Yet every time Lu Chen’s soul force neared depletion and his hand began to tremble, he pressed his consciousness against the first facet of the Chrono-Soul Prism.

While the Prism did not generate free soul force out of thin air, its icy, timeless aura acted like a cold whetstone, instantly calming the chaotic ripples in his spiritual sea so he could squeeze out every last drop of his latent mental potential. More importantly, each time his spiritual sea drained to the bottom and slowly recovered during brief five-minute meditations, his soul force became noticeably denser and tougher!

Sheet eleven: Failed at stroke ninety-four.

Sheet thirteen: Failed at stroke one hundred and three—just five strokes short of completion!

Outside the window, the dark sky was beginning to turn a pale, frosty gray before the fifth watch. In the saucer, only enough crimson ink remained for two more brush dips.

Lu Chen closed his eyes for ten minutes, breathing in the cold morning air until his heartbeat slowed to forty beats a minute.

When he opened his eyes again, he picked up the brush, dipped it into the saucer, and touched the fourteenth sheet of talisman paper.

His right hand did not move slowly anymore; it flowed with the same rhythmic, unstoppable cadence he used when deboning a spirit beast’s spine.

Stroke thirty. Stroke六十 (sixty). Stroke ninety.

At stroke one hundred and three—where he had failed twenty minutes ago—Lu Chen’s wrist gave a tiny, effortless spiral twist, bleeding off the excess fire surge into the secondary loop of the rune.

One hundred and six. One hundred and seven.

One hundred and eight—Seal!

Lu Chen lifted the brush tip squarely off the center of the diagram.

Hum—!

Instead of bursting into ash, the one hundred and eight intricate crimson lines on the yellow talisman paper flashed with a brilliant ruby light, contracted inward until they formed a thumb-sized, lifelike mark of a burning lotus-blade, and sank seamlessly into the fibers of the paper!

Even without touching it, Lu Chen could feel a wave of sharp, scorching heat radiating three inches above the paper’s surface.

“A completed Rank 1 Flame-Edge Inscription Talisman—and because the lead slag was completely purged from the cinnabar via the harmonic purification method, its amplification reaches a full 35%!”

Lu Chen let out a long breath of white mist, a genuine smile finally tugging at the corner of his exhausted lips.

With the last brush-dip of crimson ink still in the saucer, he pulled out the rusted, chipped Grade 1 Human-Step Iron Sword that had belonged to his father during his youth as a town guard, polished the flat of the blade above the hilt until it shone, and before heading to work, engraved the final Flame-Edge Inscription directly into the cold steel.
