<p>Returning from the <strong>Abyssal Chrono-Rift</strong> to <strong>Warm-Spring Immortal Manor</strong> on <strong>Star-Fall Peak</strong> that evening, the entire courtyard was filled with joy, lantern light, and the rich aroma of <strong>Meng Wan's</strong> cooking!</p>
<p>For three years, Lu Chen had told his father <strong>Lu Shan</strong> and mother <strong>Meng Wan</strong> about the benefactor <strong>Su Qingxue</strong> whose ancestral Prism had saved his life and shielded his spiritual sea in Thunder-Scar Gorge and on the Star-Burial Glacier.</p>
<p>Now, seeing the peerless, gentle young woman in snow-white robes walk through the courtyard gate in a living body alongside Lu Chen and <strong>Su Wan'er</strong>, <strong>Meng Wan</strong> and <strong>Lu Shan</strong> hurried forward with beaming, hospitable smiles!</p>
<p>"Benefactor Su! Come in, come sit at the head of the table!" Meng Wan warmly took Su Qingxue's hand—not intimidated in the slightest by her Divine Sea cultivation, treating her with the genuine warmth of a grateful mother welcoming family—and placed a steaming bowl of <strong>Ten-Thousand-Year Bamboo-Shoot &amp; Fifth-Order Snow-Carp Soup</strong> and handmade <strong>Crystal Dumplings</strong> in front of her. "Our Chen'er talked about how you protected him when he was still a fifteen-year-old boy in the mountains—today you finally have your own body back, so you must eat three bowls of Auntie's soup!"</p>
<p>Sitting under the warm lantern light of Warm-Spring Immortal Manor, surrounded by the unpretentious, loving laughter of <strong>Lu Shan</strong>, <strong>Meng Wan</strong>, <strong>Lu Chen</strong>, <strong>Su Wan'er</strong>, <strong>Murong Zi</strong>, <strong>Chu Kuang</strong>, and <strong>Elder Tie</strong>, <strong>Su Qingxue</strong>—who had spent her entire previous life in the cold, cutthroat palaces of the Upper Star-Soul Realm and ten thousand years in solitary hibernation—tasted the warm soup and felt a soft warmth mist her starry eyes.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Uncle Lu, Aunt Meng," Su Qingxue smiled gently. "This is the warmest meal I have had in ten thousand years."</p>
