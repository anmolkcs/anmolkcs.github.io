<p>With the Shadow-Sovereign excavation fleet annihilated in less than ten breaths, Lu Chen and Su Qingxue descended to the silver-jade plaza at the center of the <strong>Chasm of Eternal Yesterday</strong>.</p>
<p>Before them stood the entrance to the <strong>Ancestral Subterranean Time-Vault</strong>, sealed inside a shimmering silver dome where a single second of time—the final second before the vault doors closed three thousand years ago—repeated endlessly in a closed loop!</p>
<p>At the center of that time-loop hung the Chrono-Yin Clan's sacred guardian artifact: a hundred-foot-tall <strong>Seventh-Order Peak Saint-Artifact: The Ancestral Chrono-Yin Bell</strong>, which had been struck by an Empyrean's palm three millennia ago, leaving <strong>twelve jagged spatial-time fractures</strong> across its silver bronze wall and missing the shard that Old Guild Master Shen Cang had kept in Myriad-Loom Star-City!</p>
<p>"Because the bell cracked while anchoring the time-loop, anyone who steps inside without repairing the twelve spatial-time fractures in perfect synchronization with the bell's one-second oscillation will have their body torn between yesterday and today," Su Qingxue explained softly, holding out the <strong>Silver Time-Bell Shard</strong> from Myriad-Loom Star-City.</p>
<p>Lu Chen smiled calmly, his antiquities-restorer's eyes shining with absolute focus under <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>:</p>
<p>"On Earth, restoring a fractured pendulum clock escapement while the balance wheel is still oscillating requires matching the exact phase angle of the swing. Here, the bell oscillates once every second—which means under <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, I have ten thousand micro-beats within that single second to solder the shard and bridge all twelve fractures!"</p>
