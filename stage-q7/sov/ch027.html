As Lu Chen and Lin Xuan stepped onto the thirty-yard white-jade central arena from opposite sides, the noise of two thousand spectators hushed into tense anticipation.

“Lu Chen,” Lin Xuan said softly, drawing his three-foot-six-inch Grade 2 Upper-Tier Frost-Moon Sword from its silver scabbard—sending a chill across the flagstones as blue frost-runes gleamed along the blade. “When you defeated Zhu Hong ten days ago, you relied on his clumsy broadsaber colliding head-on with your heavy spear. Did you think I would make the same mistake? Today, I have reached the Middle Fourth Stage, holding a Grade 2 Upper-Tier sword and a Grade 2 Martial Skill. I won’t touch the shaft of your clumsy 820-jin iron pole once—I’ll sever the tendons of your wrists and ankles before your spear can even turn around!”

Lu Chen unwrapped the oilcloth from his 820-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Spear and tossed the cloth calmly to the corner of the stage.

“Talk less,” Lu Chen said, holding the black spear level with one hand. “Show me your sword.”

“Court death!”

The instant the referee deacon’s hand dropped, Lin Xuan’s figure blurred into three afterimages across the white-jade flagstones!

《Grade 2 Martial Skill: Flowing Frost Seven Swords — First Sword: Frost Over the Winding River!》

Instead of attacking from the front, Lin Xuan’s Grade 2 footwork carried him in an S-shaped arc to Lu Chen’s left flank at lightning speed, and the Frost-Moon Sword flicked out seven cold blue sword-sparks aimed simultaneously at Lu Chen’s left knee, hip tendon, elbow, and wrist!

“So fast! That’s a true Grade 2 Sword Art combined with Middle Fourth Stage speed!” disciples in the stands cried out. Against an agile sword master who refused to clash blades and attacked only joints from the flank, a heavy 820-jin spear was universally considered too slow to recover between thrusts!

Yet in Lu Chen’s eyes—refrigerated to absolute zero by Prism Stillness—every shift of Lin Xuan’s ankle and every angle of the seven blue sword-sparks was mapped out three tenths of a second before the blade arrived.

Even more shockingly, people assumed an 820-jin spear was slow only when wielded by someone with 3,000 jin of strength.

In the hands of Lu Chen—whose Dragon-Sinew Tendons possessed 6,200 jin of baseline strength—an 820-jin spear moved as lightly as a bamboo cane!

Twang!

Lu Chen didn’t even turn his feet. His right wrist gave a two-inch spiral flick on the butt of the spear shaft—

《Azure Army Foundational Heavy Spear — Tremor-Shaft Flick!》

嗡—CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!

The eight-foot black iron shaft vibrated at high frequency, whipping out a blurred fan of seven dark-iron afterimages that struck every single one of Lin Xuan’s seven blue sword-sparks squarely on the side flat of the Frost-Moon Sword!

“What?! His 820-jin spear is moving faster than my Frost-Moon Sword?!”

Lin Xuan felt a terrifying, mountain-like tremor surge through his sword hilt, numbing his right palm down to the bone and sending him stumbling three steps sideways!

Before Lin Xuan could steady his footing, Lu Chen took a single step forward.

The white-jade flagstone beneath Lu Chen’s boot exploded into powder, and his figure closed the ten-foot gap in half a breath, the 820-jin Black Iron Spear tearing the air like a black meteor—

《Level Thrust — Nine-Layered Wave Force: Fifth Wave!》
