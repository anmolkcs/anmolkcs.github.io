<p>For three hundred and nineteen autumns after the first osmanthus blossoms crowned the stone steps of Warm-Spring Valley, the Star-Soul Universe knew a golden age of unbroken peace.</p>
<p>Beneath the ancient spirit-peach trees of the upper courtyard, Lu Shan still began every dawn in a low horse stance, his booming laughter echoing across the sea of clouds whenever his spear-tip shook the morning dew from the branches. Inside the kitchen pavilion, Meng Wan refused to let any immortal attendant touch her hearth; she still rolled out dough with her own hands, slicing single-strand golden-wheat longevity noodles into boiling broth while scolding her husband for tracking mountain mud onto the bluestone tiles. Across the silver-lotus bridge, Su Qingxue and Su Wan'er tuned their zither and jade chimes together in the warm breeze, while down on the seventh peak, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, and Elder Tie Cang sparred with generations of noisy young disciples who grew up believing the starry sky above their heads was boundless and eternal.</p>
<p>None of them suspected that their entire cosmos—the lower Sky-Spill Continent, the Upper Star-Soul Realm, and the hundred-million-mile starry void between—was no more than a wild grain of millet resting at the edge of an unfathomable threshing floor.</p>
<p>The calamity did not announce itself with thunder. On the eighteenth morning of the tenth autumn month, just as Meng Wan was setting seven porcelain bowls on the cedar table under the osmanthus tree, the silver-gold vault above the Primordial Wall simply turned the color of rusted coffin-iron.</p>
<p>Standing alone on the outermost watch-cliff of the cosmos, Lu Chen felt the hundred-million-mile Perfected Inner Universe within his dantian shudder as though a mountain of cold lead had dropped onto thin window glass. Before the first spatial rift even parted, the gentle laws of time, space, gravity, and star-fire that he had spent his youth mastering began to curdle and snap. Above the cosmic membrane, twelve colossal chains forged from black-slag bone and dull bronze descended through the chaos. Each link spanned the length of a mountain range, scored with jagged tally-marks that radiated a suffocating weight utterly alien to the Star-Soul Universe: the <strong>True Origin Great Dao Laws</strong> of the higher-dimensional <strong>Great Desolate Origin Universe</strong>.</p>
<p>Between the twelve chains drifted a single, pitted bronze vessel—an outer-patrol Harvester Barge belonging to the <strong>Heaven-Shackling Bailiff Court</strong>. A voice devoid of hatred or anger, carrying only the bored indifference of a clerk tallying winter firewood, echoed across the grinding void:</p>
<p>"Unregistered Minor Embryo Cosmos, Sector Nine-Four-Seven of the Outer Slag-Belt. World-Marrow grade: Lower-Third. Drop the Twelve-Pillar Blood-Drawing Stakes. Drain the mortal and immortal spirit-veins within three days to fill the autumn slag-iron tribute of Grey-Wall Frontier."</p>
<p>As the first bronze stake plunged toward the cosmic membrane, the mere pressure of its wake pulverized three outer asteroid belts into grey powder. Down in Warm-Spring Valley, the ten-thousand-year osmanthus tree groaned as its trunk split three inches deep. The porcelain bowls on the cedar table shattered simultaneously. Lu Shan and Meng Wan were forced to one knee on the flagstones as blood seeped from the corners of their lips; even Su Qingxue's Chrono-Yin Sacred Body and Chu Kuang's roaring battle-aura flickered like paper lanterns in a hurricane. What their sheltered lower cosmos called "Empyrean" and "True Divinity" were fragile embryonic imitations beside a True Origin world, where a single handful of barren mud carried ten times the gravitational density of lower-realm star-iron.</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not hesitate for half a breath. Rising into the pitch-black tear above the Primordial Wall, he gripped his 1,080,000-jin Primordial Sovereign Spear with both hands and thrust upward with every drop of World Force in his hundred-million-mile Inner Universe.</p>
<p><em>CLANG—KRAK!</em></p>
<p>The spear-tip met the descending point of the first Blood-Drawing Stake. A blinding ring of silver-gold star-fire flared across the dark, halting the colossal bronze stake by half a finger's breadth—yet in that same instant, the crushing recoil of True Origin Dao Laws surged down the shaft. Hairline fractures spiderwebbed across the Empyrean spearhead. The skin of Lu Chen's palms tore open to the white bone, and thirty-six constellations inside his dantian imploded into cold ash as the barge's automated wardens sent a second wave of grey, corrosive <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong> roaring down the chains.</p>
<p>"A wild lower-universe Sovereign?" the bored voice aboard the bronze barge murmured. "Barely the bone-density of a third-tier Mortal-Shedding ditch-digger in the Great Desolate Origin Universe, yet its world-core is clean. Pin its spine and draw the marrow alive."</p>
<p>Behind Lu Chen, through the trembling realm-gate of Warm-Spring Valley, Su Qingxue, Su Wan'er, Lu Shan, Meng Wan, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang were charging up the stone steps, ready to ignite their own souls to die beside him. Looking back once through the sleet of falling grey ash, Lu Chen saw his mother's tear-streaked face, his father's white-knuckled grip on his old iron spear, and the desperate, unwavering eyes of the two women who had waited for him across lifetimes.</p>
<p>If they crossed the barrier, the Desolate Ash-Qi would turn their lungs and souls to cinder in three breaths. And if he fought a stubborn war of attrition here, his Inner Universe would be ground to dust before noon, leaving everyone he loved to be boiled into tributary slag in the Bailiff Court's cauldrons.</p>
<p>"Father. Mother. Qingxue. Wan'er," Lu Chen whispered, using the last warm breeze of his dantian to carry his voice gently into the courtyard below while sealing the valley gate against their advance. "Don't come out. The soup on the table isn't cold yet—wait for me under the osmanthus tree. Even if I have to crawl through the mud of their heavens on broken knees, I will tear their Bailiff Court down stone by stone and come back to open this door."</p>
<p>Turning his back on Warm-Spring Valley, Lu Chen pressed his bleeding forehead against the nine-faceted Chrono-Soul Prism fused with his soul. With a silent roar that tore his vocal cords, he reversed the rotation of his hundred-million-mile Perfected Inner Universe—detonating every spirit mountain, every longevity ocean, his 1,080,000-jin spear, and all twenty-seven Technique Bubbles inside the Chrono-Soul Prism at once!</p>
<p>He did not waste that cataclysmic sacrifice against the bronze barge's hull. Instead, drawing upon two lifetimes of antiquities restoration and micro-structural precision, he wove the dying origin of the Chrono-Soul Prism and his shattered universe into an inward-folding law shell: <strong>The Nine-Epoch Inverse Chrono-Cocoon</strong>.</p>
<p>Silver-gray time-crystal instantly encased the entire Star-Soul Universe, folding its coordinates nine layers beneath the riverbed of chaos and petrifying its causal flow into an unmoving fossil. Within Warm-Spring Valley, Lu Shan's outstretched hand, Meng Wan's falling tear, and Su Qingxue's silent cry were frozen in unharmed stasis—sealed inside a cosmic tortoise-shell that no outer Harvester Barge could crack or locate for ten thousand years, yet impossible to unseal until the Heaven-Shackling Mother-Tally in the upper universe was destroyed.</p>
<p>At the exact instant the cocoon closed, the rebounding Twelve-Pillar Stakes slammed against Lu Chen's unprotected chest, tearing open a jagged <strong>Dimensional Slag-Trench</strong>. His divine marrow turned to ash; his meridians snapped like dry reeds; the Chrono-Soul Prism in his brow cracked into a dark, silent pebble of gray slate. Clutching only the rusted three-inch iron collar of his broken spear in his bleeding fist, Lu Chen was swept into the black vortex—falling alone into the bottomless mud of the Great Desolate Origin Universe.</p>
