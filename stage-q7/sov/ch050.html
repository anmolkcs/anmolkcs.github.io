Stepping past the threshold where tenfold subterranean gravity and pure crimson earth-fire origin energy surged like a warm ocean, Lu Chen walked to the deepest, highest-pressure platform at the very center of the cave—the 12x Gravity Core Altar—and planted his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear upright in the crimson stone beside him.

Sitting cross-legged on the altar with his one hundred new True Essence Stones and the remaining half-bottle of Jade-Scale Flood-Dragon Blood in front of him, Lu Chen closed his eyes and looked inward at the nine-faceted Chrono-Soul Prism floating quietly above his spiritual sea.

Down in the VIP family gallery beside the arena the previous evening, his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan had cheered until their voices went hoarse when Palace Master Qin crowned their son #1 of the Seven Star Martial House, and they had sent him off to the cave gates this morning with warm spirit-lotus porridge and proud smiles.

From a fifteen-year-old apprentice butcher holding a thirty-two-jin boning knife in Cloudstride City to the #1 Core Prodigy of the Seven Star Martial House, he had taken his first solid step across this vast martial world through his own blood, sweat, and relentless grit.

And beyond the Profound Gravity Cave lay the Sixth Stage Perfection, the Houtian Realm, the Seven Star Valley Sect, and the boundless heavens of the Sky Spill Continent.

Lu Chen inhaled a long, deep breath of scorching earth-fire origin energy, circulating the 《Chaotic Star Body Art — Gate Six: Twelve Meridians Pulse Condensation》, and a sound like twelve waking dragons roared inside his blood.
