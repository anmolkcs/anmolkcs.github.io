At dawn the next morning, fifty miles above Central Emperor City, the two-mile-long golden-purple Grade 6 Saint-Vessel—The Primordial Star-Ark—hovered beneath the newly veiled Star-Spill World Gate.

While his parents Lu Shan and Meng Wan stood on the main viewing deck of the Primordial Star-Ark alongside Elder Tie, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang (ready to step into Lu Chen's 200-Mile Inner World whenever the void-storms grew rough), eighteen-year-old Lu Chen and Su Qingxue stood side by side at the prow of the Star-Ark.

"Beyond this Star-Spill World Gate lies the Amber-Fossil Star Road—an ancient interstellar highway of petrified star-corpses and spatial storms that leads to the Outer Star-Continent of the Upper Star-Soul Realm," Su Qingxue said softly, her snow-white robes fluttering beside Lu Chen's black robe.

"In the Upper Star-Soul Realm, the Seven Sovereign Clans who ambushed me ten thousand years ago rule over thousands of star-continents, led by Great World Kings and Empyrean Ancestors whose Inner Worlds span hundreds of thousands of miles. Are you ready, Lu Chen?"

Lu Chen gripped his 108,000-jin Epoch-Sovereign Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear, feeling the warm laughter of his parents and friends on the deck behind him and the 200-Mile Inner World pulsing like a rising sun in his dantian.

"Let's go, Senior Su—it's time to see the stars of the Upper Realm!"

With a single thrust of Lu Chen's heavy spear injecting Seventh-Order World Force into the Star-Spill World Gate, the ancient bronze-and-starlight portal spanning ten miles of the stratosphere swung wide open!
