Every gaze on the harbor plaza turned toward the eighteen-year-old youth in a simple black martial robe with a nine-foot-six-inch, dark-gold dragon-bell spear strapped across his back.

When Deputy Guild Master Shen Lan looked at Lu Chen, her eyes fell upon the Restored Pre-Epoch Star-Compass and the Purple-Gold Loom-Star Medallion that Lu Chen held up in his left palm!

"That... that is the ancestral Founder's Star-Compass that our branch ancestor carried down to the lower Sky Spill Continent ten thousand years ago!" Deputy Guild Master Shen Lan gasped in shock and joy, sensing the flawless 1,440 micro-gears humming inside the compass. "Young Grandmaster, you restored our founder's Star-Compass down in the lower world and ascended through the Amber Star-Gate?!"

"Yes, Deputy Guild Master Shen," Lu Chen nodded warmly. "In the lower world, Guild Master Yun of Loom-Star City gifted me five spools of Innate Heaven-Silkworm Silk for my parents and entrusted me to pay my respects to the ancestral Artisan-Star Guild once I reached the Upper Star-Soul Realm."

Across the plaza, however, Master Dan Chen and Commander Xue Ao of the Scarlet-Sun Blood Hall let out a roar of mocking laughter!

"A lower-realm country bumpkin who just crawled out of the Amber Star-Gate?!" Master Dan Chen pointed his golden fan at Lu Chen with a sneer of utter disdain. "Kid, do you think a lower-realm Rank 5 or Rank 6 badge means anything in the Upper Star-Soul Realm? Up here, our spirit materials carry World-Origin Law! Even a single grain of Astral Petrification Rust on that Seventh-Order Bell weighs ten thousand jin of law-pressure. Touch it with your lower-realm brush, and your arm will turn into stone before you draw a single stroke!"

"Whether my arm turns to stone or your Scarlet-Sun Blood Hall rolls out of Myriad-Loom Star-City," Lu Chen said calmly, not even glancing at Dan Chen as he walked toward the central bell-tower with Deputy Guild Master Shen Lan, "you'll know within one hour."
