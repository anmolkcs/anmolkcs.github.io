Back in the Royal Palace that evening, Third Prince Zhao Heng—still bitter over Lu Chen crushing Ouyang Ming and Lin Sen and ruining his faction’s dominance in the Martial House—secretly sent a fast-horse courier across the northern border to the camp of the Black-Wind Kingdom’s #1 genius, Yan Luo, offering thirty Middle-Grade True Essence Stones if Yan Luo “accidentally” crippled Lu Chen’s spear-arm inside the Ancient Sky-Fall Battlefield.

Lu Chen, however, didn’t waste a single breath worrying about political schemes.

In his final two days before departure, he spent his mornings in Star-Peak Pavilion #9 compressing the origin energy in his twelve meridians through the First and Second Revolutions of the 《Nine-Revolution Star Cyclone》—bringing himself to the very razor’s edge of breaking into the Houtian Realm whenever he found a suitable high-energy catalyst!

In the afternoons, using the Fourth-Order Flame Drake Crystal’s remaining ink-essence, he inscribed six Rank 2 Peak Purple-Thunder & Earth-Bind Talisman Jades—giving two to Su Wan’er, two to Murong Zi, and keeping two for himself.

And on the final evening before the flying chariot’s departure, Lu Chen stayed at Clear-Brook Manor with his parents.

Under the warm lamplight of the living room, Meng Wan tied a braided red-silk cord holding a smooth, hand-polished Peace-and-Safety Peachwood Charm around Lu Chen’s wrist—and Lu Chen immediately smiled and engraved a microscopic Rank 3 Spirit-Nourishing Rune onto the peachwood charm before tying a matching pair around his mother’s and father’s wrists.

“When you’re in Sky-Fall Canyon, remember what your father told you,” Lu Shan said earnestly as he walked Lu Chen to the manor gate at dawn, his Fourth-Stage posture tall and steady beside Meng Wan. “Fight fiercely when you must, protect your fellow disciples Su Wan’er and Murong Zi, and never let pride blind your caution. When you enter the Seven Star Valley Sect, your mother and I will have reached the Fifth Stage—we’ll come visit our son on the sect mountain ourselves!”

“Wait for my good news, Father, Mother!”

Lu Chen hugged his parents warmly, waved once at the gate of Clear-Brook Manor, and strode toward the waiting Azure-Winged Cloud Eagle Chariot at the summit of Seven Star Mountain.
