SHHHK—BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Five consecutive sonic booms erupted along the dark-red spearhead of the 820-jin Black Iron Spear, compressing the air in front of the tip into a visible crimson cone of scorching fire and crushing gravity!

With 6,200 jin of baseline Dragon-Sinew strength amplified by the Fifth Wave of the Nine-Layered Wave Force and the 38% Dual Inscription, that single unadorned Level Thrust carried a terrifying 8,500 jin of concentrated penetrative power!

Facing that oncoming black spearhead, Lin Xuan felt every hair on his neck stand straight up as an icy premonition of death gripped his throat.

He couldn’t dodge—the spear-wind had locked down the entire ten-foot space around him!

“I am Lin Xuan of the Royal Lin Clan! How can I lose to a country pariah—《Flowing Frost Seven Swords — Third Sword: Frozen Waterfall Curtain》!”

Lin Xuan bit his tongue until blood sprayed across his Frost-Moon Sword, activating the Rank 1 Frost Rune engraved on his Grade 2 Upper-Tier blade and weaving a dense, spinning shield of thirty-six icy blue sword-shadows in front of his chest!

At the exact instant the black spearhead touched the outer layer of the thirty-six blue sword-shadows, Lu Chen’s dark eyes flashed, and from between his eyebrows, a microscopic, invisible spike of compressed soul force shot straight into Lin Xuan’s forehead—

《Instant Soul Ripple!》

“Ugh—!”

Even though Lu Chen used only thirty percent of the Instant Soul Ripple’s power so as not to cripple Lin Xuan’s spiritual sea permanently, it felt to Lin Xuan as though an ice pick had been driven into his temple! For one fatal fifth of a second, his mind went completely blank, and the flow of blood-qi into the final twelve sword-shadows of his Frozen Waterfall Curtain stuttered!

CRACK—SHATTER—BOOM!

The 820-jin Black Iron Spear tore through the thirty-six blue sword-shadows like a red-hot iron rod plunging through paper!

The tip of the heavy spear struck the flat of the Grade 2 Upper-Tier Frost-Moon Sword crossed over Lin Xuan’s chest. Under the five chained waves of 8,500-jin spiral force, the prized Frost-Moon Sword bent into an exaggerated bow, let out a mournful metallic wail, and snapped cleanly in half!

The blunt throat of the spearhead continued forward half an inch and tapped squarely against the center of Lin Xuan’s sternum!

PUH—!

Lin Xuan’s silk breastplate disintegrated into shreds, a spray of bright red blood burst from his mouth as his breastbone cracked, and his body was launched fifty feet backward across the white-jade arena like a stone skipped across a lake—sliding all the way off the edge of the platform and crashing unconscious at the base of the core disciples’ viewing terrace!

In the center of the arena, the broken upper half of the Grade 2 Frost-Moon Sword spun three times in the air before clattering onto the flagstones beside Lu Chen’s boot.

Two thousand outer and inner disciples around the Central Martial Arena stared in petrified silence.

Middle Fourth Stage Lin Xuan—armed with a Grade 2 Upper-Tier sword and a Grade 2 clan martial skill—had had his sword snapped in two and been blasted off the stage in two moves!

Up on the core viewing terrace, Su Wan’er let out a soft breath of amazement, while beside her, #11 Ranked Core Disciple Lin Sen slowly stood up from his armchair, his hand crushing the sandalwood armrest into sawdust as his cold, venomous gaze locked onto Lu Chen below.
