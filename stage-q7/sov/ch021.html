Inside the quiet inscription room of Courtyard #72, a single tallow lamp cast a warm golden circle over the stone workbench.

Resting on a white jade saucer in front of Lu Chen were the three Rank 1 Origin-Gathering Pills he had received as part of his Entrance Trial first-place reward.

In the Sky Spill Continent, even a low-grade Rank 1 spirit pill like the Origin-Gathering Pill cost four Low-Grade True Essence Stones on the open market. However, because mortal-realm alchemists in the Azure Sky Kingdom used crude earth-fire furnaces and incomplete sealing mud, roughly 60% of the medicinal essence escaped into the air during the final condensation of the pill, while 30% of the remaining weight consisted of charred herb dregs and erysipelas (pill toxins).

That was why even aristocratic geniuses like Lin Xuan could only swallow one Origin-Gathering Pill every five days; taking more without letting the internal organs metabolize the charred pill toxins would clog their meridians and ruin their future cultivation foundation.

“With the Nine-Fold Medicinal Lock Rune from the Second Technique Bubble’s Pill-Inscription compendium, however, I don’t just seal the leaking medicinal qi—I can use harmonic soul-force threads to draw the grey pill toxins out to the surface crust and peel them away before locking the core!”

Lu Chen dipped a fine Snow-Rabbit Hair Brush into the bowl of shimmering silver ink mixed from Moonlight Dew, Spirit-Jade Powder, and Golden Cicada Silk.

Unlike Weapon Inscription, where the rune was engraved onto hard steel that could withstand heavy soul-force pressure, Pill Inscription had to be drawn suspended half an inch in midair around a fragile, thumb-sized medicinal pill while spinning the pill with an invisible thread of soul force! If the soul force trembled by a hundredth of a fraction, the pill would crumble into useless powder on the spot.

Lu Chen sank his consciousness into the deepest state of Prism Stillness.

With his left hand, he released a gossamer thread of soul force—sharpened and condensed by absorbing two ancient soul fragments—and lifted the first Origin-Gathering Pill two inches above the jade saucer, spinning it slowly in midair.

With his right hand, the Snow-Rabbit Hair Brush danced across the empty air like a dragonfly skimming water.

Stroke one. Stroke twenty. Stroke forty-nine.

Silver-white lines of Moonlight Dew ink did not fall to the table; held aloft by Lu Chen’s precise soul force, they wove into a glowing, three-dimensional spherical cage of sixty-four tiny interlocking runes around the spinning pill!

Hum—Sss!

As the forty-ninth harmonic stroke closed around the equator of the pill, Lu Chen’s wrist gave a sharp, microscopic vibration.

Under the resonance of the silver rune cage, tiny black specks of charred herb slag and pill toxins were forced out from the core of the Origin-Gathering Pill, forming a thin grey shell on the outside that flaked off and fell into the saucer like burnt ash!

At the same instant, the remaining sixty-four silver runes contracted inward and branded a delicate, glowing silver cloud-pattern directly onto the surface of the newly purified, emerald-green pill!

Clink.

The inscribed Origin-Gathering Pill dropped back onto the jade saucer.

Previously, its medicinal aroma had been leaking constantly into the room; now, not a single trace of scent escaped its silver cloud-rune seal—every bit of origin energy was locked 100% inside the core, and its purity had soared from 40% to a terrifying 92%!

“One Origin-Gathering Pill engraved with the Essence-Locking Pill Rune now contains nearly two and a half times the usable origin energy of a standard pill—and because 95% of the pill toxins have been purged, I can take all three pills consecutively without a trace of meridian clogging!”

Over the next two hours, despite the splitting exhaustion in his temples, Lu Chen gritted his teeth and inscribed the remaining two Origin-Gathering Pills without a single failure.

Carrying the three silver-runed pills and the bowl of purified Peak Second-Order Flame Lion Blood into the meditation room, Lu Chen swallowed the first inscribed Origin-Gathering Pill and smeared a third of the golden-crimson Flame Lion Blood across his tendons and joints.

BOOM—!

The moment the silver rune dissolved in his stomach, a torrent of pure, warm heaven-and-earth origin energy erupted through his digestive tract, merging with the ferocious Yang blood-qi of the Flame Lion!

Circulating the Second Volume of the 《Chaotic Star Body Art — Dragon-Sinew Tendon Forging》, Lu Chen endured the bone-grinding pain as his five internal organs reached 100% Viscera Perfection (Peak Third Stage) on the second night, and by the dawn of the fourth day, after swallowing the third inscribed pill, the major tendons along his spine, shoulders, hips, and knees began to emit a crisp, metallic twang—twang—twang like steel bowstrings being tightened on a siege crossbow!
