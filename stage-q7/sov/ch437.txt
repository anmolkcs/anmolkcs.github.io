<p>With two thousand jin of prime boar carcasses, four heavy crossbows, four steel bear-traps, and a sixty-jin saber, there was no way four men and one wounded hunter could haul the entire load back across the Dead-Pine Boundary in a single trip under tenfold gravity.</p>
<p>Working with razor-sharp skinning knives beside the creek so the running water washed away the scent of blood, Uncle Shi, Big-Tall Zhao, and Lu Chen field-dressed both the six-hundred-pound sow and the fourteen-hundred-pound <strong>Scarred King-Boar</strong> in less than an hour.</p>
<p>From the King-Boar's chest cavity, Lu Chen carefully extracted the <strong>eight-pound Heart</strong>, the <strong>fist-sized Purple-Black Gallbladder</strong> (worth at least four taels of silver at any apothecary in Black-Rock Town for tempering Tier-Two flesh), the two fourteen-inch ivory tusks, and the four-inch-thick <strong>Iron-Bristle Shoulder Hide</strong>.</p>
<p>They hoisted two-thirds of the butchered, salted quarters—wrapped tightly in the sow's hide and smeared with pungent sulfur-mud to mask the smell from scavengers—twenty feet up into the fork of an iron-pine tree using rawhide pulleys, and hauled the remaining six hundred jin of prime loin, ribs, marrow-bones, and the King-Boar's heart back up to the basalt overhang where <strong>Old Ma</strong> and <strong>Cross-Eyed Liu</strong> were waiting.</p>
<p>Inside the rock shelter, once the basalt boulders were rolled back across the entrance and a small, smokeless fire of dry <strong>Bitter-Pine Charcoal</strong> was lit at the back of the cave, the sight of six hundred jin of red, marbled <strong>Peak Tier Two Boar Meat</strong> and the eleven taels of silver made Old Ma and Cross-Eyed Liu weep openly.</p>
<p>"Eleven taels of silver, plus a Peak Tier Two Boar Gall worth four taels, and all this meat..." Old Ma choked out, gripping Uncle Shi's wrist from his sheepskin pallet. "Uncle Shi... my kids and Widow Qin's little girl won't have to eat elm-bark this winter! And we're already halfway to the thirty taels needed to clear <strong>Tie Niu's</strong> axle-debt!"</p>
<p>"Don't thank me—thank Lu Chen," Uncle Shi said solemnly, slicing the eight-pound <strong>King-Boar Heart</strong> into thick, dripping crimson steaks with his skinning knife. "If he hadn't bled that King-Boar's neck vein in midair and held its fourteen-thousand-jin charge on his spear, all four of us would be rotting in the mud wallow right now. And by the rules of the frontier hunt, the heart and the marrow-spine of a Tier-Two King-Boar belong to the man who took its charge—because if you don't feed torn <strong>Half-Step Tier Two</strong> muscles within six hours of the strain, the fibers scar over and lose half their strength!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not stand on false ceremony. He knew that in the coming weeks, facing <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> (Peak Tier Three) and <strong>Pit Overseer Luo Shan</strong> (Peak Tier Four), every single ounce of strength he could forge into his body was the village's only shield.</p>
<p>Even so, he cut three thick slices from the outer rim of the King-Boar's heart—one for Old Ma to rebuild his poisoned blood, one for fifty-year-old Uncle Shi whose old joints were aching from the damp, and one for fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong>, whose young frame was just approaching the doorway of Tier One—before skewering the remaining five pounds of dense core-heart on an iron crossbow bolt over the glowing charcoal.</p>
<p>As the <strong>Peak Tier Two Boar Heart</strong> roasted over the coals, it did not drip watery grease; instead, a rich, nutty, metallic aroma filled the basalt cave as beads of dark-red essence oil sizzled on the dense muscle fibers.</p>
<p>Lu Chen ate the five pounds of scorching boar-heart slowly, chewing each tough, sinewy mouthful thirty times before washing it down with a ladle of boiled marrow broth.</p>
<p>The moment the digested meat-essence entered his bloodstream, it was as if a thousand tiny smithy hammers began tapping inside the micro-torn muscles of his forearms, biceps, shoulders, and upper back! Under the containment of his <strong>Complete Tier One</strong> ox-hide skin, the hot, ferocious vitality of the King-Boar's heart flooded straight into the dehydrated muscle bundles he had wrung out during the boar's charge—knitting the torn fibers back together at twice their previous density!</p>
