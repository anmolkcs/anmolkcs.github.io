On the upper VIP deck of the Star-Weaver VII, delegations from over a dozen Fourth-Grade and Third-Grade sects across the southern and western borderlands had gathered for the twenty-hour voyage to Loom-Star City.

Because Lu Chen wore a plain black cloth robe with his Rank 3 Inscription Grandmaster Badge tucked inside his lapel and his 3,600-jin Star-Shattering Heavy Spear stored inside his spatial ring, his quiet sixteen-year-old appearance drew little awe from the flashier sect delegations—until a group of five young men and women wearing fiery crimson robes embroidered with gold hammers walked out of the first-class cabin.

Leading them was a twenty-four-year-old Early Xiantian Realm youth with arrogant, upturned brows and a Rank 3 Peak Inscription Badge pinned prominently to his chest:

Yan Chi—the prized direct disciple of Vice-Guildmaster Yan of the Scarlet-Kiln Pavilion in Loom-Star City!

Seeing Su Wan'er standing by the cloud-railing in her pale-cyan dress—her beauty and pure spiritual aura standing out like a snow lotus among mortal flowers—Yan Chi's eyes lit up, and he strolled over with a folding jade fan in hand, glancing dismissively at Grandmaster Gu's Seven Star Valley badge.

"Grandmaster Gu of the southern Seven Star Valley Sect, isn't it?" Yan Chi said with fake politeness, barely bowing an inch before turning his gaze to Su Wan'er. "This junior is Yan Chi, a Rank 3 Peak Inscription Master and Early Xiantian cultivator of Loom-Star City's Scarlet-Kiln Pavilion. I heard your remote southern border hasn't produced a single Rank 4 Inscription Master in eighty years. If this lovely junior sister wishes to purchase Innate Spirit-Silk at the Assembly, your Seven Star Valley's meager points won't even get you through the outer hall—why not join my Scarlet-Kiln Pavilion's table tonight?"

Beside Su Wan'er, Chu Kuang let out a loud snort, his hand resting on his nine-ringed broadsaber: "Who asked for a peacock to fan his tail feathers over here? In our Seven Star Valley Sect, our Junior Brother Lu passed the Rank 3 Grandmaster assessment at age sixteen—eight years younger than you!"

"A sixteen-year-old Rank 3 Inscription Master from a southern country sect?" Yan Chi burst out laughing, his four Scarlet-Kiln companions sneering along with him. "In Loom-Star City, we call those watered-down border badges 'courtesy titles.' Wait until the Grand Artisan Assembly opens in three days—if a sixteen-year-old country boy can even read the first stroke of a Rank 4 Spatial-Weaving Diagram, I, Yan Chi, will swallow my own inscription brush!"

"Remember your words, Senior Brother Yan," Su Wan'er said coolly, stepping half a pace closer to Lu Chen's side without sparing Yan Chi another glance. "Because brushes made of iron-wood are quite hard on the stomach."

Lu Chen didn't waste a single breath arguing with Yan Chi. Why? Because beneath the cloud sea ten miles ahead of the Star-Weaver VII, his thirty-acre Golden-Jade Xiantian Spiritual Sea had just detected an ominous, violent fluctuation along the third star-iron cable of the Sky-Rail!
