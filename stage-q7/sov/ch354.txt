<p>Beyond the Southern Causeway Gate stretched the <strong>Creation Stone Causeway</strong>—a three-thousand-mile bridge of ancient, fire- glazed star-basalt suspended across a boiling ocean of <strong>Nine-Colored Origin-World Fire</strong>.</p>
<p>Every ten miles along the causeway, towering geysers of nine-colored Origin-World Fire erupted ten thousand feet high, carrying a temperature and <strong>World-Melting Entropy Law</strong> that forced the thirty-some other <strong>Peak Lesser World King</strong> and <strong>Early Great World King</strong> cultivators from neutral sects along the causeway to hide inside heavy defensive turtle-shells, advancing at a snail's pace of ten miles an hour.</p>
<p>Behind Lu Chen and Su Qingxue, <strong>Blood-King Xue Yan</strong>, <strong>Flame-King Yan Fen</strong>, and <strong>Bone-King Gu Ming</strong> charged through the gate in humiliation and fury:</p>
<p>"Don't panic! Ahead at the <strong>Two-Thousand-Mile Molten-Star Gorge</strong>, the Creation Causeway has been broken for ten thousand years—forcing everyone to fly five hundred miles directly through the <strong>Eighth-Order Nine-Colored Origin Fire-Storm</strong>! With our 100,000-mile Great Worlds, we can endure the Eighth-Order Fire-Storm, whereas their 30,000-mile Minor Worlds will have their outer world-membranes burned to ash! We'll catch and slaughter them inside the Fire-Storm!"</p>
<p>Yet as Lu Chen and Su Qingxue reached the <strong>Two-Thousand-Mile Molten-Star Gorge</strong> where the ancient causeway bridge had collapsed into a five-hundred-mile sea of roaring <strong>Eighth-Order Nine-Colored Origin-World Fire</strong>, Lu Chen didn't raise a defensive turtle-shell at all!</p>
<p>Instead, looking at the nine-colored primordial fire—which contained the raw <strong>Creation-Metal, Creation-Fire, and Tectonic-Earth Laws</strong> of the universe—Lu Chen's eyes lit up with the joy of a master porcelain-and-bronze restorer standing before the ultimate kiln:</p>
<p>"Qingxue, in Nine-Kiln Crucible City back in the lower realm, I learned that high-temperature kiln fire only destroys un-glazed vessels; for a vessel engraved with <strong>Thermal-Vitrification Runes</strong>, ten-thousand-degree kiln fire is the supreme medium to temper its body!"</p>
<p>Stepping into the roaring five-hundred-mile <strong>Eighth-Order Origin Fire-Storm</strong> alongside Su Qingxue, Lu Chen used his <strong>180,000-jin Primordial World-Sovereign Spear</strong> as a carving brush, drawing <strong>3,600 Rank 7 Peak (Half-Step Rank 8) World-Kiln Vitrification Runes</strong> across the outer crystal membranes of both his <strong>30,000-Mile Golden-Jade Minor World</strong> and Su Qingxue's <strong>30,000-Mile Chrono-Lotus Minor World</strong>!</p>
<p><em>HUMMMMM—SZZZZZ!</em></p>
<p>Instead of burning their Minor Worlds, the terrifying <strong>Eighth-Order Nine-Colored Origin-World Fire</strong> was drawn into the 3,600 vitrification runes, firing and hardening the outer crystal membranes of both of their 30,000-mile Minor Worlds until they gleamed like indestructible, nine-colored celadon-diamond glass—while expanding their Minor Worlds from <strong>30,000 miles</strong> to <strong>48,000 miles across</strong> on the spot!</p>
<p>By the time Blood-King Xue Yan and his two fellow Great World Kings struggled halfway across the Fire-Storm with their world-membranes scorched and smoking, Lu Chen and Su Qingxue had already glided effortlessly out the northern side of the storm—arriving first at the central <strong>Altar of the Pre-Epoch Creation Tablet</strong>!</p>
