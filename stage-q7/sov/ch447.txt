<p>On the seventh night in Bitter-Pine Ravine—the seventeenth day of the eleventh month—all seven earthen kilns had reached the final twelve hours of the "blue-smoke" carbonization phase. By dawn tomorrow, the vents would be sealed completely to cool the three thousand jin of finished <strong>Black-Gold Pine Charcoal</strong>.</p>
<p>Around the second watch of the night (10:00 PM), a thick, freezing ground-fog rolled up the ravine floor from the west.</p>
<p>Big-Tall Zhao and Cross-Eyed Liu were asleep inside the pine-bough lean-to between Kilns #2 and #3, while fourteen-year-old Shi Tou was nodding off beside the warm brick of Kiln #1. Uncle Shi was up on the eastern rock shelf checking the trip-wire alarm strung across the village trail.</p>
<p>Lu Chen sat cross-legged atop the flat, warm clay crown of Kiln #4 at the center of the camp, his eighty-jin solid black-iron boar-spear resting across his knees.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Lu Chen's eyes opened in the dark.</p>
<p>Up in the stunted iron-pines along the western mouth of the ravine—the direction leading toward <strong>Dead-Crow Gorge</strong>, NOT the village trail—the harsh, clicking calls of the nocturnal <strong>Frost-Cicadas</strong> had just stopped one tree after another in a moving wave two hundred yards long.</p>
<p>And thirty seconds later, carried on a faint draft of western breeze down the ravine, a microscopic trace of scent entered the three-pace sphere of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>: not the natural musk of wild wolves or boars, but the rancid odor of <strong>boiled dog-fat boot-grease</strong>, rusted iron mail, and cheap grain-liquor!</p>
<p>Lu Chen didn't shout and alert the approaching raiders that they had been spotted.</p>
<p>Sliding silently off the clay dome of Kiln #4 like a falling leaf, he landed without a sound beside the lean-to, clapped a calloused hand gently over Big-Tall Zhao's mouth as he shook the two hunters and young Shi Tou awake, and gave the two-finger hand-sign for <em>Bandits from the West—Total Silence!</em></p>
<p>Just as Uncle Shi glided down from the eastern rock shelf after seeing Lu Chen's hand-signal, the dense ground-fog at the western mouth of the ravine parted.</p>
<p>Twelve dark figures crept up the snow-covered ravine bed in two spread skirmish lines, their boots wrapped in strips of rough sacking to muffle their footsteps on the frozen gravel!</p>
<p>Ten of the raiders were seasoned <strong>Tier One</strong> and <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> cutthroats of the <strong>Broken-Tooth Outlaws</strong>, armed with notched sabers, iron-boss bucklers, and hooked boar-spears.</p>
<p>And walking at the center of the line—standing six feet four inches tall with a bald head wrapped in a red wolf-skin bandana and a purple scar splitting his nose and upper lip into three pieces—was <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> sworn second brother and cruelest enforcer:</p>
<p><strong>Blood-Axe Scar-Six</strong>!</p>
<p>In each of his massive, hairy hands, Scar-Six carried a <strong>fifty-pound Broad-Bladed Butcher's Battle-Axe</strong>, and every step of his sacking-wrapped boots crushed the frozen basalt gravel beneath him into powder—radiating the ferocious, waterless muscular pressure of a warrior standing at the very pinnacle of <strong>Peak Tier Two of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Iron-Flesh Perfection (2,450 jin force)</strong>!</p>
<p>"Boss Han said Ash-Brook's charcoal kilns always burn in this ravine before the twelfth month," Scar-Six whispered in a voice like grinding millstones as he spotted the seven warm clay domes fifty yards ahead and the empty pine-bough lean-to. "Ma Liu and his three boys vanished in the eastern basin ten days ago—these Ash-Brook mud-legs must know something! Surround the lean-to, butcher every man inside, smash three of the kilns to ruin their iron tax, and haul the rest of the charcoal back to Dead-Crow Gorge!"</p>
