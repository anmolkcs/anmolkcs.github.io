At the southern bridgehead, ten thousand disciples and elders of the Seven Star Valley Sect—led by Lu Chen's radiant, youthful Revolving Core parents Lu Shan and Meng Wan, Elder Tie, Fang Shi, and Grand Marshal Su—gathered to send off the four young legends of the south:

  • Lu Chen (18 years old — First Life-Renewal Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel Realm, Rank 5 Inscription Grandmaster);
  • Su Wan'er (18 years old — Early Revolving Core Realm: Flawless Zither-Star Core, Rank 4 Peak Inscription Master);
  • Murong Zi (20 years old — Early Revolving Core Realm: Flawless Sword-Star Core);

Even Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng waiting on the Central Domain Sky-Barge gasped in disbelief when he sensed the unmistakable First Life-Renewal Wheel humming inside eighteen-year-old Lu Chen's dantian!

Turning back one last time at the entrance of the rainbow Astral Ferry-Bridge, Lu Chen smiled warmly and waved to his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan standing hand-in-hand in the morning sunlight.

Inside his spiritual sea, all nine outer facets of the Chrono-Soul Prism gleamed around the blooming Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus where Su Qingxue watched the horizon beside him, and the Second Inner Ring of Divine-Sea & Inner-World Technique Bubbles had already begun to shimmer with golden light.

Gripping his 18,000-jin Star-Sovereign Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear, eighteen-year-old Lu Chen stepped onto the Astral Ferry-Bridge alongside Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang, striding toward the Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain—and the vast, unending heavens beyond.
