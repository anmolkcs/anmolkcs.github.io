<p>Even with all eighty bars of tribute iron completed on the rack, Lu Chen did not relax his vigilance for a single hour.</p>
<p>During the afternoon of the twenty-fifth and all through the twenty-sixth day of the eleventh month, while thirty-six hundred jin of Black-Gold Charcoal still remained in the vault, Lu Chen and Old Tie Shan kept the furnace roaring to smelt the last sixty jin of leftover <strong>Black-Marrow Ore</strong>.</p>
<p>They did not forge farm tools with that remaining sixty jin of iron.</p>
<p>Instead, keeping in mind that <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> (Peak Tier Three) was still lurking in Dead-Crow Gorge eight miles away and had an under-the-table pact with <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong>, Lu Chen redesigned the village's two heavy oak <strong>Tribute Sledges</strong> from the ground up.</p>
<p>First, he shod the six-inch-wide oak runners of both sledges with quarter-inch-thick strips of polished high-carbon steel so they could glide over jagged ice and rock under a thousand-jin load without splintering.</p>
<p>Second, along the outer edges of both sledges, Lu Chen mounted hinged, chest-high <strong>Iron-Clad Oak Side-Boards</strong> studded with iron bolts and pierced with square crossbow loopholes. In thirty seconds on the trail, the two sledges could be pulled end-to-end and locked together with iron pins, dropping their hinged side-boards outward to form a twelve-foot-long, arrow-proof <strong>Mobile Sledge-Barricade</strong> anchored by the eight hundred jin of iron bars stacked inside!</p>
<p>Third, with the final thirty jin of core steel, Lu Chen forged <strong>eighteen heavy, two-foot-long Armor-Piercing Spearheads</strong>, mounting them on seasoned <strong>Bitter-Iron Pine</strong> shafts for Uncle Shi, Big-Tall Zhao, Cross-Eyed Liu, Old Ma, Shi Tou, and the village militia, plus thirty armor-piercing bodkin bolts for the four <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong>.</p>
<p>Just as Lu Chen finished riveting the final iron hinge onto the second tribute sledge at dusk on the twenty-seventh day of the eleventh month, a furious clanging of the bronze alarm-gong erupted from the southern watchtower at the village gate!</p>
<p><em>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</em></p>
<p>"Riders or beasts at the south gorge?" Uncle Shi shouted, snatching up his sixty-pound horn-bow and new iron-pine spear as the twenty-man village militia sprinted to the southern palisade in under thirty breaths!</p>
<p>Lu Chen vaulted lightly onto the ten-foot firing platform of the southern gatehouse—his <strong>Thirty Drawn Sinews</strong> carrying him up the timber ladder without even touching the rungs—and looked down the snow-choked southern road through the gathering twilight.</p>
<p>Staggering up the frozen ruts toward the gate were not bandits, nor bailiff riders, but <strong>four blood-streaked, half-frozen villagers</strong> dragging a makeshift toboggan made from a broken cottage door!</p>
<p>On the cottage-door toboggan lay an old man with a saber-slash across his chest, and clinging to his coat was a ten-year-old boy whose bare feet were black with frostbite.</p>
<p>"Open the side-postern!" Uncle Shi gasped, recognizing the hemp-and-slate badges on the survivors' coats. "That's <strong>Hunter Feng</strong> and his brother from <strong>Mud-Well Village</strong>, four miles down the western fork!"</p>
