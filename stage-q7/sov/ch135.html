When Lu Chen soared effortlessly out of the Kiln #1 hatch holding half of the blooming Ten-Thousand-Year Earth-Core Fire Lotus (having left the other half and the root-stem intact for the Nine-Kiln Guild!), the entire hall of sixty Master Smiths erupted into thunderous cheers!

Presented with the expelled lump of Crimson-Furnace Sulfur-Lead Slag and the restored conduit, Elder Huo and Yan Chi's faces turned as black as pot-soot.

Refusing to leave empty-handed without testing Lu Chen's combat power, Yan Chi (Late Xiantian Realm, 255,000 jin of fire-hammer strike force) stepped blocking the entrance of the Main Forging Platform:

"Restoring an old pipe with trick runes doesn't make you a true Weapon Grandmaster, Lu Chen! In the Nine-Kiln Sacred Fire Festival, smiths prove their worth by clashing the weapons they forged themselves! My Grade 3 Peak Blazing-Tyrant War Hammer weighs 2,400 jin—dare to clash your spear against my hammer once before you touch Kiln #1?"

"If your hammer can stay in your hands for a single breath, I'll give you this Earth-Core Fire Lotus," Lu Chen said calmly.

BOOM—CLANG!

Yan Chi swung his 2,400-jin Blazing-Tyrant War Hammer with all 255,000 jin of his Late Xiantian fire-essence!

Lu Chen didn't even use two hands—holding his 3,600-jin Star-Shattering Heavy Spear in his right hand alone, he flicked the shaft horizontally with 《Acoustic-Gravity Resonance Spear — Second Chime》!

DONG—CRACK!

A single bronze bell-chime echoed across the hall! Yan Chi's 2,400-jin war hammer had its head cracked three inches deep on impact, ripping out of Yan Chi's bleeding hands and embedding itself into the ceiling beam as Yan Chi was blasted forty paces out the front doors of the pavilion onto the street!

Humiliated in both craft and combat, Elder Huo and Yan Chi fled Nine-Kiln Crucible City in disgrace—though as Elder Huo looked back at the city gates, he sent a secret blood-talisman message northward toward the Star-Burial Glacier on the border, where the Blood-Moon Demonic Cult and the Shadow-Moon Sect were gathering!
