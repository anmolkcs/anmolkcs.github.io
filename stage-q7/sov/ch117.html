At sunrise the next morning, the Star-Weaver VII glided off the final terminal pier of the Cloud-Sea Sky-Rail into one of the grandest cities of the southern-central borderlands:

Loom-Star City!

Built atop nine terraced white-marble plateaus fed by giant water-driven Spirit-Silk Looms and sky-forges, Loom-Star City was governed by the Grand Artisan & Meridian-Weaver Guild—a neutral Third-Grade Power headed by three Revolving Core Realm Grandmasters!

When the Middle Xiantian captain of the Star-Weaver VII submitted his voyage log—reporting that a sixteen-year-old youth named Lu Chen from the Seven Star Valley Sect had not only slain a Middle Fourth-Order Cloud-Abyss Kraken in midair, but had also repaired a Rank 4 Gyroscopic Star-Compass live under 100,000 jin of magnetic torque—the presiding Guild Master Yun (an Early Revolving Core && Rank 4 Peak Inscription Grandmaster) personally came out to the main guild hall to meet Lu Chen!

"Young friend Lu Chen!" Guild Master Yun's eyes shone with amazement as she inspected both the ship's recalibrated keel compass and the fully restored Pre-Epoch Star-Compass Lu Chen had brought from the Sunken Bronze Bell. "Even our Guild's senior Rank 4 masters have been stuck on the seventy-second magnetic-bearing rune of the Pre-Epoch Star-Compass for thirty years—yet you restored every single micro-gear without a flaw! Tell this old woman—are you here to participate in tomorrow's Rank 4 Inscription Grandmaster Assessment for the Ten-Thousand-Year Innate Heaven-Silkworm Silk?"

"Yes, Guild Master Yun," Lu Chen bowed respectfully. "I wish to obtain three spools of Rank 4 Innate Heaven-Silkworm Silk to weave meridian-reinforcing tapestries for my parents and my master."
