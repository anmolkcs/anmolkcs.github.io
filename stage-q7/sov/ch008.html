That night, instead of sleeping in his shed, Lu Chen carried a heavy clay cauldron, the two jars of Ironwood Marrow Paste, and the stalk of Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng three miles outside Cloudstride City’s southern wall into a secluded ravine on Back-Cloud Ridge.

In a hidden rock hollow beside a mountain stream, he built a smokeless charcoal fire, filled the cauldron with spring water, and dissolved both jars of silver-gray Ironwood Marrow Paste along with half of the Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng into the boiling water.

Within minutes, the liquid in the cauldron turned as thick and dark as molten bronze, bubbling with a ferocious medicinal aroma that made the wild insects within fifty yards flee in terror.

Ironwood Marrow Paste was a luxury body-tempering medicine used only by core disciples of wealthy clans who had already reached the Peak of the Second Stage (Flesh Training). Dissolving two whole jars at once—and spiking them with a Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng—created a medicinal potency violent enough to burst the blood vessels of an ordinary Second Stage martial artist!

Lu Chen stripped off his hemp tunic, revealing a torso covered in compact, steel-like muscle lines, and stepped straight into the bubbling cauldron.

HISS—!

The moment the scalding, bronze-colored broth closed over his shoulders, Lu Chen’s vision flashed white.

It felt as if ten thousand red-hot sewing needles had pierced every single pore of his skin simultaneously, pouring liquid fire straight into his sub-dermal fascia, muscle fibers, and blood vessels!

Lu Chen’s teeth clenched so hard his gums bled, yet not a single groan escaped his throat. Sitting cross-legged in the boiling cauldron, he clutched one of the Low-Grade True Essence Stones in his right palm and began circulating the 《Chaotic Star Body Art — Silk-Unwinding Star Breath》 at maximum capacity!

Thrum—! Thrum—! Thrum—!

Inside the cauldron, the boiling broth began to vibrate in concentric ripples around Lu Chen’s body.

Through the thirty-six micro-meridian nodes of the Chaotic Star Body Art, the violent medicinal essence of the Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng and Ironwood Marrow was sucked greedily into his skin and muscle fibers, while a wisp of pure heaven-and-earth origin energy from the True Essence Stone in his hand merged with the cold, stabilizing aura of the Chrono-Soul Prism.

Under Prism Stillness, Lu Chen watched his own body transform from the inside out: - The outer layer of his skin shed its old, dead epidermis in dark flakes, revealing new skin beneath that gleamed with a tough, warm bronze sheen capable of deflecting ordinary iron daggers. - Beneath the skin, his muscle fibers and fascia twisted tighter and tighter in nine-layered spirals, drinking in the ginseng blood-qi like dry sponges. - Even more remarkably, as the scorching tide of blood-qi surged repeatedly through his cramped, Grade 2 meridians, the calcified impurities choking his meridian walls were scoured away bit by bit, widening the channels by nearly a fifth!

1,400 jin… 1,600 jin… 1,850 jin… 2,100 jin!

By the time the eastern sky turned crimson with dawn on the third consecutive night of secret tempering on Back-Cloud Ridge, the thick bronze broth in the cauldron had turned completely clear—every last trace of medicinal essence absorbed into Lu Chen’s flesh!

BOOM!

Lu Chen stood up from the cauldron, shattering the clay pot into dozens of pieces with the sheer expansion of his aura. He threw a single, unhurried straight punch at a three-foot-thick granite boulder beside the stream.

CRACK—BOOM!

Fist-wind snapped like a whip. Where Lu Chen’s bare knuckles struck the granite, spiderweb cracks exploded two feet deep across the stone face, sending fist-sized rock fragments whistling into the stream! On the back of his knuckles, there wasn’t even a scrape—only a faint white mark that vanished in a single breath.

“Peak of the Second Stage—Flesh Training, with 2,300 jin of raw physical strength!” Lu Chen exhaled a foot-long jet of hot white breath that took five seconds to disperse in the chilly mountain air.

In the Sky Spill Continent, an ordinary martial artist at the Peak of the Second Stage possessed around 1,500 jin of strength, and even an Early Third Stage (Viscera Training) practitioner barely reached 2,000 jin. Yet through the Chaotic Star Body Art and the Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng, Lu Chen’s physical power at the Peak of the Second Stage had already surpassed Early Third Stage warriors!

Pulling on a clean black martial robe he had bought at the bazaar and strapping his inscribed iron sword to his waist, Lu Chen descended Back-Cloud Ridge just as the morning gates of Cloudstride City swung open.

Yet the moment he turned onto Willow-Lane Alley toward his parents’ small tavern, he saw a crowd of neighbors gathered outside the wooden courtyard gate, pointing and whispering anxiously.

On the flagstones in front of the tavern, two bruised tables had been kicked over, and four burly retainers wearing the black-and-gold cuffs of the Wang Merchant House stood with their arms crossed, blocking the entrance.

Inside the courtyard, Lu Chen’s father, Lu Shan, was pressing a bloodied cloth against his forehead while standing protectively in front of Lu Chen’s mother. Facing them with a folding fan in hand was a seventeen-year-old youth in a silver-embroidered silk robe—Wang Yigao!

“Uncle Lu, today is the tenth day,” Wang Yigao said with a lazy, condescending smile, tapping his folding fan against his palm. “Thirty taels of silver for a Seven Star Martial House Recommendation Token that your Grade 2 waste of a son can’t even use is charity from my Wang Clan. Did you really think hiding your son in a butcher’s kitchen would change anything? Hand over the jade token now, or my men will tear down every beam of this tavern before noon—and when we hold the public sparring wager at Cloudstride Martial Plaza in an hour, I’ll personally break both of Lu Chen’s arms so he never holds a kitchen knife again.”

“Looking for me, Wang Yigao?”

A calm, ice-cold voice cut cleanly across the noisy alley.

The crowd parted instinctively as Lu Chen walked forward step by step, his black robe fluttering lightly in the morning breeze and his dark eyes fixed squarely on Wang Yigao.
