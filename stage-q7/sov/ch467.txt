<p>In the howling whiteout of Howling-Wind Pass, the only sound left was the wind whistling through the broken crenels of the watch-fort and the heavy, joyous breathing of the twelve men from Ash-Brook and Mud-Well.</p>
<p>Not a single villager had been killed!</p>
<p>Thanks to the twelve-foot <strong>Armored Sledge-Bastion</strong> Lu Chen had built before leaving the village, the worst injuries among the twelve men were a shallow saber-nick on Big-Tall Zhao's shoulder and a bruised rib on Cross-Eyed Liu where a bandit's shield had slammed against the oak side-board.</p>
<p>Walking over to the center of the blood-stained road where Lu Chen stood planting the butt of his eighty-jin spear into the ice, sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> and <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> stared down at the corpse of <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> and the two shattered halves of the 110-jin military Ghost-Head Saber lying in the snow.</p>
<p>"You... you snapped all thirty-six bowstring sinews right under his execution chop," Old Tie Shan breathed, looking at the translucent, faint gold-white sheen beneath the torn skin of Lu Chen's bleeding palms—which were already closing up under the surging vitality of a true <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Drawing)</strong> powerhouse. "And you pierced right through the flat of a hundred-and-ten-jin garrison great-saber! Boy, do you know how many thirty-year-old clan heirs in <strong>Black-Rock Town</strong> are still stuck at Early Tier Three?!"</p>
<p>"The two halves of that broken Ghost-Head Saber are good military spring-steel, Old Tie," Lu Chen said calmly, bending down to pick up the two heavy broken blade-halves and tossing them onto the sledge. "Keep them. When we reach <strong>Deep-Shaft Pit #7</strong>, eighty jin of spear won't be heavy enough for a <strong>Peak Tier Four</strong> pit-overseer."</p>
<p>Leading the men into the stone blockhouse at the rear of the ruined watch-fort, they discovered the full hoard of <strong>Broken-Tooth Camp</strong>.</p>
<p>Stacked inside the dry inner vault were: - All <strong>forty leather sacks of winter Iron-Husk Millet</strong> and <strong>three hundred jin of smelted tax-iron</strong> that Han Wu had looted from <strong>Mud-Well Village</strong> two days ago; - Three healthy, armored <strong>Scale-Hoofed Crag-Mules</strong> stabled in the rear shed; - And inside a locked iron strongbox beneath Han Wu's wolf-skin cot—which Lu Chen pried open with a single twist of his fingers—lay <strong>forty-eight taels of Prefectural Silver</strong>, two porcelain bottles of <strong>Military-Grade Bone-Tempering Pill Powder</strong>, and a folded sheet of oiled yellow silk!</p>
<p>Uncle Shi held the oiled yellow silk up to the brazier light and read the official red thumb-seal at the bottom. His face turned dark purple with rage.</p>
<p>"It's a secret letter stamped with the personal seal of <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong>!" Uncle Shi hissed, showing the silk to Old Tie Shan, Hunter Feng, and Lu Chen. "Written on the eighteenth of last month! Look at what it says: <em>'Brother Han—Ash-Brook's one-armed smith passed the tenth-month mattock test by fluke, and Mud-Well Village is twenty jin short. Wipe out Mud-Well before the solstice and intercept Ash-Brook's winter iron caravan in Howling-Wind Pass. Once both villages default on the twelfth-month ledger, I will sign the Magistrate's forfeiture warrant to turn their valleys into private pasture for Garrison Captain Luo's Crag-Mules, and your boys get thirty percent of the convict-sale silver!'</em>"</p>
<p><strong>Hunter Feng</strong> and his two Mud-Well Village comrades trembled from head to foot, tears of hatred streaming down their frostbitten faces. "Eight of our brothers in Mud-Well died in the snow... just so Zhao Kuan and Garrison Captain Luo could turn our homes into mule pasture!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen took the oiled yellow silk letter, folded it neatly, and placed it inside his tunic over his heart.</p>
<p>"Not yet," Lu Chen said in a quiet, bone-chilling voice as Hunter Feng opened his mouth to curse. "Captain Luo in the Black-Rock Garrison is the brother-in-law of <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> in Pit #7. In a frontier county town where the Bailiff riders and Garrison captains Drink at the same table, waving this letter in the street without the strength to crush a Tier-Four warrior will only get us shot down as 'forgers and rebels.' First, we use their own rules to clear the tax ledger and get into Pit #7 to bring <strong>Tie Niu</strong> out alive. Once Tie Niu is safe—we settle the ledger with Luo Shan and Zhao Kuan in blood."</p>
