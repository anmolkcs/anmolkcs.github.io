<p>Fifty thousand miles north of the Primordial Origin Kiln, the starry void was bisected by a breathtaking,million-mile-wide river of silver-gray crystalline stone and flowing liquid time-sand:</p>
<p><strong>The River of Petrified Time!</strong></p>
<p>During the primordial war at the birth of the Upper Star-Soul Realm, an <strong>Empyrean Sovereign of Time</strong> had severed a tributary of the cosmic River of Time here. Over the epochs, seventy percent of the tributary's time-water had petrified into billions of jagged <strong>Chrono-Silicate Reefs and Time-Amber Islands</strong>, while the remaining thirty percent flowed between the reefs as treacherous <strong>1,000x Time-Erosion Rapids</strong>!</p>
<p>Any ordinary Great World King whose ship touched a <strong>1,000x Time-Erosion Rapid</strong> without a time-law guide would watch their hull age ten thousand years in ten seconds and crumble into rusted dust!</p>
<p>Yet standing at the helm of the <em>Primordial Star-Ark</em> were <strong>Lu Chen</strong> (holding the <strong>Chrono-Soul Prism</strong>) and <strong>Su Qingxue</strong> (holding the <strong>Ancestral Chrono-Yin Bell</strong>), while <strong>Lu Shan</strong>, <strong>Meng Wan</strong>, <strong>Su Wan'er</strong>, <strong>Elder Tie</strong>, <strong>Murong Zi</strong>, and <strong>Chu Kuang</strong> channeled their newly ascended <strong>Great World King</strong> force into the ship's keel!</p>
<p>"Look at the time-rapids ahead—every reef of <strong>Petrified Time-Silicate</strong> has natural <strong>Chrono-Cleavage Rings</strong> just like petrified wood and ancient silicate artifacts on Earth!"</p>
<p>Entering <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen read the age-rings of the petrified time-reefs fifty miles ahead with 100% precision, steering the <em>Primordial Star-Ark</em> smoothly along the zero-erosion laminar seams between the rapids—while two hundred miles ahead in the <strong>Central Time-Amber Shoal</strong>, an urgent battle roar echoed across the river!</p>
