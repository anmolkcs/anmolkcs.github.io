<p>In front of the Bailiff Tally-House, over two hundred townsfolk, merchant smiths, and village elders craned their necks to stare at the eighty gleaming dark-blue iron bars sitting on the stone scale pan.</p>
<p>Senior Clerk <strong>Master Sun</strong> stepped down from his desk, adjusted his jade spectacles, and tapped the top bar with a small brass tuning-mallet.</p>
<p><em>DING—!</em></p>
<p>A clear, resonant note like struck temple jade lingered in the freezing air for five full breaths—the unmistakable acoustic signature of <strong>Grade-One Nine-Folded Cold-Slag Steel</strong> completely purged of spiral phosphorus-ash!</p>
<p>Up on the terrace steps, <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan's</strong> face turned the color of pig-liver. If Senior Clerk Sun stamped the official tax-clearance and Pit #7 release warrant right here in public, not only would Zhao Kuan lose the forfeiture bounty on Ash-Brook and Mud-Well's land, but once <strong>Tie Niu</strong> walked out of Shaft #7 alive, the secret of the <strong>Natural Cold-Iron Marrow Brine</strong> that <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> was illicitly mining in Drift Four might leak out to the County Magistrate!</p>
<p>"Hold the stamp, Master Sun!"</p>
<p>Zhao Kuan strode down the stone steps with his hand on his ninety-jin iron whip-rod, his <strong>Early Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining)</strong> aura flaring out to intimidate the crowd.</p>
<p>"Twenty days ago, I kicked that old cripple Tie Shan's collarbone in half myself!" Zhao Kuan sneered, pointing an iron-gauntleted finger at Old Tie Shan. "A one-armed old man with a broken collarbone couldn't forge eighty bars of nine-folded Grade-One steel in twenty days if he had ten spirits pulling his bellows! They must have washed the outside of cheap bog-slag bars with blue vitriol acid to fake the color! Unless one of these bars can cleave the Tally-House's <strong>Cast-Iron Test Anvil</strong> without dulling its edge, I declare the whole batch counterfeit contraband!"</p>
<p>An outraged murmur rose from the village elders in the square—everyone knew the Tally-House's <strong>Cast-Iron Test Anvil</strong> was a solid two-hundred-pound block of white pig-iron used to test military broadswords, not unsharpened rectangular ingots! Asking an unsharpened rectangular bar of iron to split a two-hundred-pound cast-iron block was naked bullying!</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan's face flushed crimson with fury, his hand tightening on his hammer.</p>
<p>"Let me test it, Old Tie."</p>
<p>Lu Chen stepped calmly up onto the stone terrace between Old Tie Shan and Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan.</p>
<p>Standing five feet from Zhao Kuan, Lu Chen didn't cower beneath the Sub-Bailiff's <strong>Early Tier Four</strong> pressure. Instead, he picked up one random ten-jin rectangular bar of Ash-Brook's <strong>Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron</strong> from the scale pan with his right hand, walked over to the two-hundred-pound <strong>Cast-Iron Test Anvil</strong> sitting on an oak stump in the center of the terrace, and held the ten-jin bar by one end like a short iron ruler.</p>
<p>"Sub-Bailiff Zhao says an unsharpened bar must split the test-block without nicking its corner," Lu Chen said, his quiet voice carrying clearly across the silent square. "Watch closely, Master Sun."</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not use a weapon.</p>
<p>Planting his boots on the flagstones, he coiled all <strong>Thirty-Six Translucent Gold-White Bowstring Sinews</strong> from his heels through his spine to his right fingertips—and whipped the square corner-edge of the ten-jin iron bar straight down onto the center of the two-hundred-pound Cast-Iron Test Anvil!</p>
<p><em>TWANG—KRAK—BOOOOOOOOOM!!</em></p>
<p>Four thousand four hundred jin of <strong>Tier Three Bowstring-Sinew</strong> force crashed onto the square block!</p>
<p>With an ear-splitting metallic explosion that made the ears of every bystander in the front row ring, the two-hundred-pound Cast-Iron Test Anvil split cleanly down the center into two hundred-pound halves that crashed onto the flagstones on either side of the oak stump!</p>
<p>Lu Chen tossed the ten-jin bar of Ash-Brook iron lightly across to Senior Clerk Sun.</p>
<p>Master Sun caught the bar with both hands and looked down at the striking edge: not only was the ninety-degree corner of the dark-blue bar completely unchipped and unbent, it had actually polished itself brighter against the shattered cast iron!</p>
<p>And three paces away, <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong> stared at the two halves of the test-block, then at the young man who had just unleashed <strong>full Thirty-Six-Sinew Tier Three power</strong> with a bare ten-jin ingot—his pupils shrinking in shock as he realized why <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> had never come out of Howling-Wind Pass!</p>
<p>"True Grade-One Nine-Folded Cold-Slag Steel—superior even to the Garrison Foundry standard!" Senior Clerk Sun declared loudly, slamming the heavy bronze <strong>County Treasury Clearance Seal</strong> onto both Mud-Well Village's tax roll and Tie Niu's Pit #7 debt-ledger in red cinnabar ink! "Both villages' taxes and the eight-hundred-jin Pit #7 redemption bond are paid in full!"</p>
