<p>"How the hell did those rats crawl out of Drift Four?!"</p>
<p>At the head of the twenty mounted guards, <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan's</strong> hideous pulled-down left eye bulged in shock as he saw the forty armed miners pouring out of the Witness-Shack—and then narrowed into murderous fury as he realized that if <strong>Audit Clerk Fang</strong> escaped down the road to the Five-Mile Post-Station where the Grey-Wall Prefectural Censor was staying, his entire clan would be executed for mine-fraud and mass murder!</p>
<p>"Block the Stone Bridge across the canyon gorge!" Scarface Luo Shan roared, brandishing his <strong>220-Jin Eight-Flanged Iron Mace</strong> as his eighteen armored pit-wardens fanned out across the thirty-foot-wide basalt bridge that formed the only exit from the Pit #7 rim! "Kill every last one of those forty miners, chop up the clerk, and throw their corpses back down the shaft!"</p>
<p>"Brother Tie Niu—form the forty brothers in three ranks at the narrow head of the bridge around your father and Clerk Fang!" Lu Chen's calm, resonant voice cut cleanly across the night wind as he stepped out alone onto the snow-covered basalt bridge, his seven-foot-six-inch, <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> held diagonally across his body. "Nobody steps past my back."</p>
<p>Seeing Lu Chen step onto the bridge alone to face twenty riders, <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong>—riding half a length ahead of Luo Shan on his armored Crag-Mule—let out a savage, arrogant bark of laughter!</p>
<p>Twelve days ago at the town square, Zhao Kuan had seen Lu Chen display <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Drawing)</strong> strength of four thousand four hundred jin. And just half an hour ago down in Drift Four, Zhao Kuan had heard Luo Shan laugh about how the fifteenfold gravity had left the boy puking sulfur-bile on his knees!</p>
<p>"Brother Luo—hold the rear!" Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan shouted, eager to claim the credit of killing the ringleader while showing off his <strong>Early Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining — 4,800 jin force)</strong> power! Digging his iron spurs into his Crag-Mule's flanks, Zhao Kuan charged straight down the center of the bridge at a full gallop, swinging his <strong>ninety-jin Iron-Ringed Whip-Rod</strong> in a whistling diagonal smash aimed directly at Lu Chen's right collarbone!</p>
<p>"Twenty days ago in Ash-Brook, I kicked that old cripple's collarbone in half!" Zhao Kuan snarled as the ninety-jin iron whip-rod screamed down with five thousand jin of mounted momentum! "Tonight I'll smash yours and feed your heart to my mule!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen stood motionless in the center of the bridge.</p>
<p>He did not dodge the charging Crag-Mule, nor did he use a spiral deflection.</p>
<p>To Lu Chen—whose skeleton now stood at <strong>Late Tier Four (7,200 jin baseline force)</strong> forged in eighteenfold-gravity boiling sulfur-brine, wielding a <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Spear</strong>—an Early Tier Four town bully who had soaked his bones in wooden tubs was brittle as rotten firewood!</p>
<p><em>Spear One: Break the Rod!</em></p>
<p>Lu Chen whipped the wrist-thick octagonal shaft of his 160-jin spear upward with one hand in a direct, unadorned <strong>Iron-Bar Clash</strong>!</p>
<p><em>BOOOOOOOOM—KRAK!!</em></p>
<p>Seven thousand two hundred jin of Late Tier Four bone-and-sinew power met the falling ninety-jin iron whip-rod head-on!</p>
<p>Before Zhao Kuan's smirk could even freeze on his face, his ninety-jin iron whip-rod bent at a ninety-degree angle and <strong>snapped cleanly in half</strong> with a shower of sparks, while the terrifying rebound tore the skin of Zhao Kuan's right palm open and shattered three of his finger-bones!</p>
<p>"Late Tier Four Slag-Bone?!" Zhao Kuan shrieked in utter terror as his mount thundered past Lu Chen!</p>
<p><em>Spear Two: The Collarbone Debt!</em></p>
<p>Without pulling back the point, Lu Chen pivoted his hips and lashed the heavy octagonal butt-end of the 160-jin spear sideways across Zhao Kuan's chest—striking the exact spot on the right collarbone where Zhao Kuan had kicked Old Tie Shan twenty days ago!</p>
<p><em>CRUUUUUUNCH!</em></p>
<p>Zhao Kuan's iron-mail coat caved in three inches deep as his right collarbone, right shoulder blade, and four upper ribs were pulverized into bone-meal, blasting the Sub-Bailiff out of his saddle like a struck sack of grain!</p>
<p><em>Spear Three: Execution!</em></p>
<p>Before Zhao Kuan's flying body even hit the basalt flagstones of the bridge, Lu Chen took half a step forward and drove the eighteen-inch four-edged <strong>Cold-Marrow Spearhead</strong> straight through the center of Zhao Kuan's throat in midair, pinning the corrupt Sub-Bailiff dead into the stone parapet of the bridge!</p>
