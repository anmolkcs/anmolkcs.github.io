Standing at the bottom of Thunder-Scar Gorge with 9,000 jin of baseline strength coursing through his golden-jade bones, Lu Chen picked up his 820-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Spear from the ground.

Previously, at the Third and Fourth Stages, the 820-jin spear had felt pleasantly heavy and solid in his grip.

Now, however, as he swung the 820-jin black spear in a full-strength 《Mountain-Splitting Smash》 infused with 12,500 jin of burst force—

WHIR—CRACK!

Before the spearhead even struck a boulder, the sheer centrifugal whip of 12,500 jin of Dragon-Elephant strength caused three hairline stress fractures to appear along the middle of the Hundred-Refined Black Iron shaft!

“At 9,000 jin of baseline strength and 12,500 jin of burst power, a Grade 1 Heavy Spear can no longer withstand my own force,” Lu Chen noted, running his fingers over the cracked black iron shaft. “Even a standard Grade 2 weapon would bend under 12,500 jin. To unleash my full strength in the Seven Star Martial House, I need a Peak Grade 2 or Grade 3 Heavy Profound Spear weighing at least 1,200 jin!”

Fortunately, sitting inside his spiritual sea, the third facet of the Chrono-Soul Prism—which had absorbed the entire Third-Order Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros’s Core Blood Essence five days ago—was now shining with a bright, metallic silver-violet light!

Sitting back down on a flat magnet-stone slab, Lu Chen entered Prism Stillness and drew his consciousness into the gray expanse of the Prism.

In the center of the void, the Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus housing the slumbering snow-robed woman remained in quiet, undisturbed hibernation beneath the Prism’s timeless light. Lu Chen glanced gratefully at the Prism’s central barrier—whose automatic rebound had shielded his spiritual sea from Gui Yan’s curse—and then turned toward the Third Technique Bubble hovering inside the third facet.

Because this third bubble had been unlocked by Third-Order Spirit Beast Blood Essence and his own soul force had grown vastly stronger, the sphere was the size of a fist, blazing like a miniature violet star!

Lu Chen reached out and merged the violet Technique Bubble into his forehead.

BOOM—!

Though the torrent of pure technical diagrams was twice as dense as the second bubble, Lu Chen’s strengthened spiritual sea absorbed the nameless, identity-wiped legacy in less than half an hour!

When he sorted through the pure technical manuals inside the violet sphere, a look of genuine joy spread across his face. Just like the previous bubbles, there were no personal memories or world histories—only two supreme arts tailored as if by heaven itself for his exact path: 1. The 《Cold-Flame Micro-Forging & Rank 2 Dual-Array Inscription Method》—which allowed an Inscription Master to use aThird-Order Beast Core and magnetic iron ore to re-forge and compress an existing heavy weapon’s core, embedding an internal Gravity-Amplification & Thunder-Penetration Inscription directly inside the molten metal! 2. And a true, Earth-Step foundational spear art condensed into three devastating battlefield chapters:

《Wildfire Prairie Spear — Chapter One: Hundred-Step Crushing Thunder; Chapter Two: Wildfire Sweeps the Plains; Chapter Three: Meteor Pierces the Sun!》

Looking at the cracked 820-jin Black Iron Spear in his hand, the Third-Order Thunder-Horned Rhino Horn & Beast Core beside him, and the glowing vein of Purple-Magnetic Iron Ore right at his feet, Lu Chen didn’t even wait to return to the Royal Capital to hire a blacksmith.

Using the natural subterranean lightning-fire fissure at the bottom of Thunder-Scar Gorge as his forge, Lu Chen decided to forge his own 1,200-jin Peak Grade 2 Heavy Spear right here in the mountains!
