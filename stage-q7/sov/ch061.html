The black ironstone plaza outside Sky-Fall Canyon hummed with the clashing auras of fifty elite youths from the five southern kingdoms.

Among the five kingdoms, the Great Yue Kingdom and the Black-Wind Kingdom were the strongest, each boasting three disciples who had already broken through the Sixth Stage of Body Transformation to condense a Houtian True Essence Cyclone: - Standing at the head of the Great Yue Kingdom was Chu Kuang, an eighteen-year-old giant of a youth carrying a nine-ringed golden broadsaber, whose cultivation had reached the Middle Houtian Realm with over 32,000 jin of surging saber essence! - And leading the Black-Wind Kingdom was Yan Luo, a narrow-eyed eighteen-year-old youth in blood-trimmed black leather armor holding a pair of serrated wind-blades, who stood at the Peak of the Early Houtian Realm (26,000 jin)!

When Yan Luo’s gaze swept across the ten disciples of the Azure Sky Kingdom, he immediately spotted the sixteen-year-old youth at the very front carrying the eight-foot-six-inch Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear—matching the exact description in Third Prince Zhao Heng’s secret letter.

“Palace Master Qin, has your Azure Sky Kingdom run out of people this year?” Yan Luo sneered loudly so all пять (five) kingdoms’ delegations could hear, tapping his serrated wind-blades together with a metallic ring. “Putting a sixteen-year-old boy who hasn’t even condensed a Houtian Cyclone in the #1 lead spot ahead of Early Houtian Murong Zi? Inside the Ancient Sky-Fall Battlefield, Third-Order beasts don’t care about nursery games—be careful your #1 doesn’t lose his spear-arm on the first day!”

Behind Lu Chen, the other Azure Sky disciples flushed with anger, and Murong Zi stepped half a pace forward with his hand on his Star-River Sword, saying coldly, “Yan Luo, Junior Brother Lu took #1 in our Martial House by defeating me head-on.”

Lu Chen rested a calm hand on Murong Zi’s shoulder and looked evenly at Yan Luo without a ripple of anger.

“Whether a spear is sharp isn’t tested with the mouth, Yan Luo,” Lu Chen said quietly. “The canyon gates are opening—we’ll see on the Sky-Fall Stele in seven days.”
