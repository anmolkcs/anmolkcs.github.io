However, our scouts reported yesterday that the <strong>Vice-Patriarch of the Nether-Blood Cult</strong> (<strong>Xue Wuya</strong>, a ruthless <strong>Third Revolution / Second Life-Renewal</strong> overlord of the Life-and-Death Wheel Realm) is also heading toward Jingde-Star in secret to seize the <strong>Earth-Core Nirvana Glaze</strong> and avenge Elder Yin-Sha!"</p>
