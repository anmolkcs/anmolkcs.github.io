Descending from two thousand feet in the sky on a cushion of golden-purple Xiantian Origin Qi, Lu Chen landed lightly on the viewing cliff right in front of his parents Lu Shan and Meng Wan, Elder Tie, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang.

Before the Sect Master or the Grand Elders could even speak, Lu Chen dropped to one knee on the flagstones before his father and mother with a bright, warm smile:

"Father, Mother—your son has stepped into the Xiantian Realm!"

"Good! Good! Our Lu family has produced a sixteen-year-old Xiantian Grandmaster who can walk across the clouds!" Lu Shan laughed until tears rolled down his cheeks, pulling Lu Chen up by the elbows while Meng Wan brushed a stray spark of golden tribulation static from his sleeve, her heart overflowing with relief that her son had crossed the heavenly lightning unharmed.

Noticeably, because the golden rain of Xiantian Law-Blessing had fallen most densely around the viewing cliff where Lu Shan and Meng Wan stood, and because they had already drunk five pounds of the Innate Acoustic Star-Marrow over the past ten days, both Lu Shan and Meng Wan's auras surged on the spot—breaking cleanly through to the Middle Houtian Realm together!

"Today is a double celebration for our Seven Star Valley Sect!" Sect Master Tian Shu declared joyfully, presenting Lu Chen with the Purple-Star Xiantian Elder & Core Pillar Medallion. "At sixteen years old, Lu Chen has stepped into the Xiantian Realm with combat power rivaling a Late Xiantian Peak Master! From today onward, Lu Chen holds the status of Honorary Peak Co-Master of Broken Spear Peak!"

That evening, during the warm celebration dinner at Warm-Spring Courtyard #1, as Lu Chen used Prism Stillness to carefully examine his parents' Middle Houtian meridians—and Elder Tie's old lung-meridian scar—he noticed a crucial detail:

Because his parents had begun cultivating in their late thirties rather than at age twelve, their meridian walls were slightly stiffer than a young prodigy's. While they could reach Late and Peak Houtian smoothly using his inscribed pills and Star-Marrow, if they tried to shatter their Upper Dantian barriers to enter the Xiantian Realm in the future, their mortal meridian walls would risk tearing under the impact unless reinforced beforehand with a rare treasure:

Rank 4 Innate Spirit-Silk woven by the Meridian-Weaver Guild of Loom-Star City across the Thousand-Fathom Wind-Chasm, paired with Millennium Halite-Marrow Lotuses from the Inland Abyssal Salt-Sea!
