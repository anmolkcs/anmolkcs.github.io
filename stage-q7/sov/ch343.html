When Lu Chen and Su Qingxue returned to Myriad-Loom Star-City displaying the shattered banners of the three slain Lesser World King Branch Lords, the entire Southern Outer Star-Continent erupted like a boiling ocean!

For three thousand years, the independent sects, artisan guilds, and surviving vassals of the Primordial Chrono-Yin Clan across the Outer Star-Continent had been ground under the iron heel of the Blood-Sovereign, Flame-Sovereign, and Bone-Sovereign Clans' Outer Citadels, forced to pay 70% of their Star-Essence Crystals in blood-tax every decade.

Now, seeing eighteen-year-old World King Lu Chen and the reborn Princess Su Qingxue slay four Lesser World Kings in a single month, Old Guild Master Shen Cang of the Artisan-Star Guild—joined by twelve Inner-World Sect Masters and the thirty Rank 6 & Rank 7 Inscription Smiths Lu Chen had rescued at the Star Ring—knelt in unison on Star-Bell Plaza to form the Chrono-Star Alliance!

"Wait no longer for the three Sovereign Clans to summon reinforcements from the Central Imperial Sea," Lu Chen declared from the prow of The Primordial Star-Ark, his 180,000-jin Primordial World-Sovereign Spear gleaming in the light of the three suns. "In seven days, we strike all three Outer-Continent Citadels in sequence—starting with the Flame-Sovereign Clan's Nine-Drake Fire Citadel!"
