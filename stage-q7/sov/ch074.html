On his third morning in the Seven Star Valley Sect, Lu Chen walked across the rainbow suspension bridge to Pill-Star Peak, where Su Wan’er was waiting for him outside the Grand Inscription Hall.

In the Seven Star Valley Sect, even among ten thousand Houtian disciples, fewer than twenty had passed the Official Rank 3 Inscription Master assessment—because a Rank 3 Inscription required Early Xiantian (or mutated Peak Houtian) soul force and the ability to engrave Grade 3 Earth-Step / Peak Human-Step spirit runes onto Houtian treasures and Rank 3 pills!

Presiding over the assessment hall was Grandmaster Gu, a Middle Xiantian elder and the Deputy Peak Master of Pill-Star Peak, alongside a proud twenty-two-year-old youth in silver robes—Situ Hao, a Late Houtian genius from Alkaid Sword Peak (Ranked #48 on the Sect’s prestigious Heavenly Dragon Roll) who had come to commission a Rank 3 Wind-Thunder Rune for his sword.

Seeing a sixteen-year-old Early Houtian freshman step forward to apply for the Rank 3 Inscription Master badge, Situ Hao let out a dismissive chuckle:

“Junior Sister Su Wan’er, is this the #1 freshman from your backwater Azure Sky Kingdom? Even Pill-Star Peak’s senior apprentices spend five years at the Late Houtian Realm before daring to touch a Rank 3 Talisman Jade. Does he think the Grand Inscription Hall is a country bazaar?”

Lu Chen didn’t argue with Situ Hao. Stepping up to the white-jade assessment altar before Grandmaster Gu, he picked up a Third-Order Star-Weasel Brush, ground Rank 3 Starlight Cinnabar & Third-Order Thunder-Drake Blood, and entered Prism Stillness.

Instead of choosing a basic entry-level Rank 3 diagram, Lu Chen selected a Rank 3 Middle-Tier Pill & Treasure Dual Assessment: 1. In the first half hour, spinning a fragile Rank 3 Houtian Cyclone-Consolidating Pill in midair, his brush danced through 360 flawless three-dimensional silver runes, completing a Rank 3 Nine-Star Essence-Locking Pill Rune that purged 98% of the pill toxins and boosted efficacy to 300%! 2. In the second half hour, on a blank Grade 3 Lower-Tier Iron-Wood Shield, he engraved a 540-stroke Rank 3 Crushing-Thunder Defense Array in a single unbroken breath!

HUM—ZAP!

When the golden-purple rune light sank seamlessly into the Grade 3 shield without a single hair of energy leakage, Grandmaster Gu stood up so fast his jade tea cup shattered on the floor!

“Three hundred percent efficacy on a Rank 3 Pill Inscription—and zero soul-force tremor across 540 continuous Rank 3 strokes?!” Grandmaster Gu’s beard trembled with excitement as he pinned a Gold-Trimmed Rank 3 Inscription Grandmaster Badge onto Lu Chen’s chest on the spot! “From today onward, Lu Chen holds the same privileges on Pill-Star Peak as an Inner Sect Elder, with ten hours of monthly access to the Starlight Essence Pool!”

Behind them, Situ Hao’s mocking smile froze solid on his face, his ears burning red as Grandmaster Gu waving him aside said, “Situ Hao, your sword inscription wait-time just moved back a month—Master Lu Chen has priority over the main furnace room!”
