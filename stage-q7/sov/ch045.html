At dawn on the twentieth day, the summit of Seven Star Mountain was draped in ceremonial crimson-and-gold banners as every elder, instructor, and all three thousand outer, inner, and core disciples gathered around the Grand Star Ranking Arena.

Sitting on the high golden dais at the northern end of the arena were the three supreme figures of the Seven Star Martial House: - Palace Master Qin (a true Early Xiantian Realm powerhouse whose aura merged with heaven and earth); - Grand Marshal Su (Su Wan’er’s grandfather, an iron-blooded Peak Houtian general); - And Third Prince Zhao Heng, representing the Crown Prince’s Faction of the Azure Sky Royal Family!

In the center of the arena stood the thirty-foot-tall Core Ranking Stone, where the names of the current Top 10 Core Disciples gleamed in golden script: 1. Murong Zi (Peak Sixth Stage: Pulse Condensation — 18 years old, Grade 5 Upper-Tier Talent) 2. Ouyang Ming (Middle-to-Late Sixth Stage: Pulse Condensation — 20 years old, Grade 5 Middle-Tier Talent) 3. Su Wan’er (Late Sixth Stage: Pulse Condensation — 16 years old, Grade 6 Lower-Tier Talent) 4. Zhao Kuo (Early Sixth Stage: Pulse Condensation — 19 years old) … 9. Lu Chen (Fifteen years old — First-year Freshman!)

“Today’s Semi-Annual Core Ranking Tournament follows the open ascending challenge rule,” Palace Master Qin declared, his quiet voice echoing clearly in every disciple’s ears. “Any Top 10 Core Disciple may challenge those ranked above them. At the conclusion of today’s matches, the Top 3 Core Disciples will receive one hundred Low-Grade True Essence Stones, a Grade 3 Human-Step Treasure from the Royal Vault, and a Thirty-Day Cultivation Quota inside the Core Profound Gravity & Earth-Fire Cave!”

In the early rounds, #3 Su Wan’er—who had broken through smoothly to the Late Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation) thanks to Lu Chen’s three 280%-potency Rank 2 Inscribed Pills—easily defeated a challenge from #4 Zhao Kuo in ten moves of her Celestial Frost-Zither Sword, cementing her hold on the Top 3!

Then, #2 Ouyang Ming stood up from the core seats, leaped onto the center of the Grand Star Ranking Arena in a swirl of blazing crimson True Essence, and pointed the tip of his Peak Grade 2 Raging-Flame Longsword straight at Seat #9:

“Before I exchange pointers with Senior Sister Su Wan’er, I remember a certain #9 Freshman boasting twenty days ago that he wanted one of the three spots in the Profound Gravity Cave!” Ouyang Ming’s voice boomed across the arena as he glanced toward the Third Prince on the dais. “Lu Chen, get up here! Let everyone in the Seven Star Martial House see whether a Fifth-Stage country frog can survive three breaths against a Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation) master!”

Under the gaze of three thousand disciples and the Xiantian Palace Master, Lu Chen stood up from Seat #9, lifted his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear, and stepped calmly onto the Grand Star Ranking Arena.
