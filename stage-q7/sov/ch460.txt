<p>The southern gate of Ash-Brook Village groaned open, and the twelve-man column marched straight into the teeth of the Great Solstice Blizzard.</p>
<p>Out on the exposed mountain road leading south toward <strong>Howling-Wind Pass</strong>, the wind did not merely blow—it shrieked down the funnel of the peaks with a physical weight that could knock an unrooted mortal off his feet under tenfold gravity. Snow and gritty black shale-dust whipped horizontally across the trail, cutting visibility down to less than six paces.</p>
<p>Yet what would have been a fatal death-march for an unprepared caravan was the exact cover Lu Chen had planned for.</p>
<p>With the quarter-inch high-carbon steel runners Lu Chen had forged under the two oak sledges, the two thousand jin of iron bars and gear glided smoothly over the packed ice-crust. Six men in rotating teams hauled each sledge from inside the shelter of the raised <strong>Iron-Clad Oak Side-Boards</strong>, while Lu Chen walked ten paces ahead of the lead sledge as the lone pathfinder.</p>
<p>Even in a blinding whiteout where normal eyes could see nothing beyond five paces and the howling wind drowned out every shout, Lu Chen's <strong>Three-Pace Prism Stillness</strong> and his <strong>Thirty-Three Drawn Bowstring Sinews</strong> turned him into an unerring snow-compass. Through the soles of his boots, the taut Achilles and spinal sinews sensed every hidden frost-crevasse, ice-sheet, and loose boulder beneath the snowdrifts before the sledges reached them, while hismatte-bronze skin and <strong>Complete Tier Two</strong> iron-flesh generated such steady internal furnace-heat that the driven snow melted and evaporated half an inch before touching his eyelashes.</p>
<p>For four hours, they pushed steadily southward through the storm, covering the nine miles to the northern entrance of <strong>Howling-Wind Pass</strong> by late morning.</p>
<p>Ahead of them, two sheer vertical walls of black slate rose a hundred feet on either side of the frozen creek-bed, squeezing the mountain road into a winding, fifty-foot-wide stone corridor where the blizzard wind accelerated into a deafening, continuous roar like ten thousand war-drums.</p>
<p>One mile inside the pass—just before the sharp S-bend where the ruined stone watch-fort and <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> six-foot ice-and-boulder barricade blocked the road—Lu Chen raised his left fist.</p>
<p>Behind him, the two iron-clad sledges halted immediately against the sheltered inner curve of the cliff wall.</p>
<p>Uncle Shi crept forward through the swirling snow to Lu Chen's shoulder, squinting into the grey whiteout ahead. "We're two hundred yards from the S-bend barricade, Lu Chen. Even in this blizzard, Han Wu will have two lookouts huddled behind the ice-wall with a signal gong, while the rest of his eighteen men stay warm around a fire inside the roofless stone watch-fort thirty yards behind the barricade."</p>
<p>Lu Chen looked up at the sheer, snow-plastered fifty-foot slate buttress forming the inner corner of the S-bend—a near-vertical rock face coated in black ice that directly overlooked the rear courtyard of the ruined stone watch-fort and the back of the ice-barricade.</p>
<p>To a Tier-One or Tier-Two warrior under tenfold gravity, climbing a fifty-foot vertical black-ice cliff in a sixty-mile-an-hour gale was suicide.</p>
<p>To Lu Chen—with <strong>Thirty-Three Drawn Bowstring Sinews</strong> and a solid eighty-jin iron spear—that overhang was an open highway straight onto <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> head.</p>
<p>"Lock the two iron-clad sledges together across the mouth of the S-bend and drop the hinged side-boards," Lu Chen instructed Uncle Shi in a low voice that cut cleanly under the wind. "Give me three minutes to climb the black-ice buttress above the fort. When you hear my spear strike from above, advance the sledge-barricade around the bend and open fire with all four Triple-Prod Crossbows!"</p>
