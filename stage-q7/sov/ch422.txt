<p>Old Tie Shan looked from the four skinny, determined village boys lined up by the bellows lever, to the foot-treadle vise bolted around his anvil, and finally to Lu Chen, who was already raking glowing red embers into the stone hearth.</p>
<p>For forty years, the old blacksmith had believed that a man without a clan or a spiritual root in the Great Desolate Origin Universe could only wait like an ox for the butcher's knife when calamity struck. Yet standing in his smoky shed before dawn, watching a stranger he had pulled out of a mud gully ten days ago refuse to let his son die in Pit #7, a fierce, hot ember seemed to relight inside Old Tie Shan's sixty-year-old chest.</p>
<p>"Get out of my way, then, you clumsy pups!" Old Tie Shan barked, though his voice cracked thick with emotion as he sat down heavily on the backed log stool four feet from the hearth, where he could see straight into the tuyere nozzle. "Shi Tou! Bring that lever down smooth—not jerky like you're hauling a goat! Lu Chen! Bank the wet fines around the outer rim of the charcoal bed so we build a hollow bee-hive dome! If we want <strong>Grade-One Refined Cold-Slag Iron</strong> that even Scarface Luo Shan's clerks can't fault, every single twenty-pound billet has to be folded and hammered <strong>nine times</strong> until fifty jin of raw ore shrinks down to ten jin of pure dark-blue steel-iron!"</p>
<p><em>WHOOOOOOOSH—!</em></p>
<p>Shi Tou threw his tough fourteen-year-old frame into the bellows handle, his left ankle locked straight in the boot Lu Chen had wedged for him. A roaring jet of winter air blasted into the hollow charcoal dome, driving the temperature inside the stone hearth past cherry-red, past bright orange, to a blinding, sputtering lemon-white!</p>
<p>"First billet—turn it left a quarter-roll!" Old Tie Shan shouted from his stool, squinting his good right eye into the glaring core of the fire. "Wait for the blue sulfur-flame to die down—NOW! Pull it before the skin burns!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen moved with the calm, terrifying economy of a man whose mind rested at the bottom of a frozen lake.</p>
<p>With his left hand gripping the long iron tongs, he plucked the sputtering thirty-pound bloom of <strong>Black-Marrow Ore</strong> out of the white-hot furnace, swung it across to the anvil face in half a heartbeat, and stamped his left heel down onto the oak treadle!</p>
<p><em>CLACK!</em></p>
<p>The serrated iron jaws of the mounted vise bit down on the glowing bloom, pinning it rock-solid against the anvil. In the same fluid motion, Lu Chen's left hand joined his right on the shaft of the forty-pound iron sledgehammer.</p>
<p>"Corner strike—three blows on the heel to bleed the spiral slag!" Old Tie Shan roared.</p>
<p><em>BOOM—CLANG!</em> <em>BOOM—CLANG!</em> <em>BOOM—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Three earth-shaking blows of the forty-pound sledge fell within three breaths. Under the <strong>Early Tier One (Ash-Skin Scouring)</strong> strength of nine hundred and fifty jin surging through Lu Chen's back and shoulders—multiplied by the centrifugal whip of the four-foot ash shaft—jets of impure yellow-white slag burst out of the bottom of the bloom like squeezed water from a sponge!</p>
<p>"Lift heel! Quarter-turn! Flat draw—twelve blows from center to tip!"</p>
<p><em>Clack—BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</em></p>
<p>Left heel releasing for a tenth of a second to flick the glowing bar ninety degrees with the hand-tongs, left heel stamping down to lock the vise, both hands whirling the forty-pound sledge in a blinding vertical figure-eight!</p>
<p>The heat radiating three feet off the thirty-pound white-hot bloom was ferocious enough to singe eyebrows and crack dry wood. Every time the forty-pound hammer struck, a ring of incandescent iron-scale exploded outward across Lu Chen's bare chest, arms, and face.</p>
<p>Ten days ago, those flying white-hot scales had torn bloody pits in his mortal skin.</p>
<p>Now, as hundreds of glowing sparks pelted his chest and shoulders, they struck the third layer of <strong>Granny Liu's Blood-Briar Paste</strong> baked into his <strong>Early Tier One</strong> epidermis—sizzling against his sweat and actually driving the trace mineral iron deeper into the cross-linked collagen of his hide!</p>
<p>By noon, when Er Gou relieved a soaked, gasping Shi Tou at the bellows lever, three ten-jin bars of nine-folded <strong>Grade-One Refined Cold-Slag Iron</strong>—shining with a dense, oil-dark blue luster completely superior to the standard village mattocks—lay cooling on the slate rack!</p>
