Twenty thousand miles east of the Northern Chrono-Rift Prefecture, the sky above the Outer Star-Continent rose into an awe-inspiring astronomical phenomenon where the boundary between the planetary atmosphere and the starry void blurred into a sea of white-hot metallic fire:

The Ten-Thousand-Forge Star Ring (Wan-Duan Xing-Huan)!

Suspended fifty thousand feet above a desolate basalt plateau spun a collapsed, fifty-mile-wide Ancient White-Dwarf Star-Core that had fallen from the deep cosmos during the primordial era. Orbiting that blinding white-hot dwarf star in three concentric rings were ten thousand metallic asteroids composed of ultra-dense Seventh-Order and Eighth-Order Dwarf-Star Heavy Iron, connected by colossal Rank 7 Saint-Grade Chain-Bridges built by ancient weapon-forging grandmasters!

Because the collapsed White-Dwarf Star-Core radiated One-Hundred-Fold Stellar Gravity and temperatures exceeding a hundred thousand degrees of Astral Solar-Fire, not even an ordinary Peak Inner Embryo World cultivator could fly within ten miles of the inner ring without having their Inner World scorched dry!

When Lu Chen, Su Qingxue, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang stepped off The Primordial Star-Ark onto Outer Forge-Platform #1 of the Star Ring (having stored the ark safely inside Lu Chen's 1,000-mile Golden-Jade Inner World), they saw hundreds of upper-realm master smiths from across the Outer Star-Continent hammering Saint-Artifact embryos on the outer asteroids.

However, today the entire Middle and Inner Ring of the Ten-Thousand-Forge Star Ring had been cordoned off by a domineering cordon of crimson-gold fire-banners bearing the insignia of a nine-headed fire drake:

The Flame-Sovereign Clan—the most aggressive weapon-forging hegemon among the Seven Sovereign Clans!

"Why are the chain-bridges to the Middle Ring sealed?" Chu Kuang asked an elderly Middle Inner Embryo World dwarf-smith sitting by a cooled anvil on Platform #1.

The old smith sighed bitterly, pointing up toward the blazing white-hot Inner Ring five miles closer to the White-Dwarf Star-Core:

"Three days ago, the Central Star-Anvil Asteroid in the Inner Ring cracked open from a solar flare, exposing a three-cubic-foot block of Eighth-Order World-King Metal: Primordial Dwarf-Star Marrow-Iron—along with a hibernating Early Eighth-Order (Lesser World King Level!) Beast: The Dwarf-Star Flame-Leviathan! The Young Patriarch of the Flame-Sovereign Clan's Outer Branch—Yan Wuji (Half-Step Lesser World King — 2,500-Mile Half-Step Minor World!)—brought fifty inner-world experts and a Seventh-Order Peak Star-Net Array here to trap the Eighth-Order Leviathan and monopolize the Dwarf-Star Marrow-Iron! Worse, they forcibly conscripted thirty independent Rank 6 and Rank 7 Inscription Smiths from our outer platforms to act as living array-batteries to absorb the Leviathan's solar-fire backlash!"

Hearing that the Flame-Sovereign Clan—one of the very Seven Sovereign Clans that had besieged Su Qingxue's clan—was using thirty innocent independent smiths as living shields to seize the exact Eighth-Order Dwarf-Star Marrow-Iron and Eighth-Order Leviathan Lu Chen needed for his spear and the Chrono-Soul Prism, Lu Chen's dark eyes flashed with cold purple-gold lightning.

"Stay on Platform #1 with Murong Zi and Chu Kuang to guard the outer bridge, Senior Sister Wan'er," Lu Chen said calmly, stepping toward the sealed Middle-Ring Chain-Bridge alongside Su Qingxue. "Qingxue and I are going to the Inner Ring to free those smiths—and take the Dwarf-Star Marrow-Iron."
