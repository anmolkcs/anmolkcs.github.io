The instant Lu Chen’s fingertips grazed the pale-crimson Technique Bubble, it dissolved into a torrent of pure, nameless runic diagrams and meridian charts that surged straight between his eyebrows.

“Ugh—!”

On the wooden plank bed inside the shed, Lu Chen’s physical body arched backward like a drawn bow. Every blood vessel across his neck and temples stood out against his skin, and he clamped his teeth down on a folded hemp towel he had prepared in advance.

Even though the Chrono-Soul Prism had completely stripped away every trace of the original owner’s personal identity, ego, and world memories—leaving only pure, crystal-clear martial and runic techniques—assimilating decades of high-density technical diagrams into the mind of a fifteen-year-old youth still felt like having hot iron characters branded onto his mind!

Hold steady!

Lu Chen forced his consciousness into the deepest layer of Prism Stillness.

Like a reef standing firm in the middle of a surging tide, his mind anchored itself to the cold gray wall of the Chrono-Soul Prism, absorbing and organizing the stream of pure technique imprints line by line through sheer willpower.

One quarter of an hour. Two quarters of an hour.

By the time the last wave of mental pressure subsided, Lu Chen’s hemp shirt was drenched in sweat. He set aside the towel, his chest rising and falling deeply as he leaned against the mud-brick wall and closed his eyes to inspect the pure technical inheritance inside his mind.

True to the Prism’s nature, there was not a single word about who the fallen practitioner had been or what world they had lived in—only two pristine, systematic bodies of knowledge suspended like glowing scrolls in his consciousness:

First was a complete compendium of Foundational Runic Inscription Arts—containing seventy-two Weapon, Armor, and Spirit-Herb Inscription Diagrams (ranging from Rank 1 to Rank 3), along with harmonic material-purification methods for mixing blood-cinnabar ink and controlling brush strokes with micro-fluctuations of soul force!

In the Sky Spill Continent, Inscription Masters were even rarer and more revered than alchemists. Why? Because an ordinary Grade 1 Human-Step sword, once engraved with a single Rank 1 Inscription Symbol, could see its sharpness and True Essence conductivity increase by 30% to 50%! Even more miraculously, a Pill Inscription placed onto a medicinal pill could lock in 90% of the escaping medicinal efficacy that normally dissipated during refining, turning a single pill into the equivalent of two or three!

Yet Inscription Masters were rarer than phoenix feathers because a single symbol required drawing hundreds of interlocking elemental runes in midair using pure soul force without a single hair’s breadth of error; fail on the ninety-ninth stroke, and all the expensive materials turned to ash.

With the Prism’s pure Inscription technique templates and my Prism Stillness to monitor every brush stroke, Lu Chen realized, his eyes brightening in the dark shed, I have a way to earn True Essence Stones and refine my own body-tempering medicines!

Then, Lu Chen turned his attention to the second intact memory shard—a dark bronze scroll etched into the deepest corner of Cang Wu’s memory:

《Chaotic Star Body Art — Opening Volume: The Six Mortal Gates》

In the Sky Spill Continent, ordinary body-tempering manuals simply taught martial artists how to circulate氣血 (blood-qi) to harden muscles and bones. Even top-tier manuals in the Seven Star Martial House only allowed a genius to reach 5,000 to 6,000 jin of strength at the Peak of the Fifth Stage (Bone Forging).

The Chaotic Star Body Art, however, was the foundational physical conditioning manual used by outer disciples of an ancient Divine Realm sect!

Instead of passively hardening muscle fibers, its core breathing technique—the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath—treated every muscle fiber, tendon, and bone cell in the human body as a tightly coiled metallic spring. By inhaling and compressing blood-qi through thirty-six specific micro-meridian nodes while performing high-intensity physical strikes, the practitioner twisted their muscle fibers into nine-layered spiral strands, and later tempered their marrow with heavy mineral and beast-blood essences.

A martial artist who cultivated the Chaotic Star Body Art to perfection through the Six Mortal Gates would not stop at 5,000 jin at the Bone Forging stage—they would awaken Ten Thousand Jin of Primordial Dragon-Elephant Strength, able to crush ordinary Houtian practitioners with their bare hands before even condensing a drop of True Essence!

More shockingly, the manual contained a section called Meridian Tempering via Kinetic Resonance.

“Talent in the Sky Spill Continent is judged by how open one’s meridians are at age twelve,” Lu Chen whispered, his pulse quickening as he read the bronze scroll’s annotations. “People here believe meridian width is fixed at birth unless one consumes heavenly spirit fruits worth ten thousand gold. Wait—according to the Chaotic Star Body Art, clogged mortal meridians can be gradually scoured and widened from the inside by vibrating blood-qi through the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath during extreme physical exertion!”

In other words, his pathetic Grade 2 Upper-Tier talent was not a permanent shackle. As long as he was willing to endure ten times the physical agony of ordinary cultivators and feed his body enough beast blood and medicinal herbs, his meridians would slowly widen with every realm he broke through!

Lu Chen stood up from the plank bed in the cramped shed, ignoring the soreness in his joints.

He didn’t have rare spirit pills tonight, nor did he have an alchemy cauldron. What he had were two taels of silver in his pouch, the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath in his mind, and a kitchen yard full of tough spirit beast carcasses waiting for him at dawn.
