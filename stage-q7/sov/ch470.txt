<p>On the morning of the third day of the twelfth month, as the great bronze drum atop the Black-Rock Town Bell-Tower sounded the opening of the winter tax courts, a crowd of more than two hundred village elders, town merchants, and armored garrison patrols gathered in the vast flagstones square in front of the <strong>Heaven-Shackling Bailiff Tally-House</strong>.</p>
<p>The Tally-House looked less like a civil office and more like a black-iron prison fortress. Built from massive ten-ton blocks of dark basalt, its twenty-foot bronze double-doors were embossed with the relief of <strong>Twelve Coiled Chains Binding the Sun and Moon</strong>.</p>
<p>Out on the stone terrace in front of the bronze doors sat the <strong>Public Stone Balance</strong>—a colossal iron-beam scale capable of weighing three thousand jin at once—presided over by <strong>Senior Clerk Master Sun</strong>, an elderly, white-bearded official in grey brocade robes who represented the <strong>Black-Rock County Magistrate's Treasury</strong>, flanked by four <strong>Tier-Three</strong> Bailiff Enforcers.</p>
<p>And standing at the top of the steps with a cup of hot spiced wine in his hand, wearing a fresh wolf-skin cloak over his black iron mail and his ninety-jin iron whip-rod at his hip, was <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan (Early Tier Four)</strong>!</p>
<p>Zhao Kuan was currently laughing with a scarred captain from the Black-Rock Garrison, waiting for the northern ridge villages to fail to appear so he could submit the default forfeiture warrants before noon.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the rhythmic <em>clomp-clomp-clomp</em> of iron-scaled hooves and the low rumble of steel-shod sledge runners echoed up the main flagstone avenue!</p>
<p>Through the parting crowd marched the eight men of <strong>Ash-Brook Village</strong>, led by <strong>Lu Chen</strong>, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, and sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>, pulling their two iron-clad oak sledges directly up to the foot of the Tally-House terrace!</p>
<p>Up on the steps, the smile froze on Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan's square face. His eyes narrowed in shock as he looked past the sledges into the morning mist—waiting to see <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> and how the caravan could have possibly passed through the blockade at Howling-Wind Pass without losing a single man!</p>
<p>"Ash-Brook Village—and Mud-Well Village!" Uncle Shi's booming voice carried across the entire square as he stepped forward and presented both villages' wooden tax-tallies to Senior Clerk Sun. "Delivering Mud-Well Village's full winter quota of three hundred jin of pig-iron—and delivering <strong>eight hundred and twelve jin of Grade-One Nine-Folded Cold-Slag Iron</strong> to redeem the three-year bond and the fifty-tael axle-debt of <strong>Tie Niu, Convict #704 of Deep-Shaft Pit #7</strong>!"</p>
<p>At the mention of eight hundred and twelve jin of Grade-One Nine-Folded Cold-Slag Iron coming from a seventy-person mountain village whose blacksmith only had one arm, a loud buzz of disbelief swept through the two hundred spectators in the square!</p>
<p>Before Zhao Kuan could open his mouth to shout down the clerks, Lu Chen stepped to the rear of the lead sledge, tossed off the oiled canvas tarpaulin, and grabbed <strong>four ten-jin bars</strong> of the gleaming, oil-blue <strong>Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron</strong> in each hand—lifting eighty jin in each fist as if they were wooden blocks—and stacked all <strong>eighty bars (812 jin)</strong> onto the pan of the Public Stone Balance in ten trips!</p>
<p><em>CLANG—CLANG—CLANG—HUMMMMMM!</em></p>
<p>The massive iron beam of the Public Stone Balance groaned and tipped straight past the eight-hundred-jin mark, every single rectangular bar shining in the winter sunlight with the unmistakable, mirror-smooth, dark-blue grain of nine-folded <strong>Black-Marrow Core Steel</strong>!</p>
