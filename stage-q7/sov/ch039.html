“He wants to charge from Rank #56 into the Top 20 of the Killing Array Stele in a single attempt—and then challenge #11 Ranked Lin Sen on the arena stage right after?!”

The entire Killing Array Square exploded into an uproar as Lu Chen turned and strode onto Platform #1 at the center of the array!

Even inner-court elders who had been meditating in the nearby pavilions flew out onto the rooftops to watch.

In the Seven Star Martial House, the gap between Rank #56 and Rank #20 on the Killing Array Stele was a massive watershed: - Below Rank #50, the array phantoms were primarily Fourth Stage warriors with a solitary Early Fifth Stage leader. - To break into the Top 30, a challenger had to slaughter waves of Middle and Late Fifth Stage (Bone Forging) elite phantoms and Peak Second-Order Spirit Beasts. - And to break into the Top 20, one had to defeat the sixty-minute boss wave guarded by a Peak Fifth Stage / Half-Step Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation) Phantom Commander!

“Arrogant lunatic!” Lin Sen sneered coldly on the pavilion steps, suppressing the shock in his heart as he watched the blood-red pillar of light rise over Platform #1. “Even if he hid his strength or used some trap to survive Gui Yan in the mountains, the Ten Thousand Killing Array tests pure frontal combat power! It took me a full year at the Fifth Stage to reach Rank #11 with 1,850 Killing Points. If he dares step onto the arena stage against me after exhausting his stamina in the array, I’ll break every bone in his body cleanly within the rules!”

Inside the Ten Thousand Killing Array, however, a massacre unlike anything the array spirit had recorded in ten years had just begun.

On the desolate canyon plain, Lu Chen unwrapped the oilcloth from his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear.

BOOM—!

When the first thirty-minute fast-forward wave of twenty Fourth Stage & Early Fifth Stage Phantoms charged toward him, Lu Chen didn’t even use two hands.

Holding the 1,220-jin heavy spear in his right hand alone, his Golden-Jade Bones chimed like a temple bell as 9,000 jin of baseline strength surged through the 58% Rank 2 Dual Core Inscription—

《Wildfire Prairie Spear — Chapter Two: Wildfire Sweeps the Plains!》

ZAP—KABOOM!

A thirty-foot crescent tornado of dark-purple fire and silver lightning swept horizontally across the canyon! Every single Fourth and Early Fifth Stage phantom touched by the purple-thunder spear wind didn’t just break—they disintegrated into light motes instantly before coming within ten paces of Lu Chen!

Outside on the square, the hundred-foot Killing Array Ranking Stele began to flash with blinding gold light every five minutes:

  • 25 Minutes: 1,280 Killing Points — Rank #41!
  • 35 Minutes: 1,510 Killing Points — Rank #28!
  • 45 Minutes: 1,740 Killing Points — Rank #19! (Entering the Top 20!)

“Forty-five minutes—and he’s already passed #20!” an inner-court deacon shouted in disbelief. “He’s clearing Fifth-Stage Bone Forging waves three times faster than Lin Sen did last month!”

Inside the array at the fiftieth minute, the sky turned pitch black as the sixty-minute boss wave descended early due to Lu Chen’s terrifying kill rate: eight Peak Fifth Stage Iron-Pagoda Phantom Guards surrounding a Half-Step Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation) Blood-Armored General!

Instead of retreating to conserve stamina for his arena match with Lin Sen, Lu Chen let out a long, exhilarating whistle, his Golden Roc Nine-Flash Step carrying him straight into the sky above the nine bosses as he drove the 1,220-jin spear downward with the Seventh Wave of the Nine-Layered Wave Force:

《Wildfire Prairie Spear — Chapter Three: Meteor Pierces the Sun!》

CRACK-BOOM—!

Over 14,000 jin of cumulative purple-thunder spiral force crashed down like a falling star, obliterating the Half-Step Sixth Stage Phantom General and all eight guards in a single cataclysmic impact before Lu Chen voluntarily exited the array!

DONG—DONG—DONG!

Three golden bells tolled from the top of the hundred-foot Killing Array Ranking Stele as a blazing purple-gold line carved itself into the very top tier of the monument:

Rank #9: Lu Chen (Inner Abode — Middle Fifth Stage: Bone Forging) — Challenge Score: 2,040 Killing Points!
