<p>Two hundred miles of condensed golden starlight and interlocking bronze runes stretched across the roaring void above the <strong>Ten-Thousand-Mile Astral Storm-Trench</strong>, forming a highway across the heavens that opened only once every ten years:</p>
<p><strong>The Astral Ferry-Bridge!</strong></p>
<p>Below the translucent rune-floor of the bridge, silver-black <strong>Astral Wind-Blades</strong> and inverted rivers of purple planetary lightning churned thirty thousand feet down in the abyss—the very abyss where, less than two weeks ago, eighteen-year-old <strong>Lu Chen</strong> had slain the corrupted <strong>Early Sixth-Order Astral-Thunder Leviathan</strong> and re-anchored the ancient <strong>Sunken Bronze Dynasty Sky-Anchor</strong>.</p>
<p>Now, standing at the prow of the three-hundred-yard <strong>Grade 5 Spirit-Metal Sky-Barge</strong> as it glided smoothly across the rainbow span of the Astral Ferry-Bridge, Lu Chen rested his right hand lightly on the shaft of his newly forged nine-foot-six-inch, <strong>18,000-jin Star-Sovereign Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear</strong>.</p>
<p>Dressed in a clean black martial robe with his mother <strong>Meng Wan's</strong> braided red-silk <strong>Peace-and-Safety Peachwood Charm</strong> tied securely around his wrist, Lu Chen looked back one last time toward the southern bridgehead. There, bathed in the golden morning sun, his forty-two-year-old father <strong>Lu Shan</strong> and forty-year-old mother <strong>Meng Wan</strong>—both now <strong>Early Revolving Core Realm</strong> masters with <strong>Flawless Gold-Jade Cores</strong> and <strong>one-thousand-year lifespans</strong>—stood hand-in-hand beside <strong>Elder Tie Cang</strong>, <strong>Fang Shi</strong>, and <strong>Grand Marshal Su</strong>, waving until the southern peaks receded into the sea of clouds.</p>
<p>Touching the warm <strong>Rank 5 Peak Two-Way Spatial Teleportation Jade</strong> tucked inside his inner lapel—whose matching twin sat inside <strong>Warm-Spring Courtyard #1</strong> on Broken Spear Peak—Lu Chen smiled softly.</p>
<p>Unlike ordinary cultivators who left their families behind for decades when ascending to the <strong>Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain</strong>, he had already promised his parents: the moment he established his own peak courtyard inside the <strong>Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect</strong> and anchored the receiving spatial array, he would activate the Two-Way Spatial Jade and bring his father and mother straight across the border so their family would never be separated for more than a few days!</p>
<p>"Little friend Lu Chen," <strong>Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng</strong> (<strong>Second Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel Realm</strong>) walked up to the prow railing, his weathered face still carrying a look of sheer amazement as he sensed the deep, unshakeable vitality radiating from Lu Chen's spine. "When you shattered Elder Yin-Sha's First Life-Wheel above the trench eleven days ago, you were at the <strong>Peak of the Revolving Core Realm</strong>. Yet during the past ten days on Broken Spear Peak... you actually shattered your Twelve-Faceted Star-Crystal Core and completed the <strong>First Life-Renewal Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel Realm</strong> at eighteen years old?!"</p>
<p>Standing beside Lu Chen at the railing, eighteen-year-old <strong>Su Wan'er</strong> (<strong>Early Revolving Core — Flawless Zither-Star Core</strong>), twenty-year-old <strong>Murong Zi</strong> (<strong>Early Revolving Core — Flawless Sword-Star Core</strong>), and twenty-year-old <strong>Chu Kuang</strong> (<strong>Early Revolving Core — Gold-Scale Core</strong>) smiled with proud familiarity. Ever since the Entrance Trial of the Seven Star Martial House three years ago, watching Lu Chen turn impossible cultivation thresholds into flat cobblestones under his boots had become a law of nature to them.</p>
<p>"The Sixth-Order Astral-Thunder Leviathan's core-marrow happened to be rich in primordial life-essence, Senior Cang Feng," Lu Chen replied modestly, bowing with a calm smile. "Combined with the starlight vein on Broken Spear Peak, the breakthrough went smoothly."</p>
<p>"Smoothly?! Even in our <strong>Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain</strong>, where the heaven-and-earth origin energy is five times denser than the southern borderlands and where <strong>Second-Grade Continental Overlord Sects</strong> rule over hundreds of millions of miles, any prodigy who steps into the <strong>Life-and-Death Wheel Realm</strong> before age twenty-five is enshrined directly on the <strong>Continental Young Sovereign Roll</strong>!"</p>
<p>Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng laughed heartily, pointing northward as the Sky-Barge passed through the final spatial membrane at the northern end of the Astral Ferry-Bridge:</p>
<p>"Look ahead, all four of you! Welcome to the true heartland of our world—the <strong>Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain</strong>!"</p>
<p><em>HUMMMMM—!</em></p>
<p>As the Grade 5 Sky-Barge glided out of the rainbow bridge corridor, the clouds parted before a landscape of staggering, primordial grandeur.</p>
<p>In the Southern Region, a ten-thousand-foot mountain like Seven Star Mountain had been considered a towering sacred peak. Here in the <strong>Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain</strong>, endless chains of <strong>thirty-thousand-foot violet-gold mountain ranges</strong> floated half in the earth and half in the clouds, linked by waterfalls of liquid silver starlight!</p>
<p>Even more astonishing was the ambient atmosphere: every single breath of air contained dense <strong>Innate Origin Qi</strong> mixed with faint traces of <strong>Heaven-and-Earth Law Fragments</strong>! Just inhaling a single breath on the deck made the Revolving Cores inside Su Wan'er's, Murong Zi's, and Chu Kuang's dantians hum with joy, while inside Lu Chen's body, his <strong>First-Revolution Chaotic Star Dharma Body</strong> (<strong>3,500,000 jin</strong> baseline strength) resonated naturally with the heavier gravitational field of the Central Domain!</p>
<p>Inside Lu Chen's spiritual sea, standing within the fully bloomed <strong>Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus</strong> at the center of the nine-faceted <strong>Chrono-Soul Prism</strong>, <strong>Su Qingxue</strong> looked out through Lu Chen's perception at the thirty-thousand-foot mountain ranges and spoke softly in his mind:</p>
<p><em>"The gravitational constant and law density of this Central Imperial Domain are three times stronger than the southern borderlands because the <strong>Continental Star-Core</strong> is buried directly beneath <strong>Central Emperor City</strong>. More importantly, Lu Chen—I can already sense a faint resonance of the <strong>First Saint-Grade Lotus Treasure</strong> we need to reconstruct my physical body: the <strong>Nine-Cycles Reincarnation Root</strong>, somewhere in the ancient kiln-ruins to the east."</em></p>
<p><em>"Once we settle into the Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect and bring my parents over, Senior Su, finding the Three Saint-Grade Lotus Treasures is at the top of my list,"</em> Lu Chen transmitted back warmly.</p>
