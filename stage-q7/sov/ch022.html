On the morning of the eighth day since entering the Seven Star Martial House—the exact morning Lu Chen’s seven-day freshman protection period expired—heavy, arrogant footsteps echoed down the pine-shaded stone path leading to Courtyard #72.

Walking at the front was the scar-faced inner disciple Zhu Hong (Ranked #86 on the Killing Array Stele, Peak Fourth Stage: Altering Muscle & Tendons), flanked by his younger brother Zhu Ming, Zhang Cang, and over thirty outer and inner disciples who had come to watch the show. Ten paces behind them walked Lin Xuan, his hands clasped calmly behind his back as if merely passing by for a morning stroll.

BANG!

Zhu Hong didn’t knock on the courtyard gate; he raised his heavy iron-plated boot and kicked the wooden gate of Courtyard #72 with three thousand jin of force, blasting the iron bolt off its bracket and slamming the two gate doors open against the stone walls!

“Freshman Lu Chen!” Zhu Hong’s voice boomed like a cracked gong into the quiet courtyard. “Your seven-day protection period is up! According to the rules of the Seven Star Martial House, Pine-Wind Cliff’s private courtyards belong to those with the strength to hold them. Roll out here within ten breaths, hand over your courtyard jade key, and kowtow three times for making my younger brother wait outside—or I’ll drag you onto the Pine-Wind Challenge Stage and cripple your right arm for three months!”

Inside the meditation chamber, the heavy stone door slid open slowly.

Lu Chen stepped out into the morning sunlight, dressed in his clean black martial robe, with the eight-foot 820-jin Black Iron Heavy Spear resting across his right shoulder.

Over the past four days of digesting three Rune-Inscribed Origin-Gathering Pills and the Peak Second-Order Flame Lion Blood, Lu Chen had not only perfected the Peak of the Third Stage (Viscera Training)—he had stepped half a foot across the threshold into the Fourth Stage: Altering Muscle & Tendons!

Even without fully completing the Fourth Stage, his twelve major spinal and brachial tendons had already been forged once by the Chaotic Star Body Art, pushing his baseline physical power from 3,900 jin straight to a monstrous 4,800 jin—and when combined with the Fourth Wave of the Nine-Layered Wave Force, his burst strength exceeded 6,200 jin!

Lu Chen glanced once at the splintered wooden gate and the broken iron bolt on the ground, then raised his calm, dark eyes to Zhu Hong.

“Breaking a private courtyard gate without presenting an official Deacon Challenge Writ costs five Low-Grade True Essence Stones in damages,” Lu Chen said evenly. “Since you’re in such a hurry to step onto the Pine-Wind Challenge Stage, Zhu Hong, let’s add a side wager before the patrol deacon: if you lose, you pay twenty Low-Grade True Essence Stones—five for my gate, and fifteen for wasting my morning cultivation.”

“Twenty True Essence Stones? If I lose to a Third Stage freshman?!”

Zhu Hong stared at Lu Chen for a second before bursting into savage laughter, his thirty followers roaring with him.

“Everyone heard him! Patrol Deacon Wu is right there on the path—bear witness!” Zhu Hong slapped a leather pouch containing twenty glowing Low-Grade True Essence Stones onto the stone boundary pillar outside the gate, drawing a four-foot Grade 2 Human-Step Ghost-Head Broadsaber from his back. “I am at the Peak of the Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) with 5,200 jin of dragon-tiger strength, ranked #86 on the Killing Array Stele! Put your courtyard key on the pillar and step into the ring—if you don’t beg for mercy within three saber chops, I’ll shatter every tendon in your right shoulder!”

Thirty paces back under the pine trees, Lin Xuan’s eyes narrowed slightly as he watched Lu Chen walk unhurriedly into the twenty-yard ironstone challenge ring beside the cliff path.

Even if Lu Chen used some hidden body-tempering secret to reach 3,900 jin at the Middle Third Stage, Lin Xuan calculated coldly, a Peak Fourth Stage martial artist like Zhu Hong has already completed tendon alteration—his explosive speed and 5,200-jin saber force are on a completely different level. Today, I’ll see every card this country bumpkin is hiding—and watch his right arm break!
