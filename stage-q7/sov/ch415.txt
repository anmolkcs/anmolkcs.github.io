<p>The alpha female let out a ear-splitting shriek of pain and fury, twisting her massive neck so violently that the thirty-pound iron pry-bar bent five degrees in Lu Chen's hands!</p>
<p>Before Lu Chen could wrench the wedged chisel-tip free from the beast's spine, the other two <strong>Iron-Jaw Ridge Hyenas</strong> spun around in the narrow mud lane and lunged at his exposed flank from left and right simultaneously!</p>
<p>In the pitch-black fog, an ordinary village hunter would have been blind to the exact angle of their jaws. Within the three-pace sphere of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, however, every twitch of the hyenas' bristling hackles, the stink of rotten carrion on their breath, and the trajectory of their yellowing fangs registered in Lu Chen's mind with icy clarity.</p>
<p>He could not dodge both in the four-foot-wide doorway without exposing Widow Qin and little Xiao Ya behind him.</p>
<p><em>Take the left one on the forearm bone,</em> Lu Chen calculated in a hundredth of a breath, <em>and break the right one's nose with the butt of the bar!</em></p>
<p>Keeping his right hand anchored on the middle of the wedged pry-bar, Lu Chen whipped his left forearm up in a tight cross-guard directly into the gaping jaws of the left hyena—deliberately angling the thick, herb-crusted outer edge of his ulna bone toward the back of the beast's molars where its bite leverage was weakest—while snapping his right hip forward to drive the blunt iron butt of the pry-bar into the snout of the right-hand beast!</p>
<p><em>CRUNCH! WHACK!</em></p>
<p>The iron butt smashed the right hyena's nasal cartilage into a spray of black blood, sending it tumbling backward into the mud ruts. Simultaneously, the left hyena's serrated teeth slammed shut around Lu Chen's raised left forearm!</p>
<p>Even with twelve days of <strong>Granny Liu's Blood-Briar Paste</strong> hardening his outer hide, Lu Chen had not yet fully completed <strong>Tier One (Ash-Skin Scouring)</strong>. The beast's four-hundred-pound bite force punctured three-quarters of an inch through his blackened skin and muscle, grinding hard against the outer periosteum of his forearm bone while hot, septic blood poured down his elbow!</p>
<p>Yet the bone did not snap—and Lu Chen did not let go of the doorway!</p>
<p>Instead of pulling his arm back and letting the hyena's curved teeth rip his tendons out, Lu Chen stepped <em>into</em> the bite! Shoving his bleeding left forearm deeper into the back of the hyena's hinge-jaw to gag its throat, he freed his right hand, yanked the thirty-pound pry-bar out of the alpha female's neck with a spray of hot arterial blood, and brought the heavy iron bar down like a guillotine across the skull of the beast clamped onto his arm!</p>
<p><em>CLANG—KRAK!</em></p>
<p>The forty-pound striker's power he had honed over a thousand anvil blows caved in the hyena's parietal bone in a single strike! As the dead beast's jaws spasmed open, the wounded alpha female—bleeding furiously from her neck—lunged one last time at Lu Chen's chest!</p>
<p>Behind him in the doorway, Widow Qin thrust her short iron turnip-hoe with all her desperate strength into the alpha hyena's hind leg, making the beast's leap falter by two inches!</p>
<p>Those two inches were all Lu Chen needed.</p>
<p>Planting his back heel against the stone threshold of the hut, his chest heaving and blood streaming from his punctured left forearm, Lu Chen felt the violent, boiling surge of his own adrenaline collide with the twelve days of accumulated forge-heat and <strong>Blood-Briar Paste</strong> locked beneath his skin!</p>
<p><em>RIP—CRACKLE!</em></p>
<p>Like dry clay baking in a kiln that suddenly reaches peak vitrification temperature, the last remnants of soft mortal dead-flesh across his chest, arms, and back split and shed beneath his hemp shirt! A wave of fierce, metallic heat rushed from his heart out to every pore of his epidermis, tightening the dermal grain into a uniform, resilient layer of <strong>Early Tier One Mortal-Shedding: Ash-Skin Scouring</strong>!</p>
<p>With a surge of <strong>nine hundred jin</strong> of raw, compact physical strength coursing through his shoulders, Lu Chen drove the chisel-point of the thirty-pound pry-bar straight through the open mouth of the leaping alpha hyena and six inches out the back of its skull, pinning the thrashing carcass dead into the frozen mud!</p>
<p>Thirty yards up the lane, flaring orange pitch-torches and the twang of horn-bows finally broke through the fog as <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> (wielding a glowing red-hot iron tongs in his single hand), and four village hunters charged around the grain shed, Putting two iron arrows through the last wounded hyena as it tried to scramble back over the palisade!</p>
<p>"Widow Qin! Xiao Ya!" Uncle Shi roared, rushing up with his torch raised—only to stop dead in his tracks before the hut door.</p>
<p>In the red torchlight, Lu Chen stood planted in the narrow stone doorway like a blood-soaked iron statue. Two three-hundred-pound Iron-Jaw Ridge Hyenas lay dead at his boots, his thirty-pound iron pry-bar bent at an angle through the alpha's skull, while dark blood dripped steadily from the deep fang-punctures on his left forearm onto the snow.</p>
<p>Behind him, completely unharmed, little blind <strong>Xiao Ya</strong> reached out in the dark, found the hem of Lu Chen's blood-wet tunic, and held onto it with both tiny hands.</p>
