“A full incense stick… and his breathing didn’t even speed up?!”

Across the viewing terrace, the elders of the Seven Star Martial House exchanged shocked glances. In the last ten years of entrance trials, only Su Wan’er and one other monstrous prodigy—now the #1 Ranked Inner Disciple, Murong Zi—had ever sat through a full incense stick of the Illusion Array of Ten Thousand Wills without coughing blood!

Down at the edge of the square, Lin Xuan wiped the blood from his chin with a silk handkerchief, his eyes narrowing into cold slits as he stared at Lu Chen’s back. As the proud nephew of the Empress Consort’s clan, he had expected to sweep first place in every single round today, yet in the very first round, a nameless boy in coarse cotton from a third-rate city had stolen his thunder!

“A fluke,” Zhang Cang hissed beside Lin Xuan, trying to curry favor. “Some country bumpkins are born with thick, dull nerves so illusions don’t scare them as easily. Wait until the second round—the Pagoda of Crushing Gravity tests pure flesh-and-blood cultivation and meridian endurance! A borderline Grade 3 waste at the Early Third Stage won’t even make it past the third floor!”

As if echoing Zhang Cang’s words, Presiding Elder Sun stood up at the terrace railing and pointed toward a seven-story black-iron pagoda standing on the eastern cliff of Seven Star Mountain.

“Second and Final Round of the Entrance Trial: The Pagoda of Crushing Gravity!” Elder Sun’s voice echoed across the mountain peaks. “Inside the Pagoda, an ancient Earth- Vein Gravity Array increases the pull of gravity with every floor and every step! Moreover, from the third floor upward, condensed Origin Energy Shockwaves will slam against your bodies every ten breaths! Your final score is determined by how many steps you climb before being forced to your knees. Enter!”

The eighty-eight candidates who had survived the first round surged toward the open bronze doors of the black-iron pagoda.

Noticeably, before stepping through the bronze doors, every candidate unstrapped their swords, scabbards, and even heavy belt ornaments, tossing them into the storage baskets outside to reduce every possible ounce of burden on their bodies.

Lu Chen, however, walked straight through the bronze doors with the 820-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Heavy Spear still strapped tightly across his back!

“Look at #189—he didn’t even take off that heavy iron pole on his back before entering the Gravity Pagoda!” Zhang Cang sneered as he bounded onto the first-floor stairs. “Is he trying to crush his own kneecaps on the second floor?”

Hum—!

The moment Lu Chen’s boots stepped onto the dark ironstone floor of the First Story, a heavy downward pull settled over his shoulders—1.5 times normal gravity.

For Lu Chen’s body weighed down by an 820-jin spear, 1.5x gravity instantly turned the spear on his back into 1,230 jin of crushing dead weight!

Any ordinary Early Third Stage martial artist would have had their spine bent double on the spot.

Yet inside Lu Chen’s chest, his heart—tempered by the Scarlet Serpent Blood Paste and the 《Chaotic Star Body Art》—gave a deep, resonant BOOM like a war drum!

Why take off the spear? Lu Chen thought, a faint smile touching his lips as he climbed the stairs to the Second Story (2x Gravity) and then the Third Story (3x Gravity) without breaking his stride. In Cloudstride City, I had to boil medicinal baths just to push my muscles and viscera to their limits. Here in the Pagoda of Crushing Gravity, the Seven Star Martial House is providing a priceless Rank 4 Earth-Vein Gravity Array for free!

Before leaving the city inn this morning, Lu Chen had chewed and swallowed all three stalks of Three-Leaf Jade Marrow Grass he had obtained from Su Wan’er, storing their rich, cool medicinal essence deep inside his stomach and spleen.

Now, as he stepped onto the Fourth Story—where gravity surged to four times normal and his 820-jin spear weighed an astonishing 3,280 jin—over sixty of the eighty-eight candidates had already collapsed on the third-floor landing behind him!

Ahead on the fourth-floor staircase, only fifteen candidates were still climbing, led by Lin Xuan, Tuoba Lie, and Zhang Cang, their faces purple with exertion as their joints popped under the fourfold gravity and the periodic Origin Energy Shockwaves sweeping down from the ceiling.

BOOM—!

A visible wave of condensed silver origin energy crashed down the fourth-floor stairwell like a tidal wave!

At the ninth step of the fourth floor, Zhang Cang let out a strangled cry as both of his knees buckled, smashing onto the ironstone steps and sliding backward—eliminated at Fourth Floor, Step 9!

Just as Zhang Cang looked up weakly to see how far ahead Lin Xuan had gotten, a steady, metallic clank… clank… clank echoed right beside his ear.

Lu Chen—still carrying the massive oilcloth bundle on his back, which was pressing half-inch cracks into the ironstone stairs with every step—walked calmly past the kneeling Zhang Cang without even slowing down!

“You… you’re still carrying that iron pole on the fourth floor?!” Zhang Cang’s eyes nearly bulged out of their sockets.

Lu Chen didn’t answer. Inside his abdomen and chest, under the crushing 3,280-jin load and the battering Origin Energy Shockwaves, the stored medicinal essence of the three stalks of Three-Leaf Jade Marrow Grass burst open completely!

Driven by the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath, every drop of Jade Marrow essence was hammered into his five internal organs and the thirty-six micro-meridian nodes of his torso!

Step 15… Step 24… Step 30 of the Fourth Floor!

By the time Lu Chen stepped onto the Fifth Story (5x Gravity)—a floor that only Grade 4 Upper-Tier geniuses at the Fourth Stage of Body Transformation could ever reach—only Tuoba Lie (stopped at Fifth Floor, Step 4) and Lin Xuan (straining with every vein bulging at Fifth Floor, Step 11) remained ahead of him!
