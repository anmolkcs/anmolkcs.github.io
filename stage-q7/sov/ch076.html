At the end of his first month in the sect, when Eldest Senior Brother Fang Shi went to the Sect Resource Hall to collect Broken Spear Peak’s quarterly allocation of Star-Iron Ore, he returned with a bruised shoulder and a grim expression.

Situ Hao (Ranked #48 on the Heavenly Dragon Roll, Late Houtian Realm) and three inner disciples of Alkaid Sword Peak had intercepted Broken Spear Peak’s ore quota at the Resource Hall, claiming that since Broken Spear Peak had no disciples ranked in the Top 50 of the Heavenly Dragon Roll this quarter (after Fang Shi had been injured in a beast tide last month), half of Broken Spear Peak’s Star-Iron Ore quota should be reallocated to Alkaid Sword Peak!

According to the Seven Star Valley Sect’s rules, each peak’s extra resource quota was tied to how many disciples it held in the Top 50 of the Heavenly Dragon Roll (which ranked the top 100 Houtian disciples under age twenty-five across all seven peaks).

“Situ Hao deliberately challenged me while my left meridian was still recovering from the beast-tide poison, knocking me from Rank #49 down to #53 so he could seize our peak’s Star-Iron Ore—and he also wanted to retaliate against Junior Brother Lu for embarrassing him on Pill-Star Peak!” Fang Shi blamed himself bitterly, clenching his bandaged fist.

Lu Chen gently checked Fang Shi’s injured meridian, handed him a Rank 3 Rune-Inscribed Meridian-Mending Pill he had refined yesterday, and smiled warmly:

“Eldest Senior Brother, take this pill and rest for three days. Tomorrow is the first of the month when the Seven Star Mirage Tower and the Heavenly Dragon Roll Arena open. Once I step out of the Mirage Tower, I’ll go take back our Broken Spear Peak’s Star-Iron Ore—and Situ Hao’s #48 spot on the Heavenly Dragon Roll.”
