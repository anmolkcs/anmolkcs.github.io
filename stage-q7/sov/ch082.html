Stepping through the shimmering silver portal into the Secret Realm of the Fallen Star, the five-person squad led by Lu Chen found themselves in an ancient, gravity-warped landscape under a purple nebula sky.

While ordinary disciples fought over outer-ring herbs, Lu Chen used his Rank 3 Inscription & Formation perception under Prism Stillness to follow the subterranean geomantic lines thirty miles deep into the Ancient Star-Forge Ruins.

Inside a collapsed underground weapon hall guarded by twelve Late Houtian Star-Iron Golems—which Lu Chen and Chu Kuang dismantled in fifteen minutes of coordinated spear-and-saber strikes—Lu Chen discovered a glowing, three-foot-wide block of silver-black metal resting inside a cold star-fire furnace:

Twelve hundred pounds of pure Grade 3 Upper-Tier Metal: Ten-Thousand-Year Star-Core Heavy Iron!

Even a fist-sized piece of Ten-Thousand-Year Star-Core Heavy Iron weighed a hundred jin and was worth fifty Middle-Grade True Essence Stones; this entire twelve-hundred-pound block possessed a natural Threefold Gravitational Field and weighed over 3,000 jin!

“My 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear is already starting to feel light against my 38,000-jin baseline strength,” Lu Chen’s eyes lit up as he stored the massive Star-Core Heavy Iron block into his spatial ring. “Once I obtain Fourth-Order Spirit Beast Blood Essence inside the core of the ruins to unlock the fifth facet of the Chrono-Soul Prism, I can re-forge my spear into a true Grade 3 Upper-Tier Xiantian Treasure Spear!”
