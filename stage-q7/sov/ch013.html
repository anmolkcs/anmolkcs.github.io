Thirty-six towering obsidian pillars surrounded the central square of the mountain gate, etched from top to bottom with thousands of silver spirit runes.

As the two hundred Recommendation Candidates stepped inside the circle of pillars, Elder Sun tossed thirty-six Middle-Grade True Essence Stones into the array sockets.

嗡 (Weng)—!

A thick, colorless mist rolled out from the obsidian pillars, enveloping all two hundred youths in the blink of an eye.

The moment the mist touched Lu Chen’s forehead, the white-jade square around him vanished.

Instead of Seven Star Mountain, he found himself standing in the middle of a blood-soaked, burning battlefield beneath a sky of crimson lightning! Thousands of headless armored soldiers and hideous Second-Order and Third-Order spirit beasts charged directly at him from all directions, their rusted halberds and dripping fangs accompanied by a deafening roar of murderous intent that struck straight at his spiritual sea!

In the real world outside the array, dozens of sixteen-year-old youths immediately turned pale, screaming and swinging their fists wildly at empty air before collapsing to their knees with blood trickling from their noses—eliminated in less than a tenth of an incense stick!

“The Illusion Array of Ten Thousand Wills doesn’t test cultivation realm; it uses ancient battlefield slaughter-intent to batter a candidate’s spiritual sea,” Elder Sun explained to the noble guests on the viewing terrace. “The first quarter of an incense stick simulates the murderous aura of a Peak Body Transformation battlefield; the second quarter rises to Houtian Realm slaughter-intent; and the final quarter unleashes a trace of Xiantian Realm pressure! Even Lin Xuan with his Grade 4 Upper-Tier talent will likely tap out around eighty percent of the incense stick.”

Inside the array, however, as the first wave of bloodthirsty phantoms lunged at Lu Chen’s throat with halberds raised, Lu Chen didn’t even blink.

Why?

Because three nights ago, when he had entered the Chrono-Soul Prism and assimilated the high-density First Technique Bubble, his spiritual sea had already been tempered by the timeless pressure of the Prism’s inner void!

Compared to the crushing vastness of the Chrono-Soul Prism, this mortal illusion array felt like a painted paper tiger fluttering in the wind.

Even better, as Lu Chen looked closely at the swirling crimson mist around him under Prism Stillness, his eyes lit up with sudden realization.

“Wait—this Illusion Array of Ten Thousand Wills is constructed from thirty-six interlocking Rank 3 Spirit-Shock Inscription Runes!”

To every other candidate in the square, the illusion was a terrifying mental torture to be endured with clenched teeth.

To Lu Chen, who possessed the Prism’s foundational runic compendium and the crystal clarity of Prism Stillness, standing inside a live Rank 3 Spirit-Shock Array was an invaluable, once-in-a-lifetime textbook!

Instead of blocking out the illusionary pressure, Lu Chen sat down cross-legged right on the flagstones in the center of the mist, closed his eyes, and extended his spiritual sense outward to trace the invisible flow of soul-energy threads connecting the thirty-six obsidian pillars!

Outside on the viewing terrace, half the incense stick burned down.

One hundred and ten candidates had already stumbled or crawled out of the mist, drenched in cold sweat and gasping for breath.

Three-quarters of the incense stick burned down.

Now, the obsidian pillars hummed with a low crimson light as Houtian-level slaughter intent descended. Even Zhang Cang (Grade 4 Lower-Tier) let out a muffled groan, his knees buckling as he was teleported out of the array at the seventy-five-percent mark, his face as white as chalk.

“What is going on with that #189 from Cloudstride City?!” Zhang Cang wiped the cold sweat from his forehead, staring in disbelief at the mist. “He’s only a borderline Grade 3 waste! Why hasn’t his mind collapsed under the Houtian slaughter-intent yet?!”

At the ninety-two-percent mark, the final trace of Xiantian pressure roared down from the obsidian pillars like a collapsing mountain peak!

Puh!

Both Tuoba Lie and Lin Xuan spat a mouthful of blood simultaneously as their spiritual defenses shattered, tumbling out onto the white-jade steps at the ninety-three-percent mark!

In the entire thirty-six-pillar square, only Lu Chen remained inside.

Not only was his breathing completely steady as the Xiantian mental storm battered his spiritual sea, but his right index finger was unconsciously moving in tiny, rhythmic circles across his knee—tracing and memorizing the core Soul-Piercing Rune Node of the thirty-six obsidian pillars!

Snap.

The final ash of the incense stick fell into the bronze burner.

The thirty-six obsidian pillars powered down, and as the mist dispersed, Lu Chen opened his eyes—bright, clear, and sharper than two cold stars—and stood up casually, brushing a speck of dust off his black robe as two hundred pairs of stunned eyes stared at him in dead silence.
