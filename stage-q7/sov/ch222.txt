<p>To cross two thousand miles of the <strong>Floating Mercury Sea</strong> during the approaching mid-month <strong>Quicksilver Inverted Tide</strong>, ordinary flying mounts were useless because the chaotic magnetic loops between the floating lodestone islands scrambled spatial bearings every ten miles.</p>
<p>Fortunately, Lu Chen's reputation as the eighteen-year-old <strong>Rank 5 Peak (and secret Rank 6) Inscription &amp; Restoration Grandmaster</strong> who had restored both the <strong>Sundial of Ten Thousand Epochs</strong> and the <strong>Imperial Celadon Dragon-Kiln</strong> had already spread across the entire Central Imperial Domain!</p>
<p>The moment Lu Chen presented his <strong>Honorary Peak Master Medallion</strong> at the <strong>Silver-Anchor Alchemist &amp; Navigator Guild</strong>, the Guild Master—<strong>Master Gongsun</strong>, a respectable <strong>Third-Revolution Life-and-Death Wheel</strong> alchemist—hurried out personally to welcome them into the inner dockyard.</p>
<p>"Grandmaster Lu Chen! Heaven truly sent you to our Silver-Anchor Guild at the critical hour!" Master Gongsun bowed with deep relief, leading Lu Chen to a dry-dock where a hundred-foot, streamlined <strong>Grade 5 Star-Lodestone &amp; Gold-Amalgam Skiff</strong> (<em>The Quicksilver Dragon</em>) sat resting on cradles. "Three days ago, our Guild's exploration team returned from the outer rim of <strong>Inverted-Hourglass Island</strong> after discovering that the ancient <strong>Pre-Epoch Mercury-Lock Astrolabe</strong> guarding the island's inner temporal barrier has begun to open! However, our ship was ambushed on the way back by the <strong>Quicksilver Blood-Fleet</strong>—commanded by the <strong>Fourth-Revolution (Second Death-Tribulation) Blood-Sea Elder</strong> of the <strong>Nether-Blood Cult</strong>, who is trying to harvest the <strong>Peak Sixth-Order Quicksilver Dragon-Tortoise</strong> and the <strong>Chrono-Yin Jade Marrow</strong> inside!"</p>
<p>Master Gongsun pointed to the cracked <strong>Bronze-Amalgam Keel Engine</strong> of the hundred-foot skiff:</p>
<p>"Worse still, during the escape, the ship's <strong>Pre-Epoch Gold-Mercury Amalgam Keel Seal</strong> was corroded by mercury-poison! In the entire port, no blacksmith dares repair a Gold-Mercury Amalgam seal with fire, because heating mercury amalgam releases lethal <strong>Rank 5 Neuro-Toxic Mercury Vapor</strong> and destroys the magnetic superconductivity of the lodestone!"</p>
<p>Looking at the cracked <strong>Gold-Mercury Amalgam Keel Seal</strong>, Lu Chen smiled faintly.</p>
<p>In his past life on Earth, when restoring <strong>Warring States and Qin Dynasty Fire-Gilded Bronze Chariot Fittings</strong>—just like the famous bronze chariots and mercury-rivers of the First Emperor's mausoleum—restorers worked daily with <strong>Gold-Mercury Amalgam</strong> and knew the exact chemical-physical equilibrium curves where gold, silver, and mercury bonded into a stable intermetallic crystal without vaporizing!</p>
