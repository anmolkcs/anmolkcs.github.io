Before the first rooster crowed in the southern quarter of Cloudstride City, Lu Chen had already stopped by the night-watch window of the Hundred Herb Hall and spent both taels of his silver reward on six packets of Crimson Ironwood Bark & Blood-Clover Powder—the coarsest, most pungent body-tempering medicinal bath powder on the market.

Ordinary martial apprentices avoided Crimson Ironwood Bark because its medicinal properties were violent and unrefined; soaking in it felt like having thousands of fire ants chewing through one’s pores, and without tremendous physical exertion to work the heat into the fascia, the harsh sap would leave dark bruises beneath the skin.

For the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath, however, violent medicinal heat was fuel.

Before setting out for the Crimson Scale Pavilion at the fifth watch, Lu Chen boiled two packets of the dark red powder into a thick paste, smeared it directly across his chest, back, and forearms, wrapped himself tightly in coarse linen bands, and walked into the frosty morning air with steam rising faintly from his collar.

By the time he picked up his thirty-two-jin Black-Iron Boning Knife in the third kitchen yard, the Crimson Ironwood paste had begun to burn like hot coals against his skin.

“Little Chen, after yesterday’s Second-Order Python, Steward Zhao assigned four Iron-Hide Wild Yaks and ten Flood Fish to your bench today,” Master Liu said, tossing him an oiled apron. “Don’t push yourself too hard—you can split two of the yaks with the second bench.”

“No need, Master Liu. Leave all four on my rack,” Lu Chen said calmly.

He stepped in front of the first nine-hundred-jin Iron-Hide Wild Yak carcass, planted his feet in the half-sunken star stance of the Chaotic Star Body Art, and closed his eyes for a single breath.

Inhale—three short, one long. Draw the burning heat of the Crimson Ironwood paste from the skin pores into the twelve major tendons of the back and right arm!

Hummm.

Inside Lu Chen’s torso, a low, rhythmic thrumming sound began to vibrate—so faint that someone standing three feet away wouldn’t hear it, yet inside his own ears it sounded like a bowstring being drawn taut inch by inch.

Exhale—unwind the silk!

Shhhk—!

Lu Chen’s waist snapped three inches to the left, and the thirty-two-jin black-iron knife flashed downward in a silver-black arc.

Unlike the previous days when he had merely used gravity and leverage to glide around bones, today every single stroke of the thirty-two-jin knife was driven by the spiral contraction of the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath!

As the heavy blade sheared effortlessly through the tough neck fascia of the Iron-Hide Wild Yak, the counter-shock from the beast’s dense muscle traveled back up the hilt into Lu Chen’s palm, forearm, shoulder, and spine.

Instead of dispersing that shock, Lu Chen used the Chaotic Star Body Art to trap the kinetic vibration inside his flesh, slamming the fiery medicinal energy of the Crimson Ironwood paste straight into the microscopic gaps between his muscle fibers!

Rip. Tear. Knit.

Every cut felt as though his forearm muscles were being combed with hot iron needles, torn at a microscopic level, and immediately re-braided into tighter, denser spiral strands by the warm current radiating from the Chrono-Soul Prism and the medicinal paste.

One hour. Three hours. Six hours.

Other apprentices in the yard had to rest for fifteen minutes every hour to rub their cramping arms and drink water. Lu Chen didn’t put down the thirty-two-jin knife once.

Sweat mixed with dark, foul-smelling grayish impurities oozed out from beneath the linen wraps on his arms, staining his tunic black. Under the scrutiny of Prism Stillness, he corrected the angle of his spine and the rhythm of his breath hundreds of times:

Stroke 1,420: Right scapula tensed half a beat too early; three percent of the waist force leaked.

Stroke 2,890: Breath held a fraction too long at the dantian; shift compression to the brachial tendon.

Stroke 4,100: Force, breath, and blade edge aligned into a single unbroken line!

Around mid-afternoon, as Lu Chen raised the thirty-two-jin black-iron knife before the fourth and final Iron-Hide Wild Yak, the burning heat of the Crimson Ironwood paste on his torso had been completely devoured by his muscles.

Beneath his pale, sweat-sheened skin, the muscle contours were no longer bulky or swollen like Zhao Hu’s; instead, they looked streamlined and compact, like coiled strips of tempered spring steel.

Lu Chen inhaled deeply. His chest expanded, and the thirty-six micro-meridian nodes along his arms and back gave a simultaneous, muffled pop—like thirty-six silk knots snapping tight at once!

His wrist flicked forward.

SHHHK—CLANG!

The thirty-two-jin black-iron knife didn’t just strip the dorsal fascia from the yak’s spine—the sheer spiral force humming along the unsharpened spine of the blade split the yak’s four-inch-thick iron-hard thigh bone cleanly in two before burying half an inch into the seasoned ironwood chopping block beneath!

Silence fell over the third kitchen yard.

Zhao Hu dropped the sheep leg he was holding into a bucket with a splash, his jaw hanging open.

An Iron-Hide Wild Yak’s femur was one of the hardest bones among First-Order beasts; even a martial artist at the Peak of First Stage Strength Training (1,000 jin) needed an eighty-jin two-handed war-cleaver and a full running swing to crack it. Yet Lu Chen had split it cleanly in half with a single one-handed flick of a boning knife!

Master Liu walked over slowly, his eyes fixed not on the split bone, but on the back of Lu Chen’s right forearm, where the skin had taken on a faint, tough bronze-jade luster that rebounded instantly when pressed.

“Skin tightening like drumhide, fascia condensing into a single sheet…” Master Liu sucked in a cold breath. “Little Chen, you just broke through to the Second Stage of Body Transformation—Flesh Training, with over 1,200 jin of fist strength?!”

Six days ago, this boy had fainted from exhaustion with barely five hundred jin of strength. Today, his raw power had more than doubled, stepping past the 1,000-jin threshold of the First Stage and charging straight into the Second Stage!

Lu Chen pulled the thirty-two-jin blade out of the chopping block with two fingers, feeling the surging, tireless torrent of blood-qi coursing beneath his toughened skin.

“The medicinal paste worked well with the heavy knife, Master Liu,” Lu Chen said with a calm nod.

Yet inside his heart, Lu Chen remained completely sober.

Reaching the Second Stage (Flesh Training) was enough to silence kitchen bullies like Zhao Hu, but Wang Yigao—who had given the Lu family ten days to hand over the Seven Star Martial House Recommendation Token—was already at the Early Third Stage: Viscera Training, backed by a rich merchant clan and Human-Step martial skills. And beyond Wang Yigao lay the monstrous entrance trial of the Seven Star Martial House in half a month.

Relying on thirty-copper-a-day kitchen wages and coarse Ironwood Bark would take months to reach the Third Stage. To buy true spirit herbs like Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng and Scarlet Serpent Paste, he needed silver by the hundreds of taels—or better yet, True Essence Stones.

And the fastest path to that wealth lay in the seventy-two Inscription Diagrams sitting inside his mind.
