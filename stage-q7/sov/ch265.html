At dawn on the seventh day inside the Abyssal Chrono-Rift—

BOOOOOOOOONG—HUMMMMMMM!

As the final thread of the 36,000 Rank 6 Peak Life-Weaving Runes fused seamlessly into the brow of the newly formed body, a pillar of nine-colored lotus light and silver time-snow soared ten thousand feet into the morning sky above the Sunken Star-Sea Basin, causing every petrified silver tree across three hundred miles of the rift to burst into living, fragrant snow-blossoms simultaneously!

At the center of the white-jade altar, the blinding light slowly receded.

Standing barefoot atop the blooming Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus—no longer a translucent phantom soul trapped inside a stone, but a living, breathing woman of flesh and blood dressed in flowing snow-white immortal robes—was Su Qingxue!

She slowly raised her slender, jade-white hand to the morning sunlight filtering down into the canyon.

She felt the warm pulse of blood beating in her wrist; she felt the cool mountain breeze brushing against her eyelashes; and within her lower dantian and upper spiritual sea, her reconstructed Innate Chrono-Yin Sacred Body instantly restored her cultivation to the Early-to-Middle Divine Sea Realm (with a 3,000-mile Chrono-Yin Divine Sea)—the highest cultivation level permitted by the Sky Spill Continent's planetary Star-Seal without triggering immediate forced ascension!

When Su Qingxue turned and looked at eighteen-year-old Lu Chen—whose face was pale with sweat after seven consecutive days and nights of unbroken Rank 6 micro-inscription focus—her peerless, frost-clear eyes warmed into the brightest, most genuine smile Lu Chen had ever seen.

Stepping lightly off the white-jade altar onto the grass in front of Lu Chen, Su Qingxue bowed with deep, heartfelt grace:

"Ten thousand years in the dark void, and three years watching you grow from Cloudstride City to the pinnacle of this continent... Thank you for giving me back my life under the sun, Lu Chen."

"You shielded my spiritual sea when I was still a mortal in Thunder-Scar Gorge and on the Star-Burial Glacier, Senior Su," Lu Chen smiled warmly, handing her a cup of warm Astral Lotus Tea as Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang walked over to congratulate her in awe. "From today on, you never have to be locked inside the Prism again."

"And this little sister who just played the zither for seven days to stabilize my soul-harmonics must be Su Wan'er," Su Qingxue turned warmly to Su Wan'er, sensing a faint, distant kinship in the girl's pure zither-and-frost soul frequency, and placed a Rank 6 Chrono-Frost Jade Bracelet condensed from her own lotus petals onto Su Wan'er's wrist. "Your zither heart is as pure as winter snow, Little Sister Wan'er—let me teach you our Su Clan's Nine-Heavens Frost-Zither Immortal Art while we travel together!"
