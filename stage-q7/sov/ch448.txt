<p>Twelve seasoned Broken-Tooth bandits led by a <strong>Peak Tier Two</strong> butcher with twin fifty-pound battle-axes against four adult villagers and one fourteen-year-old boy in a narrow ravine!</p>
<p>If Uncle Shi, Big-Tall Zhao, and Cross-Eyed Liu fought the ten bandits in an open melee while Lu Chen faced Scar-Six, at least two of the village men would die within the first thirty breaths.</p>
<p>Lu Chen had no intention of fighting a fair battle in the open snow.</p>
<p>Over the past six days of tending the seven earthen kilns, whenever the twenty thousand jin of resin-heavy <strong>Bitter-Iron Pine</strong> sweated out its liquid sap, the boiling <strong>Black Pine-Tar and Wood-Vinegar</strong> drained through clay pipes at the base of each kiln into seven covered three-hundred-gallon stone tar-troughs dug right beside the narrow ravine path!</p>
<p>And inside Kilns #5, #6, and #7 along the western approach, two thousand cubic feet of superheated, oxygen-starved <strong>Yellow Carbon-Monoxide &amp; Tar Gas</strong> was churning at eight hundred degrees under the sealed clay domes—waiting for a single spark of air to erupt!</p>
<p>"Wait until they pass between Kilns #6 and #5," Lu Chen breathed into Uncle Shi's ear behind the brick wall of Kiln #3, his voice calm as winter ice. "Uncle Shi, Zhao, Liu—when I smash the clay front-wall of Kiln #6, tip the three boiling tar-troughs down the slope and shoot into the smoke with the two Triple-Prod Crossbows! Shi Tou—keep behind Kiln #1 and reload the spare crossbows!"</p>
<p>Out in the fog, believing the five charcoal burners were still fast asleep inside the pine-bough lean-to, <strong>Blood-Axe Scar-Six</strong> waved his left fifty-pound axe forward.</p>
<p>Ten outlaw raiders crept at a crouched trot right up the narrow twelve-foot trench between the glowing clay walls of Kilns #7, #6, and #5, their blades drawn to butcher sleeping men!</p>
<p>Thirty paces. Twenty paces. Ten paces.</p>
<p>The moment the tenth bandit's boot stepped onto the slick clay slope directly below the brimming stone tar-troughs of Kilns #5 and #6—</p>
<p><em>BOOM—CRACK!</em></p>
<p>Stepping out from behind the shadow of Kiln #4, Lu Chen swung the thirty-six-pound felling axe with his left hand and hurled it like a spinning meteor straight through the thin, red-hot clay firing-door at the base of Kiln #6, while Uncle Shi and Big-Tall Zhao pried open the wooden sluice-gates of all three boiling <strong>Pine-Tar Troughs</strong>!</p>
<p><em>WHOOOOOOOOOOOSH—KABOOOOOOOM!</em></p>
<p>As freezing night air rushed through the shattered firing-door into the oxygen-starved heart of Kiln #6, the superheated yellow tar-gas inside detonated in a roaring horizontal backdraft of blinding yellow smoke, flying red-hot pine coals, and suffocating wood-gas!</p>
<p>Simultaneously, six hundred gallons of <strong>boiling, sticky black Pine-Tar</strong> surged out of the breached stone troughs and cascaded down the narrow twelve-foot trench over the feet and legs of the ten charging outlaws!</p>
<p>"AAAHHH! MY EYES! MY LEGS ARE BOILING—!"</p>
<p>Four Tier-One bandits in the front rank slipped violently in the scalding, glue-thick black pitch and plunged face-first into the boiling tar and burning coals! Three more behind them inhaled a lungful of the toxic, eight-hundred-degree yellow kiln-gas and collapsed to their knees, clawing at their blistered throats and vomiting blood in complete blindness!</p>
<p><em>TWANG! TWANG! TWANG! TWANG!</em></p>
<p>From the clean air upslope behind Kiln #3, Uncle Shi, Big-Tall Zhao, and Cross-Eyed Liu fired two volley-cycles of heavy <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong> straight down into the illuminated silhouettes thrashing in the burning tar! Four thumb-thick iron bolts punched through the chests and necks of four more bandits before they could even crawl out of the trench!</p>
<p>In less than five breaths, without a single villager suffering a scratch, eight of the ten Broken-Tooth raiders were either dead or writhing crippled in the flaming pine-tar!</p>
