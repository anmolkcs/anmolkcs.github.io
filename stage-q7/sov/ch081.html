Two months after Lu Chen entered the Top 40 of the Heavenly Dragon Roll, the decennial grand event of the southern Sky Spill Continent arrived:

The opening of the Secret Realm of the Fallen Star!

Located in the Star-Mist Mountain Range at the border between the Seven Star Valley Sect and two rival Fourth-Grade Xiantian Sects (the Shadow-Moon Sect and the Raging-Flame Sect), the Secret Realm of the Fallen Star was an ancient pocket dimension left behind by a fallen Revolving Core Realm powerhouse five thousand years ago. Only martial artists below the Xiantian Realm (at the Houtian stage) could pass through its spatial restriction without collapsing the entrance.

Inside the Secret Realm grew rare Earth-Step (Grade 3) Spirit Herbs, veins of Ten-Thousand-Year Star-Core Heavy Iron, and ancient Fourth-Order (Xiantian-Level) Spirit Beasts whose blood essence could propel a Houtian cultivator straight to the threshold of the Xiantian Realm!

Each of the three sects sent fifty elite Houtian disciples from their Heavenly Dragon Rolls.

Before departing from Broken Spear Peak, Elder Tie handed Lu Chen a protective Xiantian Jade Talisman, and Lu Chen teamed up with Su Wan’er (now Middle Houtian), Murong Zi (Middle-to-Late Houtian), Chu Kuang (Late Houtian), and Eldest Senior Brother Fang Shi (Peak Houtian).
