"Little Chen! Come to the North-Valley Abyssal Rift immediately!" Elder Tie's booming voice rang out from the jade talisman. All three Revolving Core Grand Elders and Grandmaster Gu of Pill-Star Peak are already down there, and Grandmaster Gu specifically asked for your Rank 3 Inscription eyes!"

Standing up and bidding his parents a brief farewell, Lu Chen tapped his toes on the courtyard flagstones.

Even without yet stepping fully into the Xiantian Realm to fly on pure Innate Origin Qi, his Embryonic Concept of Mountain-Gravity allowed him to lighten his own body's gravitational pull by 70%, gliding across the suspension bridges between the peaks like a black swallow.

Fifteen minutes later, Lu Chen descended through a mile-deep vertical mine shaft into the newly opened Subterranean Bronze Chasm.

The cavern was vast enough to hold an entire mortal city, lit by veins of glowing blue Star-Phosphor Stone embedded in the vaulted ceiling. In the center of a dried subterranean lakebed stood a colossal, three-hundred-foot-tall Nine-Ear Sunken Bronze Bell, half-buried in petrified silver-gray silt!

Surrounding the giant bronze bell were the seventy Xiantian Elders of the Seven Star Valley Sect, Sect Master Tian Shu, Elder Tie, Grandmaster Gu, Su Wan'er, and floating in midair above the bell, three white-haired ancients whose auras radiated the terrifying, solid pressure of the Revolving Core Realm (Ning Dan)!

Yet despite the presence of three Revolving Core Grand Elders, every single one of them wore a frustrated frown.

"Master Lu Chen, you're here!" Grandmaster Gu hurried over, pointing toward the surface of the three-hundred-foot bronze bell. "According to our sect's oldest records, ten thousand years ago before the Seven Star Valley Sect was founded, this region belonged to the Antiquarian Bronze Dynasty—an ancient civilization that cast Acoustic-Law Vessels to anchor the continent's spirit veins. Inside the core chamber of this Nine-Ear Sunken Bronze Bell sits a pool of condensed Ten-Thousand-Year Innate Acoustic Star-Marrow—a priceless treasure that can wash a cultivator's Xiantian Spiritual Sea!"

"Why haven't the Grand Elders opened the bell chamber?" Lu Chen asked, his gaze narrowing as he looked at the six-inch-thick crust of dull, greenish-gray mineral rust covering the bell's surface.

"Because that greenish-gray crust isn't ordinary bronze verdigris—it's Ten-Thousand-Year Petrified Spirit-Rust fused with a Rank 5 Self-Destructing Acoustic Seal!" Elder Tie explained grimly. "Half an hour ago, First Grand Elder tried to shave off a piece of the rust using Revolving Core Sword Qi. Not only did the petrified rust deflect a 500,000-jin Revolving Core strike without a scratch, but the vibration triggered a counter-shock chime from the bell that knocked three Early Xiantian elders unconscious!"

Looking at the three-hundred-foot bronze bell encrusted in petrified mineral rust, Lu Chen felt a sudden, surreal sense of nostalgia.

On Modern Earth, before he had ever touched the Chrono-Soul Prism, what had he spent eight years doing for ten hours a day in the provincial museum's basement lab?

Restoring ancient, mineral-encrusted bronze vessels and finding the microscopic cleavage planes of petrified rust without triggering structural fractures!
