<p>The night of the twenty-first of the twelfth month was the <strong>Great Winter Solstice</strong> in the borderlands of the Great Desolate Origin Universe.</p>
<p>For the first time in thirty years, there were no tax-bailiff whips cracking at the gate of Ash-Brook Village, no empty grain jars in the communal shed, and no empty corner beside the smithy hearth.</p>
<p>Inside the expanded smithy courtyard—where the forty freed miners led by <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong> were already helping build twenty new stone cottages along the lower creek—ten great bonfires blazed against the winter night.</p>
<p>At the center of the smithy shed, sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> and his twenty-two-year-old son <strong>Tie Niu</strong> stood opposite each other at the stone anvil just to hear it ring: the one-armed father tapping the guide-hammer (<em>Tink!</em>), and his broad-shouldered son bringing down the forty-pound striker's sledge (<em>BOOM-CLANG!</em>) while <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, <strong>Old Ma</strong>, <strong>Hunter Feng</strong>, and young <strong>Shi Tou</strong> raised bowls of hot spiced berry-wine and roared with laughter.</p>
<p>Beside the warm stove, <strong>Widow Qin</strong>—wearing the carved <strong>Iron-Pine Osmanthus Hairpin</strong> in her neatly braided hair—ladled out steaming bowls of rich <strong>Iron-Husk Millet and Boar-Rib Noodles</strong> for little <strong>Xiao Ya</strong> and <strong>Granny Liu</strong>, setting the biggest, most fragrant bowl at the head of the bench for Lu Chen.</p>
<p>Eating the hot millet noodles surrounded by the rough, honest warmth of the people he had bled beside for sixty-five days, Lu Chen felt the deep scar in his chest quiet into a steady, unyielding anchor.</p>
<p>The next morning before dawn, while the village still slept beneath a blanket of peaceful white solstice snow, Lu Chen walked alone up the steep shale switchbacks of <strong>Frost-Tooth Gully</strong>—climbing all the way past the Dead-Pine Boundary to the highest wind-scoured granite crag of the <strong>Black-Briar Barren Ridge</strong>.</p>
<p>He stood barefoot in the snow wearing a simple black hemp tunic.</p>
<p>Two months ago, when he had washed down this mountain after sacrificing his <strong>Hundred-Million-Mile Perfected Inner Universe</strong> and the <strong>Chrono-Soul Prism</strong> to seal his parents <strong>Lu Shan &amp; Meng Wan</strong>, <strong>Su Qingxue</strong>, and <strong>Su Wan'er</strong> inside the <strong>Nine-Epoch Inverse Chrono-Cocoon</strong>, he had been a crippled, dying mortal who could not even lift his own elbow under the tenfold Desolate Gravity or breathe the gritty <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong> without coughing blood.</p>
<p>Now, after sixty-five days and nights of pulling bellows, swinging sledges, hunting in boiling mud-wallows, tending charcoal kilns, drawing bow-racks, and soaking in eighteenfold-gravity subterranean brine, he stood on the mountain peak with a foundation forged purely from sweat, iron, and indomitable will: - <strong>Cultivation Realm</strong>: <strong>Peak Tier Four of the Mortal-Shedding Stage — Hundred-Refined Cold-Iron Bone Perfection</strong>; - <strong>Physical Vessel</strong>: Outer hide tough as three-layered ox-leather (<strong>Tier 1</strong>); every muscle bundle compacted into waterless iron cables (<strong>Tier 2</strong>); all thirty-six major sinew lines drawn into translucent gold-white dragon-horn bowstrings (<strong>Tier 3</strong>); and all <strong>206 bones</strong> calcined from inner marrow-wall to outer periosteum into ringing Cold-Slag Steel (<strong>Peak Tier 4</strong>), carrying <strong>8,200 jin</strong> of baseline force and over <strong>10,000 jin</strong> of six-harmony explosive power; - <strong>Weapon</strong>: The seven-foot-six-inch, <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong>, with the <strong>rusted three-inch iron collar</strong> of his former Primordial Sovereign Spear locked beneath his left thumb.</p>
<p>Looking north from the summit of Black-Briar Ridge across the vast, frozen expanse of the <strong>Great Desolate Origin Universe</strong>, Lu Chen knew clearly how long the road ahead still was.</p>
<p>Beyond <strong>Peak Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining)</strong> lay five more grueling tiers of the <strong>Mortal-Shedding Stage</strong> (<strong>Tier 5: Lead-Marrow Washing → Tier 6: Five-Viscera Tempering → Tier 7: Mercury-Blood Coagulation → Tier 8: Nine-Aperture Furnace → Tier 9: Complete Mortal-Husk Shedding</strong>) before he could even chisel open the first of his <strong>Twelve Heavy Origin Meridians</strong> in the <strong>Desolate Pulse Realm</strong>, march three hundred miles north across the badlands to <strong>Grey-Wall Prefectural City</strong>, and begin his climb across the <strong>Thirty-Six Prefectural Heavens</strong> toward the <strong>Nether-Chain Star Sea</strong>.</p>
<p>It would be a journey of a thousand battles and ten thousand miles.</p>
<p>Yet Lu Chen was in no hurry to skip a single step.</p>
<p>Sinking his mind into the quiet, three-pace clarity of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, he planted his bare iron-boned feet shoulder-width apart in the summit snow, gripped the <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> in both calloused hands, and thrust forward into the rising winter sun:</p>
<p><em>One thrust.</em> <em>Two thrusts.</em> <em>Ten thousand thrusts.</em></p>
