Twenty miles southeast at Wind-Howl Valley, four of the lower-ranked Azure Sky Kingdom disciples (all at the Peak of the Sixth Stage) had banded together for safety, only to be cornered against a cliff wall by a pack of six Early Third-Order Iron-Winged Wyverns (each equivalent to an Early Houtian martial artist!)—and worse, watching coldly from the ridge above without lifting a finger were three disciples of the Black-Wind Kingdom, who had deliberately used lure-incense to drive the wyvern pack into the Azure Sky disciples’ camp!

“Crush your teleportation tokens and hand over half your points—or get torn to pieces by those six Third-Order Wyverns!” one of the Black-Wind disciples laughed from the ridge as the lead Iron-Winged Wyvern dived down with razor-sharp talons aimed at an injured Azure Sky girl’s chest!

Refusing to abandon her wounded teammate, the Azure Sky girl gritted her teeth and raised her cracked sword to block a strike that would shatter her ribs—

ZAP—KABOOM!

A streak of dark-purple lightning tore across two hundred yards of sky faster than sound!

Before the lead Early Third-Order Iron-Winged Wyvern’s talons could come within three feet of the girl, an eight-foot-six-inch Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear arrived first!

《Wildfire Prairie Spear — Chapter One: Hundred-Step Crushing Thunder + Embryonic Mountain-Thunder Spear Intent!》

With 28,000 jin of spiral dragon-elephant force erupting from the spearhead, the diving Early Third-Order Wyvern was blasted sixty feet backward in midair with its iron wing-bone shattered in a single thrust!

Landing lightly on the boulder in front of the four wounded Azure Sky disciples, Lu Chen’s black robe fluttered in the canyon wind as he swept his 1,220-jin spear in a thirty-foot crescent—

《Wildfire Sweeps the Plains — Ninth Wave!》

BOOM-BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!

All five remaining Early Third-Order Iron-Winged Wyverns were sent tumbling out of the sky in a single hurricane sweep of purple lightning!

“Senior Brother Lu Chen!” the four Azure Sky disciples cried out in overwhelming relief and gratitude as Lu Chen tossed them a bottle of his Rune-Inscribed Healing Pills while sending the three panicked Black-Wind Kingdom disciples fleeing down the ridge with a single cold glance!
