<p>"Wait," Lu Chen whispered, pressing his palm gently against Big-Tall Zhao's shoulder so the burly hunter wouldn't shift a pebble on the ledge. "Don't move a muscle. Look at the mouth of the granite cave."</p>
<p>Down in the steaming basin, the yellow sulfur-mist in front of the northern boulder-cave suddenly parted as if blown back by a smithy's bellows.</p>
<p><em>SNORT—GRUUUUUUNT!</em></p>
<p>A sound like two millstones grinding together echoed off the basalt walls, making the loose mud on the surface of the wallow dance in ripples.</p>
<p>Out of the dark cave mouth stepped a mountainous beast that made the dead six-hundred-pound sow staked in the mud look like a half-grown piglet.</p>
<p>It stood nearly five feet high at the humped shoulder and weighed well over <strong>one thousand four hundred pounds</strong>—meaning under the tenfold Desolate Gravity of the basin, its charging mass carried the unstoppable momentum of fourteen thousand jin! Its entire back and flanks were armored in four-inch-long, overlapping <strong>Black-Iron Bristles</strong> crusted with decades of baked volcanic mud and hardened pine resin, forming a natural cuirass four inches thick. Across its left eye and snout ran a jagged white scar where an old iron spearhead had snapped off years ago and been swallowed right into its skull-bone, and curling from its lower jaw were two fourteen-inch, yellow-ivory tusks honed sharp as scythes against granite rocks.</p>
<p>This was the overlord of the eastern basin: a <strong>Peak Tier Two Mortal-Shedding Beast</strong>—the <strong>Scarred Iron-Bristle King-Boar</strong>!</p>
<p>Seeing its dead mate staked and gutted in the mud flat thirty yards away, the King-Boar's small, bloodshot eyes turned the color of glowing furnace coals. With a deafening squeal of rage that shook snow from the high ledges, the fourteen-hundred-pound titan lowered its scythe-like tusks and charged straight across the steaming mud flat!</p>
<p><em>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</em></p>
<p>Each plunge of its cloven hooves threw geysers of black mud ten feet into the air.</p>
<p>Ten yards from the dead sow, the King-Boar's right front hoof slammed directly onto the trigger-pan of the first buried <strong>Iron-Jaw Bear Trap</strong>!</p>
<p><em>CLACK—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Two serrated steel jaws powered by a double leaf-spring snapped shut around the boar's ankle with two thousand jin of force—and yet, against the four-inch crust of resin-baked iron bristles and dense <strong>Peak Tier Two</strong> muscle on the King-Boar's leg, the steel teeth didn't even break the bone! With a furious jerk of its shoulder, the monstrous boar ripped the buried pine anchor-log straight out of the mud and kept charging toward the poachers' blind with the fifty-pound iron trap dangling from its ankle like an anklet!</p>
<p>"Shoot! Shoot now!"</p>
<p>Down in the pine-branch blind, the pigeon-toed <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> poacher squad-leader—a pockmarked cutthroat named <strong>Ma Liu</strong>—screamed at the top of his lungs!</p>
<p><em>TWANG! TWANG! TWANG! TWANG!</em></p>
<p>Four heavy <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong> fired simultaneously at fifteen paces! Four thumb-thick iron bolts smeared with green <strong>Corpse-Rot Poison</strong> streaked through the sulfur mist!</p>
<p>Two bolts struck the King-Boar's sloped forehead and shoulder-plate at an angle, exploding into showers of sparks as they richocheted harmlessly off its petrified mud-and-resin armor! The third bolt punched three inches into its right flank, and the fourth—fired by the Early Tier Two squad leader Ma Liu—buried itself half a foot deep into the softer crease of the boar's neck!</p>
<p>Instead of dropping, the wounded King-Boar went completely berserk!</p>
<p>Dragging the chained bear-trap behind it, the fourteen-hundred-pound beast smashed straight through the pine-branch blind like a runaway boulder crashing through a wicker basket!</p>
<p>"AAAHHH—!"</p>
<p>The three-fingered Tier-One poacher didn't even have time to drop his empty crossbow before the King-Boar's fourteen-inch left tusk hooked him beneath the ribs and tossed him twenty feet into the boiling mud pool with his chest ripped open! A second Tier-One poacher tried to slash the boar's snout with a notched woodsman's saber, only to be bowled flat by the swinging pine anchor-log chained to the boar's foot, snapping both of his thighs with a wet crunch!</p>
