Before departing for Jingde-Star City, Lu Chen spent three quiet days inside Warm-Spring Immortal Manor on Seventh Spear Peak.

Using the Sixth-Order Astral-Thunder Leviathan's remaining alchemy materials, fifty High-Grade True Essence Stones, and his newly unlocked Rank 6 Quasi-Saint Pill-Inscription Mastery, Lu Chen refined twelve 380%-efficacy, zero-toxin Nine-Star Revolving Core Advancement Elixirs!

  • He presented four of the elixirs to his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan—helping both of his parents advance smoothly from Early Revolving Core into the Middle Revolving Core Realm within three days!
  • He gave six of the elixirs to Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang—propelling all three of his companions from Early Revolving Core straight into the Middle-to-Late Revolving Core Realm!
  • And after fortifying Warm-Spring Immortal Manor with a Rank 6 Quasi-Saint Nine-Star Dragon-Bell Guardian Array (and leaving Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng right next door on Seventh Spear Peak), eighteen-year-old Lu Chen strapped his 18,000-jin Star-Sovereign Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear across his back and set out eastward with Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang toward the Imperial Artisan Capital of Jingde-Star—and the First Saint-Grade Lotus Treasure!
