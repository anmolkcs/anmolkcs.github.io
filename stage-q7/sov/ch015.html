Outside on the white-jade viewing terrace, the giant Seven-Story Array Projection Stele displayed the golden name-dots of every candidate still inside the Pagoda of Crushing Gravity.

“Three people made it to the Fifth Floor of the Gravity Pagoda this year! One is Young Master Lin Xuan at Step 11, one is the northern barbarian Tuoba Lie at Step 4, and the third—Wait! Look at the number beside the third dot!”

“#189?! Isn’t #189 that borderline Grade 3 kid from Cloudstride City who got first place in the Illusion Array?!”

“How can an Early Third Stage youth with borderline Grade 3 talent climb onto the Fifth Floor of the Gravity Pagoda?!” an inner-court deacon exclaimed in shock. “Under fivefold gravity and the fifth-floor Origin Energy Shockwaves, anyone without 3,800 jin of physical endurance and Mid-to-Late Third Stage viscera strength would have their internal organs ruptured instantly!”

“Look closer at his pace,” Su Wan’er said softly, her bright eyes fixed on the projection stele where dot #189 was climbing Step 5, Step 6, Step 7 at an eerie, metronome-steady rhythm. “He isn’t just enduring the gravity array… he’s using the第五层 (Fifth Floor) Origin Energy Shockwaves to temper his viscera on the spot!”

Inside the Fifth Story of the Pagoda, the air was as thick and suffocating as liquid mercury.

With fivefold gravity pressing down on his body and the 820-jin Black Iron Spear on his back, Lu Chen’s knees and spine were bearing over 4,100 jin of continuous crushing weight!

Sweat mixed with traces of dark blood oozed from his pores, and every breath felt like inhaling molten lead. Ahead at Step 11, Lin Xuan heard the heavy, rhythmic footsteps approaching behind him and turned his head slightly, his eyes wide with disbelief and humiliation when he saw Lu Chen only four steps below him—still carrying the long black bundle on his back!

“A lowly Grade 3 ant from a prefectural town dares step alongside me?!” Lin Xuan roared in his throat, burning a drop of his prized meridian blood-qi to force his trembling right leg up to Step 12 and then Step 13, before collapsing to one knee as a fifth-floor Origin Energy Shockwave slammed into his chest!

Step 13 was Lin Xuan’s absolute limit.

Four steps below him, as that same fifth-floor Origin Energy Shockwave crashed into Lu Chen’s chest, Lu Chen didn’t kneel.

Instead, inside his spiritual sea, the Chrono-Soul Prism hummed with an icy stillness, while inside his torso, the five internal organs completed their final harmonic resonance under the 《Chaotic Star Body Art》!

BOOM—!

A sound like a muffled thunderclap rolled through Lu Chen’s chest cavity. The last shred of medicinal essence from the Three-Leaf Jade Marrow Grass merged completely into his heart and lungs, pushing his cultivation from the Early Third Stage straight into the Middle Third Stage of Body Transformation—Viscera Training, while his raw physical strength surged from 3,200 jin to a terrifying 3,900 jin!

Under the explosive surge of newly broken-through blood-qi, Lu Chen lifted his right boot:

Step 11. Step 12. Step 13—stepping right alongside the kneeling, blood-coughing Lin Xuan!

Lin Xuan stared up at the hem of Lu Chen’s plain black robe passing his shoulder, his fingernails digging so hard into the ironstone stair that they split and bled.

Step 14. Step 15!

Only at Fifth Floor, Step 16—three full steps above Lin Xuan—did Lu Chen calmly stop, feeling that his newly broken-through viscera had reached optimal saturation, and press the teleportation rune on his wooden plaque to exit the Pagoda.
