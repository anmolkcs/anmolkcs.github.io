Ten days flew by in a rhythm of spear practice, Pill Inscription trades with Su Wan’er, and grueling midnight sessions refining the Fifth Wave of the Nine-Layered Wave Force.

By the thirtieth day after the Entrance Trial—the day of the mandatory Monthly Freshman & Outer Abode Ranking Battle—Lu Chen had consolidated his cultivation firmly at the Middle Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons), pushing his baseline strength to 6,200 jin!

More importantly, inside the Chrono-Soul Prism, his repeated inscription work and daily soul-force exhaustion had tempered his spiritual sea until he could execute the ancient 《Instant Soul Ripple》 silently within a range of ten paces.

At dawn on the first of the month, the Central Martial Arena of the Seven Star Martial House was surrounded by over two thousand outer and inner disciples, dozens of instructors, and several core disciples sitting on the high jade pavilion—including Su Wan’er and a cold, regal nineteen-year-old youth in purple-gold robes sitting at the second seat of the core terrace:

Lin Sen—Lin Xuan’s older cousin, ranked #11 on the entire Killing Array Stele, who had already reached the Peak of the Fifth Stage (Bone Forging)!

“Look at the freshman section today—Lin Xuan just stepped out of twelve days of closed-door seclusion!” a crowd of disciples whispered, pointing toward the eastern platform.

Walking onto the arena flagstones in a spotless white-and-gold robe, Lin Xuan’s aura was noticeably sharper and heavier than twenty days ago. To ensure that his younger cousin crushed Lu Chen today and washed away the humiliation of the Entrance Trial and the Killing Array Stele, Lin Sen had personally gifted Lin Xuan a Grade 2 Upper-Tier Human-Step Weapon—the Frost-Moon Sword—along with two Rank 2 Tendon-Tempering Pills that had propelled Lin Xuan straight to the Middle Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) with 4,600 jin of strength!

Even more dangerously, over the past twelve days, Lin Xuan had mastered the first three stances of a Grade 2 Lower-Tier Martial Skill from the Lin Clan’s secret vault: 《Flowing Frost Seven Swords》!

“In the Freshman Ranking Battle, the top ten freshmen fight round-robin challenges on the central stage to determine the final monthly allocation of True Essence Stones and Ten Thousand Killing Array hours,” Presiding Elder Sun announced from the high terrace.

In the first four rounds, Tuoba Lie (who had also broken through to the Early Fourth Stage) swept aside Zhang Cang and the other noble freshmen with his heavy wolf-fang club, before losing narrowly to Lin Xuan’s blindingly fast Flowing Frost Seven Swords in forty moves.

Then, with four wins and zero losses each, the final match of the Freshman Ranking Battle was called:

“Final Match for #1 Freshman Rank: #189 Lu Chen vs. #001 Lin Xuan!”
