<p>All through the daytime shift of the seventeenth day of the twelfth month, the forty men of Drift Four worked with quiet, coiled intensity.</p>
<p>According to their original schedule, <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> was due to come down for his final crystal collection at midnight on the eighteenth, and detonate the thirty barrels of <strong>Black-Oil Fire-Powder</strong> after the cages went up. Lu Chen had planned to launch the breakout up the cleared <strong>Emergency Ventilation Chimney</strong> at noon on the eighteenth while the surface guards were changing shifts.</p>
<p>Yet at the first watch of the night (8:00 PM) on the seventeenth—<strong>twenty-eight hours early</strong>—Lu Chen was sitting at the mouth of the hidden geode cavern polishing the eighteen-inch diamond point of his <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> when his <strong>Prism Stillness</strong> suddenly picked up a faint, rapid tremor traveling through the solid granite floor from the mouth of Drift Four half a mile away!</p>
<p>It was not the heavy, careless tramp of the usual six night-wardens coming to bring the evening slop-buckets.</p>
<p>It was the hurried, silent metallic clink of <strong>twelve heavily armored men</strong> rolling barrels across the timber ties at the tunnel entrance—followed ten seconds later by the heavy <em>CLANG—CLICK</em> of the four-inch-thick <strong>Outer Iron Flood-Gate</strong> at the mouth of Drift Four being dropped and bolted from the outside!</p>
<p>Lu Chen stood up instantly, his dark eyes flashing like cold lightning.</p>
<p>"They're sealing the drift tonight—a day early!" Lu Chen's voice rang down the tunnel like an iron war-horn. "Every man drop your false shackles, grab your trench-spears from the cavern, and get into the <strong>Emergency Ventilation Chimney</strong> NOW!"</p>
<p>Half a mile away, on the safe side of the bolted four-inch Iron Flood-Gate at the mouth of Drift Four, <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> and <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong>—who had ridden down from Black-Rock Town an hour ago in a lather of sweat—watched twelve pit-wardens finish piling thirty barrels of <strong>Black-Oil Fire-Powder &amp; Sulfur-Pitch</strong> directly against the four giant <strong>Main Timber Roof-Pillars</strong> supporting the fault-line of Drift Four!</p>
<p>"Good thing you rode out to warn me tonight, Brother Zhao!" Scarface Luo Shan spat through his scarred lip, holding a blazing pitch-torch in his iron-boned hand. "So the County Magistrate's winter mine-censor is arriving from <strong>Grey-Wall Prefecture</strong> tomorrow afternoon instead of the twenty-first?! If that old bastard censor looked down Pit #7 tomorrow and saw that Tier-Three brat Lu Chen and Tie Shan's son still alive in Drift Four, he'd insist on inspecting the rock face himself!"</p>
<p>"Light the fuse now, Brother Luo!" Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan urged maliciously, his hand resting on his ninety-jin iron whip-rod. "Once those thirty fire-barrels blow the four main fault-pillars, ten thousand tons of black shale will drop straight across the entire half-mile of Drift Four! We report to the censor tomorrow morning that a sudden subterranean sulfur-methane pocket ignited at the first watch tonight—killing all forty debt-miners and their Tier-Three guarantor on the spot! And as soon as the censor rides up to the rim, my guards will arrest that one-armed old cripple <strong>Tie Shan</strong> in the witness-shack for 'bribing clerks with counterfeit iron' and slit his throat in the cells!"</p>
<p>"Hahaha—clean as a wiped blade!"</p>
<p>Scarface Luo Shan laughed savagely and tossed his blazing pitch-torch straight into the oil-trough linking the thirty barrels of Black-Oil Fire-Powder!</p>
<p><em>WHOOOOOOOOOOSH—</em></p>
<p>Five seconds later, at the mouth of Drift Four:</p>
<p><em>KABOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!</em></p>
