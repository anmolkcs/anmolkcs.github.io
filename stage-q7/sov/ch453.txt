<p>After finishing the midnight forging shift, Lu Chen did not go to sleep.</p>
<p>While Granny Liu boiled the four thick leg-tendons of the <strong>Peak Tier Two Scarred King-Boar</strong> and the <strong>Early Tier Two Silver-Backed Alpha Wolf</strong> with distilled <strong>Bitter-Pine Turpentine</strong>, <strong>Red-Shale Salt</strong>, and crushed <strong>Centipede-Root</strong> in a sealed clay cauldron for four hours until they dissolved into a sticky, amber-gold <strong>Sinew-Suppling Paste</strong>, Lu Chen worked behind the smithy shed with an auger, rawhide cables, and four green <strong>Bitter-Iron Pine</strong> trunks.</p>
<p>In the lower worlds, cultivators tempered their tendons using gentle Qi circulation or spirit pills. In the harsh physical reality of the Great Desolate Origin Universe, <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Drawing)</strong> required mechanical traction capable of overcoming tenfold gravity and the stubborn Origin-Grain of human connective tissue.</p>
<p>By the fourth cock-crow, Lu Chen had constructed a formidable eight-foot structure anchored four feet deep into the frozen permafrost behind the lean-to: <strong>The Four-Cornered Iron-Pine Bow-Rack</strong>.</p>
<p>Four thick green trunks of Bitter-Iron Pine were planted at the corners of a ten-foot square and winched inward at their tops using twisted rawhide windlasses, bending each bone-hard pine trunk into a monstrous vertical bow storing over <strong>eight hundred jin of outward spring-tension</strong>. Running from the tips of the four bent pine trunks to the center of the rack were four braided rawhide cuffs reinforced with iron rings—two at ankle height, and two overhead at wrist height.</p>
<p>When Granny Liu hobbled out into the torchlight carrying the steaming clay pot of amber-gold <strong>Sinew-Suppling Paste</strong>, she took one look at the four bent iron-pine trunks straining against their windlass pins with loud, ominous creaks, and her wrinkled face paled.</p>
<p>"Merciful Mountain God, boy..." Granny Liu breathed, setting the pot down on a flat stone. "Even the military torture-racks in Black-Rock Garrison only use two bent pine poles to stretch a recruit's tendons! You've rigged four six-inch Bitter-Iron Pines at eight hundred jin apiece! When you knock the windlass pin out, those four trees will pull your wrists and ankles in four directions with over <strong>three thousand two hundred jin of continuous traction</strong>! If your muscles slip for half a breath, it'll rip all four of your limbs clean out of their sockets like quartering a horse!"</p>
<p>"If the tension is less than my own <strong>Complete Tier Two</strong> muscle strength of twenty-five hundred jin," Lu Chen replied calmly as he stripped off his hemp tunic in the sub-zero cold, "my muscles will bear the load automatically, and the force will never reach the deep white tendons attached to the bones. Only three thousand two hundred jin of outward traction—seven hundred jin heavier than my muscles can hold alone—will force the thirty-six deep sinew lines to take the strain and lengthen."</p>
<p>Stepping into the center of the Four-Cornered Bow-Rack, Lu Chen scooped handfuls of the scalding amber-gold <strong>Sinew-Suppling Paste</strong> from the clay pot and rubbed it thickly over every major tendon junction across his body: - The thick <strong>Achilles cords</strong> at his heels and the plantar fascia under his soles; - The patellar and hamstring tendons behind his knees; - The deep iliopsoas and lumbar fascia anchoring his hips to his spine; - And the rotator cuffs, biceps tendons, elbows, and flexor-tendon sheaths of his wrists and fingers.</p>
<p>As the hot turpentine and dissolved Tier-Two beast-collagen soaked through his matte-bronze skin straight into the cold, fibrous tendon sheaths around his joints, a penetrating, fiery itch spread through every joint in his frame.</p>
<p>Lu Chen strapped his ankles into the two lower iron-ringed rawhide cuffs, reached up to lock his wrists into the two overhead cuffs, sank his mind into the absolute diamond clarity of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, and kicked the wooden windlass release-lever at his feet!</p>
