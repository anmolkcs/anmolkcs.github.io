After leaving fifty Rank 6 Quasi-Saint Thunder-Bell Talismans and a full month's supply of inscribed Rank 6 Second-and-Third Revolution Nirvana Pills with his parents Lu Shan & Meng Wan and Elder Tie inside the impregnable Warm-Spring Immortal Manor, eighteen-year-old Lu Chen set out southwest toward the Continental Abyssal Trench accompanied by Su Qingxue (Middle Divine Sea), Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang (all Second Revolution Life-and-Death Wheel).

Flying across five thousand miles in less than two hours using Lu Chen's Spatial-Folding Stride, the five companions arrived above the Continental Abyssal Trench—a bottomless, hundred-mile-wide chasm in the southwestern ocean floor where seawater evaporated into void-mist before reaching fifty thousand feet of depth!

At eighty thousand feet below the surface, resting on the planetary mantle bedrock, stood a colossal, three-hundred-mile subterranean mausoleum cast entirely from Grade 6 Quasi-Saint Star-Bronze:

The Tomb-Fortress of the Pre-Epoch Bronze Emperor!

However, outside the towering nine-hundred-foot bronze gates of the tomb, three black-red Spatial Drill-Towers had already been erected!

Surrounding the drill-towers were sixteen Life-and-Death Wheel Overlords and four Early-to-Middle Divine Sea Ancestors from the Western Blood-Sea Alliance (secretly converted into blood-thralls by the Blood-Sovereign Clan's remaining continental apostle, Peak Divine Sea Overlord Xue Wuji!), using corrosive blood-lightning to crack the tomb's outer Rank 6 Peak Bronze Bell-Lock!
