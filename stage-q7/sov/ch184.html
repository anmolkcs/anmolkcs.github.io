At eighteen thousand feet of depth inside the trench, along a suspended ledge of Ancient Star-Magnet Stone, Lu Chen's sharp ears under Prism Stillness caught the sound of desperate explosions and metallic grinding half a mile ahead!

"Destroy Master Mo Ban's airship and blame it on the Astral Manta Beasts!" Xue Mingyuan laughed coldly as he swung a Grade 4 Middle-Tier Nether-Blood Scythe toward the airship's main hull. "Without the Artisan Guild's schematics, that brat Lu Chen will walk blind into our Rank 5 Blood-Corpse Trap at the bottom of the trench!"

"You won't need to wait until the bottom of the trench, Xue Mingyuan!"

DONG—ZAP!

Lu Chen's voice rang like heavenly thunder across the chasm!

Before Xue Mingyuan's scythe could touch Master Mo Ban's airship, Murong Zi and Chu Kuang flashed out on the flanks—their newly condensed Flawless Sword-Star Core and Gold-Scale Core erupting to cleave four Fifth-Order Astral-Wind Manta Beasts out of the sky in a single breath—while Lu Chen's figure appeared directly in front of Xue Mingyuan!

"《Primordial Star-Forge Spear — Second Form: Drawing the Star River + Second-Tier Gravitation-Thunder Domain》!"

KABOOOOOM—!

With 2,800,000 jin of spiral star-thunder and acoustic-space force erupting from the 7,200-jin Primordial Dragon-Bell Spear, Xue Mingyuan's Grade 4 Nether-Blood Scythe snapped cleanly in two on impact!

Xue Mingyuan's eyes bulged in sheer terror as his Peak Revolving Core defense shattered like paper, and before he or his twelve Nether-Blood cultists could crush their escape tokens, Lu Chen's sweeping spear-wind blasted all thirteen ambushers into the howling Astral Lightning River below!
