When the spatial portal of the Secret Realm of the Fallen Star opened on the tenth day and Lu Chen’s team stepped out onto Star-Mist Peak, the presiding Revolving Core Grand Elder and Elder Tie were overjoyed to see all thirteen Seven Star Valley disciples return alive with a harvest three times larger than any previous decade!

However, the moment Lu Chen presented the captured Shadow-Moon Sect Spirit-Vein Eroding Flags and reported that a branch of Shadow-Moon infiltrators—colluding with remnants of the Azure Sky Kingdom’s disgraced Third Prince Zhao Heng—had headed south to raid the Azure Sky Royal Capital’s spirit vein and seize hostages from the Seven Star Valley’s core disciples’ families, the Sect Master’s expression turned ice-cold!

“The Shadow-Moon Sect dares send Peak Houtian and Half-Step Xiantian raiders toward the Azure Sky Kingdom?!” Elder Tie immediately summoned his fastest mount—a Peak Third-Order (Half-Step Xiantian) Golden-Winged Roc capable of flying five hundred miles an hour—and tossed the reins to Lu Chen:

“Little Chen, I’m leading three Xiantian Elders two hours behind you after securing the sect vein—ride my Golden-Winged Roc ahead to the Azure Sky Royal Capital right now and protect your parents at Clear-Brook Manor!”
