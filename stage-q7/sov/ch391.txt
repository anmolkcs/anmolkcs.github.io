<p>In the month following the fall of the Three Undying Empyrean Ancestors and the surrender of the Seven Sovereign Clans' branch forces, a golden era of peace, order, and restoration dawned across the ten-billion-mile <strong>Central Star-Soul Imperial Sea</strong>.</p>
<p>Unlike the Seven Sovereign Clans, who had ruled through blood-taxation and world-siphoning chains for half an epoch, eighteen-year-old <strong>Peak Empyrean Lu Chen</strong> and <strong>Peak Empyrean Su Qingxue</strong>—assisted by newly ascended <strong>Empyrean Su Wan'er</strong>, <strong>Empyrean Lu Shan</strong>, <strong>Empyrean Meng Wan</strong>, <strong>Empyrean Tie Cang</strong>, <strong>Empyrean Murong Zi</strong>, and <strong>Empyrean Chu Kuang</strong>—issued the <strong>New Star-Court Covenant</strong>:</p>
<ol type="1">
<li>All world-siphoning chains across the ten thousand star-fields of the Upper Star-Soul Realm and the lower mortal continents were permanently abolished and melted down into <strong>Public Star-Rail Transit Bridges</strong>;</li>
<li>The seven ancestral stars of the former Seven Sovereign Clans were reorganized into the <strong>Seven Halls of the Primordial Star-Court</strong>—with <strong>Elder Tie Cang</strong>, <strong>Murong Zi</strong>, and <strong>Chu Kuang</strong> overseeing the <strong>Martial &amp; Spear-Saber Hall</strong>, <strong>Su Wan'er</strong> and <strong>Star-Artisan Sovereign Mo Jing</strong> presiding over the <strong>Grand Inscription &amp; Artisan Hall</strong>, and <strong>Su Qingxue</strong> restoring her ancestral <strong>Chrono-Yin Imperial Hall</strong>;</li>
<li>Most impressively, over seven consecutive days, Lu Chen used his <strong>Rank 9 Peak Empyrean Inscription Mastery</strong> and his antiquities-restorer's hand to repair every single tectonic scar inflicted upon the million-mile <strong>Chrono-Yin Mother-Star</strong> over the past three thousand years, restoring its silver time-lotus oceans and spirit-mountains to a beauty surpassing even its ancient golden age!</li>
</ol>
<p>And on the sunniest southern mountain peak of the restored Chrono-Yin Mother-Star—overlooking a sea of blooming ten-thousand-year spirit-peach orchards and silver time-springs—Lu Chen personally built an exact, expanded replica of their beloved family home:</p>
<p><strong>Warm-Spring Valley #1!</strong></p>
