BOOM—BOOM—BOOM—BOOM!

At that exact instant—

RUMBLE—CRACKLE—ROOOOOAAAR!

Not just above the Seven Star Valley Sect, but across three entire prefectures (three thousand miles of sky!), the night heavens turned as bright as noon as a Three-Hundred-Mile Nine-Colored Starry Sky Phenomenon and a Nine-Wave Purple-Gold Heavenly Core Tribulation descended upon Dubhe Peak!

"A three-hundred-mile Nine-Colored Starry Sky Phenomenon?! And a Nine-Wave Heavenly Core Tribulation that only appears when a Twelve-Faceted Supreme Core is born?!"

Down on the seven peaks, the Sect Master, Elder Tie, Grandmaster Gu, and all ten thousand disciples—alongside Lu Shan, Meng Wan, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang—watched in awe as seventeen-year-old Lu Chen rose into the center of the three-hundred-mile starry sky!
