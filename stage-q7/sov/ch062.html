DONG—!

High above the canyon rift, the five presiding Xiantian Envoys of the Seven Star Valley Sect struck five ancient bronze seals simultaneously, tearing open a hundred-foot spatial rift into the Ancient Sky-Fall Battlefield!

“All fifty trial disciples—activate your life-saving teleportation tokens if you face fatal danger, though doing so forfeits half your points! The seven-day trial begins—enter!”

Fifty streaks of light shot through the spatial rift into a vast, desolate basin crisscrossed by jagged meteor craters, petrified forests, and roaring rivers of wild origin energy.

Because the spatial rift scattered entrants randomly within a fifty-mile outer ring, Lu Chen landed alone atop a blackened basalt ridge in the northern sector of the battlefield.

Before his boots even settled on the basalt rock, two Late Second-Order Iron-Clawed Panthers (equivalent to Peak Fifth Stage warriors) lunged silently from the petrified trees behind him!

Lu Chen didn’t even draw the 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear from his back. Pivoting half a step, he sent out two crisp palm strikes infused with 14,000 jin of baseline Golden-Jade Bone strength, knocking both panthers unconscious into the ravine and collecting two 10-Point Star-Fallen Tokens that condensed from the battlefield’s array law!

Checking his jade compass—which showed the faint signal of his fellow Azure Sky Kingdom disciples thirty miles to the southeast near Wind-Howl Valley—Lu Chen tapped his toes with the 《Golden Roc Nine-Flash Step》 and sped across the petrified forest like a bolt of black lightning.
