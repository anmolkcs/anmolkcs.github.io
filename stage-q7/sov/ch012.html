At dawn two days later, the grand plaza at the foot of Seven Star Mountain ten miles outside the Royal Capital was a sea of boiling noise and gleaming steel.

Once every year, the Seven Star Martial House—the highest martial institution of the Azure Sky Kingdom, founded jointly by the Royal Family and a retired Xiantian Elder of the Seven Star Valley Sect—opened its gates to recruit external and inner disciples between the ages of fifteen and seventeen.

Over three thousand youths from all thirty-six prefectures of the kingdom had gathered below the towering white-jade mountain gate.

However, less than two hundred of those youths held Ancestral Recommendation Tokens or came from great aristocratic families; those two hundred stood in the front queue, eligible to enter the Core Two-Stage Trial directly, while the remaining two thousand eight hundred had to fight through a brutal three-day preliminary elimination just for the right to touch the mountain steps.

In the front queue, Lu Chen stood quietly in his plain black martial robe, the eight-foot oilcloth bundle strapped to his back.

Around him, the sons and daughters of the Royal Capital’s great noble clans wore boots of spirit-beast leather, silver silk tunics, and Grade 2 Human-Step swords inlaid with gems. Seeing a boy in ten-copper coarse cotton standing in the VIP Recommendation Queue immediately drew sidelong glances and quiet sneers.

“Look at that country bumpkin with the charred iron pole on his back,” a tall, hawk-nosed seventeen-year-old youth in crimson brocade laughed, nudging his companions. This was Zhang Cang, the eldest grandson of the Capital Garrison Commander, possessing Grade 4 Lower-Tier Talent and a cultivation at the Peak of the Third Stage (Viscera Training). “Which dead branch of a country clan sold their chicken coop to send him here?”

Beside Zhang Cang stood an even more imposing youth wearing a golden-threaded robe embroidered with a coiled flood-dragon—Lin Xuan, a nephew of the Empress Consort’s clan, whose talent reached Grade 4 Upper-Tier and who had already stepped half a foot into the Fourth Stage: Altering Muscle & Tendons!

When the registration deacon at the jade desk took Lu Chen’s bronze Recommendation Token and held a crystal Meridian Testing Orb to Lu Chen’s wrist, the orb lit up with two full white rings and a third ring that glowed halfway bright.

“Lu Chen, fifteen years old, Cloudstride City!” the deacon read aloud, frowning slightly at the orb before writing on the roster. “Cultivation: Early Third Stage (Viscera Training). Meridian Talent: Grade 3 Lower-Tier (Borderline Grade 2)!”

Because the Chaotic Star Body Art had scoured away a fifth of the impurities in his meridians over the past ten days, Lu Chen’s talent had risen from Grade 2 Upper-Tier to the threshold of Grade 3 Lower-Tier—yet in the front queue of the Seven Star Martial House, where Grade 3 Middle-Tier was the bare minimum and Grade 4 was common, a borderline Grade 3 rating triggered a burst of open laughter.

“Grade 2 talent barely scraping Grade 3? Hahaha!” Zhang Cang laughed so hard he slapped his thigh. “Even if a pig eats spirit herbs for fifteen years, its meridians are wider than that! Why waste a precious Recommendation Token that could have been sold for three hundred taels of silver?”

Even the registration deacon shook his head as he tossed Lu Chen’s numbered wooden plaque (#189) onto the desk. “In the eighty-year history of the Seven Star Martial House, only eighty candidates out of three thousand pass the entrance trial each year, and never once has anyone below Grade 3 Middle-Tier survived both the Illusion Array of Ten Thousand Wills and the Pagoda of Crushing Gravity. Step onto the square, #189—don’t block the noble candidates behind you.”

Lu Chen picked up wooden plaque #189 without a ripple of anger on his face.

High above on the white-jade viewing terrace overlooking the mountain gate, several elders of the Seven Star Martial House sat behind sandalwood tables, and standing beside the white-browed Presiding Elder was Su Wan’er.

Seeing Lu Chen walk calmly onto the formation square despite the laughter around him, Su Wan’er blinked in surprise.

“Only borderline Grade 3 talent?” Presiding Elder Sun stroked his beard as he glanced down following Su Wan’er’s gaze. “Wan’er, you know that boy in the black robe?”

“Two days ago at the Sky Treasure Pavilion, he acted as the envoy for a mysterious Inscription Master,” Su Wan’er said softly, her brows knitting in puzzlement. “With borderline Grade 3 talent, it should be impossible for him to reach the Third Stage of Body Transformation at age fifteen without a great clan feeding him spirit pills… Wait—look at his footsteps on the white-jade tiles, Elder Sun. Even carrying that heavy iron bundle, his center of gravity doesn’t waver by a hair.”

Before Elder Sun could reply, three bronze bells tolled across Seven Star Mountain.

DONG—DONG—DONG!

“First Round of the Entrance Trial: The Illusion Array of Ten Thousand Wills!” Elder Sun’s voice boomed across the plaza, amplified by Houtian True Essence. “Talent determines how fast a martial artist gathers qi, but Martial Will determines whether you become a dragon or die in the mud! Step into the stone pillars—anyone who lasts half an incense stick passes; anyone who lasts a full incense stick earns ten bonus points!”
