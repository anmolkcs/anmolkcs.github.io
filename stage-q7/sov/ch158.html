The Sunken Bronze Bell Key-Shard from Star-Fall Lake; 2. The Bronze Tide-Controlling Seal from the Abyssal Salt-Sea; 3. And the Bronze Star-Astrolabe Map from the Star-Burial Glacier.

At the center of the holographic map glowed an upside-down, subterranean mechanical-and-runic metropolis buried beneath shifting glass dunes:

The Imperial Capital of the Buried Clockwork & Bronze Dynasty—and its central core: The Heaven-Mending Bronze Pillar!
