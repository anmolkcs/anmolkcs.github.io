Early the next morning, Lu Chen walked along the mist-shrouded stone path to the Martial Skill Library Pavilion at the center of the Seven Star Martial House.

The Library Pavilion was a majestic five-story octagonal tower guarded by an ancient Rank 4 defensive formation and overseen by a sleepy, wizened old man in gray hemp robes sitting by the entrance desk, warming his hands over a small charcoal brazier.

When Lu Chen handed over his free Library Token, the gray-robed old man barely cracked open one eyelid.

“First floor holds Grade 1 Human-Step Martial Skills and foundational manuals; second floor holds Grade 2 Human-Step Martial Skills, accessible only to disciples who have entered the top hundred of the Ten Thousand Killing Array or paid twenty True Essence Stones,” the old man rasped. “You have one hour to select a single jade slip to copy. Break a slip, and you’ll sweep this pavilion for three years.”

Stepping into the first floor, Lu Chen saw rows of tall rosewood bookshelves housing over three thousand bamboo and jade slips, categorized into Sword, Saber, Fist, Palm, Movement, and Polearm sections.

Already inside the hall were a dozen new disciples from yesterday’s trial—including Zhang Cang and several noble youths—clustered excitedly around the Sword and Saber aisles, competing over flashy Grade 1 Peak manuals like the Gale-Shadow Nine Swords and the Raging Flame Saber Art.

Lu Chen bypassed the crowded Sword aisle completely and walked straight to the quiet, dust-covered corner at the back: the Spear & Heavy Polearm Section.

In the Sky Spill Continent, the sword was revered as the gentleman of weapons, agile and versatile, while the heavy spear was shunned by nine out of ten noble disciples. Why? Because the spear was a battlefield weapon of life-and-death slaughter—difficult to master in a single month, demanding monstrous waist-and-spine strength, and lacking flashy defensive parries unless one’s foundation was absolute.

Under Prism Stillness, Lu Chen scanned through twenty Grade 1 Human-Step spear manuals in half an hour—Flickering Snake Spear, Cloud-Piercing Thirteen Thrusts, Whirlwind Halberd—and shook his head at each one.

“Every single one of these Grade 1 spear arts relies on wrist-flicks to create dazzling spear-flowers,” Lu Chen thought. “With an 820-jin heavy spear, trying to flick the wrist in fancy circles wastes forty percent of the kinetic momentum from the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath.”

Just as he reached the very bottom shelf beside the corner pillar, his gaze paused on an unnumbered, blackened iron-bamboo scroll buried beneath an inch of dust:

《Azure Army Foundational Heavy Spear — Eight True Moves》

There were no flashy names inside the scroll—only eight unadorned, brutal battlefield movements recorded by the founding general of the Azure Sky Kingdom three hundred years ago: 1. Level Thrust (Zhong Ping Ci) — Concentrating 100% of the body’s spiral force into a single point the size of a sesame seed. 2. Mountain-Splitting Smash (Pi Shan Za) — Using the full weight of the heavy shaft to crush enemy guards and shields like rotten wood. 3. Coiling Dragon Sweep (Pan Long Sao) — Whipping the spear shaft horizontally with waist-and-tendon torque. 4. Rising River挑 (Tiao) — Upward bone-tearing lift from beneath the horse’s belly. 5. Iron Bridge Block (Tie Qiao Lan), Tremor-Shaft Flick (Chan Gan Beng), Returning Horse Thrust (Hui Ma Qiang), and Collapsing Star Charge (Beng Xing Chong).

An ordinary disciple wouldn’t look twice at this scroll because any army recruit could learn the posture of these eight moves in three days.

Yet as Lu Chen read the fine annotations at the end of the iron-bamboo scroll under Prism Stillness, his breath quickened:

“When the Eight True Moves are practiced ten thousand times with a spear exceeding five hundred jin, the practitioner may compress nine consecutive waves of spiral vibration into a single thrust—creating the lost battlefield art: Nine-Layered Wave Force!”

“Nine-Layered Wave Force!” Lu Chen’s eyes lit up. “The Chaotic Star Body Art’s Silk-Unwinding Star Breath naturally twists my muscle fibers into nine-layered spirals! If I combine the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath with this Azure Army Foundational Heavy Spear, a single basic Level Thrust of my 820-jin spear won’t just strike with 3,900 jin of brute force—it will unleash consecutive internal shockwaves that can shatter a Fourth Stage martial artist’s defenses from the inside out!”

When Lu Chen brought the dusty Azure Army Foundational Heavy Spear scroll to the front desk for copying, Zhang Cang—who was registering a flashy Crimson Thunder Sword Art—let out a mocking laugh.

“Wait—you wasted a precious free Library Token that’s worth five True Essence Stones on the free army recruit manual that city guards practice?!” Zhang Cang sneered loudly so the whole hall turned to look. “I thought winning first place in the trial meant you had some hidden brain, #189, but it turns out a country butcher only knows how to read picture books!”

By the charcoal brazier, however, the sleepy gray-robed old man suddenly opened both eyes, glancing from the blackened iron-bamboo scroll in Lu Chen’s hand to the 820-jin spear strapped across the boy’s back, a faint, inscrutable smile flickering across his wrinkled face as he stamped the copy slip without saying a word.
