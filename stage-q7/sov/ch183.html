One thousand miles north of the Seven Star Valley Sect, the continent ended abruptly at a terrifying, bottomless chasm that looked as though a primordial god had split the earth in two with a single spear stroke:

The Astral Storm-Trench!

Ten thousand miles long, two hundred miles wide, and over thirty thousand feet deep, the trench was filled with howling silver-black Astral Wind-Blades and upside-down rivers of Nine-Heavens Purple Astral Lightning that poured up from the planetary core!

Leaving Elder Cang Feng and Elder Yin-Sha on the upper cliff terrace (where any Life-and-Death Wheel aura descending below five thousand feet would trigger a catastrophic spatial collapse), seventeen-year-old Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang stepped off the cliff edge into the roaring silver-black abyss!

WHOOSH—SHRIEK!

At ten thousand feet of depth, the Astral Wind-Blades blew at twenty times the speed of sound, carrying a crushing Fivefold Spatial-Gravity Shear that could rip ordinary Grade 3 treasures to pieces!

Yet as Lu Chen unfolded a fifty-yard Second-Tier Gravitation-Thunder & Acoustic-Space Domain around his three companions, the howling Astral Wind-Blades didn't smash against them—instead, guided by the acoustic standing waves of Lu Chen's 7,200-jin Primordial Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear, the silver astral wind flowed smoothly around their domain like water parting around a diving dragon!
