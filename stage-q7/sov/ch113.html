"Our keel's Gyroscopic Star-Compass is jammed—if someone doesn't recalibrate the Rank 4 Magnetic-Bearing Gears inside the wheelhouse within ten minutes while we hold off Captain Xue Jiao and the Kraken, the keel array will overload and drop the entire ship twenty thousand feet into the Wind-Chasm!" the Middle Xiantian captain roared in desperation as he and Grandmaster Gu flew up to clash with the Peak Xiantian pirate overlord Captain Xue Jiao!

"Impossible! The magnetic torque is tearing the rune-lines apart—anyone who puts their soul force into that compass right now without Rank 4 Peak Micro-Restoration control will have their spiritual sea shredded!"
