<p>Following the deep, water-filled cloven tracks of the frightened Iron-Bristle Boar, the six-man hunting party crept eastward in single file through a narrow tunnel of overarching black-briar thorns for nearly a mile.</p>
<p>Here the yellow sulfur-mist hung so thick that visibility dropped to less than ten paces, and the air smelled of rotten eggs, decaying pine needles, and the rank, musky stench of wild swine.</p>
<p>Walking second in line right behind Uncle Shi—having moved up from the flank so his <strong>eighty-jin boar-spear</strong> and three-pace <strong>Prism Stillness</strong> could cover the point—Lu Chen suddenly stopped and shot his left hand out to grab the back of Uncle Shi's boar-skin collar.</p>
<p>"Hold," Lu Chen breathed.</p>
<p>Ten feet ahead, where the briar tunnel opened onto the marshy bank of <strong>Bog-Viper Creek</strong>, the deep tracks of the six-hundred-pound Iron-Bristle Boar ended abruptly in a churned pit of black mud and dark blood.</p>
<p>Yet there was no boar carcass in the pit.</p>
<p>Instead, three freshly severed <strong>human fingers</strong> still wearing a rusted iron thumb-ring lay in the trampled mud beside a snapped bamboo blow-dart, and leading away up the opposite bank were the boot-prints of four men dragging the six-hundred-pound boar on a hastily lashed pole-litter!</p>
<p>"Broken-Tooth Poachers!" veteran hunter <strong>Old Ma</strong> hissed from two paces behind Lu Chen, his hollow cheeks twitching as he peered past Lu Chen's shoulder at the severed fingers in the mud. "That's a notched bone-ring from <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> camp! The bastards must have ambushed the boar with blow-poison at the creek crossing and lost a man's fingers when it thrashed!"</p>
<p>"Wait—don't step off the pine root, Old Ma!" Lu Chen snapped, his head whipping around as a tiny, almost imperceptible <em>slither</em> of displaced mud registered at the very outer three-pace edge of his <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>!</p>
<p>In his eagerness to examine the severed fingers, forty-five-year-old Old Ma had shifted his left boot six inches off the granite root onto a mound of rotting black moss beside the creek bank.</p>
<p>Before the warning had even finished leaving Lu Chen's lips, the mound of black moss exploded upward!</p>
<p><em>SHHHHHK!</em></p>
<p>A five-foot-long, wrist-thick <strong>Grey-Bog Viper</strong>—a cold-blooded ambush predator whose scales matched the exact color of sulfur-mud and whose strike carried the explosive snap of a <strong>Peak Tier One</strong> beast—launched from beneath the moss! Its triangular head bypassed Old Ma's leather shin-guard and sank two curved, hollow fangs half an inch deep into the meat of Old Ma's left calf!</p>
<p>"AAAHH!"</p>
<p>Old Ma let out a strangled cry and stumbled backward into the briars as the viper coiled to strike a second time at his groin!</p>
<p><em>CLANG—SPLAT!</em></p>
<p>Lu Chen did not even raise his spear point. Reversing his grip on the eighty-jin solid iron boar-spear in a fraction of a heartbeat, he lashed the flat octagonal butt-end of the shaft sideways across the three-pace gap!</p>
<p>Driven by twelve hundred jin of <strong>Complete Tier One</strong> strength, the heavy iron butt caught the Grey-Bog Viper mid-coil and smashed its triangular skull and six inches of its spine flat into the granite root!</p>
<p>Before the dead snake's tail had even stopped thrashing in the mud, Lu Chen dropped his spear, vaulted across the root, and landed on one knee beside the fallen Old Ma.</p>
<p>With his bare hands—tough as cured ox-hide—Lu Chen ripped Old Ma's hemp trousers open from ankle to knee. Around the two fang-holes on the hunter's calf, the skin had already turned an ominous, spreading ash-purple as the neurotoxic <strong>Bog-Rot Venom</strong> rushed upward toward his femoral vein!</p>
<p>"If it passes his knee, he's dead in ten breaths!" Uncle Shi paled, fumbling frantically for his skinning knife. "I have to cut the leg off at the knee—!"</p>
<p>"Hold his shoulders down and give me the knife and a dry cupping-horn!" Lu Chen commanded, his voice cold and steady as surgical steel.</p>
<p>Using the three-pace microscopic clarity of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen could see the exact subterranean boundary where the purple venom-front was climbing four inches below the knee joint—it had not yet entered the deep popliteal artery!</p>
<p>Before Uncle Shi could blink, Lu Chen whipped three turns of rawhide cord around Old Ma's upper calf just above the purple line, cinching it tight with eight hundred jin of finger-strength to arrest the venous return completely. Then, with two lightning-fast, quarter-inch cross-incisions of the skinning knife directly over the fang-holes, Lu Chen clapped a heated ox-horn cup—warmed over a flint-sparked pinch of sulfur powder—over the wound while his right thumb hammered five rapid percussive strikes down Old Ma's calf muscle from the knee toward the incision!</p>
<p><em>SPLURT!</em></p>
<p>Nearly half a cup of thick, black-purple poisoned blood shot out of the cross-cut into the vacuum of the ox-horn, followed two seconds later by bright, uncorrupted red blood!</p>
<p>Lu Chen immediately yanked the horn off, packed the bleeding cross-cut with a thick plug of <strong>Granny Liu's Ash-Root Powder</strong> and crushed <strong>Grey-Bog Viper Gall</strong> ripped straight from the dead snake's belly, and bound the calf tightly with clean linen.</p>
<p>Old Ma lay gasping in the mud, his face drenched in cold sweat, staring down at his intact left leg as the purple discoloration around the ankle slowly faded to a dull red bruise.</p>
<p>"The... the leg is saved," Uncle Shi whispered, staring at Lu Chen's blood-stained hands as if looking at a miracle worker. "In thirty years on this ridge, I've never seen a man bitten by a Grey-Bog Viper keep both his life and his foot!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen wiped the skinning knife on the moss and handed it back to Uncle Shi, his eyes turning back toward the four sets of poacher boot-prints heading up the eastern ravine.</p>
<p>"Old Ma can't walk on that calf for two days, and we can't send him back through the mist alone," Lu Chen said quietly, picking up his eighty-jin iron spear. "There's an overhanging rock-cave half a mile up this ridge where we can build a thorn-blind. Cross-Eyed Liu stays in the cave to guard Old Ma—and the four of us follow the ridge crest above those poachers. They didn't drag that boar toward their main camp; they're heading toward the <strong>Hot-Mud Wallow</strong> to bait the boar's mate."</p>
