Seeing that Lu Chen’s spear was momentarily wedged two feet deep inside the giant rhino’s skull, Gui Yan didn’t use a physical weapon. Instead, he bit the tip of his tongue and spat a mouthful of black blood onto the green skull at the tip of his bone staff!

That Hundred-Ghost Devouring Spike was his trump card that had once killed a full Sixth-Stage (Pulse Condensation) martial master in a single ambush; against a fifteen-year-old Peak Fourth Stage boy, it was certain death!

Inside Lu Chen’s spiritual sea, the pitch-black Hundred-Ghost Spike tore howling through the outer mist, aiming straight for Lu Chen’s consciousness—and for the nine-faceted Chrono-Soul Prism floating behind him!

Instantly, the nine-faceted gray Prism hummed with its innate Soul-Preservation Law.

Deep inside the Prism’s core, the snow-robed woman slumbering within the central lotus remained completely unconscious—yet the autonomous preservation barrier that the Prism maintained around her soul and Lu Chen’s spiritual sea reacted automatically to the intruding curse!

A single, hair-thin ripple of silver-gray timeless light rebounded off the Prism’s third facet.

Pffft.

The terrifying Hundred-Ghost Devouring Spike that Gui Yan had spent ten years refining didn’t even let out a sound; the instant it touched the Prism’s passive preservation ripple, it was erased like a snowflake falling into a furnace—and the backlash of the broken curse rebounded straight along the spiritual link into Gui Yan’s own skull!

Outside in Thunder-Scar Gorge:

“ARGH—MY EYES! MY SOUL SEA!”

Ghost-Eye Gui Yan hadn’t even finished his savage smile when both of his green-glowing eyeballs ruptured in a spray of blood! His bone staff shattered into powder in his hand, and he collapsed to his knees clutching his head as seventy percent of his spiritual sea froze and crumbled on the spot!

Golden Roc Nine-Flash Step — Fourth Flash!
