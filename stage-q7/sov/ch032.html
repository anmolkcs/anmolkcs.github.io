At the bottom of Thunder-Scar Gorge, a thirty-foot-wide fissure split the red ironstone cliff where subterranean lightning had struck seven days ago, exposing a glowing vein of raw Purple-Magnetic Iron Ore.

Growing right at the lip of the warm magnetic fissure were three fist-sized lotuses with petals that shimmered like spun red gold:

Three stalks of mature Crimson-Gold Earth-Core Lotus!

And sleeping twenty paces in front of the magnetic fissure was a twelve-foot-long, six-foot-tall behemoth covered in overlapping plates of blue-black iron armor, with a curved, two-foot-long spiral horn on its snout that crackled periodically with arcs of silver lightning—the Early Third-Order Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros!

Along the beast’s left flank, a three-foot-long fissure charred by heavenly lightning exposed raw, pulsing muscle and blackened rib bone. Every time the giant rhino exhaled, blood-tinged mist puffed from its nostrils as it absorbed the magnetic essence of the Crimson-Gold Earth-Core Lotuses to repair its ruptured lung meridian.

If I wait until it swallows those three Crimson-Gold Earth-Core Lotuses tomorrow morning, its injury will heal by half and I won’t have a chance, Lu Chen judged instantly from the cliff ledge eighty feet above. I have to strike tonight while its lung meridian is at its weakest during the midnight Yin-qi tide!

However, just as Lu Chen crouched behind the red-pine ridge waiting for midnight, his sharp ears—enhanced by Prism Stillness—caught the faint, almost imperceptible sound of five sets of breathing approaching along the windward cliff trail half a mile behind him!

Lu Chen melted silently into the shadow of an overhanging magnet-stone crevice, concealing every trace of his blood-qi with the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath.

Moments later, five figures dressed in dark gray leather armor slipped onto the western ridge overlooking the gorge.

Four of the men carried heavy crossbows and notched broadsabers, their auras all at the Early to Middle Fifth Stage (Bone Forging)—veterans of the notorious Black-Skull Mercenary Band!

And walking at the head of the four mercenaries was a tall, gaunt fifty-year-old man holding a dark-green bone staff, whose eyes glowed with an eerie,phosphorescent green light and whose cultivation stood at the Half-Step Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation)!

“Boss Gui Yan (Ghost-Eye),” one of the Fifth Stage mercenaries whispered, checking a slip of paper tied to a black hawk’s leg before glancing down into the gorge. “Young Master Lin Sen paid us fifty Low-Grade True Essence Stones to follow that Seven Star Martial House brat Lu Chen out of Iron-Pass Outpost and snap his neck in the wilderness. Why did his tracks vanish right above this gorge?”

The gaunt man holding the bone staff—Ghost-Eye Gui Yan, a renegade Rank 1 Peak Soul-Curse Practitioner wanted in three prefectures—let out a hoarse, grating chuckle as he looked down at the injured Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros and the three glowing Crimson-Gold Earth-Core Lotuses below.

“Forget the fifty True Essence Stones for a moment—that brat Lu Chen actually led us straight to an injured Third-Order Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros and three stalks of Crimson-Gold Earth-Core Lotus worth over two hundred True Essence Stones!” Gui Yan licked his pale lips greedily. “That kid must be hiding somewhere near the gorge entrance waiting to steal a lotus. Let’s set up the Four-Corner Iron-Net Trap at the gorge mouth—once the Thunder-Horned Rhino wakes at midnight, we’ll use my Blood-Rot Poison Bolts to finish off the beast, harvest its Third-Order horn and marrow, and then hunt down that Peak Fourth Stage brat like a rabbit!”

Hidden thirty yards away in the magnet-stone crevice, Lu Chen’s eyes turned colder than eternal winter ice.

Lin Sen paid a band of Fifth-Stage mercenaries and a Half-Step Sixth-Stage Soul-Curse practitioner to murder me outside the Martial House?

Instead of stepping out prematurely, Lu Chen slowly drew three Rank 1 Peak Exploding-Flame Inscription Talismans from his pouch and waited quietly in the dark as Gui Yan and his four mercenaries crept down into the gorge to lay their iron nets around the sleeping Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros.
