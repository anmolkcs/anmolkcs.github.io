On the eighteenth day of seclusion inside the Profound Gravity Cave, the underground river of crimson magma beneath the platforms suddenly began to churn violently.

RUMBLE—ROAR!

Once every three years, the subterranean earth-fire vein beneath Seven Star Mountain experienced a natural Earth-Fire Magnetic Tide. Normally, the tide occurred in late winter, yet a subterranean shift in the mountain range had triggered it two months early!

Within three breaths, a thirty-foot-high tidal wave of condensed Crimson Earth-Fire Poison & Magnetic Shockwaves erupted from the magma trench, sweeping outward toward the 7x and 8x Gravity Platforms where Su Wan’er and Murong Zi were in the middle of critical meridian condensation!

If a martial artist in mid-cultivation was struck by the Earth-Fire Magnetic Tide, their open meridians would suffer a violent qi deviation, leaving them severely injured for half a year!

“Not good—the Earth-Fire Tide erupted while my eleventh meridian is half-open!” On the 7x platform, Su Wan’er’s face turned pale as she tried to pull back her origin energy, only for the magnetic shockwave to lock down the space around her cushion!

On the 8x platform, Murong Zi also coughed a trace of blood as his Star-River Sword hummed wildly under the magnetic interference.

At that critical split second, on the 12x Gravity Core Altar directly ahead of them, Lu Chen’s eyes snapped open!

Instead of retreating toward the cave exit to protect himself, Lu Chen stood up under the crushing twelvefold gravity, gripped his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear, and leaped straight between the roaring thirty-foot magma wave and his two fellow disciples!

“Senior Brother Murong, Senior Sister Su—stabilize your meridians! Leave the tide to me!”

DONG—!

Lu Chen planted his boots on the stone bridge linking the platforms. His nine opened meridians and 10,800 jin of Golden-Jade Bone strength erupted simultaneously as he swung the 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear in a continuous, blazing figure-eight vortex—

《Wildfire Prairie Spear — Wildfire Sweeps the Plains + Embryonic Mountain-Thunder Spear Intent!》

ZAP—BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Like an immovable mountain peak standing in the middle of a raging sea of fire, Lu Chen’s heavy spear wove an impenetrable twelve-foot wall of purple lightning and spiral gravity!

Wave after wave of scorching Earth-Fire Magnetic force slammed against his spear curtain. The tremendous counter-force tore open the skin of Lu Chen’s palms and sent trickles of blood down the black-purple spear shaft, yet his boots did not slide backward by a single tenth of an inch!

Even more miraculously, instead of merely blocking the Earth-Fire Magnetic Tide, Lu Chen used the 《Chaotic Star Body Art》 to act as a lightning rod—drawing the wildest magnetic fire-essence into his own body and hammering it straight against the barriers of his Tenth and Eleventh Primary Meridians!

For two full hours, Lu Chen stood alone in front of Su Wan’er and Murong Zi, thrusting and sweeping his 1,220-jin spear three thousand times until the subterranean magma tide finally subsided.

When Su Wan’er and Murong Zi opened their eyes safely—having used the purified origin energy behind Lu Chen’s spear curtain to reach the Peak of the Sixth Stage—they saw Lu Chen standing tall at the head of the bridge, his robe singed at the hem, yet his aura burning twice as bright as before with Eleven Primary Meridians wide open!

“Junior Brother Lu… you stood under twelvefold gravity for two hours to block the Earth-Fire Tide for us?” Su Wan’er’s eyes shimmered with deep gratitude, while Murong Zi bowed solemnly to the floor: “Murong Zi owes you a life-saving grace, Junior Brother Lu.”
