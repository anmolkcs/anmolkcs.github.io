Inside the twenty-yard ironstone challenge ring on Pine-Wind Cliff, Zhu Hong rolled his broad shoulders, and an ominous crack-crack-crack echoed from his neck and spine as his altered tendons tensed like iron cables.

“Freshman, don’t say this Senior Brother bullied you without giving you a chance to lift your heavy pole!” Zhu Hong grinned ferociously, gripping the hilt of his four-foot Grade 2 Ghost-Head Broadsaber with both hands. “Take my strongest Grade 1 Peak Martial Skill—《Raging Tiger Splits the Mountain》!”

BOOM!

The ironstone slab beneath Zhu Hong’s right boot cracked into spiderwebs as he lunged across the twenty-yard ring in a single breath!

Because a Peak Fourth Stage martial artist had completed Altering Muscle & Tendons, their explosive sprint speed was nearly twice that of an ordinary Third Stage warrior. In the blink of an eye, Zhu Hong’s burly shadow loomed three feet above Lu Chen, and the heavy Ghost-Head Broadsaber whistled down toward Lu Chen’s right shoulder with 5,200 jin of ferocious downward force!

“He’s aiming straight for Lu Chen’s right collarbone and tendon plexus!” several watching freshmen cried out, pale-faced. If an 820-jin spear was caught off-balance by a 5,200-jin overhead chop from a Grade 2 broadsaber, the shockwave alone would tear the wielder’s shoulder socket apart!

Beneath the descending shadow of the broadsaber, Lu Chen didn’t retreat half an inch.

Inhale—Silk-Unwinding Star Breath!

Along his spine and arms, the newly forged Dragon-Sinew Tendons gave a low, resonant twang like a giant horn bow pulled to the full moon!

Instead of raising the spear horizontally to block passively, Lu Chen gripped the butt of the 820-jin Black Iron Heavy Spear with both hands, sank his waist three inches, and drove the dark-red spearhead straight upward into the belly of Zhu Hong’s descending broadsaber—

《Azure Army Foundational Heavy Spear — Rising River Lift + Nine-Layered Wave Force: Fourth Wave!》

“Blocking my Grade 2 Ghost-Head Saber head-on from below?! Break for me!” Zhu Hong roared in midair, pouring every ounce of his 5,200-jin strength into the blade.

CLANG—BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

When the dark-iron spearhead collided with the edge of the Grade 2 Ghost-Head Broadsaber, there wasn’t just a single metallic clash—four chained thunderclaps detonated in rapid succession within a tenth of a breath!

Wave one: 4,800 jin of baseline spiral strength halted the 5,200-jin broadsaber dead in midair! Wave two: The 38% Flame-Edge & Heavy-Mountain Dual Inscription erupted, scorching a thumb-deep notch straight into the Grade 2 steel of the broadsaber! Wave three & Wave four: Over 6,200 jin of cumulative internal spiral vibration blasted straight through the broadsaber’s hilt into Zhu Hong’s wrists, elbows, and shoulders!

“What—?!”

Zhu Hong’s savage grin froze in terror as the tiger-mouth webbing between both of his thumbs split open in a spray of blood! His four-foot Grade 2 Ghost-Head Broadsaber was sent spinning wildly into the pine trees with a fractured blade, and before his boots could even touch the ground, Lu Chen reversed the 820-jin black iron spear shaft in a blurring horizontal arc—

《Coiling Dragon Sweep!》

WHAM—CRACK!

The two-inch-thick black iron spear shaft slammed squarely across Zhu Hong’s crossed forearms and chest!

Even with Peak Fourth Stage tendons, taking a 6,200-jin sweep from an 820-jin meteoric-iron shaft was like being hit head-on by a galloping Iron-Armored Rhinoceros! Holding back his killing force as a fellow sect disciple, Lu Chen used the flat of the heavy spear shaft to cleanly dislocate Zhu Hong’s right wrist and sweep him thirty feet out of the challenge ring, where he landed winded and stunned against the stone boundary pillar!

From the moment Zhu Hong leaped into the air to the moment he lay unconscious in the dirt with broken arms, less than two breaths had passed.

On the stone path under the pine trees, the thirty outer and inner disciples who had come to cheer for Zhu Hong stood frozen like wooden statues, the morning wind rustling through the pine needles in absolute silence.

Even Lin Xuan, whose hands had been clasped leisurely behind his back, felt his pupils contract to pinpricks as his fingers clenched involuntarily inside his sleeves.

Over 6,000 jin of explosive spear force… and he shattered a Peak Fourth Stage inner disciple’s Grade 2 saber and blasted him out of the ring in a single exchange?!

Lu Chen grounded the butt of his 820-jin Black Iron Spear on the ring flagstones, walked over to the boundary pillar, picked up the leather pouch containing twenty Low-Grade True Essence Stones right off the unconscious Zhu Hong’s chest, and glanced calmly at the stunned Patrol Deacon Wu.

“Deacon Wu, the wager is settled. Please remind his younger brother to carry him off my path before he bleeds on my flagstones.”
