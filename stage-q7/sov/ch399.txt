<p>The next morning at dawn, after the joyful banquet had lasted late into the starry night, a light, silvery-gold mist drifted through the spirit-peach orchards of <strong>Warm-Spring Valley #1</strong>.</p>
<p>Beside the warm spring pool, <strong>Lu Shan</strong> stood in a low horse stance, practicing his basic spear thrusts with a hearty grin while <strong>Meng Wan</strong> watered the spirit-herbs under the grape trellis and <strong>Su Wan'er</strong> and <strong>Su Qingxue</strong> played a tranquil duet of zither and jade-chimes in the pavilion.</p>
<p>Out on the quiet bluestone cliff terrace overlooking the sea of clouds, nineteen-year-old <strong>Lu Chen</strong> stood barefoot in a simple black linen tunic, holding his eleven-foot <strong>Primordial Sovereign Spear</strong> in both calloused hands.</p>
<p>Even though a single thought from his <strong>Hundred-Million-Mile Perfected Inner Universe</strong> could now create or extinguish galaxies and step across epochs of time, Lu Chen sank his mind into the quiet, familiar clarity of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, planted his feet shoulder-width apart, and thrust his spear forward into the morning mist:</p>
<p><em>One thrust.</em> <em>Two thrusts.</em> <em>Ten thousand thrusts.</em></p>
<p>Every thrust was unadorned, steady, and grounded—the exact same <strong>Level Thrust</strong> he had practiced before dawn on frozen courtyard flagstones on Earth and in the back alleys of Cloudstride City.</p>
