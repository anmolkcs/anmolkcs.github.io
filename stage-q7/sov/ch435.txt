<p>In front of him, the <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> poacher Ma Liu was already scrambling to his feet with his sixty-jin ring-saber raised; three paces behind him, fourteen hundred pounds of armored <strong>Peak Tier Two</strong> King-Boar was hurtling through the mud like an iron battering ram!</p>
<p>Up on the basalt ledge, fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> screamed a warning: "Big Brother Lu—behind you!" while <strong>Uncle Shi</strong> loosed a second broadhead arrow that sparked off the charging boar's forehead!</p>
<p>Lu Chen had nowhere to dodge in the knee-deep mud.</p>
<p>And inside the glacial stillness of his mind, he saw the only path through the jaws of death: <em>Use the boar to stop the bandit—and use the impact to hammer my own flesh!</em></p>
<p>Pivoting on his heels in the sucking clay, Lu Chen dropped into a deep, waist-high <strong>Rooted Dragon Stance</strong>, tucked the butt-spike of his eighty-jin solid Cold-Slag Spear firmly against a submerged granite boulder beneath the mud at a thirty-five-degree angle, locked both hyena-braced forearms around the middle of the shaft, and aimed the fourteen-inch four-edged diamond point straight into the open, screaming mouth of the charging King-Boar!</p>
<p><em>BOOOOOOOOOOM—CRACK!</em></p>
<p>Fourteen thousand jin of tenfold-gravity kinetic force slammed directly onto the point of the eighty-jin spear!</p>
<p>The four-edged <strong>Cold-Slag Core</strong> spearhead punched straight through the roof of the King-Boar's palate and wedged deep into the thick base of its cranium—until the horizontal boar-wings at the base of the spearhead caught against the beast's curved upper tusks!</p>
<p>The monstrous impact did not break the nine-folded spear shaft, nor did it shatter the submerged granite boulder behind the butt-spike. Instead, the entirety of the remaining shockwave crashed directly into Lu Chen's braced hands, forearms, shoulders, back, and thighs!</p>
<p><em>RIP—THUMP!</em></p>
<p>Beneath his tough <strong>Complete Tier One</strong> ox-hide skin, which held together without splitting, every major muscle group in Lu Chen's upper body and legs was compressed as if caught beneath a falling mountain! Stagnant mortal tissue-fluid and interstitial water were violently wrung out of his biceps, lats, quadriceps, and glutes by the crushing load; dark red blood burst from his pores as tiny crimson beads against his bronze hide; and his shin-bones sank six inches deeper into the gravel bed of the wallow!</p>
<p>For three agonizing heartbeats, the dying fourteen-hundred-pound King-Boar thrashed against the horizontal boar-wings of the spear, trying to crush the human holding the shaft into paste!</p>
<p>Lu Chen's teeth ground together until his jaw ached, his eyes blazing like dark stars in the sulfur mist as he refused to yield a single half-inch of knee-bend!</p>
<p>And in that crucible of unbearable mechanical compression—fueled by the dense nutrition of the <strong>Tier-One Hyena Marrow</strong> and <strong>Iron-Husk Millet</strong> still circulating in his blood—the torn muscle fibers inside his forearms and shoulders did not fray into ruin. Instead, wrung dry of weak mortal water under the fourteen-thousand-jin press, the muscle bundles twisted and compacted together like braided iron cables!</p>
<p><strong>Half-Step Tier Two of the Mortal-Shedding Stage!</strong></p>
<p>His physical strength surged from twelve hundred jin to <strong>one thousand five hundred jin</strong> in a single explosive pulse!</p>
<p>With a guttural roar that blew the sulfur mist apart, Lu Chen twisted his waist and wrenched the eighty-jin spear upward with both hands—using the seven-foot iron shaft as a lever to flip the dying fourteen-hundred-pound King-Boar sideways through the air and hurl its massive carcass straight onto <strong>Ma Liu</strong>, who was charging from behind with his sixty-jin saber raised!</p>
<p><em>CRASH—CRUNCH!</em></p>
<p>Caught mid-stride by fourteen hundred pounds of flying iron-bristled boar meat, Ma Liu only had time to let out a single terrified squawk before the carcass slammed him flat into the mud wallow, pinning him beneath its thrashing bulk up to his neck!</p>
<p>Before Ma Liu could squirm free from beneath the dead boar's flank, Lu Chen wrenched his eighty-jin spear out of the boar's palate, took one long stride across the mud, and drove the four-edged diamond point straight through Ma Liu's throat into the clay below!</p>
