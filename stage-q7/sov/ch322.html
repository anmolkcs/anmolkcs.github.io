At the entrance of the Middle-Ring Chain-Bridge, four Late Inner Embryo World guards of the Flame-Sovereign Clan raised their blazing Grade 6 fire-pikes as Lu Chen and Su Qingxue approached:

"Halt! Young Patriarch Yan Wuji has sealed the Star Ring—anyone who steps onto the chain-bridge dies!"

Lu Chen didn't even touch the shaft of his spear.

As he took a single step forward, a silent ripple of 12.0 Billion Jin of 1,000-Mile Golden-Jade Inner World Force swept past his shoulders!

BOOM-BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!

All four Late Inner Embryo World guards had their fire-pikes shattered and were blasted unconscious into the outer asteroid belt without Lu Chen breaking his stride!

Crossing the ten-mile chain-bridge into the blazing Inner Ring, the gravitational pull of the fifty-mile White-Dwarf Star-Core overhead surged from twentyfold to One-Hundred-Fold Stellar Gravity, while waves of white-hot Astral Solar-Fire turned the vacuum around the asteroids into a roaring furnace!

Even a Peak Inner Embryo World cultivator had to burn 50% of their World Force just to keep their skin from melting here.

Yet to Lu Chen—whose physical body had been tempered by Golden-Jade Dharma Marrow, the Complete Law of Gravitation-Thunder, and a 1,000-Mile Inner World with nine star-suns—walking through One-Hundred-Fold Stellar Gravity felt like returning to his natural element!

Even better, with every breath of white-hot Astral Solar-Fire he inhaled, the Nine Purple-Gold Star-Suns inside his 1,000-mile Inner World grew hotter and brighter!
