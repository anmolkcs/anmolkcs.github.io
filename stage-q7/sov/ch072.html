Three thousand miles north of the Azure Sky Kingdom, the terrain rose into a magnificent, ten-thousand-foot mountain range where seven colossal peaks stood arranged like the Big Dipper (Northern Dipper) beneath a perpetual river of silver starlight!

This was the headquarters of the Seven Star Valley Sect—one of the seven great Fourth-Grade Xiantian Sects governing the southern region of the Sky Spill Continent, boasting over ten thousand Houtian disciples, seventy Xiantian Realm Elders, and three supreme Revolving Core Realm (Ning Dan) Grand Elders!

Each of the seven peaks represented a distinct martial lineage: 1. Dubhe Peak (Tian Shu) — The Sect Master’s Peak, governing comprehensive star-origin cultivation. 2. Alkaid Sword Peak (Yao Guang) — The largest and wealthiest combat peak, home to over three thousand sword cultivators. 3. Pill-Star Peak (Yu Heng) — The sacred ground of Alchemists and Inscription Masters. 4. Broken Spear Peak (Kai Yang / Mizar) — Once the fiercest battlefield peak of the sect a thousand years ago, now the smallest peak with fewer than thirty disciples because its Heavy Spear & Star-Body Lineage was ten times harder to cultivate than the sword!

When the fifteen newly recruited disciples from the Five-Kingdom Trial stood on Star-Gathering Square, the elders of Alkaid Sword Peak and Dubhe Peak immediately vied to recruit Lu Chen (#1 of the Five Kingdoms), Chu Kuang (#2), Murong Zi (#7), and Su Wan’er (#11).

Murong Zi naturally joined Alkaid Sword Peak, while Su Wan’er joined Pill-Star Peak to study both Sword and Inscription Arts.

When seven peak envoys turned expectantly to Lu Chen, however, Lu Chen looked past the luxurious pavilions of Dubhe Peak toward the rugged, thunder-scarred sixth peak—where a giant, three-hundred-foot ancient stone spear stood embedded in the cliff face—and bowed respectfully:

“Disciple Lu Chen wishes to join Broken Spear Peak, and register concurrently at Pill-Star Peak for the Rank 3 Inscription Master assessment!”
