<p>Silence fell over the steaming Boiling-Mud Wallow, broken only by the bubble of the volcanic mud spring and the ragged whimpering of the surviving <strong>Peak Tier One</strong> poacher, Blackie, who lay pinned to the pine root with Uncle Shi's broadhead arrow through his thigh.</p>
<p>Thirty seconds later, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, and fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> came sliding down the shale scree rope into the basin, their weapons drawn and their eyes wide as saucers.</p>
<p>Looking around the blood-soaked mud flat, Big-Tall Zhao's jaw dropped so low he nearly bit his tongue.</p>
<p>Staked on the left lay the six-hundred-pound female Iron-Bristle Boar. Center-stage lay the monstrous fourteen-hundred-pound <strong>Scarred King-Boar</strong>, its carotid cleanly bled before the Corpse-Rot Poison could taint a single ounce of its meat. And pinned in the mud around them lay all four members of <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> elite poaching squad—including their <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> squad leader Ma Liu, dead with a four-edged spear hole through his neck!</p>
<p>"Two... two thousand jin of prime winter boar meat, completely unpoisoned," Big-Tall Zhao stammered, dropping to his knees beside the massive King-Boar and slapping its four-inch-thick iron-bristle hide in disbelief. "And four Broken-Tooth Poachers wiped out in ten breaths! Uncle Shi—am I dreaming in the sulfur fog?!"</p>
<p>Uncle Shi didn't answer right away. The grizzled old hunter walked straight up to Lu Chen, who was leaning calmly on his eighty-jin black-iron spear while wiping the blood from his nose with the back of his bracer, and looked closely at the corded, compact contour of Lu Chen's forearms and shoulders.</p>
<p>"Your muscles..." Uncle Shi breathed, his three-fingered left hand touching the rock-hard ridge of Lu Chen's deltoid through his torn sleeve. "When you held that King-Boar's charge just now, you wrung the mortal water out of your arms and shoulders! You've already touched <strong>Half-Step Tier Two—Iron-Flesh Compacting</strong>!"</p>
<p>"Only the forearms and shoulders," Lu Chen said honestly, regulating his breathing as the intense soreness of the torn muscle fibers settled in. "To reach true <strong>Early Tier Two</strong>, every muscle group across the torso, back, and legs has to be compacted and nourished with Tier-Two beast meat over the next ten days. Right now, we need to clean this battlefield before the smell of blood draws every wolf pack in the eastern basin."</p>
<p>Walking over to the wounded poacher Blackie pinned to the pine root, Uncle Shi placed the edge of his skinning knife against the bandit's throat.</p>
<p>"How many men does <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> have at <strong>Broken-Tooth Camp</strong> this winter?" Uncle Shi demanded coldly. "And did he send another squad into the eastern basin today?"</p>
<p>Terrified out of his wits after watching Lu Chen flip a fourteen-hundred-pound King-Boar onto Ma Liu, Blackie babbled everything in one breath: "N-no other squad in the eastern basin today! Boss Han Wu is at the main camp in <strong>Dead-Crow Gorge</strong>, eight miles west! He's got twenty-two men this winter—three <strong>Tier Two</strong> lieutenants including Ma Liu, and Boss Han himself is at <strong>Peak Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Perfection)</strong>! He made a deal with <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong> in Black-Rock Town last month: we raid the outer village hunting parties and charcoal kilns so the villages default on their winter iron quotas, and Zhao Kuan splits the confiscated land and mine-bounties with Boss Han!"</p>
<p>Uncle Shi and Big-Tall Zhao both stiffened with fury.</p>
<p>"So Zhao Kuan and One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu are wearing the same pair of trousers!" Big-Tall Zhao spat into the mud. "No wonder Han Wu's poachers always knew which week our hunters went into the woods!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen's dark eyes remained completely still as he listened—adding another link to the chain connecting <strong>Ash-Brook Village</strong>, <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong>, <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong>, and <strong>Pit Overseer Luo Shan</strong>.</p>
<p>After Uncle Shi finished off the murderous poacher who had helped slaughter the Mud-Well Village hunters last winter, the four of them worked with practiced frontier efficiency. They buried the four poachers' bodies deep beneath the boiling sulfur mud-pit where neither wolves nor search parties would ever find a bone, and stripped their gear: - Ma Liu's sixty-jin <strong>Ring-Pommel Executioner's Saber</strong> and four heavy <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong> with forty iron bolts; - Four chained <strong>Iron-Jaw Bear Traps</strong> made of thirty jin of good spring-steel each; - And inside Ma Liu's oiled leather waist-pouch, <strong>eleven taels of broken Prefectural Silver</strong>, three jars of <strong>Tier-Two Sinew-Warming Ointment</strong>, and a brass tally-token from a black-market merchant in Black-Rock Town!</p>
