While Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang helped Captain Cang Hai load fresh water and spirit-stone ballast into the hold, Lu Chen leaped lightly onto the outer scaffolding along the ship's waterline, holding a pot of molten Fourth-Order Kraken-Bone & Star-Silver Ink in his left hand and his Fourth-Order Star-Silk Inscription Brush in his right.

On Modern Earth, when restoring corroded ancient bronze naval rams excavated from salt-marshes, Lu Chen had studied how sacrificial anodes and micro-grooved laminar channels deflected both electrochemical corrosion and hydrodynamic drag.

Now, combining that principle with the 《Rank 4 Compound Array Compendium》 from his Sixth Technique Bubble, Lu Chen didn't just paint a defensive rune over the iron plates.

Entering Prism Stillness, he walked around the entire sixty-foot hull of the Iron-Scale Osprey in a single, unbroken two-hour circuit, his brush moving like a silver-violet lightning bolt as he engraved 3,600 interlocking Rank 4 Runes along the keel, prow, and gunwales:

《Rank 4 Compound Naval Array: Brine-Cleaving Dragon Scale & Acoustic Counter-Shock Hull》!

HUMMMMM—!

Even before leaving the pier, the three-fold magnetic brine-drag around the hull dropped to zero, lifting the ship six inches higher in the dense salt water!

"He... he engraved a complete Rank 4 Compound Naval Array across a sixty-foot iron ship in two hours without a single draft sketch?!" "With this hull, the Iron-Scale Osprey can outrun a Fourth-Order sea-serpent!"

"Cast off!"

As the noon neap tide turned, the Iron-Scale Osprey sliced out of White-Salt Port like a silver-purple sword fish, throwing up twin wings of white salt-spray as it sped westward across the lead-gray expanse of the Inland Abyssal Salt-Sea at forty knots!
