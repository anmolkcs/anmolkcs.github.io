<p>As Senior Clerk Master Sun lifted the heavy bronze seal off the parchment, sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> let out a trembling breath that he seemed to have held for three full years. Reaching into his leather apron with his single scarred hand, the old blacksmith laid the broken half of the iron mine-tally stamped with the character <strong>"Seven"</strong> onto the clerk's desk alongside five taels of silver for the administrative release fee.</p>
<p>"Master Sun," Old Tie Shan said, his voice shaking with hope, "please issue the <strong>Convict Release Warrant</strong> for my boy <strong>Tie Niu, #704 of Deep-Shaft Pit #7</strong>, so your bailiffs can bring him up to the canyon gate today!"</p>
<p>Senior Clerk Sun picked up the broken half-tally and checked the registry scroll on his desk—then his white eyebrows knitted together, and he let out a low sigh.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, ten feet away at the edge of the terrace, <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan's</strong> ugly scowl slowly twisted back into a cold, venomous sneer.</p>
<p>"The debt-bond is stamped clear in my treasury ledger, Old Tie," Senior Clerk Sun said slowly, tapping a second yellow notice pinned to the board beside his desk, "and I can give you this stamped <strong>Redemption Certificate</strong> right now. However... my courthouse clerks cannot go down into <strong>Deep-Shaft Pit #7</strong> to fetch your son out for you."</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan froze as if struck by lightning. "Why not, Master Sun?! The eight hundred jin of iron is sitting right there on your scales!"</p>
<p>"Because four days ago—on the twenty-ninth of the eleventh month—<strong>Pit Overseer Luo Shan</strong> of Deep-Shaft Pit #7 filed an emergency report with the Garrison Command," Senior Clerk Sun explained, lowering his voice slightly as he glanced toward Zhao Kuan. "Overseer Luo reported that Drift Four of Pit #7 struck a pocket of toxic <strong>Subterranean Sulfur-Gas</strong> and unstable roof-shale, placing the entire shaft under <strong>Level-Three Winter Quarantine</strong> until the twentieth of the twelfth month, when the lower drifts are scheduled to be blasted shut to stop flooding."</p>
<p>"Under the County Mine Statute," Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan interrupted loudly, folding his arms across his iron-mail chest with a cruel grin, "when a deep shaft is under sulfur quarantine, courthouse runners are exempt from entering the pit! And no quarantined debt-miner in Drift Four is allowed to step into the winch-cage to come up until his final <strong>Hundred-Cart Ore Tally</strong> is chipped out of the rock face and verified in person at the bottom of the shaft! If you want Tie Niu out before the twentieth, old man, you have to take that Redemption Certificate to the rim of <strong>Black-Slag Canyon</strong> yourself—and provide one registered <strong>Tier-Three Striker-Miner</strong> from your own party to go down the winch-cage into Pit #7, verify his cart-tally with Overseer Luo's pit-wardens, and carry him out!"</p>
<p>Uncle Shi's hand tightened furiously on his spear shaft. "That's a death-trap! Once a man goes three hundred fathoms down the winch-cage into Overseer Luo Shan's pit, Luo Shan controls the winch-chain from the top!"</p>
<p>"Then don't go down!" Zhao Kuan laughed coldly, turning on his heel to walk back into the Tally-House courtyard so he could immediately send a fast rider three miles east to warn <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> that the Ash-Brook blacksmith and a formidable young <strong>Tier Three</strong> spearman had just cleared the tax ledger. "Wait until the spring thaw in the third month—maybe we'll pump the water out and float his bones up for you!"</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan snatched the stamped <strong>Redemption Certificate</strong> off the clerk's desk with his single hand, his eyes burning like two coals in his weathered face.</p>
<p>"I'll go down the cage myself!" Old Tie Shan rasped. "Even with one arm, I can swing a pick in Drift Four—"</p>
<p>"Look at the clause on the certificate, Old Tie," Lu Chen said calmly, laying a steady hand on the old blacksmith's shoulder and pointing to the red-ink line at the bottom of the warrant. "It says the guarantor entering a quarantined shaft must test at <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew)</strong> or higher on the canyon rim weight-stone, while the primary household head remains at the surface tally-shack to hold the counterpart seal until both men ascend."</p>
<p>Lu Chen took the stamped Redemption Certificate and folded it beside the broken iron tally <strong>"Seven"</strong>.</p>
<p>"You hold the counterpart seal at the canyon rim so Luo Shan cannot legally cut the winch-chain while Senior Clerk Sun's audit clerk is watching," Lu Chen said quietly, looking east toward the smoking chasm of Black-Slag Canyon. "I'll go down into Pit #7 and bring Tie Niu up on my back."</p>
