Above the seven towering peaks of the Seven Star Valley Sect, the sky had been dyed a murky crimson by a ten-mile Blood-Eclipse War Cloud.

Five giant Fourth-Order Blood-Bone Warships carrying two thousand elite Houtian and Xiantian cultists surrounded the mountain range, while in midair above Dubhe Peak, six Revolving Core Elders of the Blood-Eclipse and Shadow-Moon Sects—led by the terrifying Blood-Eclipse Sect Master, Xue Cangqiong (Peak Revolving Core / Half-Step Life-and-Death Wheel, 2,600,000 jin of combat power!)—were bombarding the sect's mountain-protecting barrier!

Yet to Xue Cangqiong's shock and fury, the Rank 5 Seven-Star Heaven-Locking Grand Array that Lu Chen had reconstructed before his departure was ten times stronger than any Fourth-Grade sect formation!

Inside the array: - Elder Tie, Sect Master Tian Shu, and the three Revolving Core Grand Elders manned the five outer star-nodes; - And on the southern slope of Broken Spear Peak, Lu Chen's father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan (both at the Peak of the Early Xiantian Realm!) stood calmly at the core node of Warm-Spring Courtyard #1, channeling their pure Golden-Jade Xiantian Essence into the Subterranean Star-Vein Conduit to supply steady energy to the seventh array pillar while sheltering two hundred young outer disciples inside their courtyard!

"Hold fast, everyone!" Lu Shan's voice rang out steady and resonant across Broken Spear Peak as he stood beside Meng Wan. "My son Lu Chen personally carved every rune of this Seven-Star Grand Array—and he already sent word through our family jade token five minutes ago that he has crossed the desert and is less than a hundred miles away!"
