<p>Three miles east of the town walls, the frozen tundra ended abruptly at the lip of <strong>Black-Slag Canyon</strong>.</p>
<p>It looked as though a titan's axe had split the crust of the earth for six miles from north to south, opening a jagged chasm a thousand feet wide and <strong>three hundred fathoms (1,800 feet) deep</strong>. From the pitch-black bottom of the canyon rose thick, choking plumes of yellow-green <strong>Sulfur-Ash Smoke</strong> that stained the winter clouds the color of rotten bruises.</p>
<p>Clinging to the sheer basalt walls of the chasm were dozens of creaking timber staging-platforms connected by thick iron winch-chains, where wooden ore-cages were hauled up and down by teams of blindfolded <strong>Scale-Hoofed Crag-Mules</strong> walking in endless circles around giant capstan wheels on the rim.</p>
<p>At the northernmost bend of the chasm rim stood a fortified stone guardhouse marked with a rusted iron sign:</p>
<p><strong>[Black-Slag Canyon · Deep-Shaft Pit #7]</strong></p>
<p>As Lu Chen, Old Tie Shan, and Audit Clerk Fang approached the stone guardhouse, twelve burly <strong>Pit-Wardens</strong> wearing spiked iron shoulder-guards and carrying heavy iron-shod pick-handles stepped out to block the winch-platform.</p>
<p>And standing in the doorway of the guardhouse, chewing on a roasted bone-marrow skewer while a fast Crag-Mule lathered in sweat stood tied to the post behind him—clearly ridden out from town two hours ago by <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan's</strong> messenger—was the tyrant of Pit #7:</p>
<p><strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong>!</p>
<p>Luo Shan was a squat, bull-necked man in his forties with a livid purple saber-scar carving from his left temple down across his nose to his chin, pulling his left eyelid permanently downward so the red meat showed. Unlike Tier-Three warriors whose strength lay in their taut tendons, Luo Shan moved with a terrifying, metallic heaviness: every time his iron-shod boots stepped on the basalt flagstones of the winch-platform, the stone rang with a dull <em>clink</em>, and the knuckles of his bare hands gleamed with a dark, grayish-iron luster beneath the skin—the unmistakable hallmark of a warrior who had calcined all 206 bones of his skeleton to the very pinnacle of <strong>Peak Tier Four of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Slag-Bone Perfection (7,800 jin baseline force)</strong>!</p>
<p>Beside his boot leaned his signature weapon: a five-foot, <strong>Two-Hundred-and-Twenty-Jin Eight-Flanged Iron Bone-Crushing Mace</strong>!</p>
<p>"Overseer Luo," Audit Clerk Fang said stiffly, holding up the red-stamped <strong>County Treasury Redemption Certificate</strong>. "The eight-hundred-jin Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron bond for <strong>Convict #704, Tie Niu</strong>, has been paid in full at the Public Scales. Under the Quarantine Statute, his household head <strong>Tie Shan</strong> will remain here at the rim witness-shack with me, while their registered <strong>Tier-Three Guarantor, Lu Chen</strong>, descends the winch-cage to Drift Four to verify the final hundred-cart ore tally and bring Convict #704 up before the twentieth of the twelfth month!"</p>
<p>Scarface Luo Shan tossed his chewed bone skewer over the cliff into the three-hundred-fathom abyss and looked Lu Chen up and down with his hideous, half-pulled-down left eye.</p>
<p>Zhao Kuan's messenger had already told Luo Shan everything: this twenty-year-old youth had just reached <strong>Tier Three (Bowstring-Sinew Drawing)</strong> and split the two-hundred-pound test anvil in town.</p>
<p>To a Tier-Two or Tier-Three bandit like Han Wu, a Tier-Three spearman was deadly.</p>
<p>To <strong>Scarface Luo Shan</strong>—who stood at <strong>Peak Tier Four (Slag-Bone Perfection)</strong> with nearly <strong>eight thousand jin</strong> of bone-crushing power and controlled every warden and rock-pillar three hundred fathoms underground—a Tier-Three youth walking voluntarily down into his quarantined pit was a lamb walking into an iron smelter!</p>
<p>"Paid in full, is it?" Luo Shan let out a low, grating laugh, stepping aside to wave toward the four-foot-square iron-barred winch-cage hanging over the smoking abyss. "The law is the law, Clerk Fang! Let the boy step onto the rim weight-stone to prove he's Tier Three, leave any crossbows at the top, and take the cage down to Drift Four! Today is the third of the twelfth month—he's got seventeen days down in the dark to help #704 chip out his last hundred carts of <strong>Deep-Vein Slag-Ore</strong> before we blast the shaft shut on the twentieth! Of course... Drift Four is three hundred fathoms down under fifteenfold gravity, and the roof-shale has been mighty loose lately. If an accident happens in the dark, the County Treasury doesn't refund the iron!"</p>
<p>At the edge of the platform, Old Tie Shan's single hand gripped Lu Chen's sleeve tightly, his voice dropping to an urgent whisper: "Lu Chen—Luo Shan is at <strong>Peak Tier Four Slag-Bone</strong>! His bones are harder than pig-iron! Down in the pit, don't clash head-on with his two-hundred-jin mace until—!"</p>
<p>"Rest easy at the witness-shack with Clerk Fang, Old Tie," Lu Chen said quietly, pressing his left hand gently over the old blacksmith's scarred fingers as he took the broken half of iron tally <strong>"Seven"</strong>. "Keep a pot of hot water boiling on the stove up here. In less than fifteen days, when that winch-bell rings three times, your son and I are coming up together."</p>
<p>Stepping onto the iron winch-cage with his eighty-jin spear and his canvas pack of broken steel and <strong>Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts</strong>, Lu Chen looked straight into Scarface Luo Shan's scarred eye as the heavy iron grate slammed shut and the capstan chain began to drop him into the roaring darkness below.</p>
