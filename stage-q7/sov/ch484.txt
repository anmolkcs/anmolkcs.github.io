<p>Less than half an hour after Lu Chen stepped out of the boiling pool at <strong>Early Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining)</strong> and slid the false wall of waste-shale shut across the cavern entrance, the harsh clanging of an iron hand-gong echoed down the half-mile length of Drift Four!</p>
<p><em>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</em></p>
<p>"Overseer Luo is coming down the tunnel for the sixth-night crystal inspection! All miners on your knees by the left wall—faces down in the brine!"</p>
<p>The six <strong>Tier-Two Pit-Wardens</strong>, led by toad-faced <strong>Warden He Da</strong> and wrist-sprained <strong>Warden Ma Gui</strong>, marched down the dripping rock corridor swinging sulfur-lanterns and iron-spiked clubs.</p>
<p>Behind them strode <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong>, his <strong>220-Jin Eight-Flanged Iron Mace</strong> resting effortlessly over his shoulder, accompanied by two trusted <strong>Tier-Three</strong> personal guards carrying a locked iron strongbox.</p>
<p>At the dead-end cul-de-sac of Drift Four, all forty skeletal miners—along with <strong>Tie Niu</strong> and <strong>Lu Chen</strong>—knelt on one knee along the rock wall with their heads bowed and their shoulders smeared with grey mud and coal-soot.</p>
<p>Lu Chen had deliberately slouched his shoulders by two inches, relaxed his thirty-six bowstring sinews into a slack resting state, and draped a wet, sulfur-stained rag over his knees so that even a master standing three paces away would see only an exhausted <strong>Tier-Three</strong> youth suffering from six days of fifteenfold-gravity fatigue and sulfur-fume sickness.</p>
<p>Scarface Luo Shan halted at the end of the drift, his pulled-down left eye sweeping greedily past the kneeling men toward the leather bucket sitting beside <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong>.</p>
<p>Inside the bucket lay fourteen medium-grade <strong>Natural Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> that the miners had chipped from the outer, cooler rim of the pool—while the thirty purest, fist-sized <strong>Heart-of-Marrow Crystals</strong> from the boiling center remained safely hidden under the pool floor inside the cavern!</p>
<p>"Fourteen crystals?" Luo Shan frowned, picking up two of the cold, hexagonal crystals with his bare, grayish-iron <strong>Peak Tier Four</strong> fingers and tossing them into his strongbox. "Six days ago you pulled out eighteen! Why is the yield dropping, old mole?!"</p>
<p>"Mercy, Lord Overseer!" Old Mole Qian coughed violently in the mud. "The outer shelf of the brine pool is nearly stripped bare, and the heat in the center of the cavern has risen so high this week that when #712 tried to wade in past his knees yesterday, the skin boiled right off his shins! Even this new Tier-Three guarantor boy you sent down has been puking yellow sulfur-bile for two days from the fifteenfold gravity!"</p>
<p>Scarface Luo Shan turned his scarred, sneering face toward Lu Chen, who knelt quietly beside Tie Niu with his face pale (rubbed with dry white limestone dust) and his breathing deliberately shallow.</p>
<p>Stepping over to Lu Chen, Luo Shan didn't even speak—he suddenly rested the flat iron tip of his <strong>220-Jin Eight-Flanged Mace</strong> directly onto Lu Chen's right shoulder and leaned down on the handle with <strong>two thousand jin</strong> of casual downward pressure to test whether the young guarantor still had any fight left in him!</p>
<p>Under that sudden two-thousand-jin press on top of the fifteenfold gravity, Lu Chen did not let his newly calcined <strong>Early Tier Four</strong> leg bones lock and give away his breakthrough.</p>
<p>Instead, with the microscopic precision of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen allowed his knees to sag two inches deeper into the mud with a muffled grunt, letting the stone floor take the weight while his hand trembled convincingly on the shaft of his eighty-jin spear!</p>
<p>"Hahaha! Splitting a dead cast-iron block up in the town square is easy, isn't it, kid?" Scarface Luo Shan laughed mockingly, lifting his heavy mace off Lu Chen's shoulder and spitting into the brine. "Three hundred fathoms underground under fifteenfold Earth-Marrow gravity, even a Tier-Three bull turns into a soft-legged sheep in a week! Keep digging, you dogs—you've got eleven days left until the eighteenth! Chip out at least thirty more marrow-crystals from the center of that pool, and maybe I'll let you ride the first cage up!"</p>
<p>As Luo Shan and his wardens marched back up the drift laughing over their strongbox of crystals, Warden Ma Gui lingered for a second to glare spitefully at Lu Chen—never suspecting that the "exhausted" youth kneeling in the mud could have shattered his skull before he took a second step.</p>
<p>Once the iron gate at the mouth of Drift Four clanged shut half a mile away, Lu Chen stood up effortlessly from the mud, brushed the limestone dust from his cheek, and looked toward the hidden cavern.</p>
<p>"Eleven days left," Lu Chen said quietly to Tie Niu and Old Mole Qian. "Tonight, we light the magma-vent forge."</p>
