At noon, during his half-hour midday break from the Crimson Scale Pavilion, Lu Chen pulled a wide bamboo hat low over his brow, wrapped the inscribed iron sword in coarse gray cloth, and walked into the Sky Treasure Pavilion on Central Avenue.

The Sky Treasure Pavilion was the largest trade chamber in Cloudstride City, backed by a powerful merchant consortium from the royal capital. Its floors were paved with polished bluestone, and even the guards standing beside the gilded pillars were martial artists at the Third Stage of Body Transformation.

Seeing a youth in a plain, patched hemp tunic carrying a cloth-wrapped bundle walk in, a young shop clerk near the door frowned slightly, about to wave him toward the pawnshop down the street, when an elderly man in brocade robes sitting behind the appraisal counter looked up from a magnifying loupe.

“Wait,” the old man said, his nostrils flaring slightly as he caught the faint, crisp scent of Silver-Leaf Amber and pure fire-elemental resonance lingering around the cloth bundle. “Young friend, are you here to have an item appraised?”

This old man was Appraiser Qin, the chief weapon and talisman appraiser of the Sky Treasure Pavilion’s Cloudstride branch.

“Yes, Senior,” Lu Chen said evenly, stepping up to the sandalwood counter and untying the gray cloth.

When the sword inside was revealed, the young clerk behind the counter nearly laughed out loud.

It was a standard-issue Grade 1 Human-Step Refined Iron Sword—the lowest grade of martial weapon, forged twenty years ago with mediocre iron. There were even two tiny rust pits near the crossguard; in any blacksmith shop, it wouldn’t fetch more than eight taels of silver.

Appraiser Qin, however, did not look at the rust pits.

His eyes locked onto the thumb-sized, dark-gold crimson rune etched half an inch above the crossguard—a rune so delicate and seamlessly fused into the grain of the steel that it looked as if it had grown out of the metal itself!

“A… a Rank 1 Weapon Inscription?!” Appraiser Qin stood up so abruptly that his wooden stool scraped loudly across the bluestone floor.

He pulled on a pair of white silk gloves, lifted the old iron sword with both hands as if holding priceless porcelain, and channeled a wisp of his Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) blood-qi into the hilt.

Weng—!

The twenty-year-old iron sword let out a clear, resonant hum like a waking hawk. The thumb-sized crimson lotus-blade mark above the crossguard ignited, wrapping the entire three-foot blade in a condensed, two-inch layer of blazing scarlet light!

Appraiser Qin turned and swung the sword diagonally against a test block of Black-Sand Ironwood standing beside the counter.

SHHHK!

Without meeting the slightest resistance, the old iron sword sliced cleanly through the six-inch-thick ironwood block—a block that normally required a Grade 2 Human-Step Weapon worth over two hundred taels of silver to cleave! Even more astonishingly, the cut surfaces of the ironwood were charred black and smoking, yet the old iron blade itself hadn’t suffered a single rolled edge.

“Thirty-five percent power amplification… and zero energy leakage along the rune loops!” Appraiser Qin’s hands trembled slightly as he inspected the cooling rune under his crystal loupe. “In all of Cloudstride City, there is only one registered Rank 1 Inscription Apprentice—Master Zheng at the City Lord’s Mansion—and even his Flame-Edge symbols barely reach twenty-five percent amplification while leaving scorch marks on the steel! Young friend… may I ask which Inscription Master crafted this symbol?”

“A traveling senior who passed through the southern quarter left it with me to settle a debt, Appraiser Qin,” Lu Chen replied calmly, neither arrogant nor servile. Revealing that a fifteen-year-old youth without a clan backing could draw 35%-amplification runes would only invite greed and trouble before he had the strength to protect himself. “He instructed me to ask how much the Sky Treasure Pavilion is willing to pay for this sword—and for standalone Flame-Edge Inscription Talismans that can be applied directly to any Grade 1 or Grade 2 weapon.”

Appraiser Qin’s breath hitched when Lu Chen slid the yellow talisman paper bearing the completed Flame-Edge Inscription across the counter.

Inscribing directly onto a weapon required the weapon to be brought to the master; an Inscription Talisman sealed on carrier paper, however, could be bought by any wealthy martial artist and fused onto their own prized blade at any time!

“A standard Grade 1 iron sword is worth ten taels of silver, but with a 35% Flame-Edge Inscription, its combat power equals a peak Grade 2 Human-Step weapon,” Appraiser Qin said immediately, suppressing the excitement in his voice so as not to offend the mysterious “traveling master” behind this youth. “For the sword and this standalone Inscription Talisman together, our Sky Treasure Pavilion offers three hundred taels of silver—or, if the master prefers cultivation currency, three Low-Grade True Essence Stones plus fifty taels of silver!”

In the Azure Sky Kingdom, True Essence Stones were natural spirit crystals mined from heaven-and-earth energy veins. Although officially valued at one hundred taels of silver each, in practice no martial artist would ever trade a single True Essence Stone for silver coins—because silver could only buy mundane herbs, whereas True Essence Stones were the sole currency accepted for high-grade cultivation manuals, spirit pills, and martial house arrays!

“I’ll take two Low-Grade True Essence Stones, and for the remaining one hundred and fifty taels of value, I want one stalk of Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng, two jars of Ironwood Marrow Paste, and five sets of blank Rank 1 Spirit-Ink Materials,” Lu Chen said without hesitation.

“Deal!” Appraiser Qin agreed on the spot, personally fetching a carved wooden box containing two pigeon-egg-sized, milky-white stones pulsing with pure origin energy, alongside the sealed medicinal boxes and five sets of top-grade cinnabar and spirit paper. He also handed Lu Chen a bronze guest token. “Whenever that senior wishes to sell more Inscription Talismans, bring this bronze token to me directly—our Pavilion will purchase every single one at top market price, no questions asked!”

Walking out of the Sky Treasure Pavilion fifteen minutes later with the wooden box tucked securely inside his tunic, Lu Chen looked up at the bright midday sun over Cloudstride City.

Two taels of scrap silver last night had turned into two True Essence Stones, one Hundred-Year Blood Ginseng, two jars of Ironwood Marrow Paste, and enough materials to craft twenty more Rank 1 Inscriptions!

Now, it was time to turn that wealth into raw, crushing martial strength.
