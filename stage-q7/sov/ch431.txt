<p>Half a mile above Bog-Viper Creek, tucked beneath a beetling brow of black basalt where the sulfur-mist thinned against cold rock, the hunting party found the dry overhang Lu Chen had spotted on the way down.</p>
<p>Working quickly and silently without lighting a smoke-producing fire, Lu Chen and Big-Tall Zhao rolled three five-hundred-pound basalt boulders across the mouth of the overhang, leaving only a two-foot crawl-hole that they masked with cut bundles of thorny black-briar vines. Inside the shelter, Old Ma lay wrapped in two spare sheepskins with his bandaged calf elevated on a pack-frame, while Cross-Eyed Liu sat guard behind the crawl-hole with a cocked horn-bow and a short axe.</p>
<p>"Keep this dead Grey-Bog Viper carcass in the pack, Liu," Lu Chen instructed quietly through the crawl-hole. "Its meat isn't poisoned—only the head-sacs—and a Peak Tier One viper's flesh will boil down into five bowls of blood-warming soup when we get back to the village. Don't poke your head out unless you hear Uncle Shi's three-note owl whistle."</p>
<p>Leaving Old Ma and Cross-Eyed Liu secure in the rock shelter, the remaining four—<strong>Lu Chen</strong>, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, and fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong>—climbed onto the narrow basalt goat-ledge running fifty feet above the eastern ravine.</p>
<p>From the elevated rock shelf, they did not have to wade through the snake-infested mud or leave boot-prints on the ravine floor. Moving like silent grey shadows through the swirling sulfur-mist, Lu Chen took the point position, his eighty-jin solid iron boar-spear balanced horizontally in his right hand.</p>
<p>For two hours, they paralleled the drag-marks in the ravine below.</p>
<p>Along the way, Lu Chen pointed out details in the mist that made even fifty-year-old Uncle Shi narrow his eyes in quiet respect: a smudge of dried pine-tar on a boulder where one of the four poachers had rested his shoulder-pole; a drop of bright red human blood mixed with yellowish pus where the poacher who had lost three fingers had re-wrapped his hand; and the fact that the fourth set of boot-prints walked with a heavy, pigeon-toed stride that sank half an inch deeper into the clay than the other three.</p>
<p>"Three Tier-One cutthroats, and one team leader at <strong>Early Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong>," Uncle Shi whispered, crouching behind a frost-coated boulder as the ravine ahead widened into a steaming, bowl-shaped depression known as the <strong>Boiling-Mud Wallow</strong>. "Look down there through the gap in the mist, Lu Chen. You called it right to the bone."</p>
<p>Fifty feet below them at the center of the ravine bowl bubbled a thirty-yard pool of warm, mineral-rich grey mud fed by a subterranean volcanic spring.</p>
<p>Twenty yards downwind of the mud pool, hidden inside a camouflaged blind of cut pine branches, crouched four gaunt, scar-faced men wearing mismatched pieces of rusted iron lamellar armor over greasy wolf-skin vests—members of <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> infamous <strong>Broken-Tooth Poachers</strong>.</p>
<p>Out in the open mud flat in front of their blind, the poachers had staked the bleeding, six-hundred-pound carcass of the female <strong>Iron-Bristle Boar</strong> they had killed at the creek—slitting its belly open so the hot scent of blood and entrails drifted straight up the northern gorge toward a dark cave of piled granite boulders!</p>
<p>And around the staked carcass, submerged three inches deep beneath the warm grey mud, the poachers had planted four heavy, spring-loaded <strong>Iron-Jaw Bear Traps</strong> chained to buried pine logs, while all four men held crank-spanned <strong>Triple-Prod Poison Crossbows</strong> aimed at the mouth of the granite cave!</p>
<p>"They're baiting the <strong>Scarred King-Boar</strong> out of its winter den!" Big-Tall Zhao breathed, his knuckles whitening on his spear shaft. "Uncle Shi—that cave at the north end of the wallow belongs to the old male boar that broke three of our iron snares last autumn! That beast has survived three winters of Black-Frost; its hide and muscle are at the very peak of <strong>Tier Two</strong>, almost touching <strong>Tier Three</strong>! If Han Wu's poachers take both the sow and the King-Boar, there won't be a single large beast left in the eastern basin for Ash-Brook to hunt!"</p>
