Dead silence fell over the Killing Array Square as two hundred pairs of eyes stared up at Rank #118: Lu Chen — 540 Killing Points.

Even the second-year inner disciples watching from the stone steps sucked in cold breaths. Reaching Rank #118 on one’s very first challenge meant Lu Chen had already outperformed thirty percent of the veteran second-year disciples who were at the Early Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons)—and according to Martial House rules, anyone breaking into the Top 120 on their first attempt received an automatic bonus of fifteen Low-Grade True Essence Stones!

Thirty paces away, Lin Xuan’s face turned an ugly, livid purple. Twice in six days—first at the Entrance Trial and now on the Killing Array Ranking Stele—this boy from Cloudstride City had stepped directly over his head!

Watching Lu Chen collect his fifteen True Essence Stones from the array deacon and walk calmly toward Pine-Wind Cliff, Lin Xuan turned to a burly, scar-faced eighteen-year-old youth in an inner-disciple vest standing behind him.

That youth was Zhu Hong—a distant cousin of the Lin Clan, ranked #86 on the Killing Array Stele, who had reached the Peak of the Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) with 5,200 jin of strength!

“Cousin Zhu Hong,” Lin Xuan said in a low, icy voice. “Didn’t you say your younger brother wanted a quiet private courtyard on Pine-Wind Cliff? According to Martial House rules, any inner disciple ranked in the Top 100 can issue a Courtyard Challenge to a freshman after their seven-day protection period ends tomorrow. Break his spear-arm on the challenge stage in ten days, and my father will pay for the Blood-Tempering Marrow Pill you need for the Fifth Stage.”

Zhu Hong licked his dry lips, a cruel grin stretching across his scarred face. “A Middle Third Stage kid who got lucky against array phantoms? Consider his courtyard—and his right arm—mine.”

Unaware of—and indifferent to—Lin Xuan’s scheme, Lu Chen didn’t return immediately to his courtyard. Instead, with thirty-two Low-Grade True Essence Stones now weighing down his pouch, he walked to the Imperial Beast-Material Exchange at the foot of Seven Star Mountain.

Because today was the monthly delivery day from the northern hunting garrisons, the Exchange’s auction counter was offering a freshly sealed jade vial that made Lu Chen’s heart leap the moment he sensed it:

One vial of Core Heart-Blood from a Peak Second-Order Spirit Beast: the Crimson-Maned Flame Lion—equivalent in life force to a human warrior at the Peak of the Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation)!

Because Peak Second-Order Flame Lion heart-blood was too violent for ordinary mortal alchemists to refine directly without a Rank 2 cauldron, the vial was priced at twenty-five Low-Grade True Essence Stones.

Lu Chen bought it on the spot, spending his remaining seven True Essence Stones on five sets of Rank 1 Peak Pill-Inscription Materials (Moonlight Dew, Spirit-Jade Powder, and Golden Cicada Silk Ink).

Returning to Courtyard #72 at dusk and activating the courtyard’s privacy array, Lu Chen uncorked the jade vial of Peak Second-Order Flame Lion Heart-Blood.

ROAR—!

Even though it was only three tablespoons of thick, golden-crimson liquid, a phantom roar of a blazing lion seemed to echo across the meditation room, raising the room’s temperature by ten degrees!

Without hesitation, Lu Chen pressed his palm over the mouth of the vial and channeled his spiritual sense into the Chrono-Soul Prism.

Thrum—!

Like a whale swallowing a river, the nine-faceted gray Prism inside his chest pulled the golden-crimson essence out of the Flame Lion’s heart-blood, leaving behind a bowl of purified, non-lethal Rank 2 Blood-Tempering Essence for Lu Chen’s future physical training, while the core life-imprint surged straight into the Prism’s second facet!

Crack—Shhhk!

Inside the boundless gray mist of the Prism, the frost sealing the second outer soul fragment—a glowing emerald-gold light sphere twice as large as the first crimson bubble—shattered completely!

Bracing his mind in the deepest state of Prism Stillness and biting down on a folded leather strap, Lu Chen reached out and drew the emerald-gold soul fragment into his spiritual sea!

“Hmph—!”

Blood trickled from the corners of Lu Chen’s closed eyes as a torrent of ancient memories—far denser and more specialized than the first—flooded his consciousness over two agonizing hours.

When the storm finally settled at midnight and Lu Chen opened his eyes in the dark room, his gaze was blindingly bright.

Just like the first bubble, this Second Technique Bubble had been stripped 100% clean of any personal identity, name, or world history by the Chrono-Soul Prism, containing only pure, crystalline technical diagrams and cultivation methods:

  1. Sixty-Four Complete Pill-Inscription & Medicinal Purification Techniques—including the Nine-Fold Medicinal Lock Rune, capable of doubling or tripling the efficacy of mortal body-tempering pills and purging 99% of pill toxins!
  2. The complete Second Volume of the 《Chaotic Star Body Art》, detailing how to forge Dragon-Sinew Tendons (Fourth Stage) and Golden-Jade Marrow (Fifth Stage)!
  3. And a pure defensive/stunning soul-control technique called the 《Instant Soul Ripple》—which allowed an Inscription Master to condense their soul force into an invisible harmonic wave during close combat, momentarily stunning an opponent’s focus for a tenth of a second without permanently mutilating their soul!

Better still, earlier that afternoon before visiting the Beast-Material Exchange, Lu Chen had entrusted a sealed sandalwood box containing five Low-Grade True Essence Stones, two Rune-Inscribed Origin-Gathering Pills, and a long, affectionate letter to Grand Appraiser Mu’s armed Sky Treasure Pavilion caravan heading back to Cloudstride City—ensuring his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan had everything they needed to advance toward the Third Stage (Viscera Training)!

Looking at the porcelain bottle of three ordinary Origin-Gathering Pills and the bowl of purified Peak Second-Order Flame Lion Blood on his table, Lu Chen picked up his inscription brush.

With Pill Inscription unlocked, the gap in cultivation resources between him and the richest aristocratic clans of the Azure Sky Kingdom was about to be obliterated completely.
