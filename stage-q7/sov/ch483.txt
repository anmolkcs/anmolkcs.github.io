<p>Chemical soaking in boiling sulfur-iron brine alone could only soften the porous mortal bone and feed it raw iron-marrow; to force the new iron crystals to lock into an unbreakable lattice without leaving the bone spongy, the skeleton had to be <strong>physically hammered under high gravity</strong> while hot!</p>
<p>Standing waist-deep on the sunken basalt step of the boiling pool, his shin-bones and thigh-bones glowing with dull red heat beneath his bronze skin, Lu Chen looked at <strong>Tie Niu</strong>.</p>
<p>"Brother Tie Niu," Lu Chen's voice rasped through clenched teeth, each word accompanied by a puff of metallic white steam. "Use the sixty-pound mine-sledge. Strike along the front ridge of my shin-bones, the outer shaft of my thigh-bones, and the crests of my hips—thirty-six blows per leg, following the chalk dots I marked on my skin!"</p>
<p>Tie Niu's hands trembled slightly on the handle of the sixty-pound iron sledge. "Brother Lu... under eighteenfold gravity, a swing of this sixty-pound sledge carries over <strong>fifteen hundred jin</strong> of blunt iron impact! Hitting a man's bare shin-bone with a sledgehammer—"</p>
<p>"Pretend my leg is a thirty-pound billet of <strong>Black-Marrow Core Ore</strong> on your father's anvil in Ash-Brook," Lu Chen said calmly, locking his thirty-six bowstring sinews and compacted thigh muscles around his femur and tibia to form a living shock-cushion that transmitted pure percussive vibration straight to the bone periosteum without splitting the flesh. "If you swing soft, the slag stays inside the bone, and neither of us walks out of this pit to see your father again. Strike!"</p>
<p>Tie Niu's bloodshot eyes hardened. He was a blacksmith's son—born to the sound of the anvil.</p>
<p>Spitting into his scarred palms, Tie Niu planted his splinted left leg and his strong right leg on the basalt rim, raised the sixty-pound iron sledgehammer, and brought it swinging horizontally against the front ridge of Lu Chen's submerged left shin-bone!</p>
<p><em>THOOOOOOM—CLANK!</em></p>
<p>The sound that came from Lu Chen's shin under the boiling brine was not the dull, wet thud of flesh—nor was it the brittle snap of breaking bone.</p>
<p>It was the heavy, muffled <em>CLANK</em> of a striker's sledge hitting a red-hot bar of wrought iron!</p>
<p>A violent shockwave raced from the center of Lu Chen's left tibia down to his ankle-mortise and up to his knee-cap! Inside the softened cortical wall of his shin-bone, thousands of microscopic pockets of brittle white mortal ash-calcium collapsed and squeezed out into the bloodstream as grey waste, while dense, heavy molecules of <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow &amp; Red-Lead</strong> from the boiling brine were driven straight into the compressed bone grain!</p>
<p>Lu Chen's whole body shuddered; a thin trickle of grey, ash-tainted blood seeped from the corner of his mouth; yet his feet did not shift a fraction of an inch on the pool floor!</p>
<p>"Good strike!" Lu Chen breathed out a jet of scalding steam. "Ten percent harder on the second blow—keep the rhythm!"</p>
<p><em>THOOOOOOM—CLANK!</em> <em>THOOOOOOM—CLANK!</em> <em>THOOOOOOM—CLANK!</em></p>
<p>Night after night—from the third of the twelfth month to the sixth—for four hours every midnight inside the hidden geode cavern, the muffled <em>CLANK—CLANK—CLANK</em> of the sixty-pound iron sledge echoed beneath the roar of the sulfur vents as Lu Chen cycled through the first eight of Shi Potian's <strong>Twenty-Four Bone-Hammering Postures</strong>!</p>
<p>Whenever Tie Niu's arms grew exhausted after two hundred blows, Lu Chen stepped out of the boiling pool onto the eighteenfold-gravity basalt floor, picked up his eighty-jin spear and two hundred-pound boulders of raw Earth-Marrow Ore, and performed three hundred <strong>High-Gravity Stamping Squats</strong>—driving his heels into the solid rock until the basalt cracked beneath his feet!</p>
<p>On the fourth night—just before the midnight bell of the sixth of the twelfth month—as Tie Niu delivered the three-hundred-and-sixtieth sledge blow across Lu Chen's right femur and hip-bone:</p>
<p><em>DONG————————!</em></p>
<p>A clear, metallic ring—like a cold chisel striking a block of quenched black steel—reverberated simultaneously from all <strong>sixty-two bones of Lu Chen's lower body</strong> (feet, ankles, tibias, fibulas, patellas, femurs, and pelvis)!</p>
<p>Under his bronze skin, the bones of his legs and hips had completely shed their porous white mortal state, condensing into seamless, heavy casings of <strong>Black Slag-Iron Bone</strong>!</p>
<p><strong>Tier Four of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Early Slag-Bone Calcining (5,400 jin baseline force)!</strong></p>
<p>When Lu Chen stepped out of the boiling brine pool onto the basalt floor, the eighteenfold gravity of the deep pit no longer made his shin-bones creak; instead, his iron-boned legs stood beneath him like two immovable pillars of cast iron, and a single stomp of his bare heel cracked a two-inch fissure across the black granite floor!</p>
