Outside Clear-Brook Manor, ten minutes of frantic bombardment by Deacon Hei, Deacon Yin, and ten Houtian bladesmen had only dimmed the golden-silver Rank 3 Guardian Array by thirty percent.

Inside the courtyard, Meng Wan held her husband’s hand warmly, looking up at the golden rune-dome with quiet pride: “Our Chen’er spent five days without sleep carving those thirty-six jade plates before he left—even twelve Houtian masters can’t step half an inch into our yard!”

“Focus on the southeast corner—the barrier is thinning there! One more combined strike!” Deacon Hei and Deacon Yin leaped thirty feet into the air together, condensing 160,000 jin of combined Half-Step Xiantian dark-moon claw qi to smash the array dome—

When suddenly, a thunderous roc-cry shook the entire Azure Sky Royal Capital from three miles up in the night clouds!

SCREEEEECH—KABOOOOOM!

Before Deacon Hei or Deacon Yin could even raise their heads, the black storm clouds above Clear-Brook Manor were split in half by a descending, hundred-foot pillar of purple-gold starlight and primordial thunder!

“《Primordial Star-Forge Spear — First Form: Crushing the Nine Heavens》!”

ZAP—BOOM-BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!

Descending from two thousand feet with a 3,600-jin divine spear and 110,000 jin of Concept-infused spiral force, the terrifying gravitational field crushed the air across the entire street before the spearhead even arrived!

“Half-Step Xiantian combat power?! Run—!”

Deacon Yin’s scream was cut short in his throat.

The nine-foot Star-Shattering Heavy Spear plunged straight through Deacon Yin’s crossed Grade 3 iron claws, shattering both claws and blasting Deacon Yin deep into the bluestone street outside the manor gate in a twenty-foot crater!
