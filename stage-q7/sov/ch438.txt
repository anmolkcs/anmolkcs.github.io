<p>All through that night in the basalt cave, while the other four hunters slept in shifts beside the charcoal embers, Lu Chen sat cross-legged near the boulder barricade, rubbing <strong>Ma Liu's Tier-Two Sinew-Warming Ointment</strong> into his thighs, calves, and lower back while performing silent isometric muscle contractions.</p>
<p>By dawn on the third day of the eleventh month, the microscopic muscle tears in his upper body had completely healed and compacted into dense, iron-hard ridges, and the compaction process had spread halfway down his torso and abdominal wall—pushing his baseline strength under the tenfold gravity to <strong>one thousand six hundred and fifty jin</strong>!</p>
<p>Moreover, Old Ma's calf—nourished by the slice of King-Boar heart and Granny Liu's ash-root poultice—had lost all its purple swelling, allowing the veteran hunter to hobble with a wooden staff if supported by Cross-Eyed Liu.</p>
<p>"We fetch the remaining twelve hundred jin of cached meat from the iron-pine tree by the wallow," Uncle Shi decided at first light as they strapped on their pack-frames, "build two pole-litters, and haul everything straight back across the Dead-Pine Boundary before noon. Once we're out of the inner basin, the whole village can come up Frost-Tooth Gully to carry the meat home."</p>
<p>Yet the Inner Black-Briar Woods never surrendered two thousand jin of meat without demanding a blood-price.</p>
<p>When the six men returned to the iron-pine tree beside the Boiling-Mud Wallow and lowered the hide-wrapped quarters onto two stout ash-wood pole-litters, a low, eerie chorus of rasping howls rose simultaneously from three sides of the sulfur-choked ravine:</p>
<p><em>AWOOOOOOOO—KRRK!</em></p>
<p>The howls didn't sound clean like mountain wolves; they ended in a wet, phlegmy rattle, as if the beasts' throats were lined with gravel and blood.</p>
<p>Uncle Shi's face drained of color. "The <strong>Gray-Scab Ridge Wolves</strong>! The sulfur wind shifted before dawn and carried the scent of the wallow up to their den in <strong>Rotting-Bone Gully</strong>! Move—head for the narrow <strong>Split-Stone Defile</strong> half a mile back! If they catch us in the open briar flats with twelve hundred jin of meat on the litters, we're dead men!"</p>
<p>Shouldering the heavy pole-litters—Big-Tall Zhao and Uncle Shi carrying the heavier front litter, Cross-Eyed Liu and Shi Tou carrying the second alongside the limping Old Ma, and Lu Chen guarding the rear with his eighty-jin boar-spear and Ma Liu's sixty-jin saber strapped across his back—the six men broke into a grueling double-time trot up the rocky ravine trail!</p>
<p>Behind them in the thick yellow sulfur-mist, the padded drumming of paws grew closer with terrifying speed.</p>
<p>Within ten minutes, just as the narrow, six-foot-wide granite cleft of the <strong>Split-Stone Defile</strong> loomed ahead through the fog, nine gaunt, monstrous shapes burst out of the black-briar thickets twenty paces behind the rear litter!</p>
<p>They were <strong>Gray-Scab Ridge Wolves</strong>—each the size of a frontier draft-calf, their mangy fur patchy with crusts of gray mineral scab-armor formed by wallowing in toxic ash-springs. Eight of the wolves were mature <strong>Peak Tier One</strong> predators with jaws capable of shearing through iron-pine saplings; and running at the center of the pack was a massive, silver-backed <strong>Alpha Wolf</strong> with a blind white left eye and dense, waterless shoulder muscles at <strong>Early Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong>!</p>
<p>"Keep moving up the defile with Old Ma and the meat!" Lu Chen's voice rang out without a tremor as he turned at the narrow four-foot rock throat of the Split-Stone Defile and planted the butt of his eighty-jin Cold-Slag Spear into the frozen gravel! "Uncle Shi—take the high rock on the left with the crossbows! Not a single wolf gets past this stone!"</p>
