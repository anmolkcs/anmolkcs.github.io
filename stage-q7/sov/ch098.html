As sixteen-year-old Lu Chen walked onto the hundred-yard Sky-Spill Arena carrying his nine-foot, 3,600-jin Star-Shattering Heavy Spear, ten thousand disciples from all seven sects held their breath.

“Look at the age gap—Mo Wuji is twenty-two and has stood at Half-Step Xiantian for a full year with 95,000 jin of Half-Step Xiantian strike force and the Elementary Concept of Shadow-Blade; Lu Chen is only sixteen and at the Late Houtian Realm!”

“Begin!”

The instant the match began, Mo Wuji didn’t dare underestimate Lu Chen for a fraction of a second. His figure dissolved into the shadows of the sunlight as the Elementary Concept of Shadow-Blade enveloped the arena in a fifty-yard dome of pitch-black darkness, and nine silent, invisible crescents of Half-Step Xiantian saber light slashed toward Lu Chen’s vitals from nine blind spots at once—

《Earth-Step Lower-Tier Martial Skill: Shadow-Moon Nine-Kill Slash — 95,000 Jin》!

Inside the pitch-black shadow dome where ordinary eyes and spiritual sense were blinded, Lu Chen’s mind rested in the crystal clarity of Prism Stillness.

“Shadow hides in the void, yet before the weight of a falling star and the light of primordial thunder, no shadow can exist!”

DONG—ZAP!

Lu Chen planted the butt of his 3,600-jin Star-Shattering Heavy Spear onto the arena floor.

Instantly, his Embryonic Concept of Mountain-Gravity & Primordial Thunder erupted across a sixty-yard radius!

Fivefold gravitational pressure slammed down over the entire arena, crushing Mo Wuji’s shadow dome into visible distortions and forcing Mo Wuji’s hidden figure out of the void ten paces ahead—while a blinding web of purple-gold star-lightning shattered all nine shadow-saber crescents in midair!
