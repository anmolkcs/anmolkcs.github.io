<p>At the third watch of the night, after the six <strong>Tier-Two Pit-Wardens</strong> had retreated to the dry wooden guard-room near the shaft landing to drink and sleep, the false shale wall at the back of Drift Four was slid shut from the inside.</p>
<p>Inside the thirty-foot geode cavern, under the crushing <strong>Eighteenfold Earth-Marrow Gravity</strong>, Lu Chen untied the forty-pound sack of <strong>Subterranean Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts</strong> and five catties of <strong>Red-Lead Bone-Washing Powder</strong> he had purchased at the Hundred-Herb Apothecary in Black-Rock Town.</p>
<p>He poured the entire forty-pound sack of grey-crimson mineral salts directly into the simmering twelve-foot pool of <strong>Boiling Earth-Marrow Sulfur Brine</strong>!</p>
<p><em>HSSSSSSSSSS—GURGLE!</em></p>
<p>As the apothecary's refined calcining salts reacted with the raw subterranean iron-and-sulfur brine and the dozens of <strong>Natural Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> lining the bottom of the pool, the silvery-red liquid turned the color of dark molten bronze! Heavy, metallic steam rose only three feet above the surface of the pool before being pressed back down by the eighteenfold gravity into a swirling, scorching mist.</p>
<p>"Brother Lu," twenty-two-year-old <strong>Tie Niu</strong> swallowed hard as he stood beside the pool holding a sixty-pound flat-headed <strong>Iron Mine-Sledge</strong>, feeling the skin of his face sting just from the heat radiating off the dark bronze liquid. "That brine is boiling at over a hundred and twenty degrees, and it's thick as melted lead! Even <strong>Scarface Luo Shan</strong> only dared dip his boots and hands in the shallow rim for ten minutes at a time! If you step into the deep center up to your neck—!"</p>
<p>"Luo Shan took twenty years to calcine his bones because he feared the deep pool," Lu Chen said quietly, stripping off his hemp shirt and trousers until he stood bare in the crimson glow of the magma-fissure, wearing only the <strong>red hemp cord</strong> tied by little blind Xiao Ya around his wrist. "We don't have twenty years. We have fourteen nights."</p>
<p>Without a breath of hesitation, Lu Chen stepped off the basalt lip and sank up to his collarbones into the center of the <strong>Boiling Earth-Marrow Sulfur Pool</strong>!</p>
<p><em>ZZZZZZZZZZT—!</em></p>
<p>Even with his <strong>Complete Tier One</strong> ox-hide skin, his <strong>Complete Tier Two</strong> iron-cable muscles, and his <strong>Thirty-Six Tier-Three Bowstring Sinews</strong>, the moment the boiling, heavy-metal brine closed over his shoulders, Lu Chen's vision went momentarily white!</p>
<p>It was not merely surface heat.</p>
<p>Driven by the <strong>Eighteenfold Earth-Marrow Gravity</strong> and the piercing cold-metallic radiation of the <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> under his bare feet, billions of microscopic, heavy <strong>Sulfur-Iron &amp; Red-Lead Ions</strong> bypassed his skin, muscles, and tendons altogether—boring like ten thousand white-hot iron needles straight through his flesh to strike the <strong>outer periosteum and porous cortical walls of all 206 bones in his skeleton</strong>!</p>
<p>In the Great Desolate Origin Universe, a mortal's natural bones—even after Tier Three—were porous, honeycombed structures of white calcium tainted by brittle <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong>. Under a multi-thousand-jin impact from a Tier-Four weapon, those porous mortal bones would splinter like dry chalk.</p>
<p>To enter <strong>Tier Four of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Slag-Bone Calcining</strong>, the boiling sulfur-iron brine and calcining salts had to chemically and physically <strong>dissolve and expel the porous white mortal ash-calcium</strong> from the bone matrix while replacing every microscopic pore with dense, interlocking crystals of <strong>Cold-Forged Black Slag-Iron</strong>!</p>
<p>Inside the boiling bronze pool, every bone from Lu Chen's toes and shins to his ribs and skull felt as though it were being sawed open with a rusted iron file and packed with burning coal!</p>
<p>Blood oozed from his gums; the veins at his temples pulsed like hammering pistons; yet within the absolute, three-pace glacial stillness of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen did not scream or thrash.</p>
<p>Planting his bare feet firmly on the hexagonal <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong> at the bottom of the pool, he sank into the <strong>First of the Twenty-Four Bone-Hammering Postures</strong> carved on the basalt wall by soldier Shi Potian:</p>
<p><strong>Anvil-Root Horse Stance — Carrying the Nine Mountains!</strong></p>
