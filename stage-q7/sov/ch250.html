That night, aboard the Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect's flagship sky-barge moored above the imperial gardens of Central Emperor City, a warm, joyous family banquet was held under the stars.

Sitting at the head of the table, Lu Shan and Meng Wan clinked cups with Sect Master Ling Xiao, Forge-Sovereign Ou Ye, Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng, Prince Huangfu Ji, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang, while Meng Wan served bowls of her steaming Eight-Treasure Lotus Soup.

Later that night, standing quietly on the starlit balcony of the sky-barge with his 54,000-jin Epoch-Sovereign Dragon-Bell Spear beside him, eighteen-year-old Lu Chen communicated with Su Qingxue inside the Chrono-Soul Prism:

"Senior Su, all Three Saint-Grade Lotus Treasures are complete. Once we explore the Sunken Star-Observatory tomorrow to unlock the Thirteenth Technique Bubble and enter the Abyssal Chrono-Rift, I will weave the Rank 6 Nine-Lotus Life-Weaving Formation—and welcome you back to the living world in flesh and blood!"

Inside the blooming Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus, Su Qingxue looked at the upright eighteen-year-old youth who had kept every single promise since Cloudstride City, a soft, radiant smile touching her lips.
