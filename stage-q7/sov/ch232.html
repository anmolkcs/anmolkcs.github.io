Three days later, carrying the five-hundred-foot Peak Sixth-Order Quicksilver Dragon-Tortoise Shell & Spine and ten thousand pounds of Astral-Origin Heavy Gold inside his spatial ring, Lu Chen flew three thousand miles north with Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang to the greatest weapon-refining sanctuary of the Central Sky-Spill Imperial Domain:

The Nine-Sun Imperial Forge-Citadel!

Built inside the thirty-mile crater of an ancient fallen Solar-Iron Star, the Nine-Sun Imperial Forge-Citadel was anchored by Nine Colossal Subterranean Solar-Magma Furnaces chained together by mile-long bridges of black star-iron!

Unlike ordinary smithing cities, the Nine-Sun Imperial Forge-Citadel was jointly governed by the Grand Weapon-Refining Pavilion of the Central Domain (a Second-Grade Peak Overlord Power headed by a Divine Sea Realm Grandmaster: Forge-Sovereign Ou Ye!).

When Lu Chen and his companions arrived at the towering bronze gates of the Forge-Citadel, they saw thousands of young Life-and-Death Wheel prodigies from all over the continent—including the top-ranked geniuses of the Five Great Sacred Lands & Overlord Sects—gathered in the city ahead of next month's Continental Young Sovereign Summit to have their Grade 5 Spirit Treasures tempered!

Inside the central Nine-Sun Grand Plaza, a crowd of five hundred elites surrounded Solar Furnace #1 (The Primordial Nine-Sun Crucible)—the only furnace in the city capable of reaching fifty thousand degrees to smelt Grade 5 Upper-Tier (and Half-Step Grade 6 Saint-Tier) Treasures!

However, the heavy bronze-gold dome of Solar Furnace #1 was currently locked shut, and standing in front of the furnace with a frustrated frown was Forge-Sovereign Ou Ye (an Early Divine Sea Realm grandmaster!), alongside a proud, golden-robed twenty-three-year-old youth at the Fifth Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel:

Yan Wuji—the #1 Young Sovereign of the Blazing-Sun Sacred Sect (and elder brother of Yan Chi)!
