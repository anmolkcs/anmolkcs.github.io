From the captured White-Skull Sha, Lu Chen quickly learned the full truth of what was happening thirty miles ahead at the Seven-Spire Salt Islands:

Beneath the central lagoon of the Seven-Spire Salt Islands lay an ancient Sunken Bronze Tide-Altar built during the Sunken Bronze Dynasty eight thousand years ago to regulate the subterranean brine-veins of the continent.

Around the rim of that submerged bronze altar grew nine stalks of Millennium Halite-Marrow Lotus—and sealed inside the altar's central pillar was a Fifth-Order (Revolving Core Level) Deep-Sea Leviathan Blood Pearl alongside an ancient Bronze Tide-Controlling Seal!

The leader of the Nether-Bone Armada, Gu Hai (Bone-Sea Sovereign) (Extreme Xiantian Realm, 340,000 jin of combat power!), had allied with an envoy from the Blood-Moon Demonic Cult of the western borderlands. In less than an hour at the sunset high tide, Gu Hai planned to slaughter the three hundred captured salt-mining families on the Seven-Spire Islands, using their blood to corrode the Sunken Bronze Dynasty's protective formation on the altar!

"Full speed—we use the captured Bone-Ship as a Trojan horse to sail straight into the central lagoon of the Seven-Spire Islands!" Lu Chen ordered decisively.

Forty minutes later, as the setting sun painted the white salt-spires blood-red, the captured Bone-Ship—piloted by Captain Cang Hai with Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang concealed beneath a Rank 4 Illusion-Stealth Veil drawn by Lu Chen—glided past the outer perimeter of five anchored Bone-Warships straight into the central lagoon of the Seven-Spire Salt Islands!

On the high salt-stone altar overlooking the lagoon, three hundred bound salt-miners and their wives and children were kneeling inside a crimson blood-channel, guarded by two Late Xiantian deputy fleet commanders (Blood-Hook and Bone-Claw) and two hundred elite corsairs—while hovering fifty feet above the center of the lagoon in black bone-armor stood the Extreme Xiantian overlord, Gu Hai (Bone-Sea Sovereign)!

"The sunset tide has reached its peak!" Gu Hai's gravelly voice boomed across the seven salt islands. "Executioners—raise your blades and fill the blood-trough to melt the Bronze Tide-Altar!"

"Whoever dares drop a single blade today dies!"

DONG—ZAP!

The Rank 4 Illusion-Stealth Veil over the approaching ship shattered into a hundred-yard storm of purple-gold lightning as Lu Chen, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, and Su Wan'er erupted simultaneously toward the salt-stone altar!
