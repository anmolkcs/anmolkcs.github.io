With Master Mo Ban and the Artisan Guild airship rescued and gratefully joining their descent, Lu Chen's group reached the thirty-thousand-foot abyss floor of the Astral Storm-Trench on the fourth day!

At the bottom of the trench flowed a ten-mile-wide underground river of liquid silver-purple Primordial Astral Lightning & Star-Essence.

Rising from the center of the lightning river was the Third and Greatest Relic of the Sunken Bronze Dynasty:

A three-thousand-foot-tall Bronze Sky-Bridge Anchor-Fortress, shaped like a nine-tiered bronze bell-tower linked by twelve colossal Star-Iron Chains to the spatial nodes of the continent above!

However, coiled tightly around the central bronze bell-tower of the Sky-Anchor—corrupted and driven mad by thirty-six Nether-Blood Soul-Spikes driven into its spine by the Nether-Blood Cult over the past decade—was a terrifying, three-hundred-foot-long archaic behemoth covered in dark-purple star-scales:

An Early Sixth-Order Spirit Beast (equivalent to a human Life-and-Death Wheel Overlord!): The Astral-Thunder Leviathan (Xing-Lei Kun-Jiao)!

Even with its vitality drained by forty percent from ten years of Nether-Blood corruption, the Early Sixth-Order Astral-Thunder Leviathan radiated a suffocating 4,200,000 jin of quasi-Law pressure!

"An Early Sixth-Order Astral-Thunder Leviathan!" Master Mo Ban turned pale. "To realign the Sky-Anchor, someone must sever those thirty-six Nether-Blood Soul-Spikes and defeat the Leviathan—yet its strength exceeds four million jin!"

Looking at the roaring river of liquid Primordial Astral Lightning flowing around the fortress and the Sixth-Order Leviathan coiled on the tower, Lu Chen's dark eyes gleamed with calm, resolute fire:

"Before we strike the Sixth-Order Leviathan, this Primordial Astral Lightning River is the supreme sacred ground to temper my Star-Crystal Core to the Late Revolving Core Realm—and help Senior Sister Su Wan'er cross her Heavenly Core Tribulation!"
