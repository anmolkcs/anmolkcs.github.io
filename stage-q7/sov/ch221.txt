<p>Three thousand miles south of the Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect, nestled inside a colossal ten-thousand-mile tectonic basin of the Central Imperial Domain, lay one of the most perilous natural wonders of the world:</p>
<p><strong>The Floating Mercury Sea!</strong></p>
<p>Unlike the Inland Abyssal Salt-Sea of the southern borderlands, the Floating Mercury Sea contained not a single drop of water. Instead, the entire ten-thousand-mile basin was filled to a depth of five thousand fathoms with liquid, silver-gleaming <strong>Innate Heavy Quicksilver</strong>—whose density was <strong>thirteen and a half times greater than water</strong>, and whose surface radiated a permanent <strong>Thirty-Fold Gravitation-Magnetic Repulsion Field</strong>!</p>
<p>Because of that thirty-fold magnetic repulsion interacting with the liquid mercury below, thousands of islands composed of <strong>Natural Star-Lodestone</strong> did not rest on the seabed—they <strong>floated suspended between one hundred and three thousand feet in midair</strong> above the silver mirror of the mercury sea, forming the <strong>Floating Mercury Archipelago</strong>!</p>
<p>Even more dangerously, whenever the twin moons of the Sky Spill Continent aligned at mid-month, a phenomenon known as the <strong>Quicksilver Inverted Tide</strong> occurred: millions of tons of liquid heavy mercury rose straight up from the sea into the sky like inverted silver waterfalls, pouring <em>upward</em> onto the floating lodestone islands while unleashing corrosive <strong>Mercury-Vapor Miasma</strong> that could rot the Dharma Body of an ordinary <strong>Second or Third Revolution Life-and-Death Wheel</strong> cultivator!</p>
<p>Standing at the harbor cliff of <strong>Silver-Anchor City</strong> on the northern shore of the Floating Mercury Sea, eighteen-year-old <strong>Lu Chen</strong> (<strong>Third Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel</strong>), <strong>Su Wan'er</strong> (<strong>Peak Revolving Core / Half-Step Life-Wheel</strong>), <strong>Murong Zi</strong> (<strong>Peak Revolving Core</strong>), and <strong>Chu Kuang</strong> (<strong>Peak Revolving Core</strong>) looked out across the blinding silver expanse where hundreds of lodestone islands drifted slowly through the silver mist.</p>
<p>"Look at the ships moored along the cliff docks of Silver-Anchor City," <strong>Chu Kuang</strong> marveled, leaning over the parapet. "Because liquid mercury is thirteen times denser than water, even ships forged from solid black iron float high on top of the mercury like corks—while their keels use magnetic lodestone rudders to glide between the floating sky-islands and the sea surface!"</p>
<p>Inside Lu Chen's spiritual sea, <strong>Su Qingxue</strong> stood beside the blooming <strong>Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus</strong> and pointed toward the deepest, perpetually twilight zone at the center of the Floating Mercury Sea two thousand miles out:</p>
<p><em>"Lu Chen, two thousand miles out at the center of the Floating Mercury Archipelago lies <strong>Inverted-Hourglass Island</strong>—an ancient time-anomaly island formed when a shard of a <strong>Chrono-Yin Meteor</strong> fell into the mercury basin thirty thousand years ago. At the core of Inverted-Hourglass Island grows the <strong>Second Saint-Grade Lotus Treasure</strong> needed for my physical body's bones and spiritual sea: the <strong>Ten-Thousand-Year Chrono-Yin Jade Marrow</strong>!"</em></p>
