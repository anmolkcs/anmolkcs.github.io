Stepping out of the Seven Star Mirage Tower after clearing the Sixth Floor (a feat achieved only by Top 30 Heavenly Dragon Roll geniuses!), Lu Chen first stopped by the sect’s Wind-Roc Courier Post before heading to the arena.

Inside the courier hall, a warm letter from Clear-Brook Manor in the Azure Sky Royal Capital was waiting for him, written in his mother Meng Wan’s neat script with a postscript from his father Lu Shan:

“Chen’er, your father and I are in wonderful health! The Rank 3 Guardian Array you built around Clear-Brook Manor keeps the courtyard warm as spring even now that winter frost has fallen over the capital. Three days ago, with the Star-Marrow soup and the pills you left us, your father broke through to the Peak of the Fifth Stage (Bone Forging) and opened his first two meridians toward the Sixth Stage (Pulse Condensation), while Mother has reached the Middle Fifth Stage! Granny Liu and Grand Marshal Su sent over two jars of winter plum wine yesterday. Eat well on Broken Spear Peak, and don’t worry about home—Father and Mother.”

Reading the letter, a warm, gentle smile lingered on Lu Chen’s face.

He immediately packed a spatial courier box containing two bottles of Rank 3 Rune-Inscribed Pulse-Condensing Pills, four slabs of Star-Warming Jade, and a newly written letter detailing his life with Elder Tie and Senior Brother Fang Shi on Broken Spear Peak, entrusting it to the fastest Third-Order Wind-Roc heading south.

Then, strapping his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear onto his back, he walked toward the crowded Heavenly Dragon Roll Arena at the center of the sect!
