Over the next five days following his sixteenth birthday, Lu Chen didn’t rush to leave Clear-Brook Manor.

Instead, using eighty of his Low-Grade True Essence Stones, thirty-six slabs of Profound White Jade purchased from the Sky Treasure Pavilion, and the purified residual blood of the Fourth-Order Earth-Core Flame Drake, Lu Chen locked himself in the manor’s study to craft his first Rank 3 Formation Plates.

Under Prism Stillness, his soul force—now comparable to a veteran Early Houtian / Rank 3 Inscription Master—guided a diamond-tipped spirit stylus across the thirty-six slabs of Profound White Jade.

Each jade plate required engraving seven hundred and twenty continuous Rank 3 Spirit-Array Runes without a single flaw.

By the fifth night, all thirty-six glowing jade formation plates were complete!

Around midnight, while his parents slept, Lu Chen buried the thirty-six jade plates six feet deep at the thirty-six geomantic nodes surrounding Clear-Brook Manor, placing thirty Low-Grade True Essence Stones and a sliver of the Fourth-Order Flame Drake Crystal at the central spring well as the array core.

Hum—!

As Lu Chen pressed the activation seal with his hand, an invisible, dome-shaped barrier of shimmering silver-gold starlight rose silently over the entire three-courtyard estate before fading from naked sight:

《Rank 3 Compound Formation: Nine-Star Spirit-Gathering & Mountain-Locking Guardian Array》!

Once activated: 1. The Spirit-Gathering Layer permanently increased the heaven-and-earth origin energy concentration inside Clear-Brook Manor to four times the outside world—equivalent to a Core Disciple Pavilion on Seven Star Mountain—so his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan would advance twice as fast just by breathing in the courtyard! 2. The Mountain-Locking Guardian Layer remained dormant during peaceful times, but if anyone harboring hostile killing intent attempted to force their way into the manor without a family jade token, the thirty-six formation plates would unleash a barrier capable of withstanding full-strength strikes from three Peak Houtian masters for a full hour while immediately alerting Lu Chen’s linked jade token!

Handing the two master control jade tokens to his father and mother the next morning and demonstrating how a single thought could raise a golden shield strong enough to block a 10,000-jin boulder, Lu Chen watched his parents’ eyes widen in amazement before they laughed with relief.

Now, his home was an unbreakable fortress.
