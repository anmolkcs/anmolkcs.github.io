<p><em>CLANK... CLANK... CLANK...</em></p>
<p>Link by giant iron link, the winch-cage dropped away from the pale grey square of winter sky above Black-Slag Canyon, plunging deeper and deeper into the subterranean bowels of the earth.</p>
<p>By the time the cage passed the one-hundred-fathom mark, natural daylight had vanished completely, replaced by the eerie, flickering greenish-red glow of <strong>Sulfur-Moss Lamps</strong> hung every twenty fathoms along the slick black basalt shaft walls.</p>
<p>By the two-hundred-fathom mark, the air temperature had swung from sub-zero surface frost to a stifling, humid ninety degrees saturation of <strong>Subterranean Sulfur-Steam and Dense Desolate Ash-Qi</strong>.</p>
<p>And as the iron cage finalmente hit the flooded gravel landing at the <strong>three-hundred-fathom (1,800-foot) bottom of Deep-Shaft Pit #7</strong> with a heavy <em>SPLASH-CLANG</em>, Lu Chen felt the terrifying physical reason why nine out of ten mortal convicts sent to Pit #7 died within their first year:</p>
<p>Directly beneath the floor of Pit #7 ran a titanic, primordial geological fault of <strong>High-Density Earth-Marrow Iron</strong>.</p>
<p>Because of that concentrated subterranean Origin-Vein, the downward gravitational pull at the bottom of Shaft #7 was not the tenfold gravity of the surface, nor the twelvefold gravity of Frost-Tooth Gully—it was a crushing, relentless <strong>Fifteenfold Desolate Gravity</strong>!</p>
<p>The moment Lu Chen stepped out of the iron winch-cage onto the ankle-deep, rust-colored sulfur water of the shaft landing, his own body and the two hundred and sixty jin of spear, broken saber-steel, and calcining salts on his back weighed the equivalent of <strong>nearly four thousand jin</strong> pressing straight down through the arches of his boots!</p>
<p>Even with his <strong>Complete Tier Two</strong> iron-flesh and all <strong>Thirty-Six Tier-Three Bowstring Sinews</strong> humming taut from heel to neck, Lu Chen heard a distinct, ominous <em>creak</em> echo from deep inside his shin-bones (tibias) and thigh-bones (femurs)!</p>
<p>His skin was tough as cured ox-hide; his muscles were dense as twisted iron cables; his tendons were supple as dragon-horn bowstrings—yet the <strong>206 bones of his skeleton</strong> supporting all that muscle and tendon were still <strong>uncalcined mortal bone</strong>! Under fifteenfold gravity, simply standing upright with a heavy load subjected his skeleton to a continuous compressive stress that tried to bow his shin-bones and crush the spongy trabecular marrow-cavities inside his vertebrae!</p>
<p>"Well, well—look at the fresh meat Boss Luo sent down the chute!"</p>
<p>Four burly <strong>Pit-Wardens</strong> wearing oiled leather aprons over chainmail vests and carrying sixty-pound <strong>Iron-Spiked Pick-Clubs</strong> stepped out of the timber guard-niche at the bottom of the shaft. Their leader—a toad-faced <strong>Peak Tier Two</strong> warden named <strong>He Da</strong>—took the stamped quarantine tally-slip from the cage-runner, spat into the rust-water, and pointed his spiked club down a low, dripping rock tunnel to the north where the rhythmic <em>clink... clink... clink</em> of pickaxes echoed out of the suffocating dark.</p>
<p>"Guarantor for Convict #704 in <strong>Drift Four</strong>, huh?" Warden He Da sneered, knowing full well that Boss Luo Shan had sent word via the shaft speaking-tube five minutes ago that this youth was never to leave Pit #7 alive once the twentieth arrived. "Rules of Drift Four: no guarantor is allowed back in the winch-cage until #704's team chips out <strong>one hundred full carts of Black-Vein Ore</strong> before the twentieth! And nobody down here eats a grain of millet until their daily six carts are weighed at this landing! Get your ass down that tunnel—#704 is at the very end of the wet drift!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen didn't spare the toad-faced warden a single word.</p>
<p>Adjusting the canvas roll on his back so the fifteenfold gravitational load distributed evenly down his spinal column—already turning every single step down the flooded tunnel into the beginning of <strong>Tier Four Slag-Bone Calcining</strong>—Lu Chen gripped his eighty-jin black iron spear and walked into the steaming, pitch-dark maw of <strong>Drift Four</strong>.</p>
