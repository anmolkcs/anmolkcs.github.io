Five days west of the Southern Sky-Spill border, the lush green valleys and jagged granite peaks vanished behind an endless wall of shimmering heat.

Below the wings of the Fourth-Order Peak Star-Winged Cloud Roc, the earth had been vitrified ten thousand years ago by the fall of the Sunken Bronze Dynasty's orbital sun-mirrors. For five thousand miles in every direction stretched the Mirage-Glass Desert—an ocean of translucent, amber-and-obsidian silicate dunes mixed with fine bronze-oxide sand.

Whenever the blazing desert sun struck those curved silicate dunes, the light did not simply reflect; it refracted through natural spatial-heat lenses, projecting hundreds of overlapping, upside-down mirages of ancient cities, oasis lakes, and phantom mountain ranges across the horizon.

"Even my Late Xiantian spiritual sense gets bent thirty degrees sideways after extending more than two hundred yards into those glass dunes," Murong Zi said gravely as he stood near the prow of the Cloud Roc's saddle platform, his hand resting on his Star-River Sword. "No wonder they say even Revolving Core masters lose their bearings in the Mirage-Glass Desert if they fly too low."

Sitting near the center of the platform beside Su Wan'er and Chu Kuang, seventeen-year-old Lu Chen held the Three-Piece Sunken Bronze Dynasty Astrolabe in his palm.

Before they entered the desert's spatial interference zone, Lu Chen had just finished sending a warm spatial-falcon letter and a jade box of Rank 4 Spirit-Dew Tea back to Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 on Broken Spear Peak for his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan, reminding his father to pace his spear drills at the Early Xiantian Realm and promising his mother he would bring back sweet desert crystal-dates along with the Six-Leaf Golden Core Lotuses.

"The mirages aren't natural optical illusions," Lu Chen said calmly, his dark eyes reflecting the nine-faceted stillness of the Chrono-Soul Prism as he studied the refracted light beams dancing across the glass dunes below. "On Earth, when restoring multi-layered lead-barium glass and bronze mirrors from the Warring States period, you learn that every refraction angle follows the refractive index of the silicate lattice. Here, the Sunken Bronze Dynasty deliberately vitrified the sand into a five-thousand-mile Rank 5 Acoustic-Optical Refraction Maze to hide their subterranean capital from sky-borne enemies."

Lu Chen channeled a thread of his Twelve-Faceted Flawless Star-Crystal Core essence into the three-piece bronze astrolabe.

HUM—CHIME!

The three interlocked bronze rings of the astrolabe spun in midair, projecting a golden acoustic-harmonic beam that cancelled out the false light refractions within a one-mile radius ahead of the Cloud Roc, revealing a hidden, wind-swept highway of fused black glass winding straight toward the central basin of the desert!
