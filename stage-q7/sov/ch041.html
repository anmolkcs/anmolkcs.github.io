Inside Lu Chen’s 206 golden-jade bones, a deep temple-bell chime echoed across the arena.

One spear thrust and one shaft sweep!

Rank #11 (now #12) Lin Sen—a Half-Step Sixth Stage core genius of the Royal Lin Clan—had been crushed just like his younger cousin Lin Xuan before him!
