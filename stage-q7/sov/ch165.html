At the exact center of the fifty-mile black obsidian mirror, the Blood-Eclipse Sect's camp had already blasted a hundred-yard hole through the surface crust using blood-corrosive arrays.

When Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang concealed their auras with a Rank 4 Mirage-Cloaking Talisman and glided down through the shaft into the subterranean abyss two miles beneath the desert floor, all four of them drew in a quiet breath of awe.

Hanging upside-down from the vaulted subterranean ceiling of a cavern two hundred miles wide was an entire, intact Ancient Imperial Capital forged from gleaming bronze, star-glass, and interlocking clockwork gears!

Thousands of bronze pagodas, suspended aqueducts of glowing liquid mercury, and colossal hundred-foot Astronomical Escapement Gears hung inverted above a bottomless subterranean sea of liquid starlight below—held aloft by a single, ten-thousand-foot-tall central axle that connected the cavern ceiling to the abyss floor:

The Heaven-Mending Bronze Pillar (Bu-Tian Tong-Zhu)!

"An entire inverted city powered by a subterranean star-core and astronomical clockwork..." Su Wan'er whispered, her Rank 4 Inscription Master eyes shining as she gazed up at the millions of interlocking Rank 5 & Rank 6 Ancient Mechanical-Runes ticking slowly across the bronze gears. "The Sunken Bronze Dynasty didn't perish from war—they inverted their entire capital underground to seal a spatial rift beneath the continent!"
