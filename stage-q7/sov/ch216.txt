<p>Bursting through the bronze-and-porcelain doors of the <strong>Ninth Catacomb Chamber (The Heart of the Dragon-Kiln)</strong>, Lu Chen and his companions saw a breathtaking subterranean caldera half a mile wide.</p>
<p>In the center of the caldera bubbled a hundred-yard lake of liquid, rainbow-colored <strong>Ten-Thousand-Year Earth-Core Nirvana Glaze</strong>—and growing atop a jade pedestal at the very heart of the lake was a three-foot-tall, nine-leaved crystalline lotus root whose veins pulsed like a living heart across nine cycles of life and death:</p>
<p><strong>The First Saint-Grade Lotus Treasure: The Nine-Cycles Reincarnation Root!</strong></p>
<p>Surrounding the lake of Nirvana Glaze stood <strong>sixteen First-and-Second Revolution Life-and-Death Wheel Elders</strong> of the <strong>Nether-Blood Cult</strong>, while hovering above the center of the lake in a blood-red cloak was the terrifying <strong>Vice-Patriarch of the Nether-Blood Cult, Xue Wuya</strong>—a <strong>Third Revolution (Second Life-Renewal Revolution)</strong> powerhouse whose combat strength reached <strong>14,500,000 jin</strong>!</p>
<p>"That brat <strong>Lu Chen</strong> who crippled Elder Yin-Sha at the Astral Storm-Trench?! How did he get past the thirty-six Sixth-Order Celadon Automatons so fast?!"</p>

<p>"Hahaha! Heaven helps my Nether-Blood Cult! Wait until I finish pulling the <strong>Nine-Cycles Reincarnation Root</strong> out of this Nirvana Glaze pool—Wait, what is that lunatic doing?!"</p>

<p><strong>The First Death-Tribulation Revolution — Immediately Followed by the Second Life-Renewal Revolution!</strong></p>
