On the fifth morning, as Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, and Murong Zi stepped out of the subterranean shaft of the Sunken Star-Observatory into the outer canyon thirty miles east of Central Emperor City to head toward the Abyssal Chrono-Rift, the sky above the canyon suddenly turned a eerie dark scarlet!

Why?

Because when Lu Chen had restored the Continental Star-Astrolabe and tripled the strength of the Continental Star-Seal, he had ruined the ten-year infiltration plan of the Blood-Sovereign Clan's vanguard!

Hovering in the scarlet clouds above the canyon was a figure draped in a cloak of woven blood-stars—not a native cultivator of the Sky Spill Continent, but a Half-Step Middle Divine Sea Shadow-Clone projected across the weakened spatial membrane by an Upper-Realm Envoy of the Blood-Sovereign Clan (Envoy Xue Ling), accompanied by two surviving Early Divine Sea Ancestors of the Nether-Blood Cult's hidden ancestral shrine!

"So you are the eighteen-year-old ant who killed Xue Mojun and repaired the Continental Star-Astrolabe!"

The Blood-Sovereign Shadow-Envoy's voice echoed with cold, arrogant Upper-Realm contempt as his Middle Divine Sea pressure (200,000,000 jin!) locked down fifty miles of space around the canyon!

"Wait—I sense the fluctuation of the Primordial Chrono-Yin Clan's ancestral relic inside your spiritual sea! Hand over the Chrono-Soul Prism and kneel to receive my Blood-Slave Brand, or my Middle Divine Sea palm will erase you and your two companions right here!"
