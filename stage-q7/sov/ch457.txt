<p>Moments later, the four half-frozen survivors from <strong>Mud-Well Village</strong> were carried into the warm smithy shed and laid beside the glowing charcoal hearth, while Granny Liu and Widow Qin rushed over with hot millet soup and frostbite salve.</p>
<p><strong>Hunter Feng</strong>—a forty-year-old <strong>Peak Tier One</strong> hunter whose left arm was tied in a bloody rag where a heavy saber had sheared through his leather bracer—gripped Uncle Shi's wrist with trembling fingers, his teeth chattering so violently he could barely speak.</p>
<p>"Uncle Shi... Old Tie..." Hunter Feng choked out between gulps of scalding soup. "Mud-Well Village... is gone. Yesterday before dawn, <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> came down out of <strong>Dead-Crow Gorge</strong> with all eighteen of his remaining outlaws!"</p>
<p>Inside the shed, every face turned grim.</p>
<p>"Han Wu went crazy three days ago when his scouts found the bodies of <strong>Blood-Axe Scar-Six</strong> and his eleven men buried down in Bitter-Pine Ravine," Hunter Feng continued, tears of rage and grief cutting tracks through the soot on his cheeks. "He didn't know whether it was Mud-Well Village or Ash-Brook Village that killed Scar-Six—so he hit Mud-Well first in the dark before our watchman could blow the horn! Han Wu himself chopped down our log gate in three blows of his <strong>Hundred-and-Ten-Jin Ghost-Head Saber</strong>! Eight of our men died trying to hold the square so the women and children could scatter into the caves up the high ridge!"</p>
<p>"What did Han Wu take?" Uncle Shi asked, his knuckles white on his spear shaft.</p>
<p>"He burned our smithy, took every grain of winter millet and all three hundred jin of our smelted tax-iron," Hunter Feng coughed out a fleck of blood, "and then—instead of going back to Dead-Crow Gorge—he marched his whole band of eighteen outlaws and three pack-mules straight south into the throat of <strong>Howling-Wind Pass</strong>!"</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan's single hand slammed down onto the stone anvil block.</p>
<p>"Howling-Wind Pass?!" the old blacksmith rasped. "That's the only road between our northern ridge villages and <strong>Black-Rock Frontier Town</strong>! With the ten-foot snowdrifts on the flanks of the ridge, no loaded iron-sledge can get to town or to <strong>Black-Slag Canyon</strong> without passing through Howling-Wind Pass!"</p>
<p>"That's why he took it!" Hunter Feng cried out. "As we crawled through the brush above the pass this morning to reach you, we heard Han Wu laughing with his two remaining <strong>Tier Two</strong> lieutenants at the old stone watch-fort in the center of the gorge! Han Wu said he knows whichever village killed Scar-Six has to haul their winter iron tribute through Howling-Wind Pass before the twentieth of the twelfth month! He's built a six-foot ice-and-boulder barricade right across the fifty-foot choke-point of the pass—waiting to butcher your caravan, take your iron and silver, and then come back to burn Ash-Brook to the ground!"</p>
<p>A deadly chill settled over the crowded smithy shed.</p>
<p>Outside, the wind howled down from the Black-Briar Barren Ridge, signaling the arrival of the twelfth and final month of the year.</p>
<p>If they stayed behind Ash-Brook's palisade, they could probably defend the walls—but on the twentieth day of the twelfth month, <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> would blast shut the lower drifts of <strong>Deep-Shaft Pit #7</strong>, burying <strong>Tie Niu</strong> and forty miners alive in the dark flooded rock, and five days later <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan</strong> would lead the Black-Rock Garrison up the gorge to declare Ash-Brook in default of the County Tax!</p>
