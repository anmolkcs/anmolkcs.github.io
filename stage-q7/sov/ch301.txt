<p>A river of golden-amber light parted around the two-mile-long bronze prow of <em>The Primordial Star-Ark</em> as the colossal Saint-Vessel glided out of the <strong>Amber Star-Gate</strong> and entered the boundless firmament of the <strong>Upper Star-Soul Realm</strong>.</p>
<p>Standing at the forward observation balustrade of the ark alongside <strong>Su Qingxue</strong>, <strong>Su Wan'er</strong>, <strong>Murong Zi</strong>, <strong>Chu Kuang</strong>, <strong>Elder Tie</strong>, and his parents <strong>Lu Shan</strong> and <strong>Meng Wan</strong>, eighteen-year-old <strong>Lu Chen</strong> drew in a slow, deep breath of the upper-realm air.</p>
<p>Even a single breath made the <strong>1,000-mile Flawless Divine Seas</strong> of Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang hum like plucked zithers, while inside Lu Chen's lower dantian, the <strong>300-mile Golden-Jade Inner World</strong> and its three-hundred-foot <strong>Astral World-Tree</strong> drank in the ambient energy greedily.</p>
<p>In the lower <strong>Sky Spill Continent</strong>, cultivators absorbed heaven-and-earth Origin Qi. Here in the <strong>Upper Star-Soul Realm</strong>, the atmosphere was saturated with <strong>Primordial Star-Soul Force</strong> and <strong>World-Origin Essence</strong> ten times denser and heavier than the richest imperial spirit-vein below! Even the gravitational pull of the landmass beneath the clouds was ten times stronger than the lower realm's—meaning a mortal stone dropped from a table here fell with the crushing weight of iron, while ordinary Houtian or Xiantian cultivators from the lower world wouldn't even be able to fly ten feet off the ground!</p>
<p>"Look at the sky above the clouds—there aren't just one sun and one moon," <strong>Meng Wan</strong> murmured in wonder, holding <strong>Lu Shan's</strong> hand as she looked up at the violet-gold vault of heaven.</p>
<p>High in the daytime sky hung <strong>three blazing golden-violet star-suns</strong> orbiting one another in a slow triangular dance, while along the northern horizon drifted a ring of shattered, glowing planetary fragments the size of mountain ranges, linked together by shimmering bridges of condensed starlight.</p>
<p>"We have arrived in the <strong>Southern Fringe of the Outer Star-Continent—the Myriad-Loom Prefecture</strong>," <strong>Su Qingxue</strong> said softly, her snow-white robes fluttering in the upper-realm gale as her <strong>100-mile Chrono-Lotus Inner World</strong> resonated naturally with the laws of her homeland. "The Upper Star-Soul Realm is divided into four vast <strong>Outer Star-Continents</strong>—each a hundred times larger than the entire Sky Spill Continent—surrounding the central <strong>Star-Soul Imperial Sea</strong>, where the <strong>Seven Sovereign Clans</strong> rule from their floating <strong>World-King Citadels</strong>."</p>
<p>Su Qingxue pointed toward a towering metropolis built fifty miles ahead atop a terraced mountain of silver-blue star-quartz, where hundreds of mile-high <strong>Astral Wind-Looms</strong> and <strong>Bronze Bell-Towers</strong> spun threads of liquid starlight out of the sky:</p>
<p>"That is <strong>Myriad-Loom Star-City (Wan-Zhi Xing-Cheng)</strong>, governed by the <strong>Artisan-Star Guild</strong>—the upper-realm parent guild that left behind the Cloud-Sea Sky-Rail and Loom-Star City's inheritance in the lower world ten thousand years ago. Back before my Primordial Chrono-Yin Clan was besieged by the Seven Sovereign Clans, the Old Guild Master of Myriad-Loom Star-City owed my grandfather a life-debt and refused to join the siege. However, look at the central bell-tower of the city—its <strong>Seventh-Order Guardian Star-Bell</strong> is completely silent and covered in gray stone-rot."</p>
<p>Lu Chen's dark eyes narrowed as he sank his consciousness into <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, looking across fifty miles of dense upper-realm space toward the central bell-tower of Myriad-Loom Star-City.</p>
<p>At the same time, he noticed a fleet of twelve black-iron <strong>Blood-Winged War-Galleons</strong> flying the banner of a bleeding crimson sun—the emblem of an outer vassal branch of the <strong>Blood-Sovereign Clan</strong>—blockading the main aerial harbor of Myriad-Loom Star-City!</p>
