<p>Dusk was settling over the Black-Briar Barren Ridge on the fourth day of the eleventh month when the northern watch-horn of Ash-Brook Village blew three rapid, joyous blasts.</p>
<p><em>WUUU—WUUU—WUUU!</em></p>
<p>Three blasts meant the hunting party was returning alive—and carrying heavy game.</p>
<p>Within moments, nearly every man, woman, and child of the seventy-three villagers poured out of their low stone huts into the freezing mud lane, clutching pine-pitch torches and holding their breath as the wooden bar of the northern palisade gate was swung wide.</p>
<p>Through the swirling grey snow-flurries marched <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Lu Chen</strong>, <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, <strong>Cross-Eyed Liu</strong>, fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong>, and <strong>Old Ma</strong>—who was walking on his own two feet with only a slight limp, leaning on a pine staff!</p>
<p>Every villager who had gone up Frost-Tooth Gully that afternoon to meet the hunters at the Dead-Pine Boundary was staggering under a massive load of red, frost-crusted meat. On the first two ash-wood pole-litters rested the fourteen hundred jin of butchered quarters and the four-inch-thick armored hide of the <strong>Scarred Iron-Bristle King-Boar</strong>, its two fourteen-inch yellow-ivory tusks jutting up like curved sickles in the torchlight. On the third and fourth litters lay the six-hundred-pound sow and the seven carcasses of the <strong>Gray-Scab Ridge Wolves</strong>, topped by the silver-backed pelt of the <strong>Early Tier Two Alpha Wolf</strong>!</p>
<p>For three heartbeats, the crowded village square was dead silent except for the crackle of pine pitch.</p>
<p>Then seventy-year-old <strong>Granny Liu</strong> dropped her cane in the snow, reached out with trembling, gnarled fingers to touch the four-inch-thick iron-bristle hide of the King-Boar, and burst into tears.</p>
<p>"The Scarred King-Boar of the Eastern Wallow..." Granny Liu wept aloud. "Two thousand jin of boar meat and seven ridge wolves! And all six of our men walked back through the gate with their arms and legs whole! The Mountain God hasn't forgotten Ash-Brook!"</p>
<p>A deafening cheer erupted from seventy starved throats, echoing off the frozen cliffs of the ridge. Women hugged their crying children; old Watchman Ge, his bandaged neck wrapped in sheepskin, pounded his wooden staff against the frozen mud in joy.</p>
<p>Standing beside the smithy shed with his splinted right arm strapped across his chest, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> watched Lu Chen walk smoothly under the rear pole of the heaviest litter—bearing four hundred jin of weight on one shoulder without even breathing hard—and saw the unmistakable, cable-dense contour of <strong>Early Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong> beneath Lu Chen's blood-stained sleeves.</p>
<p>When the litters were set down on the stone butchering slabs in the square, Uncle Shi raised his three-fingered left hand for quiet.</p>
<p>"Listen to me, everyone!" Uncle Shi's gravelly voice carried to every corner of the palisade. "Old Ma stepped on a Grey-Bog Viper by the creek—it was Lu Chen who cut the venom out of his leg before it could reach his knee! At the Boiling-Mud Wallow, it was Lu Chen who jumped down a fifty-foot cliff, bled the Corpse-Rot Poison out of the King-Boar's neck in midair, took the beast's fourteen-thousand-jin charge on his spear, and wiped out four of <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's</strong> poachers! And at Split-Stone Defile yesterday morning, when nine Gray-Scab Wolves cornered our litters, Lu Chen stood alone in the four-foot pass and killed seven wolves—including their <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> Silver-Backed Alpha!"</p>
<p>Uncle Shi reached into his coat and held up the oiled leather pouch taken from the dead poacher squad leader Ma Liu, along with the fist-sized purple-black <strong>King-Boar Gallbladder</strong> and the <strong>Alpha Wolf Gallbladder</strong>.</p>
<p>"Eleven taels of broken Prefectural Silver from the bandits' belts, plus two Tier-Two beast galls and nine wolf pelts worth another fourteen taels at the trading post!" Uncle Shi declared, his eyes shining as he turned toward Old Tie Shan. "That's twenty-five taels of silver toward the thirty taels—or the remaining twenty bars of Cold-Slag Iron—we need before the twentieth of the twelfth month! <strong>Tie Niu</strong> is coming home from Pit #7 this winter!"</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan's single hand covered his weathered face, his broad shoulders shaking silently under his sheepskin cloak as fourteen-year-old Shi Tou ran over and threw his arms around the old blacksmith's waist.</p>
