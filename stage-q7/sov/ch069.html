According to the rules of the Five-Kingdom Trial, before the First Place Rewards (the drop of Thousand-Year Star-Marrow Liquid and the Grade 3 Upper-Tier Xiantian Treasure) were officially conferred, the #2 finisher had the right to issue a single Final Verification Duel on the Sky-Fall Plaza Stage!

Stepping onto the center of the black ironstone plaza with his nine-ringed Grade 3 Lower-Tier Golden-Scale Broadsaber on his shoulder, the eighteen-year-old #1 prodigy of the Great Yue Kingdom—Chu Kuang (Middle Houtian Realm)—looked at Lu Chen with burning, unyielding fighting spirit.

Unlike Yan Luo, Chu Kuang wasn’t an underhanded schemer; he was a battle-obsessed saber maniac who had cultivated a rare Earth-Attribute Brute-Force Body alongside his Middle Houtian cultivation, boasting 35,000 jin of explosive saber power!

“Junior Brother Lu Chen!” Chu Kuang’s voice boomed like thunder across the canyon. “You beat me by six hundred points inside the battlefield, and I heard you sent Yan Luo flying in one spear stroke! However, I, Chu Kuang, have stood at the Middle Houtian Realm for half a year and comprehended a trace of Half-Step Mountain-Splitting Saber Intent! Exchange three strikes with me on this plaza—if your spear can withstand my saber, I’ll bow and recognize you as the undisputed #1 of the Five Kingdoms!”

“As Senior Brother Chu wishes,” Lu Chen smiled calmly, stepping onto the black ironstone plaza and lifting his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear.
