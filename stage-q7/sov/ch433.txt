<p>Down in the churned mud flat, the remaining two Broken-Tooth Poachers—the <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> squad leader <strong>Ma Liu</strong> and a lean <strong>Peak Tier One</strong> spearman—scrambled frantically up the slick clay bank toward the eastern ravine trail, leaving their two dead and crippled comrades behind as the enraged King-Boar wheeled around!</p>
<p>Yet the <strong>Corpse-Rot Poison</strong> smeared on the two crossbow bolts buried in the King-Boar's neck and flank was already beginning to take effect. Black foam bubbled from the monstrous beast's jaws; its hind legs staggered once in the deep mud as the fast-acting neurotoxin slowed its charge by thirty percent.</p>
<p>Seeing the giant boar stumble, the Early Tier Two squad leader Ma Liu halted halfway up the bank, a vicious grin twisting his pockmarked face as he drew a heavy, sixty-jin <strong>Ring-Pommel Executioner's Saber</strong> from his back.</p>
<p>"The poison's hitting its heart, Blackie!" Ma Liu shouted to his remaining spearman. "Circle behind its blinded left eye! We bleed it for three more minutes until its legs lock up, take the two tusks, the gall, and the heart-meat back to Boss Han Wu, and leave the poisoned carcass in the mud!"</p>
<p>Up on the fifty-foot basalt ledge, Uncle Shi's breath caught.</p>
<p>"If they let that Corpse-Rot Poison soak through the boar's bloodstream for three more minutes," Uncle Shi whispered urgently to Lu Chen, "all fourteen hundred jin of meat will turn black and toxic! Nobody in Ash-Brook will be able to eat a single mouthful—and once Ma Liu kills the boar, he'll follow the creek down and find Old Ma's tracks!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen didn't waste a word.</p>
<p>His eyes measured the fifty-foot drop down the forty-five-degree basalt scree slope, the position of the staggering King-Boar, and the two poachers circling in the mud.</p>
<p>"Uncle Shi—cover me with your horn-bow from this ledge! Put your first arrow through the spearman's leg when I hit the bottom! Big-Tall Zhao, Shi Tou—come down the scree rope thirty breaths behind me with the skinning knives!"</p>
<p>Before Uncle Shi could warn him how dangerous it was to face both an <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> bandit leader and a berserk <strong>Peak Tier Two</strong> King-Boar simultaneously while Lu Chen was still only at <strong>Complete Tier One (Ash-Skin Scouring)</strong>, Lu Chen stepped off the edge of the basalt shelf!</p>
<p>He did not jump wildly through the air.</p>
<p>Sliding down the near-vertical forty-five-degree shale scree on the soles of his tough rawhide boots—using the butt-spike of his eighty-jin solid iron spear as a third braking point behind his hip—Lu Chen rode a cascading chute of loose gravel down the fifty-foot cliff face in four heartbeats, building up a tremendous downward kinetic momentum under the tenfold gravity!</p>
<p>Down at the edge of the mud wallow, the Peak Tier One poacher called Blackie heard the roar of sliding shale above him and spun around with his spear raised—just as <strong>Uncle Shi's</strong> sixty-pound horn-bow twanged from the high ledge!</p>
<p><em>THWACK!</em></p>
<p>A broadhead iron arrow punched clean through Blackie's right thigh, pinning him to a dead pine root with a howl of agony!</p>
<p>At the exact same instant, Lu Chen shot off the bottom lip of the shale scree ten feet above the mud flat!</p>
<p>He did not aim his descent at the wounded poacher, nor at the Early Tier Two leader Ma Liu.</p>
<p>Instead, knowing that if the Corpse-Rot Poison stayed in the King-Boar's neck vein for another ten breaths the entire fourteen hundred jin of winter meat would be ruined for Ash-Brook Village, Lu Chen plummeted straight toward the blind, scarred left side of the raging <strong>Iron-Bristle King-Boar</strong>!</p>
<p>Midair, within the three-pace sphere of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, time seemed to slow to a crawl. Lu Chen saw the thumb-thick iron crossbow bolt sticking out of the main jugular-carotid junction on the King-Boar's neck, and he saw the green poison ring spreading four inches around the iron shaft.</p>
<p>Instead of stabbing to kill immediately, Lu Chen twisted his waist in midair and whipped the razor-sharp four-edged diamond blade of his eighty-jin boar-spear in a surgical <strong>Crescent-Moon Slicing Cut</strong> right across the base of the poisoned crossbow wound!</p>
<p><em>SHHHHK—SPLURT!</em></p>
<p>The fourteen-inch blade—driven by his falling weight and twelve hundred jin of Complete Tier One force—sliced a deep, six-inch V-shaped trough around the poisoned bolt, excising the entire chunk of green-black poisoned flesh and severing the external carotid artery so the boar's own roaring heart-pressure blasted the remaining poison outward in a ten-foot crimson geyser before it could reach the rest of the carcass!</p>
