"Father, Mother, Master Tie, Wan'er, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang—swallow the World-Seed Fruit and fuse the Constellation Cores into the center of your Nine-Span World-Bridges! Qingxue and I will anchor your meridians with the Chrono-Soul Prism!"

  2. Elder Tie Cang opened a 110-Mile Iron-Spear Inner World (Early Inner Embryo World Realm — 25,000-year lifespan)!
  3. And Su Wan'er (120-Mile Zither-Star Inner World, while also advancing to Rank 7 Saint Inscription Grandmaster!), Murong Zi (120-Mile Clear-Wind Sword Inner World), and Chu Kuang (120-Mile Golden-Scale Saber Inner World) all stepped gloriously into the Early Inner Embryo World Realm!

Watching his father and mother laugh with joy as they conjured spirit-rain and blooming peach trees inside their own 100-Mile Inner Worlds, Lu Chen felt his heart overflow with warmth.
