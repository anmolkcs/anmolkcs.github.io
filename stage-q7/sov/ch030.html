Late on the night of the seventh day, inside Courtyard #72, Lu Chen sat cross-legged as the last golden glow of two inscribed Tendon-Perfection Pills settled into his limbs.

TWANG—!

All thirty-six major tendons across his spine, arms, and legs vibrated in a single unified chord, gleaming beneath his skin like coiled golden-bronze cables.

“Peak of the Fourth Stage—Altering Muscle & Tendons, with 6,800 jin of raw baseline strength!”

When amplified by the Sixth Wave of the Nine-Layered Wave Force and his inscribed spear, Lu Chen’s full-strength burst strike now reached an astonishing 9,200 jin—already matching an elite warrior at the Late Fifth Stage (Bone Forging)!

However, as Lu Chen inspected the Second Volume of the 《Chaotic Star Body Art — Gate Five: Golden-Jade Bone & Marrow Forging》, his brows furrowed into a serious knot.

“For an ordinary martial artist in the Seven Star Martial House, breaking through from the Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) to the Fifth Stage (Bone Forging) simply requires soaking in Rank 2 Bone-Hardening Broth to calcify the outer bone surface,” Lu Chen murmured. “Yet according to the Chaotic Star Body Art, calcifying only the outer bone cortex without tempering the Bone Marrow inside is a dead-end path that caps one’s lifetime physical strength at 6,000 jin!”

To cultivate true Golden-Jade Bone & Marrow Forging and awaken the legendary Ten Thousand Jin of Primordial Dragon-Elephant Strength, the practitioner had to simultaneously absorb two supreme treasures at the moment of breakthrough: 1. The fresh, still-warm Spinal Marrow & Heart-Blood of a Live Third-Order Spirit Beast (a beast equivalent to a human Houtian Realm master!) whose bones possessed natural thunder or metal tempering. 2. A stalk of Crimson-Gold Earth-Core Lotus growing near an iron-ore spirit vein.

Even Su Wan’er’s Grand Marshal Mansion didn’t have fresh Third-Order Thunder/Metal Beast Marrow sitting in storage—because Third-Order spirit beasts lost 70% of their marrow activity within two hours of death unless extracted and refined on the spot with a Blood-Locking Inscription!

Fortunately, earlier that afternoon when delivering two inscribed pills to the Listening Rain Pavilion, Su Wan’er had shared a piece of confidential intelligence from the City Garrison:

Five days ago, two hundred miles west of the Royal Capital in the wild Crimson Iron Mountain Range, a sudden subterranean lightning strike had exposed an ancient iron-vein fissure—and scouts had spotted a heavily injured Early Third-Order Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros retreating into the inner valleys to recuperate!

An injured Early Third-Order Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros—whose spinal marrow was tempered by natural lightning and black iron, and whose heart-blood essence would be more than enough to unlock the third facet of the Chrono-Soul Prism!

Before dawn the next morning, Lu Chen packed ten lethal Rank 1 Peak Exploding-Flame & Soul-Shock Inscription Talismans, strapped the 820-jin Black Iron Heavy Spear across his back, registered a ten-day mountain hunting leave at the Deacon Hall, and rode a swift post-horse out of the western gates toward the Crimson Iron Mountain Range.

Unbeknownst to him, as his horse cleared the western ridge, a black-feathered messenger hawk rose from a courtyard near Lin Sen’s residence, streaking toward a notorious mercenary stronghold at the edge of the Crimson Iron Mountains.
