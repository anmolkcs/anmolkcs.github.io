<p>As the corpse of <strong>Scarface Overseer Luo Shan</strong> vanished into the three-hundred-fathom abyss of Black-Slag Canyon and the two broken halves of his 220-jin iron mace clattered onto the bridge flagstones at Lu Chen's boots, the eighteen mounted pit-wardens at the far end of the bridge went pale as ghosts.</p>
<p>Both <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan (Early Tier Four)</strong> and <strong>Overseer Luo Shan (Peak Tier Four)</strong> lay dead, and standing beside sixty-year-old <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> at the head of the bridge was <strong>County Audit Clerk Fang</strong>, holding up the bronze seal of the County Treasury!</p>
<p>"Drop your weapons and dismount!" Clerk Fang shouted with all the authority of the law behind him. "Overseer Luo Shan and Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan have been executed on the spot for attempting to murder a County Audit Witness and blow up Drift Four! Any warden who drops his weapon now will be treated as a coerced subordinate when we report to the <strong>Grey-Wall Prefectural Censor</strong> at the Five-Mile Post-Station in one hour—any man who runs will be hunted down as a rebel accomplice!"</p>
<p><em>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Before Lu Chen even had to take another step, all eighteen terrified pit-wardens threw down their crossbows and iron clubs and dropped to their knees in the snow!</p>
<p>By noon the next day—the eighteenth of the twelfth month—after Clerk Fang, Lu Chen, Old Tie Shan, Tie Niu, and the forty freed miners presented the thirty barrels' blast evidence, the remaining <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystals</strong>, and <strong>Sub-Bailiff Zhao Kuan's signed blood-letter</strong> directly to the visiting <strong>Grey-Wall Prefectural Censor</strong> and the <strong>Black-Rock County Magistrate</strong>, the judgment came down like thunder: - All forty debt-miners of Drift Four—including <strong>Tie Niu</strong> and <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong>—were granted permanent <strong>Free Household Certificates</strong> and full pardon of all debts; - <strong>Garrison Captain Luo</strong> (Luo Shan's brother-in-law) was stripped of his rank and clapped in irons for conspiring with the Broken-Tooth Outlaws to sack Mud-Well Village; - And in recognition of exposing the two-thousand-tael marrow-crystal fraud and wiping out One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu's band, <strong>Ash-Brook Village</strong> and <strong>Mud-Well Village</strong> were granted a <strong>three-year full exemption from all iron and grain taxes</strong>, plus a hundred-tael silver reward to rebuild Mud-Well's houses and settle the thirty-nine landless freed miners along the fertile lower valley of Ash-Brook!</p>
<p>On the afternoon of the twentieth day of the twelfth month—the exact deadline day on which <strong>Tie Niu</strong> had been scheduled to drown in Drift Four—a long, joyous caravan of six Crag-Mule wagons loaded with winter grain, salt, cotton cloth, and fifty freed men wound up through <strong>Howling-Wind Pass</strong> and approached the log palisade of <strong>Ash-Brook Village</strong>.</p>
<p>Every pine tree outside the northern and southern gates had been hung with red paper lanterns by <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Shi Tou</strong>, <strong>Granny Liu</strong>, and the cheering villagers.</p>
<p>And standing right at the front of the open southern gate, holding her mother <strong>Widow Qin's</strong> hand—without her willow tapping-stick, and without the milky grey cataracts that had clouded her eyes for three winters—stood seven-year-old <strong>Xiao Ya</strong>!</p>
<p>After twelve days of washing her eyes with morning dew boiled with the <strong>Three-Leaf Clear-Eye Frost-Grass</strong> Lu Chen had bought in Black-Rock Town, the little girl's eyes were now as clear, dark, and bright as two wet river pebbles reflecting the red lantern-light!</p>
<p>As <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong> walked through the gate arm-in-arm with his tall, healed son <strong>Tie Niu</strong>, and Lu Chen stepped behind them with his <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> on his back, little Xiao Ya let go of her mother's hand, ran straight through the snow without stumbling once, and threw her small arms around Lu Chen's knees!</p>
<p>"Big Brother Lu!" Xiao Ya cried out, looking up with shining, clear dark eyes at the wooden <strong>Nine-Petaled Lotus Charm</strong> around her neck and then up at Lu Chen's calm, warm smile. "I can see the red lanterns! And I can see the red moss-cord on your wrist!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen reached down with his calloused <strong>Peak Tier Four</strong> hand—a hand that had just shattered a 220-jin iron mace two nights ago—and lifted the little girl gently onto his shoulder as the entire village of Ash-Brook and the forty new families cheered until the snow shook from the pines.</p>
