Returning triumphantly to the Seven Star Valley Sect on the tenth day—the exact morning of the eighteenth day of the tenth autumn month, Lu Chen's eighteenth birthday—the entire mountain range was decked in red lanterns and celebratory banners!

That evening, inside Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 on the sunny southern slope of Broken Spear Peak, no grand formal speeches were allowed.

Around a large round cedar table beneath the blooming golden osmanthus tree sat Lu Chen's family and dearest friends: his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan in the seats of honor, eighteen-year-old Lu Chen beside them, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, Eldest Senior Brother Fang Shi, Grand Marshal Su, and Elder Tie!

In the center of the table steamed a huge porcelain tureen of Meng Wan's handmade single-strand Golden-Wheat Longevity Noodles, alongside roasted Fifth-Order Star-Manta Fillet, braised bamboo shoots from Cloudstride City, and sweet Jade-Crystal Desert Dates.

"Two years ago on your sixteenth birthday at Clear-Brook Manor, you were just about to head to the Five-Kingdom Trial," Meng Wan smiled with happy tears glistening in her eyes as she ladled the long, unbroken longevity noodle into Lu Chen's bowl. "Today our Chen'er is eighteen—a full-grown man who can flatten Life-and-Death Wheel monsters with one swing of his spear, yet he still comes home on time to eat his mother's longevity noodles!"

"To our son's eighteenth birthday—and to our whole family staying together for a thousand years, ten thousand years!" Lu Shan raised his wine cup with a booming, joyful laugh as everyone around the table raised their cups under the warm autumn moon.
