Three thousand years ago, when the Seven Sovereign Clans had besieged the Primordial Chrono-Yin Clan to seize the Chrono-Soul Prism, the catastrophic clash of World Kings and Empyreans had shattered the mountain range into thousands of floating islands suspended inside chaotic Time-Distortion Eddies.

In some eddies, time flowed a hundred times faster, turning iron weapons into rusted dust in ten breaths; in others, time was frozen to a ten-thousandth of a second, trapping falling raindrops in midair for three millennia!

Even more infuriatingly, hovering outside the central Frozen Time-Loop Barrier at the heart of the ancestral ruins were three pitch-black Void-Stealth Warships bearing the emblem of a nine-headed shadow serpent—an excavation fleet belonging to the Shadow-Sovereign Clan (one of the Seven Sovereign Clans!), led by a Peak Inner Embryo World (900-mile Inner World) elder named Elder Ying Gui (Shadow-Ghost)!

"Lord Elder Ying Gui," a Late Inner Embryo World shadow-deacon reported on the deck of the lead warship, pointing at a thirty-foot Blood-Time Drill boring against the silver time-barrier below. "For three thousand years, the Chrono-Yin Clan's Subterranean Time-Vault has been locked inside a One-Second Time-Loop anchored by the Broken Seventh-Order Ancestral Chrono-Bell inside! However, after three millennia of erosion, the bell's twelve fractures are widening—within three days, our Blood-Time Drill will shatter the bell completely and allow us to loot the Chrono-Yin World-Heart Crystal and their ancestral soul-tablets!"

"Good!" Elder Ying Gui (Peak Inner Embryo World — 900-mile Inner World, 8.5 Billion Jin) laughed coldly. "Once we crush the Chrono-Yin Clan's ancestral soul-tablets and refine their World-Heart Crystal, even the last trace of that dead clan's karma will be wiped from the Upper Star-Soul Realm!"

High above in the silver aurora clouds, hearing Elder Ying Gui talk about crushing her ancestors' soul-tablets, Su Qingxue's aura turned colder than eternal abyssal ice—and beside her, Lu Chen gripped his 108,000-jin Epoch-Sovereign Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear.

"Senior Sister Wan'er, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, Master Tie—use the Star-Ark's Rank 7 Saint Cannons to lock down the three Shadow-Sovereign warships' escape routes; Qingxue and I will clean out those rats!"
