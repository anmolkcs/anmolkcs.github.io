<p>Yet Lu Chen did not pull his hand back after lighting the third sector.</p>

<p>Now, with a <strong>300-yard Law Domain</strong>, <strong>Rank 5 Peak Inscription Soul Force</strong>, and <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, the three-inch crust of <strong>Calcified Chrono-Silicate</strong> covering the sixty-foot sundial became a three-dimensional map of transparent crystal lattices in his mind!</p>
<p><em>Tink... Tink... Shhhk!</em></p>
