Over the next month on Broken Spear Peak, Lu Chen lived a fulfilling, disciplined life.

Every morning, he sat cross-legged on the wind-swept platform at the base of the three-hundred-foot Ancient Star-Spear Cliff.

Embedded in the cliff face was a broken section of an ancient Saint-Grade Heavy Spear left behind by the founding ancestor of Broken Spear Peak three thousand years ago; carved into the cliff wall beside it were three deep spear-gashes that still radiated a vast, undying Mountain-Crushing & Star-Falling Spear Intent.

Meditating beneath those three ancient spear-gashes in Prism Stillness while absorbing the pure liquid starlight inside the Starlight Essence Pool twice a week, Lu Chen’s comprehension soared day by day: - First, after drinking the remaining two-thirds of the Thousand-Year Star-Marrow Liquid, his Embryonic Mountain-Thunder Spear Intent shed its embryonic shell and condensed into True First-Stage Mountain-Thunder Spear Intent—capable of projecting a ten-yard gravitational and lightning pressure field that suppressed any opponent’s speed by 30%! - Second, he mastered the complete First and Second Chapters of the 《Wildfire Prairie Spear》 alongside Broken Spear Peak’s core Grade 3 Lower-Tier Martial Skill: 《Seven Star Falling-Meteor Spear》!
