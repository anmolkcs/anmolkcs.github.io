<p>In less than twenty breaths from the moment the granite block hit the fire, Lieutenant Iron-Back Gou and nine of the twelve courtyard outlaws lay dead or crippled in the blood-stained snow!</p>
<p>Then the heavy timber door of the stone blockhouse at the rear of the courtyard didn't open—it exploded outward in a shower of two-inch oak splinters!</p>
<p><em>BOOOOOOOM!</em></p>
<p>Out of the dark doorway strode a towering, wolfish man standing six feet five inches tall, wearing a suit of heavy, double-linked <strong>Grey-Wall Garrison Infantry Chainmail</strong> over a black bear-skin coat.</p>
<p>His left eye-socket was a sunken, livid crater crossed by three claw-scars from a desolate beast; his single right eye burned with the rabid, bloodthirsty yellow gleam of a cornered timber wolf. Despite his tall, rangy frame, his arms and legs were not bloated with bulk—instead, under his rolled-up chainmail sleeves, thirty-six thick, translucent gold-white <strong>Bowstring Sinews</strong> stood out under his dark bronze skin like drawn ballista cables!</p>
<p>Every time he took a single stride across the courtyard snow, a crisp, terrifying sound echoed from inside his boots, knees, spine, and wrists:</p>
<p><em>TWANG—TWANG—TWANG!</em></p>
<p>It sounded as though ten heavy horn-bows were being drawn and released inside his body with every step!</p>
<p>And resting across his right shoulder was a monstrous weapon that made even Scar-Six's twin butcher axes look like kitchen cleavers: a five-foot-long, four-inch-wide, <strong>Hundred-and-Ten-Jin Notched Ghost-Head Great-Saber</strong> forged from garrison military steel, its thick spine cast in the shape of a grinning iron demon that whistled mournfully in the blizzard wind!</p>
<p>This was the terror of the northern Black-Rock borderlands—a former corporal of the Grey-Wall Heavy Vanguard and chief of the Broken-Tooth Outlaws:</p>
<p><strong>Peak Tier Three of the Mortal-Shedding Stage (Bowstring-Sinew Perfection — 4,600 jin explosive force): One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu!</strong></p>
<p>Behind Han Wu charged his two personal <strong>Late Tier Two</strong> bodyguards holding tower shields, along with the last three surviving courtyard bandits.</p>
<p>Taking one look at the dead Iron-Back Gou, the breached ice-barricade, the twelve-foot iron-clad sledge-fortress blocking the northern pass, and the lone young man standing in the center of the courtyard with an eighty-jin black iron spear, Han Wu's single yellow eye contracted to a pinhole.</p>
<p>"So it was Ash-Brook Village that swallowed Ma Liu and my sworn brother Scar-Six!" Han Wu's voice rasped like iron scraping bone, loud enough to cut straight through the howling blizzard. "And a twenty-year-old brat who hasn't even finished drawing his thirty-six sinews dares climb into my fort?!"</p>
<p>Han Wu didn't waste time engaging Lu Chen in a slow probing duel—with a veteran corporal's ruthless tactical eye, he saw immediately that the four <strong>Triple-Prod Crossbows</strong> behind the village's sledge-fortress were the real support pinning down his remaining five men!</p>
<p>"Shield-guards—hold the brat for three breaths!" Han Wu roared, his thirty-six bowstring sinews snapping in unison as he launched himself like a catapulted boulder straight past the ruined barricade toward Ash-Brook's <strong>Sledge-Fortress</strong>, where <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>, and fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> were spanning their crossbows! "I'll chop their wooden sledges and that old one-armed smith into mincemeat first!"</p>
<p>"You aren't taking a single step past this line!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen's boots exploded against the frozen slate courtyard!</p>
<p>With thirty-three drawn bowstring sinews snapping from his heels to his shoulders, Lu Chen didn't even bother thrusting at the two Late Tier Two shield-guards who tried to block his path—he simply lowered his left shoulder and slammed straight into the overlapping junction of their two tower shields at a dead run with <strong>three thousand four hundred jin</strong> of combined muscle-and-sinew momentum!</p>
<p><em>CRASH—KRAK!</em></p>
<p>Both Late Tier Two bodyguards were blasted sideways off their feet like ragdolls, their shield-arms fracturing inside their sleeves, as Lu Chen cleared the twenty-yard gap in two bounds and intercepted <strong>One-Eyed Wolf Han Wu</strong> ten paces in front of the village sledge-barricade!</p>
