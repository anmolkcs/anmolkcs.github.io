That evening, after celebrating Elder Tie's breakthrough at the peak banquet, Lu Chen returned to Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 on the southern slope of Broken Spear Peak.

For Lu Chen, no personal achievement or sect glory mattered more than the health and longevity of his father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan.

Over the past year, nourished by the fourfold spirit-vein of Broken Spear Peak, the Silk-Unwinding Longevity Breath, the Rank 4 Meridian-Nourishing Vests, and the Halite-Marrow Longevity Pills, forty-one-year-old Lu Shan and thirty-nine-year-old Meng Wan had both reached 100% Twelve-Ringed Peak Houtian Perfection (Half-Step Xiantian)—and their meridian talent had been gently scoured and widened to Grade 5 Middle-Tier!

However, breaking through from the Houtian Realm into the Xiantian Realm required opening the Upper Dantian (Spiritual Sea), shedding mortal air for Innate Origin Breath, and surviving the Xiantian Heaven-and-Earth Baptism—a threshold where seven out of ten ordinary Peak Houtian cultivators failed.

"Father, Mother," Lu Chen said warmly as he set two carved jade boxes on the courtyard table after dinner. "Inside these boxes are two Rank 4 Rune-Inscribed Xiantian Spirit-Sea Lotuses refined from the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade, paired with two sets of thirty-six Rank 4 Lightning-Tempering Jade Plates. Starting tomorrow morning on the Warm-Spring Star Altar behind our courtyard, I will personally guard your meridians and guide both of you into the Xiantian Realm together!"

Lu Shan and Meng Wan exchanged a look of deep emotion.

"Five hundred years of Xiantian lifespan... walking on the clouds beside our son," Lu Shan took Meng Wan's hand, smiling warmly at Lu Chen. "With our Chen'er personally setting the formation for us, your mother and I aren't afraid of any heaven-and-earth baptism!"
