<p>With the <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> completed in his hands on the eleventh night, Lu Chen immediately plunged back into the center of the <strong>Boiling Earth-Marrow Sulfur Pool</strong> to begin the second—and most dangerous—stage of <strong>Tier Four (Slag-Bone Calcining)</strong>:</p>
<p><strong>Calcining the Axial Skeleton — The Twenty-Four Vertebrae of the Spine, the Sternum, and the Twenty-Four Ribs!</strong></p>
<p>In the first stage (Early Tier Four), hammering the long bones of the legs and hips had been relatively straightforward because the femur and tibia were thick, solid shafts far from the vital organs.</p>
<p>The <strong>spine and ribcage</strong>, however, directly encased the fragile mortal spinal cord, the heart, and the lungs! If a striker's sledge hit a rib or vertebra with five thousand jin of force before the bone was properly supported by internal breath-pressure and bowstring sinews, a single inward splinter would sever the spinal cord or puncture the heart on the spot.</p>
<p>That was why soldier Shi Potian's <strong>Postures Nine through Sixteen</strong> on the basalt wall did not use an external sledgehammer—instead, they used <strong>Ultra-Heavy Spear-Vibration &amp; Overhead Mountain-Bearing</strong>!</p>
<p>Standing chest-deep in the boiling sulfur-iron brine under eighteenfold gravity, Lu Chen placed the wrist-thick shaft of his <strong>160-Jin Cold-Marrow Heavy Spear</strong> horizontally across the back of his shoulders and seventh cervical vertebra, hung two <strong>three-hundred-pound baskets of dense Earth-Marrow Ore</strong> from the two ends of the spear shaft—creating a total downward crushing load of <strong>over thirteen thousand jin</strong> under the eighteenfold gravity—and began executing violent, short-range <strong>Spinal-Wave Dragon-Shakes</strong>!</p>
<p><em>CRACK-CRACK-CRACK-CRACK!</em></p>
<p>With every upward whip of his pelvis and thirty-six bowstring sinews against the thirteen-thousand-jin iron load across his shoulders, all twenty-four vertebrae of his spine slammed together and rebounded like twenty-four iron anvil-blocks, while the boiling <strong>Earth-Marrow Brine</strong> outside his skin forced liquid iron-and-lead ions straight through his intercostal muscles into his twenty-four ribs and breastbone!</p>
<p>On the twelfth night, shortly after the third watch, as Lu Chen was deep in the sixteenth posture with thirteen thousand jin on his back, a sudden, violent <strong>subterranean fault-tremor</strong> shook the bottom of Black-Slag Canyon!</p>
<p><em>RUMMMMMMMBLE—KRAK!!</em></p>
<p>Directly above the edge of the brine pool where <strong>Tie Niu</strong> and <strong>Old Mole Qian</strong> were tending the herb-pot, a massive <strong>three-ton slab of fractured black granite ceiling</strong>—weighing <strong>over fifty-four thousand jin</strong> under the cavern's eighteenfold gravity—sheared loose from the roof fault and plummeted straight down toward the two men's heads!</p>
<p>Neither Tie Niu nor sixty-year-old Old Mole Qian had time to dive clear of the ten-foot-wide falling rock slab!</p>
<p>"Get back!"</p>
<p>Inside the boiling pool, Lu Chen did not throw off the weighted spear—instead, kicking off the bottom of the pool with his <strong>Early Tier Four</strong> iron leg-bones so hard the basalt floor shattered, he lunged under the falling three-ton granite slab, planted his feet in a wide <strong>Iron-Bridge Stance</strong>, and raised his bare forearms, collarbones, ribcage, and arched spine directly beneath the falling stone!</p>
<p><em>BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM—CRUNCH!!</em></p>
<p>Three tons of granite under eighteenfold gravity slammed squarely onto Lu Chen's crossed forearms, shoulders, sternum, and arched spinal column!</p>
<p>A mouthful of dark red blood mixed with grey mortal bone-slag burst from Lu Chen's mouth as his feet sank eight inches deep into the solid basalt floor!</p>
<p>For five terrifying seconds, every single vertebra in his spine and every one of his twenty-four ribs groaned on the knife-edge of pulverization!</p>
<p>Yet inside that crushing fifty-four-thousand-jin press, the four nights of <strong>Earth-Marrow Brine</strong> and <strong>Cold-Iron Marrow Crystal</strong> essence saturated inside his spine and ribcage suddenly underwent instantaneous, high-pressure crystalline fusion!</p>
<p><em>DONG—DONG—DONG—DONG!</em></p>
<p>Twenty-four rapid, resonant metallic chimes rang out one after another from the base of his tailbone all the way up to his neck, followed by a deep bronze hum across all twenty-four ribs and his breastbone—transforming his entire torso and spinal column into an unbreakable cage of <strong>Black Slag-Iron Bone</strong>!</p>
<p><strong>Tier Four of the Mortal-Shedding Stage: Middle Slag-Bone Calcining (6,400 jin baseline force)!</strong></p>
<p>With a thunderous roar, Lu Chen straightened his newly forged iron spine and <strong>heaved</strong>—hurling the three-ton slab of granite sideways off his shoulders to crash harmlessly into the corner of the cavern!</p>
