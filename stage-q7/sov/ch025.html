Then, sitting cross-legged in the center of his meditation room, Lu Chen swallowed the first rune-inscribed Crimson-Gold Tendon Pill and drank three mouthfuls of the thick, mercury-heavy Earth Drake Marrow Blood.

BOOM—ROAR!

If the Scarlet Serpent Paste had felt like hot needles and the Flame Lion Blood had felt like a furnace, then the combination of a 260%-potency Crimson-Gold Tendon Pill and Earth Drake Marrow Blood felt like having molten meteoric iron poured directly along every major tendon from his heels to the base of his skull!

Even with his jaw clamped on a leather strap, muffled groans rumbled in Lu Chen’s throat as every muscle and tendon in his body spasmed violently.

Hold the stance—circulate the Second Volume of the 《Chaotic Star Body Art: Dragon-Sinew Tendon Forging》!

Inside his spiritual sea, the Chrono-Soul Prism radiated an icy, unwavering calm, keeping his mind crystal clear while the rest of his body burned like an anvil under a blacksmith’s hammer.

In the human body, muscles generated raw strength, while tendons connected muscles to bones and acted as the bowstrings that stored and unleashed explosive kinetic force.

Under the spiral compression of the Silk-Unwinding Star Breath, the golden medicinal essence of the Crimson-Gold Tendon Pill soaked deep into the twelve great tendons along Lu Chen’s spine (the Great Dragon Tendon) and the twenty-four tendons of his arms and legs. One by one, the pale-white mortal tendon fibers were torn apart at a cellular level and re-woven with the heavy earth-attribute vitality of the Iron-Scale Earth Drake into gleaming, golden-bronze strands tougher than braided dragon sinew!

At the same time, the furious tide of blood-qi scoured through his twelve primary meridians for the third time since Cloudstride City—washing away another thick layer of congenital blockages and expanding his meridian talent from borderline Grade 3 straight to Grade 3 Middle-Tier!

Three days and three nights passed in unbroken seclusion.

TWANG—TWANG—TWANG!

It sounded as if twelve giant dragon-sinew bowstrings had been drawn and released simultaneously inside his flesh!

Lu Chen’s eyes snapped open, and he punched forward into the empty air three feet in front of him without taking a step.

CRACK—BOOM!

The air in the meditation room was compressed so violently by the sheer speed of his fist that a visible white vacuum ring shot across the room and blasted a two-inch dent into the ironstone wall ten paces away!

“Early Fourth Stage of Body Transformation—Altering Muscle & Tendons, with 5,600 jin of raw baseline strength!”

An ordinary martial artist at the Early Fourth Stage possessed 3,500 to 4,000 jin of strength; even Zhu Hong at the Peak of the Fourth Stage had only reached 5,200 jin.
