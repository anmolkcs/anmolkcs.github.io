At midnight, when the cold mountain mist rolled thickest through Thunder-Scar Gorge, Ghost-Eye Gui Yan raised his hand.

Twang—Twang—Twang—Twang!

From forty paces away, the four Fifth-Stage mercenaries fired four wrist-thick iron harpoon bolts coated in black Blood-Rot Poison, while Gui Yan swung his bone staff, launching a green skull-shaped bolt of corrosive soul energy straight into the open lightning wound on the Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros’s left flank!

ROOOAAAR—!

The twelve-foot Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros erupted from its slumber with an earth-shaking roar!

Although the four harpoon bolts pierced three inches deep into its injured flank and flooded its bloodstream with Blood-Rot Poison, a Third-Order spirit beast’s life force was terrifying beyond imagination. With a furious sweep of its lightning-wreathed horn, the giant rhino snapped the heavy iron chains of the mercenaries’ trap like rotten twine and charged straight into the four mercenaries!

BOOM!

One Early Fifth Stage mercenary couldn’t dodge in time; the rhino’s shoulder clipped his iron shield, shattering the shield and blasting the man against the cliff wall as a pile of broken bones!

“Don’t panic! Its lung meridian is ruptured and the Blood-Rot Poison has reached its heart—it only has ten breaths of berserk strength left!” Gui Yan shouted excitedly, waving his bone staff as the remaining three Fifth-Stage mercenaries circled the bleeding, staggering giant beast, hacking at its knee joints with their Grade 2 broadsabers.

Ten breaths later, with black blood pouring from its mouth and seven saber wounds across its legs, the mighty Early Third-Order Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros let out an unwilling bellow and crashed to its knees three paces in front of the magnetic fissure, its silver lightning horn flickering dimly on the verge of extinction!

“Hahaha! A Third-Order spirit beast’s core and marrow, plus three Crimson-Gold Earth-Core Lotuses—all ours!” Gui Yan laughed triumphantly, stepping forward with a curved skinning dagger to pierce the rhino’s eye socket.

At that exact instant—

WHOOSH—WHOOSH—WHOOSH!

From the dark cliff ledge thirty feet above, a black shadow descended like a diving golden roc!

Before his boots even touched the ground, Lu Chen flicked his left hand, sending three blazing red streaks of light—three Rank 1 Peak Exploding-Flame Inscription Talismans—straight into the faces of the three exhausted Fifth-Stage mercenaries standing beside the rhino’s flank!

“Enemy attack—!”

KABOOM—KABOOM—KABOOM!

Three overlapping fireballs of compressed Rank 1 Peak flame energy detonated simultaneously within a five-foot radius! Caught completely off-guard with their stamina half-spent from fighting the rhino, two of the Middle Fifth Stage mercenaries were blown backward with scorched faces and shattered eardrums!

And in the center of the smoke, Lu Chen didn’t look at the mercenaries first—his boots flashed with the 《Golden Roc Nine-Flash Step》, carrying him straight past the startled Gui Yan to the head of the dying Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros!

Inhale—Silk-Unwinding Star Breath!

Gripping the 820-jin Hundred-Refined Black Iron Spear with both hands, Lu Chen poured all 6,800 jin of his Dragon-Sinew strength and the Sixth Wave of the Nine-Layered Wave Force into a single, meteoric downward thrust—

《Level Thrust — 9,200 Jin Burst!》

SHHHK—BOOM!

The dark-red spearhead plunged two feet deep through the unarmored gap beneath the dying Thunder-Horned Iron Rhinoceros’s jaw, piercing straight through its brain stem and heart cavity in a single stroke!

As the Third-Order behemoth shuddered its last breath, a torrent of boiling, lightning-infused Third-Order Core Heart-Blood Essence gushed up the blood groove of the black iron spear, straight across Lu Chen’s right palm and into the Chrono-Soul Prism inside his chest!
