As golden lanterns lit up beneath the ten-thousand-year spirit-peach and osmanthus trees of Warm-Spring Valley #1, a warm, boisterous family banquet—with zero stuffy imperial etiquette allowed—filled the courtyard with laughter and the mouth-watering aroma of home cooking!

In the kitchen pavilion, Empyrean Meng Wan—refusing to let any palace maids touch her stove—was happily lifting the lid off a giant steamer of crystal buns and ladling out a huge porcelain tureen of her handmade single-strand Golden-Wheat Longevity Noodles, while Empyrean Lu Shan and Master Liu (who had insisted on using his old thirty-two-jin Black-Iron Boning Knife, now upgraded by Lu Chen with a Sovereign Rune so it could slice Ninth-Order beast meat like tofu!) carved a roasted Eighth-Order Star- Carp & Golden Goose!

Around the long cedar table under the osmanthus trees sat everyone who had walked beside Lu Chen from the mortal dust to the peak of the heavens: - His father Lu Shan and mother Meng Wan in the seats of honor; - Nineteen-year-old Lu Chen sitting between his parents, with Su Wan'er and Su Qingxue beside him; - Elder Tie Cang (holding his wine bowl with both strong hands!), Master Liu, Grand Marshal Su, Grandmaster Gu, Appraiser Qin, and Master Mo Ban; - And Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, Eldest Senior Brother Fang Shi, and Senior Sister Lin Ying clinking wine jars together!

"Eat the whole longevity strand without breaking it, Chen'er!" Meng Wan beamed with happy tears in her eyes as she placed the steaming bowl of longevity noodles topped with two golden poached eggs in front of her son. "From fifteen years old in our little six-table tavern in Cloudstride City, to sixteen at Clear-Brook Manor, seventeen in the desert, eighteen on Broken Spear Peak, and nineteen today in Warm-Spring Valley—no matter if our son is a mortal apprentice or the Primordial Sovereign of the Universe, you never missed a single bowl of your mother's birthday noodles!"

"Mother's longevity noodles are the #1 treasure in all three thousand worlds," Lu Chen smiled warmly, eating a big mouthful of the warm, fragrant noodles and pouring cups of osmanthus wine for his father, his mother, Master Liu, and Elder Tie.
