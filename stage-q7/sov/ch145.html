Descending three hundred fathoms down the glacial crevasse, Lu Chen, Elder Tie, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang stepped into a breathtaking subterranean cathedral carved out of eternal blue diamond-ice and ancient bronze pillars:

The Sunken Bronze Dynasty Ice-Vault!

At the center of the vast ice-cathedral stood a hundred-foot Bronze Star-Astrolabe Platform.

Floating above the northern half of the platform was a fist-sized, nine-colored crystalline jade that radiated pure, soothing soul-light—the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade!

And frozen inside an ancient block of Ten-Thousand-Year Profound Ice on the southern half of the platform was the preserved heart-blood crystal and feather-plume of a fallen Peak Fifth-Order (Peak Revolving Core / Half-Step Life-and-Death) Spirit Beast: an Ancient Frost-Star Phoenix!

Surrounding the central Bronze Star-Astrolabe Platform, Blood-Moon Envoy Xue Cang, First Elder Mo, and Grand Elder Yan were using three Rank 4 blood-drills to bore through the platform's final Sunken Bronze Dynasty Rune Barrier!

Seeing Elder Tie walk into the ice-cathedral holding his 6,000-jin spear with both hands at full 480,000-jin Half-Step Revolving Core power alongside Lu Chen, all three enemy overlords gasped in horror:

"Tie Cang's severed right arm was completely regenerated?! How is that possible?!"

"Master Tie, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, Wan'er—hold First Elder Mo and Grand Elder Yan on the eastern bridge!" Lu Chen's voice rang like thunder across the ice-cathedral. "Leave Blood-Moon Envoy Xue Cang and the central platform to me!"
