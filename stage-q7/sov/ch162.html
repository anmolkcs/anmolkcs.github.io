Two hundred miles along the hidden black-glass highway, the four companions descended toward the only permanent settlement in the outer Mirage-Glass Desert: Refraction Oasis (Liu-Li Zhou).

Built inside the ribcage-like crater of a vitrified meteor impact around a deep subterranean cool-water well, Refraction Oasis was home to a reclusive clan of desert artisans and cultivators known as the Liu-Li Glass-Blower Clan. For centuries, the clan had survived the deadly Glass-Sand Storms by harvesting silicate sand and blowing dome-shaped Rank 3 & Rank 4 Refracted-Glass Shields over their mud-brick courtyards.

However, as Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang dismounted outside the oasis's eastern gate, they saw the main glass dome over the oasis cracked in three places, while a pack of twenty Fourth-Order Glass-armored Scorpions—driven berserk by black blood-centipede parasites burrowed into their carapaces—were battering the eastern gate!

Inside the gate, an elderly Peak Xiantian artisan—Clan Chief Liu-Li Yan—and his sixteen-year-old granddaughter Liu-Li Yao were bleeding from the mouth as they strained to hold up a melting glass barrier to protect three hundred mortal and Houtian townsfolk behind them!

"Those Fourth-Order scorpions have Blood-Eclipse Parasites in their tails!" Su Wan'er recognized the eerie crimson worms immediately. "The Blood-Eclipse Sect's vanguard passed through here and used parasite-beasts to wipe out the oasis so no one could report their tracks!"

"Chu Kuang, Murong Zi—take the left flank; leave the lead Peak Fourth-Order Scorpion King and the gate to me!"

BOOM—CHIME!

Lu Chen didn't even draw his 7,200-jin Primordial Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear from its back-mount. Stepping out of the sky, he stamped his right boot onto the black glass road ten paces in front of the crumbling oasis gate!

《Complete First-Tier Dao Concept: Acoustic-Space Resonance + Mountain-Gravity》!

DONG—!

A golden-purple bell wave rippled through the silicate bedrock at the exact resonant frequency of the Blood-Eclipse Parasites' fragile skulls, while tenfold mountain gravity slammed all twenty Fourth-Order scorpions flat into the glass sand!

POP-POP-POP-POP!

Without harming a single stone of the oasis gate, every single Blood-Eclipse Parasite inside the scorpions' carapaces burst into mist simultaneously, and the Peak Fourth-Order Scorpion King was knocked unconscious into a dune by a single flick of Lu Chen's sleeve!

As the terrified scorpion pack burrowed back into the sand and fled, Clan Chief Liu-Li Yan and the three hundred oasis residents stared in awe at the seventeen-year-old youth in black robes who had saved their entire clan with a single foot-stomp!
