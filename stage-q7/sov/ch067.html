When Lu Chen stood up inside the Ancient Star-Fall Cave and exhaled a single breath, a three-foot stream of condensed golden-jade Houtian True Essence shot from his lips and bored a thumb-sized hole straight through a foot of solid basalt rock!

“At the Early Houtian Realm, an ordinary martial artist possesses around 15,000 to 18,000 jin of True Essence strength; even a peerless prodigy like Yan Luo at Peak Early Houtian only reached 26,000 jin,” Lu Chen murmured, feeling the nine-ringed Golden-Jade Star Cyclone roaring like a miniature galaxy in his dantian. “Yet on the very first day of my Early Houtian Realm, my baseline physical and True Essence strength already reaches 26,000 jin—and when combined with my 58%-amplification Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear, the Ninth Wave of the Nine-Layered Wave Force, and Embryonic Mountain-Thunder Spear Intent, a single full-strength spear strike now unleashes 45,000 jin of destructive power!”

Forty-five thousand jin—already surpassing ordinary Late Houtian elders!

Stepping out of the cave on the sixth afternoon of the trial, Lu Chen encountered a rampaging Peak Third-Order Sky-Splitting Ape (a beast equivalent to a Late Houtian human master, worth 500 Trial Points!) that had driven ten disciples from the Crimson Sand and Iron-Peak Kingdoms into retreat.

Before the stunned eyes of twenty watching disciples from three kingdoms, Lu Chen didn’t even use a martial skill—he simplyinfused his Golden-Jade Houtian True Essence into his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear and delivered a single Level Thrust + Ninth Wave!

KABOOM—!

The twenty-foot-tall Peak Third-Order Sky-Splitting Ape was blasted thirty yards backward through three basalt pillars in a single thrust, collapsing unconscious as a golden 500-Point Star-Fallen Token flew into Lu Chen’s palm!
