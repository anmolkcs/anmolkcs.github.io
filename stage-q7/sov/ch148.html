Inside the quiet, timeless space of the Chrono-Soul Prism, seventeen-year-old Lu Chen stood before the blooming Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus and bowed respectfully to the snow-robed woman.

"Senior Su Qingxue," Lu Chen said with sincere gratitude. "Thank you for shielding my spiritual sea in Thunder-Scar Gorge and again today."

Looking at the upright, calloused seventeen-year-old youth who had climbed from a thirty-two-jin boning knife to the threshold of the Revolving Core Realm through his own sweat, Su Qingxue's frost-white lips curved into a faint, genuine smile as her clear voice echoed softly in the void:

"You don't need to thank me, Lu Chen. When I was surrounded by the Seven Sovereign Clans in the Upper Realm and chose to self-destruct rather than let my ancestor's Chrono-Soul Prism fall into their hands, I wasn't even able to unlock a single facet of this Prism myself."

She looked down at the nine facets of the Prism beneath her lotus, her eyes holding a trace of wonder:

"Only when the Prism activated autonomously at the instant of my self-destruction did I understand why: the Chrono-Soul Prism requires two halves of the Primordial Time-Soul Frequency to awaken. My ancestral bloodline carried the Yin-Preservation Half, which is why the Prism automatically preserved my soul inside this Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus while its laws ground the souls of all the enemies who perished around me into pure, identity-free Technique Memory Bubbles... and it tore through space-time to Earth because it sensed that your soul carried the Yang-Creation Half."

Lu Chen's heart shook slightly as the mystery that had lingered since Chapter 1 finally became clear!

"Absorbing a wisp of that Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade allowed me to wake for thirty breaths today," Su Qingxue said softly, the three opened petals of the Ice Lotus beginning to close gently as her figure grew translucent again. "Keep half of the Nine-Heaven Star-Soul Jade for your teacher's Revolving Core breakthrough—the wisp I absorbed is enough to sustain my slumber until you unlock all nine facets and open your Embryonic Inner World in the Upper Realms. Walk your path well with your parents and your spear, Lu Chen... When the ninth facet lights up, I will fight beside you."

As the Nine-Petaled Ice Lotus closed back into tranquil slumber, Lu Chen bowed deeply once more and opened his eyes in the Sunken Bronze Ice-Vault!
