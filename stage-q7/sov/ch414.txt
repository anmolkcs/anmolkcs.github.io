<p>Two nights before the fifteenth-day tax inspection, the early winter freeze bit down hard across the Black-Briar Barren Ridge.</p>
<p>The temperature plummeted so low that the ash-laden creek froze solid to its gravel bed with a series of loud, pistol-like cracks, and a thick, freezing fog rolled down from the upper forests, blanketing Ash-Brook Village in pitch darkness. When the deep freeze locked the mountains, the starving predators of the lower ridge—driven down from the high crags by stronger desolate beasts—always came testing the village palisade.</p>
<p>At midnight, Lu Chen was not asleep on his straw pallet.</p>
<p>Inside the warm circle of the banked smithy hearth, he was standing in a deep, wide <strong>Dragon-Coiling Horse Stance</strong>, holding a sixty-pound block of rejected slag-iron in each hand while a third eighty-pound block rested across his thighs. His bare upper torso glistened with a fresh coat of Granny Liu's black <strong>Blood-Briar Paste</strong>, and his breathing followed a slow, silent rhythm—drawing small sips of freezing air through his nose, filtering the abrasive <strong>Desolate Ash-Qi</strong> through his tightening pores, and driving the heat of his blood out to the tips of his fingers.</p>
<p>Suddenly, within the three-pace sphere of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen felt a tiny, rhythmic vibration travel through the frozen earth beneath his rawhide sandals—not the heavy, steady tread of the village night-watchman at the northern gate, but the light, padded, uneven scrape of claws landing inside the western mud wall, fifty yards down the lane toward Widow Qin's stone hut!</p>
<p>Almost at the same instant, a muffled, strangled yelp came from the western wall—the sound of old <strong>Watchman Ge's</strong> throat being torn open before he could blow his ox-horn!</p>
<p>Lu Chen dropped the three iron blocks onto the cinders, snatched the thirty-pound, six-foot <strong>iron pry-bar</strong> from beside the anvil—the closest thing he had to a spear—and burst out of the smithy shed into the freezing black fog.</p>
<p>"Beasts inside the west wall!" Lu Chen's deep voice cut across the silent village like an iron bell, deliberately pitching his diaphragm to carry through the dense fog toward Uncle Shi's hut. "Uncle Shi! Light the pitch-torches!"</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not wait for the hunters to wake. Sprinting down the frozen mud ruts under the tenfold gravity—each stride driving his heels three inches into the hard crust—he rounded the corner of the grain shed and saw three gaunt, hunched shadows tearing at the plank door of Widow Qin's low stone hut!</p>
<p>They were <strong>Iron-Jaw Ridge Hyenas</strong>—pack scavengers of the Great Desolate borderlands. Though not true Desolate Pulse spirit beasts, a mature adult Ridge Hyena possessed bone-crushing jaws and tough, mangy hide equivalent to a <strong>Tier One Mortal-Shedding</strong> warrior, weighing nearly three hundred pounds under the tenfold gravity and carrying septic corpse-poison on its fangs. Drawn by the scent of the dried sheep's wool and roasted acorns inside the widow's hut, the three starving beasts had used a snowdrift against the western palisade to leap the briar-topped wall!</p>
<p><em>CRACK!</em></p>
<p>With a vicious lunge of its sloped shoulders, the largest of the three hyenas—a scarred alpha female missing half an ear—splintered the wooden latch of Widow Qin's door! Inside the dark hut, Widow Qin threw herself silently in front of little blind <strong>Xiao Ya</strong> with a short iron turnip-hoe in her trembling hands, her mute throat letting out a desperate, soundless gasp as the beast's yellow eyes gleamed in the doorway.</p>
<p><em>WHOOOOOOSH—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Thirty-pound iron pry-bar gripped in both hands like a northern heavy spear, Lu Chen arrived at a dead run!</p>
<p>He had no fancy martial essence, no lightning arcs, and his current physical strength—still a hair short of Early Tier One—was barely equal to a single adult hyena. If he allowed all three beasts to surround him in the open lane, their jaws would snap his shins in five breaths.</p>
<p>So he used the narrow stone doorway itself as his shield.</p>
<p>Launching himself into a low, sliding <strong>Level Thrust</strong> over the icy mud, Lu Chen drove the chisel-tip of the thirty-pound pry-bar straight into the narrow gap between the alpha female's shoulder blade and neck-vertebrae just as her jaws snapped an inch from Widow Qin's face!</p>
<p><em>SHHHHK—KRAK!</em></p>
<p>With the combined momentum of his running sprint and all six harmonies of his body locked behind the iron bar, the flat chisel-point punched four inches deep through the hyena's matted hide, wedging violently against its cervical spine and levering the three-hundred-pound beast sideways into the stone doorjamb!</p>
