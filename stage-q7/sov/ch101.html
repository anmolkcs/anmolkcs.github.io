Early autumn mist drifted through the spirit-peach orchards of Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 on the southern slope of Broken Spear Peak.

Beside the steaming jade-stone spring pool, forty-one-year-old Lu Shan stood in a low horse stance, his bare forearms gleaming with a faint bronze-iron luster as he thrust a four-hundred-jin refined iron practice spear forward ten times in rapid succession. Each thrust condensed a crisp, three-foot cone of liquid Early Houtian True Essence that snapped the morning mist apart without shaking a single peach blossom from the overhanging branches.

Under the grape trellis twenty paces away, Meng Wan—whose hair was pinned with a warm star-jade hairpin Lu Chen had carved for her—was lifting the lid off a double-layered purple-clay steamer. Inside sat twenty plump crystal buns stuffed with minced Third-Order Snow-Carp Belly and diced Five-Hundred-Year Spirit Bamboo Shoots, accompanied by a pot of golden osmanthus-lotus tea.

"Stop showing off that four-hundred-jin iron rod of yours, Old Lu," Meng Wan called out with a laugh as she saw a familiar tall figure in a clean black martial robe walking down the stone steps from the upper cliff. "Our Chen'er just finished his fifteen-day seclusion on the summit—come wash your hands before the crystal buns cool!"

"Coming!" Lu Shan grinned, planting his practice spear into the rack and wiping his brow with a towel as Lu Chen stepped through the courtyard gate.

Over the past fifteen days atop Broken Spear Peak, after digesting the Five-Thousand-Year Xiantian Spirit-Sea Lotus he had won at the Seven-Sect Martial Conference, sixteen-year-old Lu Chen's aura had undergone a subtle inward retraction.

Previously, at the Late Houtian Realm, standing near him had felt like standing beside a rumbling thunderstorm. Now, with all twelve rings of his Golden-Jade Star Cyclone perfected at 100% Peak Houtian (Half-Step Xiantian) and a hairline golden slit opened across the barrier of his Upper Dantian (Xiantian Spiritual Sea), he looked as calm and ordinary as a young scholar—except that when the mountain breeze blew past his shoulders, the wind parted naturally three inches around his cloth robe without wrinkling a single thread.

"Father, your seventh spear thrust just now connected the waist-tendon torque much more smoothly than last month," Lu Chen smiled warmly, taking the heavy clay teapot from his mother's hands to pour three cups. "Within half a year, your Early Houtian Cyclone will be solid enough to advance to the Middle Houtian Realm."

"Half a year? Your mother is already competing with me every morning to see who reaches Middle Houtian first!" Lu Shan laughed heartily, biting into a steaming crystal bun while Meng Wan piled three of the largest buns into Lu Chen's bowl.

"Eat slowly, Chen'er," Meng Wan said fondly, looking closely at the faint golden hairline mark between her son's eyebrows. "Yesterday Su Wan'er came by to bring me two baskets of spirit-berries from Pill-Star Peak, and she mentioned that opening the Xiantian Spiritual Sea is the most dangerous threshold for a martial artist below the Revolving Core Realm. Are you sure you have enough spirit-protecting pills?"

"Don't worry, Mother," Lu Chen said gently, patting the back of her hand. "Because I cultivate both the physical body and runic inscriptions, my spiritual sea is already twice as tough as an ordinary Peak Houtian cultivator's. I won't force the final breakthrough until the heaven-and-earth resonance is completely ripe."

Just as Lu Chen finished his second cup of osmanthus tea, a deep, metallic tremor—so low in frequency that ordinary Houtian disciples barely felt it, yet resonant enough to make the nine-foot, 3,600-jin Star-Shattering Heavy Spear inside Lu Chen's spatial ring hum aloud—rippled up through the bedrock of the seven mountain peaks!

BONG—!

A second later, an ancient, muffled chime echoed from ten thousand feet beneath the northern valley of the Seven Star Valley Sect, followed immediately by the fiery streak of Elder Tie Cang's communication talisman landing in front of Warm-Spring Courtyard #1.
