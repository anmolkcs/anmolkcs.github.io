<p>In the freezing wind atop the Black-Slag Canyon bridge, the duel between Lu Chen and Scarface Luo Shan surpassed anything the borderlands of Black-Rock County had seen in a decade.</p>
<p>There were no flashy elemental projections or flying illusions—only two iron-boned titans colliding bone-against-bone, steel-against-steel, with over <strong>fifteen thousand jin</strong> of combined physical force in every single exchange!</p>
<p><em>BOOM—CLANG!</em> <em>BOOM—CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Ten exchanges! Twenty-five exchanges! Forty exchanges!</p>
<p>At the head of the bridge, <strong>Old Tie Shan</strong>, <strong>Tie Niu</strong>, and the forty miners watched with white knuckles as the thirty-foot basalt bridge literally began to crumble along its edges under the footprints of the two combatants!</p>
<p>With a 220-jin mace against a 160-jin spear, and twenty years of <strong>Peak Tier Four</strong> bone consolidation against fourteen days of <strong>Late Tier Four</strong>, Scarface Luo Shan held the upper hand in raw blunt mass across the first forty exchanges.</p>
<p>Every time the eight jagged flanges of the 220-jin mace crashed against the octagonal shaft of Lu Chen's Cold-Marrow Spear, the six-hundred-jin force differential and the sixty-jin weapon weight gap drove a bone-rattling percussive shockwave straight through Lu Chen's hands, wrists, radius and ulna bones, collarbones, spine, and femurs!</p>
<p>By the thirty-fifth exchange, the skin across Lu Chen's knuckles had split open, painting the frost-blue shaft of his spear and the <strong>rusted three-inch Sovereign collar</strong> bright crimson.</p>
<p>By the forty-fifth exchange, fine hairline micro-fractures had begun to spiderweb across the <strong>innermost ten percent of uncalcined marrow-wall</strong> inside Lu Chen's forearm bones and shin-bones, and a thin thread of red blood leaked from both of his ears from the concussive thunder of the impacts!</p>
<p>"Crack! Crack for me, you stubborn little mule!"</p>
<p>Seeing blood seep from Lu Chen's ears and knuckles on the forty-eighth exchange, Scarface Luo Shan roared with ferocious triumph—even though his own palms were numb and bleeding from hitting the unyielding <strong>Cold-Marrow Spear</strong>, and his lungs were heaving like torn bellows after swinging a 220-jin mace forty-eight times at maximum output!</p>
<p>Yet what Scarface Luo Shan could not see was the interior of Lu Chen's sea of consciousness and the deep marrow of his 206 bones!</p>
<p>Within the absolute, diamond-clear tranquility of <strong>Prism Stillness</strong>, Lu Chen was guiding every single six-hundred-jin excess shockwave from Luo Shan's 220-jin mace like a master smith guiding a trip-hammer across the final uncompacted core of a sword blade!</p>
<p>Inside his bone marrow, the rich, dormant medicinal essence of the <strong>thirty Heart-of-Marrow Crystals</strong> and the forty pounds of <strong>Earth-Marrow Calcining Salts</strong> that had soaked into his medullary cavities down in the eighteenfold-gravity pool was being hammered by Luo Shan's mace straight into those tiny micro-fractures along his inner marrow-walls!</p>
<p><em>Exchange forty-nine: the inner marrow-walls of all sixty-two lower-limb bones fused shut into solid cold-iron!</em> <em>Exchange fifty-two: the inner marrow-walls of all twenty-four vertebrae and twenty-four ribs fused shut!</em> <em>Exchange fifty-six: the inner marrow-walls of both collarbones, shoulder blades, and upper arms fused shut!</em></p>
<p>And with each bone that completed its <strong>100% inner-to-outer calcination</strong>, the sound coming from Lu Chen's skeleton when the mace struck changed—shifting from a dull iron <em>clank</em> to a higher, purer, crystalline <strong>bronze-bell chime</strong>, while his counter-thrusts grew heavier and faster instead of weaker!</p>
