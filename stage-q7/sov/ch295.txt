<p>On the morning before the <em>Primordial Star-Ark's</em> departure, over <strong>one hundred thousand cultivators</strong> from every sect, clan, and kingdom of the <strong>Sky Spill Continent</strong>—from <strong>Emperor Jun Tian</strong> and <strong>Spear-Sovereign Cang Feng</strong> of the Central Imperial Domain, to <strong>Palace Master Qin</strong>, <strong>Grand Marshal Su</strong>, and <strong>Master Liu</strong> (whom Lu Chen had invited via the Sub-Space Portal from Cloudstride City for the feast!)—gathered across the ten-mile sky-plaza of <strong>Star-Fall Peak</strong> to toast <strong>World-Sovereign Lu Chen</strong> and his family!</p>
<p>Around the main family table in <strong>Warm-Spring Courtyard</strong>, <strong>Master Liu</strong>—now a robust <strong>Revolving Core</strong> elder thanks to the pills Lu Chen had sent back over the years—clinked his wine bowl with <strong>Lu Shan</strong> and laughed until his gray beard shook:</p>
<p>"Three years ago in the third kitchen yard of the Crimson Scale Pavilion, when Little Chen picked up that thirty-two-jin black-iron boning knife and stripped a Black-Spined Boar without nicking the bone membrane, this old butcher thought he'd make a fine head chef one day! Who knew that thirty-two-jin boning knife would turn into a <strong>108,000-jin World-Splitting Spear</strong>, and that he'd carry all of us up into the stars?!"</p>
<p>Everyone around the table burst into warm, hearty laughter as Lu Chen personally poured Master Liu and Grand Marshal Su a cup of <strong>Seventh-Order Immortal Peach Wine</strong>.</p>
