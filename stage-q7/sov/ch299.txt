<p>On the tenth day of cruising along the <strong>Amber-Fossil Star Road</strong>, the <em>Primordial Star-Ark</em> arrived at the halfway checkpoint before the <strong>Outer Star-Continent of the Upper Star-Soul Realm</strong>:</p>
<p>A colossal, hundred-mile-tall <strong>Pre-Epoch Star-Transfer Gate</strong> carved out of a single hollowed-out <strong>Amber Star-Meteorite</strong>!</p>
<p>However, blocking the hundred-mile <strong>Amber Star-Gate</strong> were <strong>five black-iron Void-Cruisers</strong> flying the skull-and-nebula banner of the <strong>Astral Void-Reavers</strong>—a ruthless fleet of Upper-Realm star-pirates led by a ferocious <strong>Middle Inner Embryo World Overlord (having a 250-Mile Inner World): Reaver-Lord Kui</strong> (<strong>3.2 Billion Jin</strong> of World Force!)—who were extorting a group of stranded <strong>Artisan-Star Clan</strong> merchant ships!</p>
<p>Seeing a brand-new, two-mile-long <strong>Grade 6 Golden-Purple Star-Ark</strong> emerge from the lower-realm tributary road, <strong>Reaver-Lord Kui's</strong> greedy eyes lit up:</p>
<p>"A Grade 6 Middle-Tier Star-Ark coming out of a remote lower-world branch road?! Surround them—kill the crew and strip their ship's core!"</p>
