Atop the highest star-platform of Star-Fall Peak, ten thousand feet above the cloud-sea of the Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect, the night sky was a river of blazing silver constellations.

Sitting cross-legged at the center of the platform, eighteen-year-old Lu Chen rested his 54,000-jin Epoch-Sovereign Dragon-Bell Heavy Spear across his knees.

Surrounding him in a nine-ringed array were five hundred High-Grade True Essence Stones (equivalent to five million Low-Grade True Essence Stones!), the fist-sized Peak Sixth-Order Chrono-Centipede Core, and thirty-six jars of Ten-Thousand-Year Liquid Astral-Sea Marrow.

Down on the lower terrace of Warm-Spring Immortal Manor, his parents Lu Shan and Meng Wan—glowing with their newly ascended Life-and-Death Wheel vitality—stood beside Su Qingxue, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, Elder Tie, and Sect Master Ling Xiao, watching the summit in quiet anticipation.

"In the Sky Spill Continent, stepping from the Sixth Death-Tribulation Revolution into the Divine Sea Realm is the threshold between mortal overlords and true continental ancestors," Sect Master Ling Xiao murmured in awe as he sensed the terrifying accumulation inside Lu Chen's body. "When an ordinary Six-Revolution peak master opens their Divine Sea, they expand their lower dantian into a 10-to-30-mile sea of liquid law power; even in the history of our Heaven-Splitting Spear Sect, our founding ancestor only opened a 120-mile Divine Sea!"

Beside Sect Master Ling Xiao, Su Qingxue—whose snow-white robes fluttered in the mountain breeze—smiled faintly as her starry eyes looked up at Lu Chen:

"Even in the Upper Star-Soul Realm, a genius who opens a 300-mile Divine Sea is hailed as a future World King; only those who cultivate a supreme primordial body-and-star manual through all six Life-and-Death Revolutions without a single flaw can challenge the legendary Three-Thousand-Mile Primordial Divine Sea!"

Atop the summit platform, Lu Chen closed his eyes and sank his consciousness into Prism Stillness.

Circulating the Chaotic Star Divine Sea & Void-Bridge Art — Volume Six from the Thirteenth & Fourteenth Technique Bubbles, he did not gently melt his six Life-and-Death Wheels into water as ordinary cultivators did.

Instead, he roared low in his chest and slammed all six hundred-foot Golden-Purple Wheels of Life-and-Death inward into a single microscopic point at the center of his lower dantian—creating an internal Gravitational-Time Singularity!

CRACK—BOOM—HUMMMMMMM!

At the exact instant the six immortal wheels collapsed into that microscopic point, all five hundred High-Grade True Essence Stones and the Peak Sixth-Order Chrono-Centipede Core around him disintegrated into a roaring golden-violet cyclone that poured through every pore of his Six-Revolution Immortal Star-Dharma Body!
