On the thirtieth morning, the heavy bronze gates of the Profound Gravity & Earth-Fire Cave rumbled open.

When Lu Chen, Murong Zi, and Su Wan’er stepped out into the morning sunlight of Seven Star Mountain, Palace Master Qin waiting outside couldn’t help but let out a joyful laugh:

During the thirty days inside the cave, Murong Zi had successfully condensed his dantian cyclone to step officially into the Early Houtian Realm; Su Wan’er had reached 100% Peak Sixth Stage (Half-Step Houtian); and standing between them, Lu Chen’s aura was as deep and unfathomable as an abyss—having jumped from Half-Step Sixth Stage straight to 100% Twelve-Meridian Pulse Condensation (Half-Step Houtian) in a single month!

Even better, today happened to be the eighteenth day of the tenth autumn month—Lu Chen’s sixteenth birthday!

Politely declining the banquet at the Core Abode Hall so he could spend his birthday evening with his parents—and inviting Su Wan’er and Murong Zi to join as guests—Lu Chen walked down the mountain path to Clear-Brook Manor.

Long before they reached the courtyard gate, the mouth-watering aroma of roasted spirit-lamb, braised bamboo shoots, and handmade longevity noodles drifted over the garden wall.

Inside the courtyard, Lu Shan—who had broken through smoothly into the Early Fourth Stage (Altering Muscle & Tendons) three days ago, his arms brimming with 4,200 jin of strength—was effortlessly splitting five-hundred-pound ironwood logs for the kitchen hearth with a single hand-chop, while Meng Wan (now a Middle Third Stage cultivator) moved around the kitchen with brisk, tireless grace!

“Father! Mother! I brought Senior Sister Su and Senior Brother Murong for dinner!”

“Welcome, Miss Su, Young Master Murong—come sit in the main pavilion!” Meng Wan beamed with warmth, bringing out a large porcelain tureen of single-strand Spirit-Wheat Longevity Noodles topped with two golden poached spirit-pheasant eggs and placing it right in front of Lu Chen. “Eat every bit of the longevity strand without breaking it, Chen’er—sixteen years old today, safe and sound!”

Even the usually stoic sword-genius Murong Zi felt his expression soften in the warm, unpretentious atmosphere of the Lu family dinner table, while Su Wan’er chatted happily with Meng Wan about cooking medicinal soups, feeling a familial warmth she rarely experienced in the strict Grand Marshal’s Mansion.

Raising his cup of warm osmanthus wine toward his parents, Lu Chen smiled from the bottom of his heart.

This—protecting the quiet laughter around this table—was why he held his heavy spear.
