<p>Landing knee-deep in the warm grey mud five paces from the bleeding titan, Lu Chen rolled his shoulders to absorb the tenfold-gravity impact just as the <strong>Early Tier Two</strong> poacher leader <strong>Ma Liu</strong> recovered from his shock!</p>
<p>"A village rat from Ash-Brook?!" Ma Liu snarled, his small eyes darting from the eighty-jin black-iron spear in Lu Chen's hands to Uncle Shi up on the basalt ledge. "You dare steal meat from the <strong>Broken-Tooth Camp</strong>?! Die!"</p>
<p>Knowing that Uncle Shi was reloading his heavy horn-bow on the cliff above, Ma Liu didn't retreat—he lunged straight into close quarters with Lu Chen so the archer above couldn't fire without risking hitting Lu Chen!</p>
<p>With every muscle in his stocky frame compacted to <strong>Early Tier Two (Iron-Flesh Compacting)</strong>—granting him nearly <strong>one thousand nine hundred jin</strong> of explosive power, seven hundred jin heavier than Lu Chen's current <strong>Complete Tier One</strong> baseline—Ma Liu swung his sixty-jin <strong>Ring-Pommel Executioner's Saber</strong> in a brutal diagonal chop aimed at splitting Lu Chen's collarbone!</p>
<p><em>WHOOOOOOSH—!</em></p>
<p>The heavy saber blade, notched with nine iron rings along its spine that clanged like funeral bells, carried a terrifying wind-pressure that flattened the mud ripples beneath it!</p>
<p>A normal Complete Tier One hunter who tried to block a nineteen-hundred-jin saber chop head-on would have his wrists snapped and his spear knocked into the mud.</p>
<p>Lu Chen did not block head-on.</p>
<p>Sinking his boots down to the solid gravel layer beneath the knee-deep mud, he let <strong>Prism Stillness</strong> lock onto the exact center of percussion of Ma Liu's falling saber. At the last possible tenth of a second, Lu Chen rotated his eighty-jin solid iron spear shaft thirty degrees off-center—executing <strong>Spiral-Coiling Deflection</strong>!</p>
<p><em>CLANG—SCREEEEECH!</em></p>
<p>The notched edge of the sixty-jin executioner's saber struck the twisted octagonal ridges of Lu Chen's Cold-Slag spear shaft at a glancing angle. Sparks exploded in the sulfur mist! Instead of taking all nineteen hundred jin of force on his wrists, Lu Chen's spiral deflection caused the heavy saber blade to skitter violently down the oiled iron shaft and bury itself two feet deep into the mud beside Lu Chen's left boot!</p>
<p>Even that glancing deflection sent a bone-rattling shockwave up Lu Chen's arms. Beneath his matte-bronze <strong>Tier One</strong> skin, the untempered muscle fibers of his biceps and pectorals tore in dozens of microscopic places under the seven-hundred-jin strength gap, and a warm thread of blood trickled from his right nostril!</p>
<p>Yet before Ma Liu could yank his heavy saber out of the sucking clay, Lu Chen stepped <em>inside</em> the bandit's guard and snapped the rusted three-inch collar of his spear shaft straight into Ma Liu's ribs with a short <strong>Inch-Pulse Body-Check</strong>!</p>
<p><em>THUD—KRAK!</em></p>
<p>Caught completely off-balance with his saber stuck in the mud, Ma Liu was blasted four paces backward onto his rump, coughing a mouthful of sour bile as two of his ribs cracked despite his Tier-Two compacted flesh!</p>
<p>Before Lu Chen could follow up and finish the bandit leader, the ground behind him shook like an earthquake!</p>
<p><em>ROOOOOOOOAAAAR!</em></p>
<p>The fourteen-hundred-pound <strong>Scarred King-Boar</strong>—freed of the paralyzing neurotoxin because Lu Chen had bled its neck vein, yet crazed with pain from its gushing wound—charged straight at Lu Chen's back with its fourteen-inch ivory tusks leveled at his spine!</p>
