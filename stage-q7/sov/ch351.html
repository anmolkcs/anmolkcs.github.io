Three days after the grand banquet at the Eternal Chrono-Lotus Citadel, the two-mile-long Grade 6 Peak Saint-Vessel—the Primordial Star-Ark—cruised northward across the starry void at the northernmost edge of the Outer Star-Continent.

Behind the Primordial Star-Ark, the newly liberated Southern-Western Star Prefecture was thriving under the joint protection of the 360-Moon Chrono-Yin Grand Array and the six 1,000-Mile Peak Inner Embryo World Sovereigns who remained at the citadel to consolidate their realms and guard the Lu family's home base: Lu Chen's father Lu Shan, his mother Meng Wan, his master Elder Tie Cang, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang.

Before departing, Lu Chen had left his parents and companions half of the Seventh-Order Spirit-Herb Gardens recovered from the Three Vice-Patriarchs' spatial rings, along with a Rank 7 Peak Two-Way World-Bridge Beacon linked directly to his own 30,000-Mile Golden-Jade Minor World, allowing him to step back into Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 at a moment's notice from anywhere in the Upper Star-Soul Realm.

Now, standing at the prow of the Primordial Star-Ark alongside Su Qingxue (whose cultivation also stood at the Peak of the Lesser World King Realm with her 30,000-Mile Chrono-Lotus Minor World), eighteen-year-old Lu Chen gazed ahead as the silver-blue star-mist of the Outer Star-Continent abruptly ended before a colossal, ten-million-mile wall of roaring nine-colored primordial fire and tectonic chaos:

The Primordial Origin Kiln!

Serving as the natural cosmic barrier between the Outer Star-Continent and the Central Star-Soul Imperial Sea (the true heartland of the Upper Star-Soul Realm, ruled by the Seven Sovereign Clans), the Primordial Origin Kiln was a primordial nebula where unfinished stars and world-seeds were smelted in nine-colored Origin-World Fire.

Even a Lesser World King who strayed off the ancient Creation Stone Causeway into the deeper currents of the Origin-World Fire would find their 10,000-to-30,000-mile Minor World melted back into chaotic stardust within half an hour!

Yet for any Peak Lesser World King seeking to break through into the Great World King Realm (100,000-year lifespan, expanding one's Minor World from 30,000 miles to a 100,000-Mile Great World!), the Primordial Origin Kiln was the supreme sacred ground in the universe.

Why? Because at the center of the Primordial Origin Kiln stood the Pre-Epoch Creation Tablet—an Eighth-Order (Great World King Peak / Half-Step Empyrean) relic from the birth of the Upper Star-Soul Realm, beneath which pooled the Nine-Colored Origin-World Marrow needed to ignite a Minor World's Continental Tectonic Core and expand it past the 100,000-mile Great World threshold!

"Three thousand years ago, when I reached the Peak of the Lesser World King Realm, I came to this Primordial Origin Kiln with my father to attempt the Pre-Epoch Creation Tablet's resonance test," Su Qingxue murmured softly beside Lu Chen, her snow-white robes fluttering in the hot astral gale radiating from the nine-colored fire wall ahead. "Back then, because 360,000 microscopic Primordial Entropy-Fractures had eroded the Creation Tablet over ten epochs, not even the elders of the Seven Sovereign Clans could unlock more than thirty percent of the tablet's Nine-Colored Origin-World Marrow."

Su Qingxue turned her clear, starry eyes toward Lu Chen, a faint, warm smile touching her lips:

"Wait until that ancient Creation Tablet meets an antiquities restorer from Earth who carries the Chrono-Soul Prism."
