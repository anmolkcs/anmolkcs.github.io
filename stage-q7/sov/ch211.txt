<p>Two thousand miles east of the Nine-Spear Mountain Range, the rugged granite peaks of the western prefecture gave way to rolling hills of snow-white <strong>Ten-Thousand-Year Kaolin Spirit-Clay</strong> and cobalt-blue mineral rivers.</p>
<p>At the convergence of nine subterranean fire-dragon veins stood a city unlike any other on the Sky Spill Continent:</p>
<p><strong>The Imperial Artisan Capital of Jingde-Star!</strong></p>
<p>Whereas Nine-Kiln Crucible City in the southern borderlands had been a regional town of iron-and-celadon smiths, Jingde-Star was a sprawling, hundred-mile metropolis built entirely from <strong>Grade 5 Vitrified Star-Porcelain, Imperial Jade-Glaze Bricks, and Meteoric Bronze</strong>. Towering three hundred feet above the center of the city like a sleeping azure dragon coiled around a mountain ridge was the legendary <strong>Pre-Epoch Imperial Celadon Dragon-Kiln</strong>—a mile-long, stepped tunnel-kiln left behind ten thousand years ago during the golden age of the <strong>Sunken Bronze &amp; Porcelain Dynasty</strong>!</p>
<p>As eighteen-year-old <strong>Lu Chen</strong>, <strong>Su Wan'er</strong>, <strong>Murong Zi</strong>, and <strong>Chu Kuang</strong> walked down the Cobalt-Glaze Avenue of Jingde-Star, the street pavements beneath their boots rang with a clear, musical chime like struck jade chimes with every step.</p>
<p>"Look at the porcelain pagodas along this avenue," <strong>Su Wan'er</strong> murmured in admiration, her <strong>Rank 4 Peak Inscription</strong> eyes tracing the shimmering blue-and-white underglaze rune patterns baked into the city walls. "Every single brick was painted with <strong>Cobalt-Star Ink</strong> before being fired at thirty thousand degrees, sealing the runes permanently beneath a millimeter of transparent vitrified glaze so that even five thousand years of wind and rain haven't faded their defensive power by one percent!"</p>
<p>"On Earth, we called that <em>underglaze blue-and-white</em> and <em>sky-clearing celadon</em>," Lu Chen smiled warmly, his fingers brushing an ancient porcelain stele by the bridge as a wave of fond familiarity washed through his antiquities-restorer's heart. "Metal rusts and stone weathers over ten thousand years, but once kaolin clay and mineral glaze undergo complete vitrification in a reduction kiln, their silicate crystal lattice becomes practically eternal—unless shattered by internal thermal shock."</p>
<p>Just as they reached the grand square in front of the <strong>Imperial Kiln Guild Hall</strong>, however, they saw a crowd of several hundred <strong>Life-and-Death Wheel</strong> and <strong>Revolving Core</strong> master artisans gathered outside a cordoned-off jade pavilion, their faces pale with anxiety.</p>
<p>Inside the pavilion, the leader of the Imperial Kiln Guild—<strong>Grand Kiln-Sovereign Ci Xuan</strong>, a white-robed old man at the <strong>Third Revolution of the Life-and-Death Wheel (Second Life-Renewal)</strong>—was staring helplessly at the sealed bronze-and-porcelain mouth of the mile-long <strong>Imperial Celadon Dragon-Kiln</strong>!</p>
<p>Why? Because across the thirty-foot-wide <strong>Main Porcelain Kiln-Gate</strong>—which served as the lock to the subterranean <strong>Porcelain-Bone Catacombs</strong> beneath the city—ran a terrifying, glowing crimson web of <strong>108 Crazed Ice-Crackle Fractures</strong> leaking black-red <strong>Earth-Core Nirvana Fire &amp; Corpse-Poison</strong>!</p>
