Fifty thousand miles west of the Ten-Thousand-Forge Star Ring, the western edge of the Outer Star-Continent crumbled into a bottomless cosmic ocean where silver-blue nebulae and liquid starlight replaced seawater:

The Shattered Constellation Sea (Sui-Xing Hai)!

During the primordial war at the dawn of the Upper Star-Soul Realm, twenty-eight ancient Artificial Constellation Arrays—built by the pre-epoch Star-Cast Emperors to channel energy from the deep universe—had collapsed into this million-mile sea. Over the epochs, the core engines of those fallen constellations had petrified into floating Constellation Reefs, inside which slumbered priceless Seventh-Order and Eighth-Order Constellation Origin-Cores!

As the two-mile-long Primordial Star-Ark sailed silently across the glowing silver-blue nebula waves of the Shattered Constellation Sea, Lu Chen stood on the forward deck beside Su Qingxue, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, Chu Kuang, Elder Tie, and his parents Lu Shan & Meng Wan.

"Look at those floating bronze-and-star-crystal reefs rising out of the liquid starlight ahead," Su Wan'er murmured in awe, her Rank 6 Inscription Grandmaster eyes tracing the colossal rune-lines half-submerged in the glowing sea. "Each reef is actually a broken gear-segment of an ancient Celestial Armillary Sphere the size of a mountain range!"

"More importantly," Lu Chen smiled, his antiquities-restorer perception under Prism Stillness locking onto a cluster of nine interconnected constellation reefs three hundred miles inside the Deep Nebula Zone, "that nine-reef cluster ahead is the remnant of the Ancient Nine-Luminaries Constellation Engine. Inside its Nine-Palace core chamber sit twenty-four intact Seventh-Order and Eighth-Order Constellation Origin-Cores—enough for all six of you to ignite your 100-Mile Embryonic Inner Worlds, and enough for Qingxue and me to evolve our nine suns and nine moons into the Lesser World King Realm (10,000-Mile Minor World)!"
