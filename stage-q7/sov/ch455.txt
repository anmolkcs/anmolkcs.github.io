<p>From the nineteenth to the twenty-fifth of the eleventh month, Ash-Brook Village operated like a besieged fortress racing against an hourglass.</p>
<p>Every day for fourteen hours, the blue-white flames of the <strong>Black-Gold Pine Charcoal</strong> roared inside the smithy hearth.</p>
<p>With <strong>Old Tie Shan's</strong> right collarbone now knitted firmly enough that he could stand at the side of the anvil wielding his twelve-pound guide-hammer with his single scarred hand—calling every heat and tapping every slag-line with forty years of master-smith precision—and Lu Chen swinging the sixty-pound heavy sledge with <strong>Complete Tier Two</strong> muscles backed by his newly drawn <strong>Lower-Body Bowstring Sinews</strong>, the speed of their forging astonished the entire village.</p>
<p>Every time Lu Chen stamped his left heel onto the oak treadle and drove the sixty-pound sledge down, the twelve drawn sinew lines in his feet, knees, and hips snapped like twin ballista arms—launching the heavy hammer up and down with a ferocious, elastic whip that squeezed the spiral cold-shatter slag out of a thirty-pound billet in just nine blows!</p>
<p>And every night after the forge was banked at midnight, while the rest of the village slept, Lu Chen coated his spine, shoulders, elbows, and wrists in boiling <strong>Sinew-Suppling Paste</strong> and locked himself into the <strong>Four-Cornered Iron-Pine Bow-Rack</strong> for two hours under the freezing stars.</p>
<p>Night by night, the crisp <em>twang</em> of awakening sinews echoed softly behind the lean-to: - By the twenty-first night, the <strong>Twelve Spinal &amp; Torso Sinew Lines</strong> (from the tailbone up the lumbar, thoracic, and cervical vertebrae to the base of the skull) had lengthened and turned gold-white—bringing him to <strong>Twenty-Four Drawn Sinews</strong>; - By the twenty-fourth night, six of the <strong>Twelve Upper-Limb Sinew Lines</strong> across his shoulder blades and upper arms had awakened—bringing him to <strong>Thirty of the Thirty-Six Sinews (Half-Step Tier Three — 3,200 jin explosive force)</strong>, leaving only the final six deep forearm-to-finger tendons waiting for a final life-or-death catalyst!</p>
<p>At noon on the twenty-fifth day of the eleventh month, inside the crowded smithy shed, Old Tie Shan lifted the <strong>eightieth ten-jin bar of nine-folded Grade-One Refined Cold-Slag Iron</strong> out of the brine trough with his tongs and laid it atop the slate rack!</p>
<p><em>CLANG!</em></p>
<p>Eighty gleaming, oil-blue bars of flawless nine-folded steel-iron—weighing a combined <strong>eight hundred and twelve jin</strong> on the village stone balance—sat stacked in eight neat rows of ten!</p>
<p>Around the shed, <strong>Uncle Shi</strong>, <strong>Big-Tall Zhao</strong>, <strong>Old Ma</strong>, <strong>Widow Qin</strong>, <strong>Granny Liu</strong>, and fourteen-year-old <strong>Shi Tou</strong> let out a long, collective breath that turned into a roar of triumph.</p>
<p>Old Tie Shan laid his single scarred palm flat across the top row of warm iron bars. For a long moment, the sixty-year-old blacksmith didn't speak; he simply ran his calloused fingers over the smooth, slag-free edges of all eighty bars, each one stamped near the heel with the small mark of a <strong>Broken Spear-Collar and an Anvil</strong>.</p>
<p>"Eight hundred and twelve jin of Grade-One Cold-Slag Iron," Old Tie Shan said, his voice ringing deep and clear across the shed. "Finished twenty-five days before the twentieth of the twelfth month! Not even the master smiths of the <strong>Black-Rock Garrison Armory</strong> could find a cold-shatter flaw in a single bar on this rack! Tomorrow, we shoe the two big oak sledges with iron runners—and we prepare to march down to the Black-Slag Canyon Tally-House to bring <strong>Tie Niu</strong> out of Pit #7!"</p>
