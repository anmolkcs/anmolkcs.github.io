Six hundred miles west of Loom-Star City, the lush mist-valleys of the Central-South Prefecture abruptly gave way to a blinding, snow-white expanse of crystallized salt flats and jagged halite pillars that stretched to the horizon.

Beyond those salt flats lay a geological wonder of the Sky Spill Continent: the Inland Abyssal Salt-Sea.

Three thousand miles wide and bottomless at its center, the Abyssal Salt-Sea was the remnant of a primordial ocean trapped when the continent's tectonic plates rose ten millennia ago. Because its water was ten times denser and saltier than ordinary seawater—and saturated with heavy Subterranean Brine-Magnetic Essence—ordinary wooden ships rotted into sponge within three days of touching its waves, while flying cultivators who lingered less than a hundred feet above the water found their Xiantian True Essence weighed down by three-fold magnetic brine-drag!

At the eastern shore stood White-Salt Port, a fortified harbor city carved directly out of a three-hundred-foot cliff of pink-and-white rock salt.

As Lu Chen, Su Wan'er, Murong Zi, and Chu Kuang walked down the salt-paved wharf of White-Salt Port, they saw dozens of strange, flat-bottomed vessels moored along the stone piers. These ships were not built of timber, but of riveted plates of Black Brine-Iron coated in silver-gray Salt-Repelling Runic Lacquer, powered by giant paddle-wheels turned by domesticated Third-Order Armored Brine-Turtles.

Yet today, half the piers in White-Salt Port were stained with dried blood and scorched black by eerie green bone-fire. Hundreds of salt-miners and coastal fishermen sat bandaged along the seawall while harbor guards patrolled nervously with drawn crossbows.

"Even White-Salt Port's outer piers were hit?" Chu Kuang frowned, resting a hand on the hilt of his nine-ringed golden broadsaber as he looked at a half-sunken Brine-Iron patrol boat whose hull had been pierced by a six-foot harpoon carved from fossilized whale bone.

An old harbor captain with a salt-crusted beard and an Early Xiantian cultivation—Captain Cang Hai, who had been hired in advance by Master Weaver Shen Qinghe's jade token—hurried up the pier to greet Lu Chen's party.

"Grandmaster Lu, Miss Su!" Captain Cang Hai bowed respectfully upon seeing the Gold-Dragon Rank 4 Inscription Grandmaster Badge on Lu Chen's chest and Shen Qinghe's seal. "Thank the heavens you arrived before the noon neap tide! Five days ago, the Nether-Bone Armada—a fleet of seven bone-hulled warships built from the skeletons of ancient Fourth-Order sea leviathans and commanded by the rogue Extreme Xiantian sea-lord Gu Hai (Bone-Sea Sovereign)—emerged from the central Sunken Halite Trench!"

Captain Cang Hai pointed out toward the shimmering, lead-gray horizon of the Salt-Sea:

"They've blockaded the Seven-Spire Salt Islands ninety miles offshore, seized three hundred salt-mining families as living blood-sacrifices to dredge an ancient Bronze Dynasty Tide-Anchor from the seabed, and declared that any ship entering the Salt-Sea without paying fifty Middle-Grade True Essence Stones will be sunk on sight!"

Hearing that three hundred innocent salt-mining families were being held on the Seven-Spire Salt Islands as blood-sacrifices, Lu Chen's dark eyes turned cold and steady.

Not only did the Millennium Halite-Marrow Lotuses needed for Elder Tie's arm and his own Middle Xiantian breakthrough grow inside the Sunken Halite Trench right beneath the Seven-Spire Salt Islands, but as a martial artist, he would never stand by while corsairs slaughtered three hundred mortal and Houtian families for an excavation ritual.

"Captain Cang, is your Brine-Iron ship ready to sail?" Lu Chen asked calmly.

"My vessel, the Iron-Scale Osprey, is moored at Pier #3," Captain Cang Hai gritted his teeth. "My nephew and twenty of my crew are among the captives on the Seven-Spire Islands! However, the Nether-Bone Armada's patrol ships use Rank 3 Peak Corrosive Bone-Fire Cannons that melt straight through ordinary Brine-Iron hulls in three salvos!"

"Give me two hours on Pier #3 before the tide turns," Lu Chen said, stepping aboard the sixty-foot Iron-Scale Osprey. "After I re-inscribe your hull, not even a Rank 4 cannon will scratch her paint."
