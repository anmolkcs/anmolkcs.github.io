At dawn the next morning, before visiting the Ancestral Spear-Sovereign Grotto or hunting for the First Saint-Grade Lotus Treasure, Lu Chen did the one thing closest to his heart.

In the center of the sunny courtyard of Warm-Spring Immortal Manor on Seventh Spear Peak—right beside a steaming hot-spring pool and an ancient golden osmanthus tree that looked just like their yard on Broken Spear Peak—Lu Chen laid down one hundred High-Grade True Essence Stones and thirty-six slabs of Grade 5 Star-Void Jade.

Using the newly unlocked Compendium of Rank 6 Cross-Domain Spatial Portals from the Tenth Technique Bubble, Lu Chen spent four hours engraving a permanent, self-sustaining Rank 6 Sub-Space Relay Anchor that boosted the range and stability of his Rank 5 Peak Two-Way Spatial Teleportation Jade by ten times—bridging the twelve-thousand-mile distance across the Astral Storm-Trench to Broken Spear Peak in the Southern Region!

Then, holding the communication jade in his left hand, Lu Chen sent a warm voice transmission across the void:

"Father, Mother—the courtyard on Seventh Spear Peak is ready, and I've already set up the kitchen stove and the practice spear rack by the hot spring! Step onto the jade platform in Warm-Spring Courtyard #1 and activate the Spatial Jade!"

HUMMMMM—FLASH!

Three breaths later, a gentle silver-gold spatial doorway opened smoothly in the middle of the osmanthus courtyard on Seventh Spear Peak!

Stepping out of the silver-gold light hand-in-hand—carrying three spatial hampers of freshly pickled bamboo shoots, Cloudstride cured ham, and Meng Wan's favorite purple-clay soup pots—were forty-two-year-old Lu Shan and forty-year-old Meng Wan!

"Chen'er! Wan'er! Murong Zi! Chu Kuang!"

As Meng Wan and Lu Shan stepped onto the warm jade flagstones and took in the breathtaking fifty-thousand-foot waterfalls of liquid starlight and the tenfold Central Domain Innate Origin Qi filling the courtyard, their Early Revolving Core (Flawless Gold-Jade Core) auras expanded joyfully!

"My goodness, just breathing the air in this courtyard for three breaths feels better than swallowing a Rank 4 spirit pill!" Lu Shan laughed heartily, hugging Lu Chen's shoulders before inspecting the weapon rack where Lu Chen had placed a newly forged 2,400-jin Grade 4 Star-Iron Practice Spear for him. "And that spatial doorway didn't even shake the soup pots in your mother's hamper—one step and we crossed ten thousand miles from Broken Spear Peak to the Central Imperial Domain!"

"With this Two-Way Spatial Portal anchored permanently in our yard, Father and Mother can live right here with us in the Central Domain, and whenever you want to visit Elder Tie or Uncle Su back on Broken Spear Peak, it's just a single step through the courtyard gate," Lu Chen smiled warmly, helping his mother carry the purple-clay soup pots into the spacious kitchen pavilion while Su Wan'er happily helped Meng Wan arrange spirit-tea leaves on the veranda.

That noon, sitting around the osmanthus table in the Central Imperial Domain eating his mother's hot braised bamboo-shoot pork and drinking his father's spirit wine, Lu Chen felt his martial heart settle into rock-solid perfection.
