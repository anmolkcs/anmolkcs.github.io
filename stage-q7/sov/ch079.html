Around the Heavenly Dragon Roll Arena, over two thousand inner disciples from all seven peaks had gathered as word spread that Broken Spear Peak’s sixteen-year-old freshman Lu Chen had issued an official ranking challenge to #48 Situ Hao of Alkaid Sword Peak!

Standing in the center of the hundred-yard star-stone arena in his silver Alkaid Peak robes, Situ Hao (Late Houtian Realm, 48,000 jin of True Essence strike power) sneered as he saw Lu Chen step onto the stage accompanied by Fang Shi, Chu Kuang, Su Wan’er, and Murong Zi:

“Lu Chen, I admit you have some talent in Inscription runes, and you beat a few country bumpkins in the Five-Kingdom Trial a month ago,” Situ Hao said coldly, drawing his Grade 3 Middle-Tier Alkaid-Star Longsword as a dazzling halo of seven silver sword-stars lit up around his body. “However, the Heavenly Dragon Roll is the stage of veteran Late and Peak Houtian geniuses! Today I’ll defeat you in ten moves—and Broken Spear Peak can forget about touching a single piece of Star-Iron Ore this year!”

“One move is enough to take back Senior Brother Fang’s rank and our peak’s ore,” Lu Chen said calmly, lifting his 1,220-jin Purple-Thunder Heavy Spear with one hand.
